The top excerpts gave the government-sector component of GDP, not the all-industry total. The excerpt with the total GDP series ranked third.

The same weakness showed up in two other places:

The revised instruction requires values to match the asked-for quantity. It also says that series titles or dataset descriptions without numbers do not count.
