[Governor Sheriff Oborevwori has ordered immediate medical intervention and urgent measures to address the Trans-Forcados oil spill affecting communities in Warri South-West LGA. Speaking during an inspection of Odidi Community yesterday, the Governor directed medical teams to assess residents and the health impact of the pollution, while directing Heritage Energy Operational Services Limited to provide medical support and relief materials. He disclosed that affected communities had risen from 86 to 216, with fishing and livelihoods severely impacted. He also directed the Commissioners for Oil and Gas and Environment to monitor the technical operations and cleanup and ensure the company fulfils its commitments. Governor Oborevwori stressed that the response must include environmental restoration and protection of lives and livelihoods, not just compensation. He also approved a landing jetty for Odidi and directed the Warri South-West LGA Council to construct a modern health centre, while calling for dialogue between the company and host communities.](https://www.facebook.com/100069033705823/videos/1081465614876572/?__so__=watchlist&__rv__=video_home_www_playlist_video_list&__cft__[0]=AZgqFSrUq9cpUwfWpd00-M264slItDupDC7rl5sVfLG_CD-3iFJWT3H3SzaNOHxbO-qYmzPJK5VE8uEnzBbKSmmeNfBodFZa865TL3WqIyeXs1Uou8dh02SZrKZcuE9SGEFeCz1HMSl4yGPWCk6ym4hYz2HEUWnwmnXr8PKiDTTs56SZyWvMgIWkRZz2CXFRAbcJi34kU3Q)

[Governor Sheriff Oborevwori has directed the development of a permanent solution to the washed-out section of the East-West Road at Agbarho, Ughelli North Local Government Area, following about six consecutive days of heavy rainfall that caused the affected section of the highway to collapse. The Governor gave the directive on Saturday during an inspection of the damaged section, where he said the immediate priority was to restore traffic while work begins towards a lasting solution. He commended Julius Berger Nigeria Plc for its swift response and directed the company to undertake the necessary technical studies to determine the appropriate intervention. Julius Berger has commenced surveys and hydraulic assessments to determine whether a mini-bridge or multi-cell culvert would provide the most suitable permanent solution. Governor Oborevwori said the State Government is ready to fund the remedial works, stressing that the intervention must adequately accommodate the volume of water passing through the area. Meanwhile, emergency works are ongoing to restore traffic, with Julius Berger assuring that both lanes will be reopened to motorists shortly.](https://www.facebook.com/100069033705823/videos/2068136360490826/?__so__=watchlist&__rv__=video_home_www_playlist_video_list&__cft__[0]=AZjIpooJ2MdLP-MpM6MzRqc8p6x4pTUulCt2EKQfv2tnNVvT1_vrmTM6WbXHlwYlo1z5MuY6i-T_g-zyYnPdWjbjvZ_anbwoYd04Kv-Jgp_Iy5ITT6PG3-Ge_I_-cAn8NO_urypKC0J6zF4Bexczpu20YJIhGG4-xBr0Zcs9sZRTrwU0CUkMlOVXqyytri246-wYZ4rj5Ec)
