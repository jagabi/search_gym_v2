## Frequently asked questions

##### Who is Cookiebot by Usercentrics?

Cookiebot by Usercentrics provides a plug-and-play compliance solution built around unrivaled scanning technology. It detects and controls all cookies and trackers in use on websites and automatically manages end-user consents.

Our solution fits any type of website. Organizations can automate all data privacy requirements online while providing easy and fast consent management for users.

Cookiebot Consent Management Platform (CMP) is a self-serve cloud-based service provided by Usercentrics. It enables automated compliance with global data privacy laws like the EU’s GDPR, California’s CCPA/CPRA, Brazil’s LGPD and many others.

Read more information [here](https://www.cookiebot.com/en/about/).

##### What does a Consent Management Platform (CMP) do on websites?

A consent management platform is a technology that can be implemented on websites and other applications to collect and process personal data from website users and customers. It enables companies to collect and store legally valid consent and helps the end user protect and maintain control over their personal data.

##### What is personal data?

Personal data or personal information is information that can be used to identify a person, directly or indirectly, alone or in combination with other pieces of data.

Different data privacy regulations have their own specific definitions of personal data, and there can be additional subcategories that require special handling, like sensitive personal data.

##### What happens if end users don’t give their consent?

If end users decide not to give their consent on a website for processing their personal data, the cookies and trackers that collect data will be blocked. No personal data will be collected, processed or shared with third-party providers.

End users’ consent preferences are saved in their web browser and retrieved on future visits to the website.
