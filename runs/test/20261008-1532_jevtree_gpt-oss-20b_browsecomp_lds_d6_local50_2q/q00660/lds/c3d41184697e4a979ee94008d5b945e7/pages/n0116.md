# The Churchillian and Finest Hour

A digital archive of past issues of _[The Churchillian](https://www.nationalchurchillmuseum.org/the-churchillian-magazine.html),_ the magazine of America's National Churchill Museum is an excellent resource. Within the pages of _The Churchillian_ you will find information about the Museum, its past events and activities. Additionally, you will find a publication with articles about Sir Winston Churchill's life and times written by some of the foremost historians and scholars in the world.

America's National Churchill Museum currently contributes to _[Finest Hour](https://www.winstonchurchill.org/publications/finest-hour/finest-hour-index)_. All Museum members receive this _Finest Hour_, which is published quarterly.
