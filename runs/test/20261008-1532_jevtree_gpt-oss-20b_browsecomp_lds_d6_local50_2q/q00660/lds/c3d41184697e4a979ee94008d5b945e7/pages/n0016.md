Lately, I’ve been really focused on diving deeper into how to use AI tools effectively.

As I learn new things, I save everything in my second brain.

Anyway, I wanted to share a few things that I’ve found incredibly useful to keep in mind:

*   A Markdown (.md) file is better than a PDF for Claude. It uses fewer tokens and the LLM reads it much better.

*   AI will always give you an answer. That doesn't mean the answer is right. Double-check everything.

*   Always verify facts, dates, quotes, and names.
