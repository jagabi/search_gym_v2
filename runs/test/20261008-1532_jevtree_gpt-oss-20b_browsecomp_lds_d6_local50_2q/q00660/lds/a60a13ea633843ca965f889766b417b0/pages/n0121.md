(Redirected from [Zarya (disambiguation)](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Zarya_(disambiguation)&redirect=no "Zarya (disambiguation)"))

**Zarya** may refer to:

*   [Zorya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zorya "Zorya"), personification of dawn in Slavic mythology
*   [Zarya (antenna)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zarya_(antenna) "Zarya (antenna)"), a type of medium-wave broadcasting antenna used in former Soviet Union
*   [Zarya (ISS module)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zarya_(ISS_module) "Zarya (ISS module)") is a module of the International Space Station.
*   [_Zarya_ (magazine)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zarya_(magazine) "Zarya (magazine)"), a Slavophile 1869–1872 magazine
*   [_Zarya_ (non-magnetic ship)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zarya_(non-magnetic_ship) "Zarya (non-magnetic ship)"), a Soviet research ship built in 1953 and used to research Earth's magnetic field
*   [_Zarya_ (opera)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zarya_(opera) "Zarya (opera)"), an 1877 opera by Ella Adayevskaya
*   [Zarya (_Overwatch_)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zarya_(Overwatch) "Zarya (Overwatch)"), a player character from the video games _Overwatch_ and _Heroes of the Storm_
*   [_Zarya_ (polar ship)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zarya_(polar_ship) "Zarya (polar ship)"), a Russian research ship sent to the Arctic during 1900–1902
*   [_Zarya_ (publication)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zarya_(publication) "Zarya (publication)"), a Marxist publication
*   [Zarya, Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zarya,_Russia "Zarya, Russia"), several rural localities in Russia
*   [Zarya Moonwolf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Mysticons_characters#Zarya_Moonwolf "List of Mysticons characters"), a main character in Nickelodeon's show _[Mysticons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mysticons "Mysticons")_
*   [Zarya (space capsule)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zarya_(space_capsule) "Zarya (space capsule)"), the name of a late 1980s project for a Soyuz spacecraft replacement
*   Zarya Voroshilovgrad, former name of [FC Zorya Luhansk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Zorya_Luhansk "FC Zorya Luhansk"), a Ukrainian soccer team
*   Fictional starship _Zarya_ in the 1974 Soviet science fiction film _[Moscow-Cassiopeia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow-Cassiopeia "Moscow-Cassiopeia")_ and its sequel,_[Teens in the Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teens\_in\_the\_Universe "Teens in the Universe")_
