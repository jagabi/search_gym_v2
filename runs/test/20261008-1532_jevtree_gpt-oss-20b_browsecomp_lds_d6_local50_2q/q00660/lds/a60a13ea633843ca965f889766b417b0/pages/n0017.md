**Zaria**[Listen](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/d/d0/LL-Q56475_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-Zaria.wav/LL-Q56475_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-Zaria.wav.mp3 "Play audio")[ⓘ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:LL-Q56475_(hau)-Eunice_Ameh-Zaria.wav "File:LL-Q56475 (hau)-Eunice Ameh-Zaria.wav") (formerly **Zazzau**) is a [metropolitan city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_cities_by_population "List of Nigerian cities by population") in [Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigeria "Nigeria"), located within four [local government areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_government_area "Local government area") in [Kaduna State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaduna_State "Kaduna State"). It contains Nigeria's largest university, [Ahmadu Bello University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmadu_Bello_University "Ahmadu Bello University"), and various tertiary institutions including the [Federal College of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_College_of_Education,_Zaria "Federal College of Education, Zaria") (FCE Zaria), [Nigerian College of Aviation Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerian_College_of_Aviation_Technology "Nigerian College of Aviation Technology"), Nigerian Institute of Transport Technology, Nigeria Institute of Leather and Science Technology and [Nuhu Bamalli Polytechnic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuhu_Bamalli_Polytechnic "Nuhu Bamalli Polytechnic"). Department of Agriculture Ahmed Bello University Zaria. Ameer Shehu Idris College of Advanced Diploma.

From the 2006 population census, Zaria was estimated to have 736,000 people.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-:4-2) It is home to the [Emir of Zazzau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emir_of_Zazzau "Emir of Zazzau").

## History

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Zaria&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")]

Further information: [Kingdom of Zazzau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Zazzau "Kingdom of Zazzau")

[![Image 14](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7d/Zaria_130085145_08d4889953.jpg/250px-Zaria_130085145_08d4889953.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Zaria_130085145_08d4889953.jpg)

Guard at the palace of the emir

Zaria, initially known as Zazzau, was the capital of the [Hausa kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_Kingdoms "Hausa Kingdoms") of [Zazzau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Zazzau "Kingdom of Zazzau").[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-britannica-3) Zazzau is thought to have been founded in or about 1536 and in the late 16th century it was renamed after Queen [Amina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amina_(Queen_of_Zazzau) "Amina (Queen of Zazzau)")'s sister, Zaria.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-:0-4) Human settlement predates the rise of Zazzau, as the region, like some of its neighbors, had a history of sedentary Hausa settlement, with institutional market exchange and farming.

Zaria was the most southern of the Hausa city-states. It was a trading destination for Saharan caravans as well as a prominent city in the Hausa slave trade. In the late 1450s, Islam arrived in Zaria by the way of its sister Habe cities, [Kano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kano_(city) "Kano (city)") and [Katsina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katsina_(city) "Katsina (city)"). Along with [Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam "Islam"), trade flourished between the cities as traders brought camel caravans filled with salt in exchange for slaves and grain. The city-state's power peaked under Queen [Amina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amina_(Queen_of_Zazzau) "Amina (Queen of Zazzau)") whose military campaigns established a tributary region including the kingdoms of [Kano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultanate_of_Kano "Sultanate of Kano") and [Katsina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katsina_(city) "Katsina (city)"). At the end of the 16th century, after Queen Amina's death, Zaria fell under the influence of the [Jukun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jukun_people_(West_Africa) "Jukun people (West Africa)") Kingdom and eventually became a tributary state itself.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-britannica-3) Between the fifteenth and sixteenth century the kingdom became a tributary state of the [Songhai Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songhai_Empire "Songhai Empire"). In 1805 it was captured by the [Fulani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fula_people "Fula people") during the [Fulani Jihad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulani_Jihad "Fulani Jihad"). British forces led by [Frederick Lugard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_Lugard "Frederick Lugard") took the city in 1901.[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-5)

A French hostage of the [Islamist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamist "Islamist") group [Ansaru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ansaru "Ansaru"), held captive at Zaria,[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-6) escaped in 2013 and reached a police station in the city.

In December 2015, Nigeria's military was reported to have killed 300 [Shia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shia_Islam "Shia Islam") Muslims and buried their bodies in a mass grave, following their physical attack and blockade of the Chief of Army Staff, General Tukur Yusuf Burtai in an area they claimed to own as they don't recognize the leadership of the Republic of Nigeria. Although the government denies the killing part, it has been described as a [massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Zaria_massacre "2015 Zaria massacre").[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-:5-7)

## Cityscape

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Zaria&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Cityscape")]

[![Image 16](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/88/Zaria_130085554_1ac87fb1d9.jpg/250px-Zaria_130085554_1ac87fb1d9.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Zaria_130085554_1ac87fb1d9.jpg)

Children playing in one of the streets of Zaria

The old part of the city, known as Birnin Zazzau[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-Gihring-8) or Zaria City, was originally surrounded by walls and fortress, which have been mostly removed.[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-ABU-9) The Emir's palace is in the old city. In the old city and the adjacent Tudun Wada neighbourhood people typically reside in traditional [adobe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adobe "Adobe") compounds. These two neighborhoods are predominantly occupied by the indigenous [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_language "Hausa language").[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-Gihring-8)

There is great variety in the architecture of Zaria, with buildings made of clay in the Hausa style juxtaposed with modern, multi-storied university and government buildings.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-:0-4)

Silk-cotton tree is one of the largest trees in Zazzau emirate generally and the tree has played an important role in the spiritual and economic lives of the peoples who live in Zaria especially people of Anguwan Kahu who makes Kahu for the Emirs, district heads, ward heads and village heads. silk-cotton-tree-scientific-name-is-ceiba-pentandra-under-blue-sky.

Anguwan Kahu was known to be a place of business where it use cotton to make local mattresses, pillows, [Horse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horse "Horse") shirts etc.

Wakilin Kahu Zazzau is the head of Anguwan Kahu people and their representative at the emir's palace.

The ward of Anguwan Liman is located north of the Zaria palace.[_[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation\_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")_][[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-10)

## Transport and economy

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Zaria&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Transport and economy")]

Zaria's economy is primarily based on agriculture. Staples are [guinea corn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinea_corn "Guinea corn") and [millet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millet "Millet"). Cash crops include [cotton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton "Cotton"), [groundnuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peanut "Peanut") and [tobacco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobacco "Tobacco").[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-britannica-3) Not only is Zaria a market town for the surrounding area, it is the home of artisans from traditional crafts like leather work, [dyeing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dye "Dye") and cap making, to tinkers, printshops and furniture makers.[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-Gihring-8) Zaria is also the center of a textile industry that for over 200 years has made elaborately hand-embroidered robes that are worn by men throughout Nigeria and West Africa.[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-:6-11)

[![Image 17](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/40/Life_in_Zaria%2C_Kaduna.jpg/250px-Life_in_Zaria%2C_Kaduna.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Life_in_Zaria,_Kaduna.jpg)

Zaria City Road, Kaduna

Because Zaria is north of the rail junction at [Kaduna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaduna_(city) "Kaduna (city)"), it has equal rail access to the seaports at [Lagos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagos "Lagos") and [Port Harcourt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_Harcourt "Port Harcourt"). However, only the railway between Lagos and Kano is functional, as the eastern line of [Nigeria's rail network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rail_transport_in_Nigeria "Rail transport in Nigeria") is not operational. This means that Zaria currently has rail access to Lagos and [Kano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kano_(city) "Kano (city)") to the north but not Port Harcourt.[[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-:7-12)[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-13)

From 1914 to 1927, Zaria was the [break-of-gauge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Break-of-gauge "Break-of-gauge") junction station for the [Bauchi Light Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bauchi_Light_Railway "Bauchi Light Railway") to the tin mines at [Jos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos "Jos").[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-14)

## Education

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Zaria&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Education")]

[![Image 18](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/1e/Ahmadu_bello_university_senate.jpg/250px-Ahmadu_bello_university_senate.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Ahmadu_bello_university_senate.jpg)

Senate building of the [Ahmadu Bello University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmadu_Bello_University "Ahmadu Bello University")

Zaria is home to [Ahmadu Bello University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmadu_Bello_University "Ahmadu Bello University"), the largest [university](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University "University") in [Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigeria "Nigeria") and the second largest on the African continent. The institution is very prominent in the fields of Agriculture, Science, Finance, Medicine and Law.[[15]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-15) The school is known for the large number of elites from the region that passed through its academic buildings and counts among its alumni five who were Nigerian heads of state, including the late president [Umaru Musa Yar'Adua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umaru_Musa_Yar'Adua "Umaru Musa Yar'Adua").[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-16)

Zaria is also the base for the [Nigerian College of Aviation Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerian_College_of_Aviation_Technology "Nigerian College of Aviation Technology"),[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-17) National Research Institute for Chemical Technology,[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-18)[Nigerian Army Depot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depot_Nigerian_Army,_Zaria "Depot Nigerian Army, Zaria"),[[19]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-19) Nigerian Military school, Bassawa Baracks, Federal college of Education Zaria.[[20]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-20) Some historic secondary schools in the adjoining town of [Wusasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wusasa "Wusasa"), where the former Head of the Federal Military Government [Yakubu Gowon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yakubu_Gowon "Yakubu Gowon") resides are the St. Bartholomew's School and Science School Kufena, formerly known as St. Paul's College, also MAISS-GIWA a school established by The Emir of Zazzau Dr. [Shehu Idris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shehu_Idris "Shehu Idris") is situated there. Barewa College (formerly Katsina middle school) and Alhudahuda college are other famous secondary schools in the city.[[21]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-21)

## Traditional festivals

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Zaria&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Traditional festivals")]

[![Image 19](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/2a/Hawan_Daushe_in_Zaria_2021.jpg/250px-Hawan_Daushe_in_Zaria_2021.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Hawan_Daushe_in_Zaria_2021.jpg)

Durbar celebration in Zaria 2021

Zaria is among the northern cities that celebrates the annual cultural durbar festivals in Nigeria.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-:4-2) The festival is celebrated twice a year which marks the end of Ramadan and also coincides with the Muslim festivals of [Eid al-Adha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eid_al-Adha "Eid al-Adha") and [Eid al-Fitr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eid_al-Fitr "Eid al-Fitr") respectively.[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-:6-11) In Zaria, the festival is celebrated in phases. The first day, known as Hawan Sallah, consists of the eid prayers and the subsequent tour by the emir around the city from the Eid ground to his palace in the company of District Heads and the royal guards, while the second and third days known as Hawan Bariki Sallah and Hawan Daushe respectively are for the last tour by the Emir around the city for the festival.[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-:5-7)[[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-:7-12)

## Environment

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Zaria&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Environment")]

### Air quality

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Zaria&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Air quality")]

A 2019 study of [air pollution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_pollution "Air pollution") in Zaria found that pollution concentrations were within the expected national standards for air pollution.[[25]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-25)

### Land use

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Zaria&action=edit&section=10 "Edit section: Land use")]

[![Image 20](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7f/Zaria_view_01.jpg/250px-Zaria_view_01.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Zaria_view_01.jpg)

This is a place in Zaria City, Kaduna State, high way from Kaduna to Zaria to Kano

Zaria's geography and previous land use meant that much of the city's historical land cover was barren.[[26]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-:1-26) A 2020 study found that barren land decreased from 1990 to 2020 while built environment increased 66 percent and vegetative land increased by 29%.[[26]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-:1-26) Vegetation had been decreasing from 1990 to 2005, but the study area found a dramatic increase due to [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture_in_Nigeria "Agriculture in Nigeria") and [reforestation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reforestation_in_Nigeria "Reforestation in Nigeria") afterwards.[[26]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-:1-26)[Predictive modeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Predictive_modelling "Predictive modelling") based on local policy and urban development trends suggested that increase in urban and vegetative land cover would continue through 2050.[[26]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-:1-26)

### Water supply and sanitation

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Zaria&action=edit&section=11 "Edit section: Water supply and sanitation")]

[![Image 21](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/03/Zaria_source_of_water.jpg/250px-Zaria_source_of_water.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Zaria_source_of_water.jpg)

Water provided to the city comes from [Kaduna State Water Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaduna_State_Water_Board?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kaduna State Water Board (page does not exist)").[[27]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-:2-27) As of 2012, the city of Zaria had 30% access to clean water supply.[[27]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-:2-27) The [African Development Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Development_Fund "African Development Fund") issued funding for an expansion project in 2013 for 100 million dollars of $480 million.[[27]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-:2-27)[[28]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-28) The project had problems with some of its local contractors, resulting in the African Development Bank banning four companies from further participating in bank funding projects.[[29]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-:3-29) As of August 2020, 60% of water in the system was unaccounted for because of illegal connections, poor metering practices, and poor maintenance.[[29]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria#cite_note-:3-29)
