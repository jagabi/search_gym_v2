We are specialist conservators of paintings, working with private collectors, museums, auction houses, galleries, corporate collections and artists’ estates.

Based in London, in 2025 the studio moved to larger premises near to Oval, where our team of conservators enjoy state-of-the-art facilities and have space to treat large-scale artworks.

Our studio’s work on this programme has made it possible to reattribute artworks to Botticelli, Rubens, Bruegel, Titian and others.
