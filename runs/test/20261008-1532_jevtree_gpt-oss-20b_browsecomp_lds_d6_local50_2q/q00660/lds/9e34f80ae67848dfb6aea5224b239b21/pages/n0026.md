## a studio for all styles

## a studio for all needs

Our meticulous [team](https://www.simongillespie.com/meet-the-team)of conservators and specialists breathe new life into both historic and contemporary artwork, using a combination of modern methods and traditional techniques. We undertake every element of conservation and care work from cleaning, retouching and consolidation to repairing large-scale structural damage. All our senior conservators are Icon-accredited. We specialise in [picture authentication](https://www.simongillespie.com/authentication-attribution), contributing to the discovery of previously unknown work by Titian, Rubens, Rembrandt and more. We also help with all your aftercare, hanging, storage, transportation and collection needs.

## a studio for all people

All conservation work is about people. It’s a privileged connection between artist and conservator, between conservator and client, between client and art lover. Through small acts of care, we create a dialogue between the past and the present, the present and the future. We believe in nurturing long-term, meaningful relationships with our team, our clients and the [artists](https://www.simongillespie.com/artists). We are also passionate about creating opportunities for the conservators of the future, with [workshops](https://www.simongillespie.com/workshops), lectures and [internships](https://www.simongillespie.com/internships). We seek to take you behind the canvas, showing you how we work in [our Stories](https://www.simongillespie.com/stories)here on the site and in monthly [breakfasts](https://www.simongillespie.com/breakfasts)and other events. We look forward to welcoming you to Simon Gillespie Studio.
