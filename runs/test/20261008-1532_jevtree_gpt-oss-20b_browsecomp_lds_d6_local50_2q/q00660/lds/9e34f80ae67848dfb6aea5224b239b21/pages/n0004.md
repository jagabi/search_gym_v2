Title: Simon Gillespie Studio on Instagram: "Yusuf Grillo is widely celebrated as one of Nigeria’s most influential artists. He attended the Nigerian College of Arts, graduating in 1960. As a member of the Zaria Arts Society, Grillo played a role in synthesising indigenous Nigerian themes with European techniques. His practice left a mark on the development of modern painting in the country. 

Treatment of this significant painting by Yusuf Grillo focused on stabilising its layered structure while preserving its original character.

The work was in an unstable condition, largely due to previous consolidation issues, with flaking paint, weak adhesion, and structural distortion across the support.

After careful testing, a suitable adhesive system was used to secure lifting paint, followed by controlled heat and consolidation to restore stability.

Gentle cleaning on both the front and reverse helped reduce surface dirt and staining, while respecting the sensitivity of the original materials.

Structural distortions were eased using natural adhesives and controlled pressure, improving overall stability. Losses were filled and retouched to visually reintegrate the image, and the painting was finally housed in a bespoke frame to ensure long-term support."
