Title: 4 comments | THE SURFACE IS NEVER THE FULL STORY 

My process is deliberately repetitive.

I work through accumulation. A mark sits on another mark. A layer covers another layer. Something disappears and then returns.

Sometimes I don’t know exactly what will emerge.
That is part of the process.
The surface becomes a record of decisions, revisions, concealments and discoveries.

In this sense, the painting behaves like memory.
We remember selectively.
We forget selectively.

Some things remain clear. Others become fragments. Some memories are covered by newer experiences but never completely disappear.

The same thing happens culturally.
A building may be destroyed, but photographs remain.
A ritual may disappear, but its symbols remain.
A language may weaken, but certain words survive.
A story may no longer be told in its original form, but traces of it remain inside the way people behave.

The archive is not always a book.
Sometimes the archive is a wall.
Sometimes it is a pattern.
Sometimes it is a proverb.
Sometimes it is a ritual.
Sometimes it is a painting.
