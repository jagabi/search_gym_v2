## Isaac Abramovich Charny

* * *

Ph.D. Gubkin Russian State University of Oil and Gas 1934![Image 2: Russia](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/img/flags/Russia.gif)

Dissertation: On pressure fluctuations with variable fluid movement in pipes

Mathematics Subject Classification: 76—Fluid mechanics

Advisor 1: [Leonid Samuilovich Leibenson](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=223584)

D.Sc. Gubkin Russian State University of Oil and Gas 1939![Image 3: Russia](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/img/flags/Russia.gif)

Dissertation: Applications of oscillation theory to some technical problems of hydraulic engineering and petroleum mechanics

Mathematics Subject Classification: 76—Fluid mechanics

Advisor 1: [Leonid Samuilovich Leibenson](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=223584)
