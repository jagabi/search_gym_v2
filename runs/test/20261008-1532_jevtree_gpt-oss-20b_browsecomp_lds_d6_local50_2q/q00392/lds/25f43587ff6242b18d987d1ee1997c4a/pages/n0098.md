## Abram Fjodorovich Ioffe

* * *

Ph.D. Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München 1905![Image 2: Germany](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/img/flags/Germany.gif)

Dissertation: Elastische Nachwirkung im kristallinischen Quarz

Mathematics Subject Classification: 82—Statistical mechanics, structure of matter

Advisor 1: [Wilhelm Conrad Röntgen](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=52900)

Ph.D. St. Petersburg State University 1915![Image 3: Russia](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/img/flags/Russia.gif)
