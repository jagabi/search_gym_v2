**Notes:**
*   **Required Fields:** Enter criteria in one or more of the fields above. Users **must** enter data into the Author or Title fields. 
    *   **Author:**Last Name Or Last Name, First initial; `e.g: graham; graham, r`
    *   **Title:**Use word or phrase
    *   **Journal:**Use either (part of) the full journal name or (part of) the MR journal abbreviation

*   **Boolean:**AND is permitted in the Author, Title and Journal fields
*   **Wild Cards:**Should not be used with this tool
*   **Results:**If more than three regular database items match the search criteria, only the most recent three are returned. In addition up to 100 [DML Items](https://mathscinet.ams.org/mathscinet/info/docs/search-results/results-intro#dml-dml) will be returned.
