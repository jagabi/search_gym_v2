## Leonid Samuilovich Leibenson

* * *

Ph.D. Lomonosov Moscow State University 1917![Image 2: Russia](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/img/flags/Russia.gif)

Dissertation: To the theory of without shell coatings

Mathematics Subject Classification: 74—Mechanics of deformable solids

Advisor 1: [Nikolai Egorovich Zhukovski](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=102058)

D.Sc. Lomonosov Moscow State University 1936![Image 3: Russia](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/img/flags/Russia.gif)

Dissertation: On the application of the Thomson method of harmonic functions to the question of the stability of compressed spherical and cylindrical elastic shells

Mathematics Subject Classification: 74—Mechanics of deformable solids

Advisor: Unknown
