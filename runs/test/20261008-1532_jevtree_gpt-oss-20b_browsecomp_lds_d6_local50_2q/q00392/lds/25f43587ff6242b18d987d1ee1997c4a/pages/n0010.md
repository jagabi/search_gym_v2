# Data Submission

Please enter the name of the mathematician and as much additional information as possible. When you have finished, click on the submit button. This form will then be sent via e-mail to the Mathematics Genealogy Project. If you have any questions click on the assistance link at the bottom of this page.

We receive new data every day so please understand if what you have sent to us does not appear online right away.

If you have any additional information that may be helpful to us please use our [contact form](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/contact.php).

You may want to review our [video tutorials](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/tutorials.php) for assistance with this form.
