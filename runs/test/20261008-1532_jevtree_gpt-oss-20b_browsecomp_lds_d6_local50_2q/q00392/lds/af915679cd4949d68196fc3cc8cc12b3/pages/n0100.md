# University of Chicago Law School

### WE PARTNER WITH University of Chicago Law School TO HELP THEIR STUDENTS FIND HOUSING. CONTACT US TO GET STARTED

### 1111 EAST 60TH STREET, CHICAGO, IL 60637

[The University of Chicago Law School](https://www.law.uchicago.edu/) occupies a unique niche among this country’s premier law schools. Located on a residential campus in one of America’s great cities, Chicago offers a rigorous and interdisciplinary professional education that blends the study of law with the humanities, the social sciences, and the natural sciences. Students, faculty, and staff form a small, tightly knit community devoted to the life of the mind. Learning is participatory. Chicago does not seek to impose a single viewpoint or style of thought on its students. Instead, our faculty exposes students to contrasting views, confident in students’ abilities to choose their own paths. Students live in apartments in [Hyde Park](https://www.student-space.com/hyde-park) most of the time but also have many housing options north at apartments in the [South Loop](https://www.student-space.com/south-loop) and [Loop](https://www.student-space.com/the-loop).
