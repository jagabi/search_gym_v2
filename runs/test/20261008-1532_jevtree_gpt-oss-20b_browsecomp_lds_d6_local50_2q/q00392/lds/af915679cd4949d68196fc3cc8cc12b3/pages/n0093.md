## From today's featured article

[![Image 4: Brian Vickers](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/93/BrianVickersAugust2006_%28cropped%29.jpg/250px-BrianVickersAugust2006_%28cropped%29.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:BrianVickersAugust2006_(cropped).jpg "Brian Vickers")

Brian Vickers

The **[2006 UAW-Ford 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_UAW-Ford_500 "2006 UAW-Ford 500")** was an American stock car racing competition. Held on October 8 at [Talladega Superspeedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talladega_Superspeedway "Talladega Superspeedway"), it was the 30th race in the 2006 [NASCAR Nextel Cup Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_Cup_Series "NASCAR Cup Series") and the fourth in the ten-race, season-ending [Chase for the Nextel Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_Chase "NASCAR Chase"). [Brian Vickers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Vickers "Brian Vickers")_(pictured)_ of [Hendrick Motorsports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hendrick_Motorsports "Hendrick Motorsports") won the first race of his career; [Kasey Kahne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasey_Kahne "Kasey Kahne") finished second, and [Kurt Busch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurt_Busch "Kurt Busch") came in third. [David Gilliland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Gilliland "David Gilliland"), who had the pole position, was passed by teammate [Dale Jarrett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_Jarrett "Dale Jarrett"). The race lead changed 63 times, with [Dale Earnhardt Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_Earnhardt_Jr. "Dale Earnhardt Jr.") leading for the most laps (37). On the final lap, [Jimmie Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmie_Johnson "Jimmie Johnson") and Vickers made a move to pass Earnhardt; Vickers clipped Johnson, then Johnson clipped Earnhardt, and both were knocked into the infield. The race was halted, giving Vickers the win; he was later criticized for hurting his teammate Johnson in the points standings. After the race, [Jeff Burton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Burton "Jeff Burton") maintained his [Drivers' Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_NASCAR_Cup_Series_champions "List of NASCAR Cup Series champions") points lead, while [Chevrolet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevrolet "Chevrolet") maintained its lead in the [Manufacturers' Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_NASCAR_Manufacturers'_champions "List of NASCAR Manufacturers' champions"). _(**[Full article...](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006\_UAW-Ford\_500 "2006 UAW-Ford 500")**)_

## Today's featured picture

[![Image 8: Hermann Castle](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/60/Narva_asv2022-04_img09_Castle.jpg/500px-Narva_asv2022-04_img09_Castle.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Narva_asv2022-04_img09_Castle.jpg "Hermann Castle")
**[Hermann Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermann_Castle "Hermann Castle")** is a medieval fortress in [Narva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narva "Narva"), Estonia, overlooking the river [Narva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narva_(river) "Narva (river)") on the border with Russia. Originating as a Danish border stronghold in the 13th century, it was initially built of wood and the town of Narva developed around it. In 1346, the castle passed to the [Livonian Order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livonian_Order "Livonian Order"), which completed the Hermann Tower, following the construction of [Ivangorod Fortress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivangorod_Fortress "Ivangorod Fortress") by [Muscovite Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Principality_of_Moscow "Grand Principality of Moscow") on the opposite bank of the river in 1492. In the 16th century, the Livonian Order reinforced the town's walls and gates and adapted several towers for use as cannon emplacements, two of which survive in reconstructed form in the castle's western court. This photograph shows Hermann Castle from across the river in 2022.

Photograph credit: [A.Savin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User:A.Savin "User:A.Savin")
