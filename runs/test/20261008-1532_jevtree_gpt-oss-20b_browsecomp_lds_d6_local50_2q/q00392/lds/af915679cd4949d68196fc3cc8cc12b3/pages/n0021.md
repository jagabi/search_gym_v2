Contact StudentSpace

This is the first and most important step. Beyond finding you the perfect apartment, we are here to be your consultant on every step from targeting the right neighborhood to setting up utilities and everything in between. We are accessible via phone, email, text, facetime, skype, etc.

Evaluate All Your Options

With hundreds of online scams and unreliable listings, navigating the rental marketplace is a challenge. That’s why we work closely with you to narrow down your options, and your school loves that we only work with reputable landlords so you know you’re getting the best quality.

[Get Started](https://www.student-space.com/find-your-space)

Set Up a Tour

We work with your schedule so you can visit your top choices and make the most of your trip. We drive you around to all the showings. Unable to visit prior to your move-in date? Not a problem – We will help you sign a lease remotely.

[Get Started](https://www.student-space.com/find-your-space)

Sign A Lease

The service doesn’t stop once you’ve signed a lease. We are here to answer any questions during and after your move-in, whether you need help with renter’s insurance, furnishing your apartment or even finding the best places to eat nearby.
