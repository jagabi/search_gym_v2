### [Four Black Scholars With Academic Ties Named MacArthur Fellows](https://jbhe.com/2026/10/four-black-scholars-with-academic-ties-named-macarthur-fellows/ "Four Black Scholars With Academic Ties Named MacArthur Fellows")

October 5, 2026

The four Black scholars with current affiliations with American educational institutions are Ellora Derenoncourt of Princeton University, Rodney McMillian of UCLA, Maaza Mengiste of Wesleyan University, and Immanuel Wilkins of New York University.

### [Calvin White Jr. Named the First Black Chancellor of Arkansas State University](https://jbhe.com/2026/10/calvin-white-jr-named-the-first-black-chancellor-of-arkansas-state-university/ "Calvin White Jr. Named the First Black Chancellor of Arkansas State University")

October 5, 2026

In addition to his latest ground-breaking appointment, Dr. White was also the first African American to serve as provost at Arkansas State University. He joined the university in 2023 and has served as interim chancellor for the past three months.

### [Addrain Conyers Appointed Interim Provost at Marist University in New York](https://jbhe.com/2026/10/addrain-conyers-appointed-interim-provost-at-marist-university-in-new-york/ "Addrain Conyers Appointed Interim Provost at Marist University in New York")

October 5, 2026

A scholar of criminology, Dr. Conyers has been a faculty member with Marist University in Poughkeepsie, New York, for the past 14 years. He was recently selected to serve as the university's interim provost and dean of faculty.

[](https://jbhe.com/2026/09/u-s-news-world-report-names-spelman-college-the-countrys-top-hbcu/ "*U.S. News & World Report* Names Spelman College the Country’s Top HBCU")

### [*U.S. News & World Report* Names Spelman College the Country’s Top HBCU](https://jbhe.com/2026/09/u-s-news-world-report-names-spelman-college-the-countrys-top-hbcu/ "*U.S. News & World Report* Names Spelman College the Country’s Top HBCU")

September 28, 2026

For the twentieth consecutive year, _U.S. News & World Report_ has named Spelman College in Atlanta the best HBCU in the United States. Howard University was second, Tuskegee University was third, Morehouse College was fourth, and North Carolina A&T State University was fifth.

[](https://jbhe.com/2026/09/trump-administration-announces-174-million-investment-to-support-hbcus/ "Trump Administration Announces $174 Million Investment to Support HBCUs")

### [Trump Administration Announces $174 Million Investment to Support HBCUs](https://jbhe.com/2026/09/trump-administration-announces-174-million-investment-to-support-hbcus/ "Trump Administration Announces $174 Million Investment to Support HBCUs")

September 28, 2026

The Trump Administration's latest investment in HBCUs follows a prior investment of $435 million in September 2025. These funds were initially allocated for programs supporting Minority-Serving Institutions, which include Hispanic-Serving Institutions and Predominately Black Institutions.

September 28, 2026

Deshjuana Bagley is the inaugural dean of the new College of Workforce Development and Applied Science at Louisiana State University Eunice. Montrose A. Streeter is the dean of students at Lyon College and Nolan McMurray is dean of the College of Arts and Sciences at Alcorn State University in Mississippi.

[](https://jbhe.com/2026/09/community-college-of-aurora-president-mordecai-brownlee-to-assume-new-role-at-colorado-state-university/ "Mordecai Brownlee to Assume New Role at Colorado State University")

### [Mordecai Brownlee to Assume New Role at Colorado State University](https://jbhe.com/2026/09/community-college-of-aurora-president-mordecai-brownlee-to-assume-new-role-at-colorado-state-university/ "Mordecai Brownlee to Assume New Role at Colorado State University")

September 21, 2026

Dr. Brownlee will conclude his tenure as president of the Community College of Aurora in December to begin a new appointment as vice president for Denver metro engagement at Colorado State University and executive director of Colorado State University Spur.

### [Southern University College of Business Dean to Step Down After Nearly Three Decades](https://jbhe.com/2026/09/southern-university-college-of-business-dean-to-step-down-after-nearly-three-decades/ "Southern University College of Business Dean to Step Down After Nearly Three Decades")

September 21, 2026

Donald R. Andrews has served as dean of the Southern University College of Business since 1998. At the end of the current academic year, he is slated to step down from his deanship and return to the HBCU's faculty.

Marlene Daut was named the Chester D. Tripp Professor of Black Studies and of French and Vincent Brown was named the David Brion Davis Professor of History.

### [Lonnie Bunch to Step Down as Secretary of the Smithsonian Institution](https://jbhe.com/2026/09/lonnie-bunch-to-step-down-as-secretary-of-the-smithsonian-institution/ "Lonnie Bunch to Step Down as Secretary of the Smithsonian Institution")

September 14, 2026

Dr. Bunch, the first African American to serve as secretary of the Smithsonian Institution, will step down by the end of 2026. His announcement follows significant conflict between the museum and the Trump administration.

### [Chidi Blyden Named Executive Director of the Harvard University Center for African Studies](https://jbhe.com/2026/09/chidi-blyden-named-executive-director-of-the-harvard-university-center-for-african-studies/ "Chidi Blyden Named Executive Director of the Harvard University Center for African Studies")

September 14, 2026

“Harvard’s role is to be a catalyst for educating the world about Africa today, not Africa of the past,” said Chidi Blyden, executive director of the Ivy League institution's Center for African Studies.

### [Three Black Americans Selected for New Dean Appointments](https://jbhe.com/2026/09/three-black-americans-selected-for-new-dean-appointments/ "Three Black Americans Selected for New Dean Appointments")

September 14, 2026

Rhonda L. Mingo and OL Kelly were named dean of students at Presbyterian College in South Carolina and Southeastern Oklahoma State University, respectively. Jasmin Feimster is dean of arts and sciences at Pitt Community College in North Carolina.

[](https://jbhe.com/2026/09/trump-administration-wants-to-remove-tax-exempt-status-for-private-schools-with-dei-policies/ "Trump Administration Seeks to End Tax-Exempt Status for Private Schools With DEI Policies")

### [Trump Administration Seeks to End Tax-Exempt Status for Private Schools With DEI Policies](https://jbhe.com/2026/09/trump-administration-wants-to-remove-tax-exempt-status-for-private-schools-with-dei-policies/ "Trump Administration Seeks to End Tax-Exempt Status for Private Schools With DEI Policies")

September 7, 2026

In the Trump administration's latest attempt to remove diversity, equity, and inclusion from higher education, the U.S. Department of the Treasury and the Internal Revenue Service have issued a new proposal that would strip federal tax-exempt status for any private school, college, or university with race-based policies or programs.

[](https://jbhe.com/2026/09/titus-sanders-is-the-inaugural-executive-director-of-the-elizabeth-city-state-university-school-of-aviation/ "Titus Sanders Named Executive Director of the Elizabeth City State University School of Aviation")

### [Titus Sanders Named Executive Director of the Elizabeth City State University School of Aviation](https://jbhe.com/2026/09/titus-sanders-is-the-inaugural-executive-director-of-the-elizabeth-city-state-university-school-of-aviation/ "Titus Sanders Named Executive Director of the Elizabeth City State University School of Aviation")

September 7, 2026

Sanders is a pilot for United Airlines, a retired chief warrant officer of the U.S. Army, and chair of the board of directors for the Organization of Black Aerospace Professionals. Before his new role, he led the aviation science department at Tuskegee University in Alabama.

[](https://jbhe.com/2026/09/joe-ricks-to-chair-the-acbsp-board-of-directors/ "Joe Ricks to Chair the ACBSP Board of Directors")

### [Joe Ricks to Chair the ACBSP Board of Directors](https://jbhe.com/2026/09/joe-ricks-to-chair-the-acbsp-board-of-directors/ "Joe Ricks to Chair the ACBSP Board of Directors")

September 7, 2026

The Accreditation Council for Business Schools and Programs (ACBSP) is a global accrediting body for business education. As chair, Xavier University of Louisiana's Joe Ricks will provide strategic leadership for the organization, which serves institutions in more than 60 countries.

Dawna Jones is dean of students at Duke University and Michael J. States is dean of students for the University of Miami School of Law. Kelvin Rutledge is vice president and dean of student affairs at SUNY's Westchester Community College.

### [Sarah J. Williams Is the New Leader of Penn State’s Law School](https://jbhe.com/2026/08/sarah-j-williams-to-lead-penn-states-law-school/ "Sarah J. Williams Is the New Leader of Penn State’s Law School")

Professor Williams has been selected to serve as interim dean of Penn State Dickinson Law. An expert on financial and securities regulations, she has been a faculty member with the university for the past seven years.
