The **1st [IAAF World Half Marathon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships "IAAF World Half Marathon Championships")** was held on September 19 and 20, 2001 in [Tyneside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyneside "Tyneside"), [UK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom "United Kingdom"), and was run simultaneously with that year's [Great North Run](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_North_Run "Great North Run"). A total of 204 athletes, 96 men, 84 women and 24 juniors, from 36 countries took part. Complete results were published.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships#cite_note-iaaf_01-1)

Though this competition was the first of its kind, it followed on from the [1991 IAAF World Women's Road Race Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_IAAF_World_Women's_Road_Race_Championships "1991 IAAF World Women's Road Race Championships"), which was held over the [15K distance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/15K_run "15K run").

Some athletes ran as non-scoring members and were not officially considered part of the World Half Marathon Championships despite completing the race. For example, [Paul Tergat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Tergat "Paul Tergat") finished 5th in 1:01:03 but was not listed in the results.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships#cite_note-2)
