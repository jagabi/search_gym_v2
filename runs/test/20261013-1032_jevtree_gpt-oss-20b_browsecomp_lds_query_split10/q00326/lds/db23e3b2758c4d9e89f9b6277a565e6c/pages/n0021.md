# Authors and contributors

**Authors and contributors**  
Blue Guides authors are experts in their fields, often residents of the place described, with the freshness and enthusiasm to wear their learning lightly and make their erudition accessible to the interested traveller.

**Editorial board**  
Our editors are closely involved in the process of creating a Blue Guide, often melding the work of different contributors into a consistent work and adding specific information. In this they are supported by our editorial board, specialists in history and art history.

**Write a Blue Guide**  
We are always on the lookout for new authors. Please do [contact us](https://www.blueguides.com/about/contact-us/) if you are interested in contributing to a Blue Guide, giving an indication of your areas of interest.
