# Explore the World of Skyscrapers

Indexing millions of data points across thousands of individual skyscrapers, this interactive database from the Council on Vertical Urbanism allows both professional and non-professional users to access all official information on these modern marvels of engineering.

![Image 12: Shanghai Skyline](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/img/ArialTrio_ShanghaiTower.jpg)

[![Image 13: Building Details](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/img/building-data-img.png)](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/explore-data)

#### Building Data

Access detailed information on thousands of individual skyscrapers around the world, and even create custom lists, graphs, and maps.

##### [View Data](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/explore-data)

[![Image 14: Tallest Buildings](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/img/tallest-building-img.png)](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/buildings)

#### Tallest Buildings

Discover the tallest structures on the planet— organized by city, country, region, function, material, and more.
