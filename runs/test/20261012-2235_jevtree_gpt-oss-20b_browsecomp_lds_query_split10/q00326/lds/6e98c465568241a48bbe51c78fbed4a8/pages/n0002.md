# Chicago by the Book

 A Literary Guide to the Windy City 

![Image 2](https://chicagostudies.uchicago.edu/sites/default/files/styles/max_width_full/public/2021-03/Books%20about%20Chicago%20copy.png?itok=GQBv1AXe)

Wondering what to do with your last few weeks of summer "fun" reading time? Check out our[**Chicago by the Book Tour**,](https://www.vamonde.com/adventure/link/1943) a guide to the Windy City from a literary lens! Chicago By the Book is a guide to Chicago places...from the perspective of a century's+ of different authors! We've chosen 30 different books about Chicago, from a wide range of authors and genres, each coming out of the experience of a particular neighborhood or community in the city. For each text, we'll tell you a bit about the book and its author; connect it to a specific place in the city; and then -- for those interested in exploring that neighborhood themselves -- make recommendations for things to see, do, and eat while you're there!

Whether you choose to experience this tour as a reading list, a unique window into Chicago's neighborhoods, or something in-between, you will be sure to learn (and find a few great reads) along the way!
