# Explore the ‘Chicago Minds’ campaign

[Learn about the ambitious initiative to expand UChicago’s global impact through research and education.](https://chicagominds.uchicago.edu/)

### Distinctly UChicago

Learn more about UChicago and its unique intellectual community—from its drive to discover, its determination to learn, and its work to transform the lives of its students, the community and the world.

[Watch Video](https://www.uchicago.edu/who-we-are/our-story)

## We value rigorous inquiry

A diversity of people and ideas, coupled with free and open discourse, lays the foundation for students and scholars to bring forth original ideas that define fields and enrich human life.

[LEARN MORE](https://www.uchicago.edu/who-we-are)

## We foster independent thinking

## Field-defining research

UChicago researchers have contributed to some of the world’s greatest discoveries, advancements, and bodies of knowledge.

[Learn More](https://www.uchicago.edu/research-and-scholarship)

## Transformative education

UChicago students develop the habits of mind and intellectual skills needed to confront complex challenges.

[Learn More](https://www.uchicago.edu/academics)

## We advance ideas and humanity

## A history of groundbreaking discoveries

Since UChicago’s founding, its scholars have transformed our world through their work, changing the way we live and think.

[Learn More](https://www.uchicago.edu/research-and-scholarship/breakthroughs)

## Community impact

As a community partner, we invest in Chicago’s South Side across such areas as health, education, economic growth, and the arts.

[Learn More](https://www.uchicago.edu/who-we-are/community-impact)

## Global impact

We are an international community of scholars working to solve the world's most pressing issues, with initiatives and programs on all seven continents.

[Learn More](https://www.uchicago.edu/who-we-are/global-impact)

## We call Chicago home

Chicago is not only in our name, it’s woven into the fabric of this institution. Located in the Hyde Park neighborhood, we benefit from the diversity, arts, and vibrant culture of our South Side community.

[LEARN MORE](https://www.uchicago.edu/life-at-uchicago/hyde-park)
