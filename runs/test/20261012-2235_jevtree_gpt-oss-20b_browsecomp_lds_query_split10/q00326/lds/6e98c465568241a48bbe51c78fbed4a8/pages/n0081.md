# Saving Searches

6 months ago Updated

If you periodically search for titles by the same author, or on the same subject, you can save your search and re-run it by simply clicking the search name. That saves you time, especially if the search you’re doing is complex. Saved searches are always **private**.

### To save a search

1.   [Log in](https://help.bibliocommons.com/hc/en-us/articles/31741804526612)to your account. You must be logged in to save a search.
2.   Run your search.
3.   If required, narrow your results by applying facets on the left side of the page.
4.   When you have refined your search, click the**Save Search**link above the search results.
5.   Give the saved search a name that will allow you to easily identify it later. If you already have a search by that name, you’ll have the option to choose a new name or overwrite your existing saved search.
6.   Click**Save**. A message will appear at the bottom of the screen to confirm that your search saved successfully.

You can edit your saved searches after you’ve saved them.
