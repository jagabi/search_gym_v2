## New Mexico Authors

This guide presents an ever-growing list of **New Mexico Authors**, writers who have lived in New Mexico or who have strong ties with the Land of Enchantment.

* * *

Authors are listed under their last names, through the A-Z tabs.

**Click on the author's name** to see materials in the Albuquerque / Bernalillo County Public Library collection.

**Click out on the links** to author websites, bibliographies, or historical information.

Within the author's entry, you will often find links to the author's **noted works** or **series**.

The **Mystery Series** and **Science Fiction & Fantasy** tabs lead you to genre materials by New Mexico authors.

_Note: Authors are people, and people move about, change jobs, etc. Biographical information is presented as a general guide, and may not be current._

_This is an ongoing project, and new entries will be added._
