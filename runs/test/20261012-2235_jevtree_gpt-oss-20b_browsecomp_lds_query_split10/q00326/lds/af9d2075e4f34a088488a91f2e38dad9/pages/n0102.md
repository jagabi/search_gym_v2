# Safety is Our Priority

## Tour Safely and Have a Great Time

At L Stop Tours, we’re committed to the health and safety of our guests and in an effort to help reduce the spread of COVID-19, we’ve made changes to our [Chicago walking tours](https://www.lstoptours.com/chicago-tours) so they align with guidelines issued by the CDC, Illinois Department of Public Health and the Chicago Department of Public Health. To learn more about safety precautions taken by the Chicago Transit Authority (CTA), visit [Coronavirus Info](https://www.transitchicago.com/coronavirus/).

### **Tour Size Kept Small**

Tours are limited to a maximum of 10 people.

### **Face Coverings**

Masks are optional.

### **Vaccine Requirement**

The city of Chicago no longer requires proof of vaccination to enter public spaces.

### **Train Tickets Included (Where Applicable)**

If your tour involves a train trip, your train ticket is purchased by L Stop Tours in advance, minimizing the need for additional contact with surfaces or people.

* * *

L Stop Tours is committed to providing you and your guests with a safe, enjoyable experience. We look forward to seeing you soon! Check out our [tour page](https://www.lstoptours.com/chicago-tours) for current options.
