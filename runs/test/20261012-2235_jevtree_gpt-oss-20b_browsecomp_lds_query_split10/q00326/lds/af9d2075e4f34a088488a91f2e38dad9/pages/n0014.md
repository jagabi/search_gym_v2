# Chicago Walking Tours

Experience why the "Second City" is second to none.

## Our Most Popular Tours

As a large American city, Chicago is rather unique.

Known for its architecture, public art, iconic elevated transit system and 77 ethnically diverse and vibrant neighborhoods, the city is truly one of a kind.

Whether you’re interested in Loop architecture, prohibition-era speakeasys or underground passageways, our most popular tours will take you there.

Need something different for your group or special event? Contact L Stop Tours for more information.

L Stop Tours. Let us share our amazing city with you.

## What to Expect on a Tour

![Image 14](https://www.lstoptours.com/wp-content/uploads/Smaller-Group.svg)

#### Smaller Groups

Tours run with a maximum of 10 participants — so you can count on personal attention. Do you have a bigger group? Check out our Groups & Private Tours page.

![Image 15](https://www.lstoptours.com/wp-content/uploads/Ride-The-L.svg)

#### Ride the L

Each L Stop tour includes a short ride on the L. Transportation fees are included in the tour price.

![Image 16](https://www.lstoptours.com/wp-content/uploads/Motor-Row.svg)

#### Explore Downtown Chicago

Be an insider. Our Loop walking tours take you inside buildings and other famous sites that can't be seen by boat or bus.

![Image 17](https://www.lstoptours.com/wp-content/uploads/Fun.svg)

#### Fun

From great stories and personal experiences to unique insights about the city, our local Chicago tour guides ensure a memorable experience.

![Image 18](https://www.lstoptours.com/wp-content/uploads/Food-Beverage.svg)

#### Food & Beverage

Opportunities for guests to purchase refreshments are included on every tour. Check tour pages for details.

![Image 19](https://www.lstoptours.com/wp-content/uploads/walking.svg)

#### Walking & Stairs

Participants should be able to use stairs and comfortably walk during each tour experience.
