## New Mexico Authors - S

**New Mexico Authors - S**

*    Saavedra, Lorraine  Born and raised in Albuquerque. Christian poet. Author of several collections of poetry including _Truth Is -- Why Lie Now: Inspirational Rhyming Poems_ and _The Rainbow to Poetry: Inspirational Rhyming Poems_.   
*   [Saberhagen, Fred This link opens in a new window](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/results?qu=%22Saberhagen%2C+Fred%22&te=) (1930-2007) Albuquerque. Science fiction and fantasy author. Creator of the [Berserkers](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/results?qu=Saberhagen%2C+Fred+Berserker&rw=12&isd=true*), a relentless machine race dedicated to destroying all life in the universe. Noted for the [Swords](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/results?qu=Saberhagen%2C+Fred+Swords&te=) series, the [Books of the Gods](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/results?qu=Saberhagen%2C+Fred+Book+of+the+Gods&te=) series that re-envisions Greek mythology, and the series told from the viewpoint of the character Dracula.   
*    Saberhagen, Joan  Joan Spicci Saberhagen. Albuquerque author; editor of _Golden Reflections_, a story collection in tribute to her husband **Fred Saberhagen**. Also writes as **Joan Spicci**.   
*   [Sachs, Lucinda Lucero This link opens in a new window](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/results?qu=%22Sachs%2C+Lucinda+Lucero%22&te=) Masters in History from UNM. Author of [_Clyde Tingley's New Deal for New Mexico: 1935-1938_](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD_ILS$002f0$002fSD_ILS:127367/one) and the novel [_Believe in the Wind_](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD_ILS$002f0$002fSD_ILS:150399/one), historical fiction set in Madrid New Mexico.   
*    Saenz, Adolph Bernal  (1929-2022) Served with the U.S. Foreign Service, the U.S. Treasury Department and the U.S. Customs Service; consultant to other government agencies. Degrees in Business Administration and Public Administration from UNM. Served as New Mexico Secretary of Corrections. Author of several books including _Politics of a Prison Riot: the 1980 New Mexico Prison Riot, Its causes and aftermath_.   
*   [Saenz, Benjamin Alire This link opens in a new window](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/results?qu=%22Saenz%2C+Benjamin+Alire%22&te=&rt=false%7C%7C%7CAUTHOR%7C%7C%7CAuthor) New Mexico native, raised in the Las Cruces area, now on faculty of the University of Texas at El Paso. Author of many children's books in Spanish, including [_Grandma Fina and her wonderful umbrellas = La abuelita Fina y sus sombrillas maravillosas_](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD_ILS$002f0$002fSD_ILS:37742/one) and [_A gift from papa Diego = Un regalo de papa Diego_](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD_ILS$002f0$002fSD_ILS:30324/one); author of novels including [_Last Night I Sang to the Monster_](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD_ILS$002f0$002fSD_ILS:108687/one) and [_Names on a Map_](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD_ILS$002f0$002fSD_ILS:90542/one).   
*    Saenz, Joe  Warm Springs Band of the Chiricahua Apache, for which he has served as council member. Contributor to _First & Wildest : The Gila Wilderness at 100_.   
*   [Sagan, Miriam This link opens in a new window](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/results?qu=%22Sagan%2C+Miriam%22&te=&rt=false%7C%7C%7CAUTHOR%7C%7C%7CAuthor) Santa Fe. Essayist, memoirist, teacher, and publisher. Director of the creative writing program at Santa Fe Community College. Editor of several collections of poetry; editor/author of numerous books including [_Archeology of Desire_](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD_ILS$002f0$002fSD_ILS:50846/one) and [_Searching for a mustard seed : one young widow's unconventional story_](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD_ILS$002f0$002fSD_ILS:52266/one).   
*   [Sagel, Jim This link opens in a new window](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/results?qu=%22Sagel%2C+Jim%22&te=&rt=false%7C%7C%7CAUTHOR%7C%7C%7CAuthor) (1947-1988) Moved to Espanola in 1969. Poet, storyteller. M.A. in Creative Writing from the University of New Mexico. Taught high school around northern NM, then affiliated with Northern New Mexico Community College and taught at college campuses around the state. Recipient of the Governor’s Award for Excellence in the Arts in 1993. Author of many works including [_Dancing to Pay the Light Bill : essays on New Mexico and the Southwest_](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD_ILS$002f0$002fSD_ILS:14765/one).   
*    Sagemiller, Greg  Taos. Archaeologist. Past President and Program Chair of the Taos Archaeology Society, several roles with the Archaeological Society of New Mexico including serving as President. Author of several novels including the mystery _Walking Earth_ and the science fantasy novel _Skeleton Mesa_.   
*    Saks, Claude M.  Santa Fe. Author of _Inescapable Journey: A Spiritual Adventure_ and _Strong Brew: One Man's Prelude to Change_ and _Confluence_.   
*    Salazar, Andres C.  Chaired professor at UNM, teacher at other state universities. Author of a collection of poetry, _Seasons: Sounds and Amorous Observations_, and the novel _Release from Cibola: Conquistadors, Eisenhower and Me_ about a boy growing up in northern New Mexico during the 1950s.   
*    Salman, David  Owner of High Country Gardens in Santa Fe; blogger on xeric gardening and eco-friendly landscaping. Author of [_Waterwise garden care : your practical guide_](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD_ILS$002f0$002fSD_ILS:107536/one), which won the New Mexico Book Award for 2007.   
*   [Salmon, M.H. This link opens in a new window](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/results?qu=%22Salmon%2C+M.H.%22&te=&rt=false%7C%7C%7CAUTHOR%7C%7C%7CAuthor) (1945-2019) Maynard Hubbard Salmon, _AKA: "Dutch" Salmon_. Silver City. Outdoorsman, fisherman, owner/publisher of High Lonesome Books. Author of many works about outdooring in New Mexico, including [_Gila libre! : New Mexico's last wild river_](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD_ILS$002f0$002fSD_ILS:90673/one) and [_The catfish as metaphor : a fisherman's American journey_](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD_ILS$002f0$002fSD_ILS:42824/one).   
*   [Salter, R.B. This link opens in a new window](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/results?qu=%22Salter%2C+R.B.%22&te=&rt=false%7C%7C%7CAUTHOR%7C%7C%7CAuthor) Robert Bruce Salter. Santa Fe. Works with the Pueblos as a Range / Wildlife Biologist. Author of several novels about environmental impact in the Southwest, including [_Chamisa Dreams_](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD_ILS$002f0$002fSD_ILS:23099/one).   
*   [Salzman, Gary This link opens in a new window](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/results?qu=%22Salzman%2C+Gary%22&te=&rt=false%7C%7C%7CAUTHOR%7C%7C%7CAuthor) (1942-2020) Los Alamos. Former Los Alamos Labs scientist. Co-author, with wife **Joan Salzman**, of [_Hiking Adventures in Northern New Mexico_](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD_ILS$002f0$002fSD_ILS:74035/one).   
*   [Salzman, Joan This link opens in a new window](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/results?qu=%22Salzman%2C+Joan%22&te=) Los Alamos. Former teacher. Co-author, with husband **Gary Salzman**, of [_Hiking Adventures in Northern New Mexico_](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD_ILS$002f0$002fSD_ILS:74035/one).   
*    Samaniego, Chelsea  Las Cruces. Co-author with **Dominic Bewley** of a book for children, _Hi, My Name is Corona! (Covid-19)._  
*   [Sams, Jamie This link opens in a new window](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/results?qu=%22Sams%2C+Jamie%22&te=&rt=false%7C%7C%7CAUTHOR%7C%7C%7CAuthor) Santa Fe. Co-author, with **David Carson**, of the _Medicine Cards_ book and deck. Author of _[Other council fires were here before ours : a classic Native American creation story as retold by a Seneca elder, Twylah Nitsch, and her granddaughter, Jamie Sams : the Medicine Stone speaks from the past to our future](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en\_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD\_ILS$002f0$002fSD\_ILS:141377/one)_.   
*   [Samson, Jack This link opens in a new window](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/results?qu=%22Samson%2C+Jack%22&te=&rt=false%7C%7C%7CAUTHOR%7C%7C%7CAuthor) John G. Samson. Santa Fe. War correspondent, member of the Flying Tigers, Neiman Fellow, editor of _Field & Stream_, journalist. Worked with the New Mexico Dept. of Game and Fish, during which time he organized the promotion of Smokey Bear. Recipient of the University of New Mexico Alumni Association James F. Zimmerman Award. Author of many works including _Saltwater Fly Fishing_ and [_The Flying Tiger : the true story of General Claire Chennault and the U.S. 14th Air Force in China_](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002f$002f0$002fHOOPLA:15138872/one).

*   [Samuels, Harold This link opens in a new window](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/results?qu=%22Samuels%2C+Harold%22&te=ILS&rt=false%7C%7C%7CAUTHOR%7C%7C%7CAuthor) Corrales. Co-author, with **Peggy Samuels**, of several works on art including [_Contemporary Western Artists_](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD_ILS$002f0$002fSD_ILS:3668/one) and [_The illustrated biographical encyclopedia of artists of the American West_](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD_ILS$002f0$002fSD_ILS:5520/one). These authors also wrote _Teddy Roosevelt at San Juan : the making of a president_.   
*   [Samuels, Peggy This link opens in a new window](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/results?qu=%22Samuels%2C+Peggy%22&te=ILS&rt=false%7C%7C%7CAUTHOR%7C%7C%7CAuthor) Corrales. Co-author, with **Harold Samuels**, of several works on art including [_Contemporary Western Artists_](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD_ILS$002f0$002fSD_ILS:3668/one) and [_The illustrated biographical encyclopedia of artists of the American West_](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD_ILS$002f0$002fSD_ILS:5520/one). Co-author, with wife **Laura Sanchez**, of [_Adobe houses for today : flexible plans for your adobe home_](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD_ILS$002f0$002fSD_ILS:90660/one).

Contributor to _[Turn left at the Sleeping Dog : scripting the Santa Fe legend, 1920-1955](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en\_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD\_ILS$002f0$002fSD\_ILS:43823/one)_.   
*    Sanchez, Bob  Las Cruces author of poetry and fiction, including _When Pigs Fly_ and _Getting Lucky_.   
*    Sanchez, Cassie  Grew up in Albuquerque. Author of the fantasy Darkness Trilogy.   
*   [Sanchez, Erika L. This link opens in a new window](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en_US/default/search/results?qu=%22Sanchez%2C+Erika+L.%22&te=&rt=false%7C%7C%7CAUTHOR%7C%7C%7CAuthor) Grew up in Illinois, received an MFA in Creative Writing from the University of New Mexico. Author of the Young Adult novel _[I Am Not Your Perfect Mexican Daughter](https://abq.ent.sirsi.net/client/en\_US/default/search/detailnonmodal/ent:$002f$002fSD\_ILS$002f0$002fSD\_ILS:198850/one)_ which was a finalist for the National Book Award.
