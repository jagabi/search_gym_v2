## We're looking forward to showing you around this amazing city!

Before making a tour reservation, please note the following:

*   For all of our [Chicago walking tours](https://www.lstoptours.com/), we recommend wearing comfortable shoes.
*   Some L stations are NOT handicapped accessible and require the use of stairs.
*   Dress for the weather; our [Chicago tours](https://www.lstoptours.com/chicago-tours) take place rain or shine.
*   Transit fees (where applicable) are included in the tour price.
*   Food/beverages are available for purchase on all tours.
*   Restrooms are available throughout all [Chicago walking tours](https://www.lstoptours.com/).
*   ID is required to be served alcohol.

_To learn more about precautions to prevent the spread of COVID-19, visit our [Health & Safety](https://www.lstoptours.com/reopening) page._
