Conduct and publish long-term ecological field studies that improve our knowledge of how flowers use visual and chemical signals to communicate with pollinators in the face of climate change. In the Rocky Mountain Biological Laboratory Education Program, mentor graduate and undergraduate REU students (2-3 per year) as they propose, carry out, and publish independent projects. Write, document, and maintain public software for reproducible research in chemical ecology. Serve as an instrumentation biologist to work with scientists to plan experiments in chemical ecology, operate laboratory instruments, and analyze their data. 

Pollination biology, Sakai/Weller Lab, University of California, Irvine  2015 – 2025 

Study the evolutionary diversification of floral volatiles and breeding systems in an endangered plant lineage endemic to the Hawaiian Islands. Analyze floral scent across populations and species with GC-MS and in real time with PTR-MS. Design and conduct pollinator choice experiments at field sites in Hawai'i. Collaborate with scientists on a large NSF grant to investigate how floral scent and other traits contributed to species barriers and pollination shifts during the adaptive radiation of this group. 

Population genetics, Servedio Lab, University of North Carolina  2014 – 2018 

Worked closely with collaborators to construct population genetic models that show how mate choice and parental investment explain the evolution of annual mating displays in pair-bonding birds. Created interactive visual summaries of the results. 

Protein structure, Goldenberg Lab, University of Utah  2010
