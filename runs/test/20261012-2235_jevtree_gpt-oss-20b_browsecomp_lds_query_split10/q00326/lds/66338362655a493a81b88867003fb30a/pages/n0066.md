_**With the disappearance of stand-alone book review sections in many newspapers and a drastic drop in the number of reviews in magazines, scholars have few timely ways to keep up with the latest books in the humanities and social sciences. H-Net's Book Channel is a state-of-the-art solution: A book discovery service that fills a crucial void.**_

_**--Steven Mintz, University of Texas at Austin**_

Do you want to see your book appear on the H-Net Book Channel? [Submit it today](https://networks.h-net.org/form/book-channel-submission-form). Please note that you must be logged into your H-Net account to access the form.

## About the H-Net Book Channel

**The Book Channel**is a new book announcement service from H-Net. We are continually importing new publisher catalogs to help readers discover new academic texts in their fields.Visit daily to find new lists of recently published academic titles. Browse the full lists of [categories](https://networks.h-net.org/group/1883/pages/20000007/browse-all-categories)and[publishers](https://networks.h-net.org/node/1883/pages/89156/publishers-imprints-currently-included-book-channel).

**The Book Channel**also commissions and publishes original content related to new and innovative scholarship. Explore essays on teaching, current events, and other topics of interest [here](https://networks.h-net.org/node/1883/pages/204463/table-contents).

Follow us on Bluesky[@hnetbookchannel.bsky.social](https://bsky.app/profile/hnetbookchannel.bsky.social)‬or get in touch at [bookchannel@mail.h-net.org](mailto:bookchannel@mail.h-net.msu.edu).
