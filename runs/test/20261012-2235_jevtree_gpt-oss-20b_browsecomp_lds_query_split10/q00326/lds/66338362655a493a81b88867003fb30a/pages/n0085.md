### [Announcing the United States Holocaust Memorial Museum's new publishing program: New Research in Holocaust Studies](https://networks.h-net.org/group/announcements/20127337/announcing-united-states-holocaust-memorial-museums-new-publishing)

Jenna Galberg•10/08/2025  Announcement 

Call for Publications

Date

October 1, 2025 - October 31, 2025

Location

 District of Columbia, United States 

Subject Fields

Holocaust, Genocide, and Memory Studies

The United States Holocaust Memorial Museum is pleased to invite nominations for a publication program:_New Research in Holocaust Studies_. This interdisciplinary program seeks to make cutting-edge Holocaust studies scholarship accessible to a broad audience through generous open access subventions to university presses publishing in this field. The series is now accepting nominations for books to consider for its first set of publications. The deadline is October 31, 2025.

[Read More](https://networks.h-net.org/group/announcements/20127337/announcing-united-states-holocaust-memorial-museums-new-publishing)

### [NEW book on Spinoza, New Materialism, and Marxism](https://networks.h-net.org/group/announcements/20120011/new-book-spinoza-new-materialism-and-marxism)

Claudio Lartigue•07/15/2025  Announcement 

Online Digital Resources

Date

July 7, 2025

Location

 New York, United States 

Subject Fields

Cultural History / Studies, Humanities, Intellectual History, Philosophy, Social History / Studies

_Spinoza, New Materialism and the Contemporary_ is a book about change. Through its border passing interpretations, it not only transforms the dominant contemporary views of Spinoza but, more significantly, it puts into question the assumptions of those that have produced the dominant view, such as Althusser, Negri, Deleuze, as well as the new materialism and the ontological turn, including Latour, Bennett and Braidotti.

[Read More](https://networks.h-net.org/group/announcements/20120011/new-book-spinoza-new-materialism-and-marxism)
