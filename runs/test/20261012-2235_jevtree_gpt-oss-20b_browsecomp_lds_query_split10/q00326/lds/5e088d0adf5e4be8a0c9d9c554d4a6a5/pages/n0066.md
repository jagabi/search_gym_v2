**In science, computing, and engineering, a“_Black Box_”is a device, system or object which can be viewed in terms of its inputs and outputs without any knowledge of its internal workings. Almost anything might be referred to as a “_Black Box_”: an engine, an equation, the data in an airplane that records flight, the brain, an institution or government; a podcast conversation. It ALL began with an idea… it always does, right?**

**Maybe you want to launch a business. Maybe you want to turn a hobby into something more. Or maybe you have a creative project to share with the world. Whatever it is, from the way you tell your story… or the way you learn from others, it starts with a _Black Box_. Join Kenny on the journey of discovering the stories of folks from all walks of life and how they are finding the secrets of enjoying life’s journey. Join**[**_KP’s Black Box_**](https://www.blackboxgrp.com/home)**!**

**Be clear, be confident and don’t overthink it. The beauty of your story is that it’s going to continue to evolve and your journey can evolve with it. Your goal should be to make it feel right for right now. Later will take care of itself. It always does.**
