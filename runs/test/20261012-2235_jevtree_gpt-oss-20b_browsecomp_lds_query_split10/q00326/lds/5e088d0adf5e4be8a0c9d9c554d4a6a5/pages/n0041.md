## Meet the Locals

Dreams Abroad’s Meet the Locals series began with one important realization: travel becomes more meaningful when we use that time to connect with the locals and allow ourselves to learn from their experiences.

After spending two months in Croatia in 2022, Leesa Truesdell, founder and CEO of Dreams Abroad, reflected on her [time abroad](https://www.dreamsabroad.com/prequel-7-time-to-reflect/).

“I learned how to travel deeper,” she says. “It’s not about checking an item off a bucket list. It’s about opening myself up to what I can learn from each place, experiencing a culture, and meeting the locals.”

Meet the Locals launched in 2023 along with Dreams Abroad’s updated website, and since then, the monthly series has introduced the world to dozens of locals spanning different continents, demographics, industries, and ways of life. Dreams Abroad is committed to bridging cultures and connecting people with our articles, allowing readers to see the world through one another’s eyes, without even booking a plane ticket.

But when you are ready to travel, these articles take you beyond the guidebooks for recommendations curated by the people who know your destination better than anyone else. Engaging with locals offers you the most authentic experience while traveling and the opportunity to enjoy day-to-day life the same way as the people who live there. From the food, history, attractions, and more, they’ll have you safely off the beaten path and enjoying their hometown just as they do.

Our featured locals are now regularly involved in Dreams Abroad programming, a full-circle moment for Truesdell. “This has been my vision the whole time,” she says. “To build a bridge that creates a community of locals.”

That’s the spirit of Dreams Abroad’s Meet the Locals initiative, and it’s what you’ll find as you browse our library of articles. As our featured locals share their lives, we hope you feel inspired to travel with intention. Join our community, join the conversation, and “live like a local,” no matter where the journey takes you.

**2026**: Dreams Abroad’s 10-year anniversary year celebrates the people and places that helped shape the organization into what it is today. Locals are featured from throughout Europe, with a special focus on Madrid, where Dreams Abroad first began.

In addition, a few locals are featured from Washington, D.C., in honor of the United States’ 250th anniversary.

**2025**: The focus of the series in 2025 was to highlight locals in Washington, D.C., celebrating the opening of Dreams Abroad’s brand-new headquarters in the city, while also kicking off our 10-year anniversary celebration. [Laurie Gillman](https://www.dreamsabroad.com/meet-laurie-gillman-of-east-city-bookshop-washington-d-c/) of East City Bookshop in D.C. was named Local of the Year for exemplifying what it means to be an outstanding local business owner.

The annual Meet the Locals event, [Passport to Washington, D.C.](https://www.dreamsabroad.com/north-america/united-states/passport-to-washington-d-c/), returned to the U.S. capital. This time it was hosted in the same building as Dreams Abroad’s headquarters in the Hill Center, a community center led by local [Diana Ingraham](https://www.dreamsabroad.com/north-america/united-states/diana-ingraham-hill-center-washington-d-c/).

**2024**: A new year introduced the Dreams Abroad community to 12 new locals, including [Ryne Gallien](https://www.dreamsabroad.com/meet-ryne-gallien-from-the-chelsea-tampa-in-florida/), co-owner of The Chelsea Tampa hair salon in Florida, who was honored as Dreams Abroad’s first Local of the Year. As the award recipient, he nominated two other Tampa residents for Meet the Locals, leading to [Danielle Bankz](https://www.dreamsabroad.com/meet-danielle-bankz-of-beige-beauty-lounge-in-tampa-florida/) and [Stasia Chais](https://www.dreamsabroad.com/meet-stasia-chais-of-ls-organics-in-tampa-florida/) being featured in subsequent months.

[Ron Book](https://www.dreamsabroad.com/spotlight-ron-book-in-miami-florida/), an attorney in Miami, Florida, received a special spotlight recognition for his dedication to serving his community. He was presented with Dreams Abroad’s Community Service Award at our second annual event, [Passport to Tampa](https://www.dreamsabroad.com/passport_to_tampa/).

**2023**: [Tea Vukorepa](https://www.dreamsabroad.com/tea-vukorepa-global-tourism-professional/), a tourism professional from Split, Croatia, was the first featured Meet the Local. Leesa had recently visited Croatia, where she met Tea and her husband, [Mate](https://www.dreamsabroad.com/meet-the-locals-mate-vukorepa-in-split-croatia/). Tea works as a year-round travel advisor, helping guests from around the world plan their itineraries, while her husband is a boat welder.

[Chef Padua Player](https://www.dreamsabroad.com/meet-the-locals-sugachef-washington-dc/), a bakery owner in Washington, D.C., was another local recognized in 2023. He was a featured guest at Dreams Abroad’s inaugural Meet the Locals in-person [event](https://www.dreamsabroad.com/events/) and later hosted our first holiday event at Dreams Abroad’s D.C. headquarters in 2025.
