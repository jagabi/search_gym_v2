# Episode 202: Luc Meacham

Mar 29

Written By [KPCO Team](https://www.blackboxgrp.com/episodes?author=5eac66dfe361835419479998)

![Image 3](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5ebd98016c652f5a030ae0c1/edd93991-f8e0-4c0d-8925-626a2b000bfa/Season+2+Episode+Thumbnails.jpg)

A former soccer player with a dream to play football. Luc, aka SaucyPawz288, hasn’t stopped pursuing his dreams of playing professional football. Teamwork along with always going for the hardest thing out there drew Luc to become a Navy Seal. Now that he is no longer in service it is back to the football field with this guy who just won’t stop.

[Video 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ya3UfEkO_ec)

"Too small, too slow, just quit! If I listened to all the doubters, I wouldn't have become an All-American. I wouldn't have become a Navy Seal & I wouldn't be crazy enough to think I can make an NFL roster. I might fail, but I'm gonna give it everything I got." -- Luc  
  
This week we had the privilege of having a friend of Black Box, as well as good friend of KP's in the studio. Luc Meacham, aka @saucypawz288, demonstrates determination and discipline in everything he does. Tune in to hear what mindsets he carries, where they originated, his path to the studio and the exciting next steps in his career! From Soccer player to College football to Navy Seal and now back on the football field. Luc is a force to be reckoned with.
