## About Us

**Our Mission:**

Dreams Abroad is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization that empowers, connects, and funds global professionals to work, study, or live abroad.Through our website, social media, local events, and [scholarship](https://www.dreamsabroad.com/scholarship/), we provide professionals with vital resources to inspire and guide them through the vast opportunities available overseas.

**Our Vision:**  
 To accelerate the dreams and careers of global professionals through authentic engagement with knowledgeable locals, experiential learning, and personal exploration.

**Scholarship:**

Dreams Abroad supports and assists mid-career professionals who are transitioning between jobs. We help them build new skills or strengthen existing ones, so they can prepare for the next stage of their careers. With this goal in mind, Dreams Abroad offers an annual scholarship. In 2026, we will fund an individual to study Spanish in Madrid, Spain. This provides an opportunity for our scholarship recipient to not only learn valuable language skills, but also grow through cultural exchanges and travel.
