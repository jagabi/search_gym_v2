# Eric Mathern

“Should we have stayed at home and thought of here? Where would we be today?” — Elizabeth Bishop, “Questions of Travel”

Following his [Dreams Abroad debut](https://www.dreamsabroad.com/meet-the-locals-eric-mathern-new-york-city/) via the “Meet the Locals” series in the summer of 2023, Eric Mathern has expanded his association with the network by joining its team of writers.

Eric was born and raised in West Orange, New Jersey. It was there that he fell in love with literature, and first dreamed of becoming a writer.Writing has been a part of Eric’s life since then, but he considers his arrival in New York as a college student at NYU as the moment when he truly discovered his vocation. It was there that he met and studied with distinguished poet Elizabeth Bishop.Having such a prestigious writer review his work made him aware that his ambition to become a poet could be more than an adolescent fantasy, and was a realistic, albeit challenging, goal.

While at New York University Eric progressed as a writer, and published his first poems in NYU’s literary journal, the _Minetta Review_.During his sophomore and junior years he also served as poetry editor of the publication.

Upon graduation, Eric worked at different periods as an advertising copywriter, a proofreader, and an editor, but always continued to write on his own. In 1991 he took the major step of pursuing an MFA in poetry at the University of Florida, where he was awarded both a fellowship and a teaching assistant grant. While he was there he found a second calling; he discovered the joys of teaching.

Since receiving his graduate degree, Eric has taught English courses as an adjunct professor at Seton Hall University and worked as a private tutor.And, as always, he has continued to write. His poems, as well as other writings, have appeared in several publications including _The New Republic_ and _The New York Times_.

Eric has written about a wide range of subjects, but chief among them are New York City’s people, places, and wildlife, especially its birds.

Birds have a primary place in Eric’s day-to-day life. He not only spends time observing birds in the New York City area, he has also contributed poems and articles about birds to local publications such as the Brooklyn Bird Club’s _Clapper Rail_ and Manhattan’s publication the _West Side Rag_.

In addition to birding, Eric has an ongoing interest in New York City history.His writing is often inspired by figures from the past, such as New York City’s youngest mayor, John Purroy Mitchell.

It is in conjunction with this interest that Eric recently fulfilled a long-held desire to become a New York City tour guide. He is passionate about sharing his knowledge of New York City and its history on his tours, via his writing, and public speaking engagements. His recent talks have included topics as diverse as Manhattan’s Turtle Bay neighborhood, the Statue of Liberty, and “Remembering Marilyn,” a review of Monroe’s life via a gallery of iconic photos.
