On Wednesday night I trekked down to Pomeroy for a book signing by Gary Bye. He is my cousin's daughter's father-in-law, otherwise I might not have bothered. Two of my cousins were there, so that was an unexpected pleasure. Gary is a wheat farmer and raises cows (not cattle), who has written a book, _Glory Grove_. The books centers around high school football in a small town in Eastern Washington, but it is more than that. It is about rural life just about anywhere in the country. Change the sport. Change the crop. You get the idea. His characters are very easy to relate to, almost too relatable. His descriptions are spot on and it makes for a fairly quick enjoyable read. He takes you on a bit of a emotional roller coaster, so be prepared.

He wrote for the National FFA magazine for 30 years and contributes to other rural publications. This is his first novel. He also wrote a two volume history of Pomeroy's hundred year football program.
