# Our Authors

Dreams Abroad wouldn’t be what it is today without the diverse team of authors, editors, public relations and communication professionals who share their talent, time, and unique perspectives with the community. Located around the world, these global professionals are experts in their fields, dedicated to sharing the most up-to-date and relevant content with you. With our team’s shared knowledge and experience, you’re empowered with the resources and supportive network you need to turn any dream abroad into a reality.
