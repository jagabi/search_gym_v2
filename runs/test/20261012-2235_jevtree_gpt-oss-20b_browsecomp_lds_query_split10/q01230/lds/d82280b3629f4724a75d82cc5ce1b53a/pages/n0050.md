### Unparalleled accuracy and reliability

Traffic intelligence is only as trustworthy as the information it runs on. That’s why TomTom’s data is always reputably sourced, rigorously validated and continuously updated — meaning results you can trust when making impactful decisions.

![Image 20: Traffic data for Interstate 495 Beltway in Washington area, showing a 94-min travel time, 36-min delay, and current speed of 65 km/h.](https://media.tomtom.com/f/178460/1920x1080/5ee6884177/feature_02_desktop_1280x720px.jpg)

### Strategy-shaping insights and context

Traffic data is a living record of everyday movement. TomTom’s powerful [analytics tools](https://www.tomtom.com/products/tomtom-move/) and visual reports translate this stream of information into [decision-critical insights](https://www.tomtom.com/products/origin-destination-matrix-analysis/) and real-world context for organizations across industries.

![Image 21: Split image of a heatmap showing data intensity in California and a map with percentages indicating data flow in an urban area.](https://media.tomtom.com/f/178460/1920x1080/8ef93b6deb/feature_03_desktop_1280x720px.jpg)

### Intelligence at scale

Deep dive into [mobility patterns](https://www.tomtom.com/products/area-analytics/) anywhere on earth, from [specific junctions](https://www.tomtom.com/products/junction-analytics/) and streets to national [road networks](https://www.tomtom.com/products/traffic-stats/). The richness and coverage of TomTom’s traffic data deliver fast, consistent results that scale to your needs.

![Image 22: Two maps compare traffic patterns at San Siro: 'Week before Olympics' shows lighter traffic, 'Week during Olympics' shows increased congestion.](https://media.tomtom.com/f/178460/1920x1080/a841406890/feature_04_desktop_1280x720px.jpg)

### Proactive safety and resilience

From emergency response and large-scale event preparation to road safety and risk modeling, TomTom’s [real-time traffic data](https://www.tomtom.com/products/traffic-apis/) and [historical insights](https://www.tomtom.com/products/historical-traffic-volumes/) provide unprecedented clarity and efficiency across a range of safety operations.
