# Historical Traffic Volumes

TomTom's Historical Traffic Volumes provides accurate, network-wide traffic data by estimating how many vehicles pass through road segments over time. Ideal for governments, businesses and local authorities, it helps you monitor road usage, predict trends and optimize infrastructure for economic and environmental benefits.

FEATURES AND BENEFITS

## Historical Traffic Volumes

Optimize traffic networks and inform smarter, more profitable business decisions with accurate and reliable historical traffic volumes — delivered at an annual, monthly or daily level.

![Image 14: historical traffic illustration](https://media.tomtom.com/f/178460/1280x720/534deadd57/accurate-historical-traffic-1.png)

### Save time and costs

TomTom processes 5 to 6 million kilometers of driving data every day, with underlaying data refreshed every 30 seconds. The continuous update cycle captures an up-to-date and in-depth understanding of traffic conditions across the entire road network – reducing reliance on expensive, hard-to-maintain roadside equipment.

![Image 15: traffic data illustration](https://media.tomtom.com/f/178460/1280x720/a02535383b/flexible-data-2.png)

### Get data formats tailored to your needs

Access traffic volumes at annual, monthly, daily or hourly levels to suit your needs. Analyze road traffic trends with the ability to choose between API and batch data delivery: integrate seamlessly via API for location-based services and traffic analytics or go with batch delivery for detailed traffic data reports covering national, regional or custom areas.

![Image 16: historical traffic illustration](https://media.tomtom.com/f/178460/1280x720/95954002a5/comprehensive-histroical-traffic-3.png)

### Get comprehensive historical traffic data

Use historical insights on traffic volumes to analyze trends and improve forecasting. Our data spans multiple timeframes, enabling before-and-after analyses, so you can easily measure the impact of changes and make robust decisions over time.

![Image 17: billboard showing traffic ad in highway](https://media.tomtom.com/f/178460/1280x720/5d3840c293/feature_04_traffic_volumes_desktop_1280x720px.jpg)

### Make smarter decisions across industries

Our accurate traffic volume data is leveraged for traffic management, site selection and risk modeling, supporting industries such as government, real estate, geomarketing, fleets and logistics and insurtech in making more successful decisions.
