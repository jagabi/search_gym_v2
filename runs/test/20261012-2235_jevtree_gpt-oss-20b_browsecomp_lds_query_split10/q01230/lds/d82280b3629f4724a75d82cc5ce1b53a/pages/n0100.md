FEATURES AND BENEFITS

## Faster traffic insights. Custom areas. Lower cost

From neighborhoods to countries, view precise analyses in an instant. Get a complete picture of movement within your chosen area and timeframe. Access insights when and where you need them with a highly cost-effective solution.

![Image 10: Map showing congestion levels in Los Angeles and San Diego, with a graph displaying daily fluctuations over time.](https://media.tomtom.com/f/178460/1280x720/d9223e6559/aa_feature_02_desktop_1280x720px.jpg)

### Comparative data-rich insights

See congestion levels, travel speeds, free-flow speeds and travel times per 10 km for your chosen area, organized by hour, day or date range. Easily compare areas and timeframes to identify patterns, inform strategic actions and evaluate the impact of changes.

![Image 11: Map showing traffic data in Northern Germany, with a pop-up detailing congestion level, speed, and road network length near Berlin.](https://media.tomtom.com/f/178460/1280x720/fedef8c771/aa_feature_01_desktop_1280x720px.jpg)

### Understand complex data and make informed decisions

Go beyond simple metrics. Whether you're optimizing city infrastructure, assessing mobility trends for financial forecasting or enhancing location-based marketing strategies — turn complex traffic data into clear, actionable decisions.

![Image 12: Map interface showing data download options for North America, with CSV and GeoJSON formats. Includes zoom and layer tools on the right.](https://media.tomtom.com/f/178460/1280x720/9f7cf128ef/aa_feature_03_desktop_1280x720px.jpg)

### Multiple data formats available on demand

Download data in multiple formats, including CSV and GeoJSON. Choose the format that fits your needs for seamless integration into your existing systems and processes.

![Image 13: Map showing the San Francisco Bay Area with a highlighted region, featuring options to "Upload GeoJSON" and "New report."](https://media.tomtom.com/f/178460/1280x720/16d9e0db5b/aa_feature_04_desktop_1280x720px.jpg)

### Easy-to-use reports, ready in seconds

Get the data you need at any time. View or download easy-to-use reports in an instant. Simply define your area and choose your timeframe to unlock a new level of traffic intelligence, for a fraction of the cost of traditional methods.
