FEATURES AND BENEFITS

## Instant access to travel times and speed insights

TomTom Traffic Stats gives you direct access to the industry’s largest historical road traffic database. With deep insight into road speeds, travel times and traffic density, you can identify mobility pain points, assess the impact of road network changes, improve traffic management and much more.

![Image 55: Maximize existing investments](https://media.tomtom.com/f/178460/1280x720/43c16306ea/traffic-stats_feature_01_desktop-1280x720px.jpg)

### Maximize existing investments

Understand and monitor your network performance. Complement existing infrastructure and sensor data with broader coverage and higher volume samples to gain a fuller picture of real-road conditions.

![Image 56: Rich reports, ready in minutes](https://media.tomtom.com/f/178460/1280x720/e426f8fc3b/traffic-stats_feature_02_desktop-1280x720px.jpg)

### Data-rich reports, ready in minutes

Create customized queries for specific routes or entire areas and explore the results through interactive maps and charts. Most reports are available to view or download in minutes, with even the most complicated queries taking less than 4 hours to complete.

![Image 57: Multiple formats available on demand](https://media.tomtom.com/f/178460/1280x720/b125c5730b/traffic-stats_feature_03_desktop-1280x720px.jpg)

### Multiple formats available on demand

Available 24/7, Traffic Stats lets you access powerful traffic insights whenever you need them. With data formats including Shape, Json and Excel, choose the format that works best for you.

![Image 58: Analyze traffic across every road segment](https://media.tomtom.com/f/178460/1280x720/3bdd0e390e/traffic-stats_feature_04_desktop-1280x720px.jpg)

### Analyze traffic accross every road segment

Traffic Stats provides key insights like average and median speeds, speed limits, road types, travel times and sample sizes that inform data-driven decisions across entire road networks in over 70 countries.
