# 🇧🇷 Discover the Vitoria to Belo Horizonte Train Journey: A Hidden Gem of Brazil’s Railways

Written by **Peter Lauffer**

September 15, 2024

**Brazil’s vast railway network once connected every corner of the country, making it one of the most extensive in the world. Today, while many routes have faded into history, the journey between Vitoria and Belo Horizonte remains a captivating way to explore Brazil’s southeastern region. This train ride offers travelers a unique perspective of Brazil’s landscapes, showcasing everything from lush coastal hills to vibrant urban centers. Whether you’re a seasoned train enthusiast or a first-time traveler looking for a unique experience, this guide to the Vitoria to Belo Horizonte train journey will help you make the most of your trip.**

![Image 5](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5efa2cf76690196c83784c64/66e653180c4de4395c5d4ba0_66e64aa77b969607de8b1196_Train%2520Vitoria%2520to%2520Belo%2520Horizonte.avif)

### An Overview of the Vitoria to Belo Horizonte Railroad

The Vitoria to Belo Horizonte railway line is a scenic route that stretches approximately 664 kilometers through the heart of southeastern Brazil. Starting from Vitoria, the capital of Espirito Santo, the train travels northwest, passing through the lush hills of Espirito Santo and into the rugged terrain of Minas Gerais before reaching its final destination in Belo Horizonte, the state’s bustling capital. The train departs daily from Vitoria at 7:30 am, arriving in Belo Horizonte around 8:30 pm, making it a 13-hour journey that allows passengers to relax and soak in the changing scenery from coast to interior.

![Image 6](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5efa2cf76690196c83784c64/66e653180c4de4395c5d4ba4_66e64ebce2fe35caf43952c0_Vitoria%2520Railway%2520Station.avif)

A train waiting at the Pedro Nolasco Railway Station in Vitoria, Brazil. 

The train offers two classes: Economy and Executive. Economy class provides comfortable seating, while Executive class offers extra legroom and a more spacious environment. Both classes are air-conditioned, ensuring a comfortable ride regardless of the weather outside. Onboard, you’ll find a dining car offering a selection of simple meals, snacks, and drinks. While there are no food sellers at the intermediate stations, bringing your own snacks is also an option if you prefer to have your own provisions.

![Image 7](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5efa2cf76690196c83784c64/66e653180c4de4395c5d4b9b_66e65221ac582967377f6d0e_Arrival%2520of%2520our%2520train%2520in%2520Belo%2520Horzonte.avif)

Our train arrived at the Belo Horizonte Railway Station.

### What to Expect on the Journey

Embarking on the Vitoria to Belo Horizonte train journey is like stepping back in time while experiencing the best of modern comfort. As you travel from the Atlantic Coast across the lush coastal hills, you’ll witness a vibrant mix of landscapes—from verdant valleys to rugged mountains and picturesque small towns. The train stops at various towns, offering a glimpse of local life and the lively action at the railway stations, where you’ll see vendors, commuters, and fellow travelers adding to the journey’s charm.

![Image 8](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5efa2cf76690196c83784c64/66e653180c4de4395c5d4ba8_66e64f5d0410255902654f5f_Dining%2520Car%2520of%2520the%2520Vitoria%2520to%2520Belo%2520Horizonte%2520Train.avif)

The dining car of the Vitoria to Belo Horizonte train, bustling with passengers enjoying their journey.

The train climbs to Belo Horizonte, situated 850 meters above sea level, where the landscape transitions into the rolling hills and rocky outcrops typical of Minas Gerais. This journey not only provides stunning views but also serves as a cultural corridor, linking two distinct yet complementary Brazilian regions. Remember to pack a light jacket as the air-conditioning can make it chilly inside the train, and Wi-Fi is not available on board, so plan to entertain yourself with a good book, music, or simply enjoy the view. The dining car is also a great place to meet fellow travelers, exchange stories, and make new friends.

![Image 9](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5efa2cf76690196c83784c64/66e653180c4de4395c5d4bb0_66e651880e6341d386a42dbb_Belo%2520Horizonte%2520Railway%2520Station.avif)

 The vibrant railway station in Belo Horizonte, Brazil, set against a backdrop of the city’s dynamic urban landscape. The station features a classic architectural style with white facades and red-framed arched windows, reflecting a historic charm.

### Travel Tips for Booking Tickets

Booking tickets in advance is highly recommended, especially during peak travel seasons. You can purchase tickets online through Vale’s official train ticket website or directly at the Pedro Nolasco Railway Station in Vitoria or the Belo Horizonte station. If you decide to buy your tickets on the day of departure, be sure to arrive early to avoid long lines and ensure you get a seat in your preferred class. Keep in mind that the train often fills up quickly, so planning ahead will save you time and ensure a more relaxed start to your journey.

### Explore Vitoria: A Hidden Coastal Gem

Vitoria, the vibrant capital of Espirito Santo, is a city that surprises many travelers with its rich history, beautiful landscapes, and cultural attractions. Nestled on an island surrounded by mountains and the sea, Vitoria offers a mix of modern amenities and historic charm. Start your exploration with a visit to the Convento da Penha, one of Brazil’s oldest and most significant churches, perched high on a hill with breathtaking views of the city and coastline. The Cathedral of Our Lady of Victory and the Palacio Anchieta are also must-visit landmarks, showcasing the city’s colonial past.

![Image 11](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5efa2cf76690196c83784c64/66e65b246a6fa4c89c7380be_66e654acac643abd3d6b9f5d_Vitoria%2520Espiritu%2520Santo.avif)

Vitoria’s beautiful landscape, with a sweeping view of the bay, lush green hills, and the iconic Third Bridge (Terceira Ponte) prominently crossing the water, connecting Vitoria to Vila Velha.

For railway enthusiasts, the Vale Museum offers fascinating insights into the region’s railway heritage and is located on the banks of the Vitoria River, separating Vitoria from Vila Velha. Take a stroll along the city’s seafront, where you can enjoy the fresh ocean breeze and stunning views, or relax on one of Vitoria’s beautiful beaches. Don’t miss the Mercado da Vila Rubim, where you can sample local dishes, shop for unique souvenirs, and experience the vibrant food culture of Espirito Santo. For those seeking outdoor adventures, the Morro do Moreno offers hiking trails with panoramic views, making it a perfect spot for photography enthusiasts.

![Image 12](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5efa2cf76690196c83784c64/66e65b246a6fa4c89c7380b5_66e65595399dcd6f9f15bbf0_Belo%2520Horizonte.avif)

The vibrant cityscape of Belo Horizonte, Brazil, with a view centered on the lush and historic Praca da Liberdade (Liberty Square). The square is lined with tall, majestic palm trees and lush greenery, creating a peaceful oasis amid the bustling city.

### Discover Belo Horizonte: A City of Culture, Art, and Delight

Belo Horizonte, the capital of Minas Gerais, is a city that embodies the perfect blend of tradition and modernity. Known for its lively cultural scene, historic sites, and delicious cuisine, Belo Horizonte is a destination that invites exploration. One of the city’s highlights is the Mercado Central de Belo Horizonte, a bustling market where you can find everything from traditional Minas cheese to artisanal crafts. It’s a sensory overload of sights, sounds, and tastes that reflects the heart and soul of Minas Gerais.

![Image 13](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5efa2cf76690196c83784c64/66e65b246a6fa4c89c7380c8_66e657b4fefdaea5ec10e840_Center%2520of%2520Belo%2520Horizonte.avif)

A bustling street scene in Belo Horizonte, Brazil, highlighting the city’s dynamic urban atmosphere. The image shows a busy intersection with cars, buses, and pedestrians actively moving through the streets.

Praca da Liberdade, a beautifully landscaped square surrounded by historical buildings with neoclassical architecture, offers a peaceful escape from the city’s busy streets. This area is also home to several museums and cultural centers, including the Palacio das Artes, which features contemporary art exhibitions, concerts, and theater performances. If you’re visiting on a Sunday, don’t miss the Hippie Fair at Afonso Pena Avenue, one of the largest open-air markets in Latin America, where you can find handmade crafts, jewelry, and local delicacies.

For nature lovers, a visit to the Mangabeiras Park is a must. Located on the slopes of the Serra do Curral, the park offers hiking trails with stunning city views, picnic areas, and the opportunity to see local wildlife. Belo Horizonte’s food scene is another highlight, with numerous restaurants and bars serving traditional dishes like pão de queijo (cheese bread), feijoada, and other Minas Gerais specialties. Whether you’re a foodie, an art lover, or just looking to soak in the local atmosphere, Belo Horizonte has something to offer everyone.

![Image 14](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5efa2cf76690196c83784c64/66e65b246a6fa4c89c7380c4_66e659169f754d016e097180_Busy%2520Belo%2520Horizonte%2520Railway%2520Station.avif)

The busy Belo Horizonte Railway Station, set against the backdrop of the city’s skyline. The station features classic architecture with a white facade and red-framed windows, characteristic of historic Brazilian railway buildings.
