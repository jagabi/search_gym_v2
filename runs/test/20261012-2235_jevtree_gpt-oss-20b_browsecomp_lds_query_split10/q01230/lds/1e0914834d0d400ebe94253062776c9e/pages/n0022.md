## See

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Central_Minas_Gerais&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: See")]

The **colonial towns** of [Mariana](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Mariana "Mariana"), [Tiradentes](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tiradentes "Tiradentes"), and [Ouro Preto](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ouro_Preto "Ouro Preto") are remarkably well preserved, with the latter being a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

**Pampulha** neighborhood in [Belo Horizonte](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte "Belo Horizonte") has some of the highlights of Brazil modern architecture, and one of the city's Oscar Niemeyer buildings.

The **Inhotim Museum of Contemporary Art** in [Brumadinho](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Brumadinho "Brumadinho") is one of the most important art venues of Brazil. The city also hosts the tourist circuit of **Veredas do Paraopeba**, which encompasses several landscapes that are considered heritage sites.

The **Sanctuary of Bom Jesus Matosinhos** in do [Congonhas](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Congonhas "Congonhas") is a complex of church, chapels and magnificent statues of prophets carved by the artist Aleijadinho. It is also a World Heritage Site.

The **Catedral de Nossa Senhora da Assunção** in [Mariana](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Mariana "Mariana") was constructed between 1709 and 1750.

There are several **mines** near [Ouro Preto](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ouro_Preto "Ouro Preto") that offer tours, and the city has many beautiful churches and interesting museums.

## Do

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Central_Minas_Gerais&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Do")]

Visit the **Caraça Natural Reserve** near [Mariana](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Mariana "Mariana") to explore its waterfalls, rivers, lakes and caves.

The **Itacolomi State Park**, between [Ouro Preto](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ouro_Preto "Ouro Preto") and [Mariana](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Mariana "Mariana"), is a park which contains very diverse flora and fauna with many hiking opportunities.

[Serra da Canastra National Park](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Serra_da_Canastra_National_Park "Serra da Canastra National Park") has several **waterfalls** that can be visited.

## Eat

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Central_Minas_Gerais&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Eat")]

[Tiradentes](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tiradentes "Tiradentes") is well-known amongst food lovers for its food festival in August and its many good regional and modern restaurants.

## Drink

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Central_Minas_Gerais&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Drink")]

[Divinópolis](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Divin%C3%B3polis "Divinópolis") is known as the largest per capita consumer of beer in the country, so you have trouble finding a place drink there.
