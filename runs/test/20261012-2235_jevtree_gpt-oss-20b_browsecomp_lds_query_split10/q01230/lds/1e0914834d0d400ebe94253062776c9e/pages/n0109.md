**Sete Lagoas** (meaning 'Seven Lagoons' in [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language "Portuguese language")) is a city in the Brazilian state of [Minas Gerais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minas_Gerais "Minas Gerais"). The municipal area is 537 km 2 while the population was 238,909 in 2025.

## Geography

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sete_Lagoas&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Geography")]

### Location

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sete_Lagoas&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Location")]

Sete Lagoas is situated about 70 kilometres from [Belo Horizonte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte "Belo Horizonte"), the capital of Minas Gerais. The BR 040 motorway connects Sete Lagoas with Belo Horizonte and the national capital [Brasília](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bras%C3%ADlia "Brasília"). The altitude is 761 metres above sea level. It is surrounded by the following municipalities: [Araçaí](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ara%C3%A7a%C3%AD "Araçaí"), [Funilândia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funil%C3%A2ndia "Funilândia"), [Prudente de Morais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prudente_de_Morais "Prudente de Morais"), [Capim Branco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capim_Branco "Capim Branco"), [Esmeraldas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esmeraldas,_Minas_Gerais "Esmeraldas, Minas Gerais"), [Inhaúma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inha%C3%BAma "Inhaúma"), [Paraopeba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paraopeba "Paraopeba"), and [Caetanópolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caetan%C3%B3polis "Caetanópolis").

### Climate

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sete_Lagoas&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Climate")]

According to data from the meteorological station of the municipality, the average temperature of the city between the years 1961 to 1990 was 20.9°C.

The coldest month (July) had an average of 17.5°C while the hottest month (February) had an average of 23.0°C.

The four lowest temperature records at the Sete Lagoas meteorological station were as follows: 0.8°C on July 18, 1926; 1.3°C on October 31, 1942; 1.5°C on June 26, 1937 and also on May 8, 1942.

## Economy

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sete_Lagoas&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Economy")]

The economy is based on services, industry and some agriculture. The GDP in 2005 was R$2 billion 800 million, with 1 billion 120 million from services, 1 billion 250 thousand from industry, and 17 million from agriculture. There were 824 transformation industries in 2005 with 15,000 workers. [Heavy industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heavy_industry "Heavy industry") was important with 23 factories producing mainly [pig iron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pig_iron "Pig iron").[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sete_Lagoas#cite_note-IBGE-1) There is an Iveco-Fiat plant, which opened in 2001, that produces trucks and vans.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sete_Lagoas#cite_note-6)

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sete_Lagoas&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Health and education")]

## History

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sete_Lagoas&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: History")]

Evidence of pre-Columbian presence has been found in the [Gruta Rei do Mato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gruta_Rei_do_Mato "Gruta Rei do Mato") (Cave of the Forest King).

From around 1700, colonists entered Minas Gerais in search of [gold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gold "Gold"), [gems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemstone "Gemstone") and [diamonds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond "Diamond"). Nowadays, the Santa Luzia quarter is unofficially still called "o garimpo" (meaning "the goldmine").

Between 1780 and 1867, the name 'Sete Lagoas' was used for several regional types of administrations. The municipality of Sete Lagoas was founded in 1867.

Although "Sete Lagoas" means "Seven Lakes", there are currently more than seven lakes in Sete Lagoas (10 within the urban perimeter, 17 in the municipality). Some were dug out while others became part of the town when the town expanded. There are, however, 7 'official' lakes according to a municipal law from 1989. These are: Lagoa da Boa Vista, Lagoa da Catarina, Lagoa da Chácara, Lagoa do Cercadinho, Lagoa José Felix, Lagoa do Matadouro and Lagoa Paulino.

## Food

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sete_Lagoas&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Food")]

Food is an important element of the [culture of Minas Gerais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minas_Gerais#Culture "Minas Gerais"). In Sete Lagoas, two dishes can be found that are hardly found elsewhere: Galopé and Muchacho. Galopé is a dish of chicken (galo) and pork feet (pé de porco). Muchacho is also composed of chicken and pork but it also contains cheese.

## Sports

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sete_Lagoas&action=edit&section=10 "Edit section: Sports")]

[Bela Vista Futebol Clube](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bela%20Vista%20Futebol%20Clube%20(Minas%20Gerais) "pt:Bela Vista Futebol Clube (Minas Gerais)") is one of 4 relevant [football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football "Association football") team in the city. Also is known for being the only football team from Sete Lagoas to tour over Europe in the 1959. Despite severe issues throughout the tour, the club reached some highlights scores in matches such as against [Real Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Madrid "Real Madrid") in [Santiago Bernabéu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago_Bernab%C3%A9u_Stadium "Santiago Bernabéu Stadium").

[Democrata Futebol Clube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democrata_Futebol_Clube "Democrata Futebol Clube") is a [football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_(soccer) "Football (soccer)") club based in Sete Lagoas. The club plays its home matches at the [Arena do Jacaré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arena_do_Jacar%C3%A9 "Arena do Jacaré") ("Alligator Arena", so named because of Democrata's mascot; the official name is Joaquim Henrique Nogueira stadium).

Both teams used to play the traditional "sete-lagoano" derby being a very famous and known as the "Clássico dos sertões" ( Countryside's Derby ). An interesting fact about both was its old home arenas: it was only one block distance from each, which was an extra spice for the rivality. The old Democrata's Arena was demolished, but the original and glorious BV arena is still there.

In 2010 and 2011, Arena do Jacaré served as a home stadium for [Atlético Mineiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clube_Atl%C3%A9tico_Mineiro "Clube Atlético Mineiro"), [Cruzeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cruzeiro_Esporte_Clube "Cruzeiro Esporte Clube") and [América-MG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Am%C3%A9rica_Mineiro "América Mineiro"), as both [Belo Horizonte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte "Belo Horizonte") stadia - [Mineirão](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mineir%C3%A3o "Mineirão") and [Independência](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arena_Independ%C3%AAncia "Arena Independência") - were going through reforms.

Other smaller, but very competitive clubs are [Ideal Sport Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ideal_Sport_Club?action=edit&redlink=1 "Ideal Sport Club (page does not exist)"), [América Futebol Clube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Am%C3%A9rica_Futebol_Clube_(Sete_Lagoas)?action=edit&redlink=1 "América Futebol Clube (Sete Lagoas) (page does not exist)").
