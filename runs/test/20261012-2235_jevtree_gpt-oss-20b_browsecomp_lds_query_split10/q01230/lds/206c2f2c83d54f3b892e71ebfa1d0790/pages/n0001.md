While searching the Internet, I discovered another movie theater that has scheduled building and been restored. It is the Cinema Theatro Brasil Vallourec, located in the city of Belo Horizonte, in the southeast of Brazil. It was opened in 1932 and had a capacity for almost 2,500 people. After several renovations, its capacity is now 1,000 people. It is a Historical and Cultural Heritage site of the city of Belo Horizonte and its programming consists of plays, film screenings and musical shows. I used Google Translator
