Welcome to **Wikivoyage's travel forum**, a place where you can start a discussion on anything about travel.

Use this page to ask questions, share tips or anecdotes from your travels, or start a conversation about your next destination.

Guidelines

*   Postings on this page should be in English. Use other languages as clarification as needed, but if the point of the posting cannot be understood by an English speaker, it is likely to be quickly deleted.
*   Keep discussion civil.
*   Start a new topic using the "Add topic" tab at the top of the page.
*   Archived discussions can be found at [Wikivoyage:Travel forum/Archives](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Wikivoyage:Travel_forum/Archives "Wikivoyage:Travel forum/Archives") ([archive for 2026](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Wikivoyage:Travel_forum/2026 "Wikivoyage:Travel forum/2026")).
*   For help with editing or using Wikivoyage itself, please go to the [Wikivoyage:Travellers' pub](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Wikivoyage:Travellers'_pub "Wikivoyage:Travellers' pub") instead.
*   Avoid posting personal information.
*   **No spam or self-promotion**; sharing personal reviews is encouraged, but Wikivoyage's policy [Don't tout](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Wikivoyage:Don't_tout "Wikivoyage:Don't tout") applies to this page as much as to any other page on this site.
*   To receive comment notifications, click the "subscribe" button on the top right corner of a post.
*   Discussions are archived after 60 days of inactivity.

Prague is an excellent destination so it all depends on your appetite for museums, galleries and architecture. Visitors from west Europe have a short travel time so for a first visit 4 nights / 3 days is about right. You can spend much longer, but at some point the baroque churches will blur before your eyes. Intercontinental visitors might want a week to make the trip worthwhile, but could split the days with another stand-out city such as Vienna, or for a complete change of scenery head for the mountains and national parks. You could easily spend a couple of weeks in the country, but I would suggest only spending about 4 nights in Prague (unless you can stay with friends or other cheap accommodation). Central Prague is very busy and you may find the number of other visitors overwhelming. You could spend 3 nights in [Brno](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Brno "Brno"), which has a castle, several museums and other interesting buildings. [Pilsen](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Pilsen "Pilsen") is worth 2 nights to see its cathedral, museums and the home of lager. [Český Krumlov](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/%C4%8Cesk%C3%BD_Krumlov "Český Krumlov") is a World Heritage site town which is worth visiting, but draws the crowds. Also consider a spa town like [Karlovy Vary](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Karlovy_Vary "Karlovy Vary") where you can sample the water and go for country walks, the beautiful churches in [Olomouc](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Olomouc "Olomouc"), the renaissance town of [Telč](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tel%C4%8D "Telč") or the Ossuary in [Kutná Hora](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kutn%C3%A1_Hora "Kutná Hora"). You will probably find sleeping and eating in most of theses places is half the price of Prague.
