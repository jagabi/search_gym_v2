### [Discover](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Discover "Discover")

[![Image 18](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b3/Lake_Louise_Victoria_Glacier.jpg/250px-Lake_Louise_Victoria_Glacier.jpg?utm_source=en.wikivoyage.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/File:Lake_Louise_Victoria_Glacier.jpg)

*   Founded by **French Huguenot** settlers who fled the Inquisition in Europe, [Franschhoek](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Franschhoek "Franschhoek") makes a serious attempt at being the **food and wine capital** of South Africa.
*   The main attractions at [Lake Louise](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Lake_Louise "Lake Louise") are the **vibrantly coloured glacial lakes** and the **Victoria Glacier** (_pictured_) at the head of the lake.
*   There are **no airports** in [Andorra](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Andorra "Andorra").
