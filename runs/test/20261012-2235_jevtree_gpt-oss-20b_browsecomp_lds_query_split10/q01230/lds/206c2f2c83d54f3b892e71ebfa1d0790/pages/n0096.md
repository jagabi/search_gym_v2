[Film Comment Live: Breaking Borders at Cannes Docs](https://www.marchedufilm.com/fr/programs/cannes-docs/)

 What does it mean to break borders in documentary today? This panel at the Cannes Film Festival, curated by DAE – Documentary Association of Europe and Film Comment, explores the groundbreaking networks reshaping the film landscape by creating new connections across geographies and communities. _Film Comment_ editor Devika Girish joins this conversation about cinema, power, belonging, and the future of international collaboration. 

May 15, 2026·Cannes Film Festival

[Learn more](https://www.marchedufilm.com/fr/programs/cannes-docs/)
