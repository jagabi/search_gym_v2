**Belo Horizonte**[[a]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-8) is the [sixth-largest city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_largest_cities_in_Brazil "List of largest cities in Brazil") in Brazil, with a population of around 2.4 million, and the third largest metropolitan area, containing a population of 6 million.[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-9) It is the [13th-largest city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_South_America "List of cities in South America") in South America and the [18th-largest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Largest_cities_in_the_Americas "Largest cities in the Americas") in the Americas. The metropolis is anchor to the [Belo Horizonte metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Belo_Horizonte "Greater Belo Horizonte"), ranked as the [third most populous metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_metropolitan_areas_in_Brazil "List of metropolitan areas in Brazil") in Brazil and the [17th most populous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_metropolitan_areas_in_the_Americas "List of metropolitan areas in the Americas") in the Americas. Belo Horizonte is the capital of [the state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_Brazil "States of Brazil") of [Minas Gerais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minas_Gerais "Minas Gerais"), Brazil's [second-most populous state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Brazilian_states_by_population "List of Brazilian states by population"). It is the first planned modern city in Brazil.

The region was first settled in the early 18th century, but the city as it is known today was planned and constructed in the 1890s to replace [Ouro Preto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouro_Preto "Ouro Preto") as the capital of Minas Gerais. The city features a mixture of contemporary and classical buildings and is home to several modern Brazilian architectural icons, most notably the [Pampulha Complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pampulha_Modern_Ensemble "Pampulha Modern Ensemble"). In planning the city, Aarão Reis and Francisco Bicalho sought inspiration in the urban planning of Washington, D.C.[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-10) The city has employed notable programs in urban revitalization and [food security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_security "Food security"), for which it has been awarded international accolades.

The city is built on several hills, and is completely surrounded by mountains.[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-11) There are several large parks in the surroundings of Belo Horizonte. The Mangabeiras Park (Parque das Mangabeiras), 6 km (4 mi) southeast of the city centre in the hills of Curral Ridge (Serra do Curral), has a broad view of the city. It has an area of 2.35 km 2 (580 acres), of which 0.9 km 2 (220 acres) is covered by the native forest. The Jambreiro Woods (Mata do Jambreiro) nature reserve extends over 912 hectares (2,250 acres), with vegetation typical of the [Atlantic Forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Forest "Atlantic Forest"). More than 100 species of birds inhabit the reserve, as well as 10 species of mammals.

Belo Horizonte was one of the host cities of the [1950](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1950_FIFA_World_Cup "1950 FIFA World Cup") and the [2014 FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_FIFA_World_Cup "2014 FIFA World Cup"). Additionally, the city shared as host of the [2013 FIFA Confederations Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_FIFA_Confederations_Cup "2013 FIFA Confederations Cup") and the [football tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_at_the_2016_Summer_Olympics "Football at the 2016 Summer Olympics") during the [2016 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Summer_Olympics "2016 Summer Olympics").

### Portuguese colonization

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Belo_Horizonte&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Portuguese colonization")]

The metropolis was once a small village, founded by [João Leite da Silva Ortiz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jo%C3%A3o_Leite_da_Silva_Ortiz?action=edit&redlink=1 "João Leite da Silva Ortiz (page does not exist)")[[pt](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jo%C3%A3o%20Leite%20da%20Silva%20Ortiz "pt:João Leite da Silva Ortiz")], a [bandeirante](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandeirante "Bandeirante") explorer from [São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Paulo_(city) "São Paulo (city)"). The explorer settled in the region in 1701, leading a gold rush expedition. He then established a farm called "Curral d'el Rey," archaic Portuguese for the "King's Corral". The farm's wealth and success encouraged people from surrounding places to move into the region, and Curral del Rey became a village surrounded by farms.[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-12)

Another important factor contributing to the growth of the village was people migrating from the [São Francisco River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Francisco_River "São Francisco River") region, who had to pass through Curral d'el Rey to reach southern parts of Brazil. Travelers usually visited a small wooden chapel, where they prayed for a safe trip, so the chapel was named Capela da Nossa Senhora da Boa Viagem, which means "Chapel of Our Lady of the Good Journey." After the construction of Belo Horizonte, the old baroque chapel was replaced by a [neo-Gothic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neo-Gothic "Neo-Gothic") church that became the city's cathedral.[[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-13)

### Brazilian empire and republic

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Belo_Horizonte&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Brazilian empire and republic")]

[![Image 20](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/01/Inauguracaobh.jpg/250px-Inauguracaobh.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Inauguracaobh.jpg)

Founding of the city in 1897

The previous capital of Minas Gerais, [Ouro Preto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouro_Preto "Ouro Preto") (meaning "black gold," due to dark rocks with gold inside found on the region), originally called "Vila Rica" ("wealthy village"), was a symbol of both the monarchic [Brazilian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Empire "Brazilian Empire") and the period when most of Brazilian income was from the mining industry. That never pleased the members of the [Inconfidência Mineira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inconfid%C3%AAncia_Mineira "Inconfidência Mineira"), republican intellectuals who conspired against the Portuguese dominion of Brazil.

In 1889 Brazil became a republic, and it was agreed that a new state capital, in tune with a modern and prosperous Minas Gerais, had to be established.[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-14) In 1893 due to the climatic and topographic conditions, Curral Del Rey was selected by Minas Gerais governor [Afonso Pena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afonso_Augusto_Moreira_Pena "Afonso Augusto Moreira Pena") among other cities as the location for the new economic and cultural center of the state, under the new name of _Cidade de Minas_, or City of Minas.[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-15)

[Aarão Reis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aar%C3%A3o_Reis?action=edit&redlink=1 "Aarão Reis (page does not exist)"), an [urbanist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urbanist "Urbanist") from [Pará](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Par%C3%A1 "Pará"), was then chosen to design the second [planned city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planned_city "Planned city") of Brazil after [Teresina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teresina "Teresina"). Cidade de Minas was inaugurated in 1897, with many unfinished buildings as the [Brazilian government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_Brazil "Politics of Brazil") set a deadline for its completion. The local government encouraged growth through subsidies. It offered free lots and funding for building houses. An interesting feature of Reis' downtown street plan for Belo Horizonte was the inclusion of a symmetrical array of perpendicular and diagonal streets named after [Brazilian states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_Brazil "States of Brazil") and Brazilian [indigenous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigenous_peoples_of_the_Americas "Indigenous peoples of the Americas") tribes.[_[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation\_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")_]

[![Image 21](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/ce/Belo_Horizonte_%28MG%29.tif/lossy-page1-250px-Belo_Horizonte_%28MG%29.tif.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Belo_Horizonte_(MG).tif)

Belo Horizonte, 1970s, [National Archives of Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_National_Archives "Brazilian National Archives")

In 1906 the name was changed to Belo Horizonte, which translates to "Beautiful Horizon" in English. The city's name is derived from the surrounding Curral del Rey Mountains, which create a visually striking, hilly ridge that appears as a "beautiful horizon" when viewed from the city. At that time, the city was experiencing a considerable [industrial expansion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrialization "Industrialization") that increased its commercial and [service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tertiary_sector_of_the_economy "Tertiary sector of the economy") sectors. From its very beginning, the city's original plan prohibited workers from living inside the urban area, which was defined by Avenida do Contorno (a long avenue that goes around the city's central areas). Reserved for government workers (hence the name of the trendy neighbourhood "Funcionários"), and bringing about an accelerated occupation outside the city's area well provided with infrastructure since its very beginning. Naturally, the city's original planners did not count on its ongoing population growth, which proved especially intense in the last 20 years of the 20th century.

In the 1940s a young [Oscar Niemeyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_Niemeyer "Oscar Niemeyer") designed the Pampulha Neighbourhood to great acclaim, a commission he got thanks to then-mayor and soon-to-be-president [Juscelino Kubitschek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juscelino_Kubitschek "Juscelino Kubitschek"). These two men are largely responsible for the wide [avenues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avenue_(landscape) "Avenue (landscape)"), large lakes, parks, and jutting skylines that characterize the city today.[[15]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-16) A 1949 American government film favorably reviewed the planning and building of the city.[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-17)

Belo Horizonte is fast becoming a regional center of commerce. The Latin American research and development center of [Google](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google "Google"), situated in Belo Horizonte, was responsible for the management and operation of the former [social networking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_networking "Social networking") website [Orkut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orkut "Orkut"). It continues to be a trendsetter in the arts, particularly where music, literature, architecture, and the _avant garde_ are concerned.[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-18)

### Surrounding cities and metropolitan area

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Belo_Horizonte&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Surrounding cities and metropolitan area")]

[![Image 22](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/74/Belo_Horizonte_at_night.jpg/330px-Belo_Horizonte_at_night.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Belo_Horizonte_at_night.jpg)

Belo Horizonte from [ISS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISS "ISS") at night

The term "Grande BH" ("Greater Belo Horizonte") denotes any of various definitions for the metropolitan area of Belo Horizonte. The legally defined _Metropolitan Region of Belo Horizonte_ consists of 34 municipalities in total, and a population of around six million inhabitants (as of 2021[[update]](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Belo_Horizonte&action=edit), according to Brazil's National Institute of Geography and Statistics ([IBGE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Institute_of_Geography_and_Statistics "Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics"))).[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-IBGE_Pop_2007-19)[[19]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-SIDRA-20)

The intense process of urbanization that is taking place in the metropolitan region has made some of the political boundaries between municipalities in the region obsolete. The city is now composed of a relatively contiguous urban area, centred on Belo Horizonte, which extends out into municipalities such as [Contagem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contagem "Contagem"), [Betim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betim "Betim"), [Nova Lima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nova_Lima "Nova Lima"), [Raposos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raposos "Raposos"), [Ribeirão das Neves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ribeir%C3%A3o_das_Neves "Ribeirão das Neves"), [Ibirité](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibirit%C3%A9 "Ibirité"), [Santa Luzia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Luzia,_Minas_Gerais "Santa Luzia, Minas Gerais") and [Sabará](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabar%C3%A1 "Sabará"), among others. The municipality is bounded to the north by [Vespasiano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vespasiano "Vespasiano"), to the north east by Santa Luzia, by Sabará to the east, by Nova Lima to the southeast, [Brumadinho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brumadinho "Brumadinho") to the south and Ribeirão das Neves, Contagem and [Ibirité](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibirit%C3%A9 "Ibirité") to the west.

According to the current [geographic classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Intermediate_and_Immediate_Geographic_Regions_of_Minas_Gerais "List of Intermediate and Immediate Geographic Regions of Minas Gerais") by IBGE, the municipality belongs to the [Immediate Geographic Region of Belo Horizonte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immediate_Geographic_Region_of_Belo_Horizonte "Immediate Geographic Region of Belo Horizonte"), in the [Intermediate Geographic Region of Belo Horizonte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intermediate_Geographic_Region_of_Belo_Horizonte "Intermediate Geographic Region of Belo Horizonte").

### Geology and geomorphology

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Belo_Horizonte&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Geology and geomorphology")]

Belo Horizonte lies on a region of contact between different geological series of the [Proterozoic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proterozoic "Proterozoic"). The geology largely comprises [crystalline rocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystalline_rock "Crystalline rock"), which give rise to the varied morphology of the landscape. It is located in a large geological unit known as the [craton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craton "Craton") of [São Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Francisco_River "São Francisco River"), referring to extensive crustal nucleus of central-eastern Brazil, tectonically stable at the end of the [Paleoproterozoic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paleoproterozoic "Paleoproterozoic") and bordering areas that suffered the regeneration at the [Neoproterozoic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neoproterozoic "Neoproterozoic").

The [archean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archean "Archean") rocks members of Belo Horizonte complex and supracrustal sequences of the Paleoproterozoic is predominant. The area of Belo Horizonte complex includes the geomorphological unit called _Depression of Belo Horizonte_, which represents about 70% of the municipality area and has its greatest expression in the northern [Ribeirão Arrudas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ribeir%C3%A3o_Arrudas "Ribeirão Arrudas") pipeline. The [metasedimentary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metasedimentary "Metasedimentary") rocks has its area of occurrence on the south of Ribeirão Arrudas pipeline, constituting about 30% of the area of Belo Horizonte. The characteristics of this area are lithological diversities and rugged topography, which has its maximum expression in the _Serra do Curral_ (Corral Ridge), the southern boundary of the municipality.

Its soil comprises a succession of layers of rocks of varied composition, represented by [itabirite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itabirite "Itabirite"), [dolomite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolomite_(rock) "Dolomite (rock)"), [quartzite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quartzite "Quartzite"), [phyllites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phyllite "Phyllite") and [schists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schist "Schist") different from the general direction northwest–southeast and dip to the southeast. The hills of Belo Horizonte are part of the [Espinhaço Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Espinha%C3%A7o_Mountains "Espinhaço Mountains") and belong to the larger Itacolomi mountain chain. The highest point in the municipality is in the _Serra do Curral_, reaching 1,538 metres (5,046 ft).

### Parks

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Belo_Horizonte&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Parks")]

[![Image 23](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b4/PESRM_-_Parque_Estadual_da_Serra_do_Rola_Mo%C3%A7a.jpg/250px-PESRM_-_Parque_Estadual_da_Serra_do_Rola_Mo%C3%A7a.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:PESRM_-_Parque_Estadual_da_Serra_do_Rola_Mo%C3%A7a.jpg)

[Serra do Rola-Moça State Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serra_do_Rola-Mo%C3%A7a_State_Park "Serra do Rola-Moça State Park")

A centre for conservation and preservation of animals and plants it has also developed environmental education projects. The Zoo, which encompasses a total area of 1.4 million square meters, is located at the Foundation's headquarters and is regarded as one of the most complete in Latin America. It has a collection of close to 900 animals representing 200 species, from Brazil and other parts of the world, as well as the first public butterfly sanctuary in South America.

Pampulha Ecological Park is administered by the Zoo-Botanical Foundation of Belo Horizonte and was inaugurated on May 21, 2004. It is 30 acres (12 ha) of green area that offers to the population and the tourists a permanent programming of [environmental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_environment "Natural environment"), cultural and patrimonial education.

The city contains the 102 hectares (250 acres)[Baleia State Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baleia_State_Park "Baleia State Park"), created in 1988 but still not implemented as of 2014.[[20]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-21) It contains part of the 3,941 hectares (9,740 acres)[Serra do Rola-Moça State Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serra_do_Rola-Mo%C3%A7a_State_Park "Serra do Rola-Moça State Park"), created in 1994.[[21]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-22)

### Climate

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Belo_Horizonte&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Climate")]

Further information: [Climate of Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_of_Brazil "Climate of Brazil")

[![Image 24](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/22/Tempestade_BH.jpg/250px-Tempestade_BH.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Tempestade_BH.jpg)

Storm over the city

Due to its inland location, 500 kilometres (310 mi) from the Atlantic Ocean, and elevation of 852 m (2,795 ft),[[22]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-23) Belo Horizonte has a [Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification "Köppen climate classification") of a [tropical savanna climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_savanna_climate "Tropical savanna climate") (_Aw_). Summers are humid and rainy, but milder than those in nearby, lower-altitude cities, and winters are dry and sunny. The coldest month is generally July, with a lowest recorded temperature of 3.1°C (38°F), and the hottest month is usually January.

According to the [2022 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Brazilian_census "2022 Brazilian census"), there were 2,315,560 people residing in the city of Belo Horizonte.[[33]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-:0-34) The census revealed the following numbers: 1,008,878 [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Brazilian "White Brazilian") people (43.6%), 986,302 [Pardo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pardo "Pardo") ([Multiracial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiracial#Brazil "Multiracial")) people (42.6%), 312,920 [Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afro-Brazilian "Afro-Brazilian") people (13.5%), 4,881 [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Brazilian "Asian Brazilian") people (0.2%), 2,486 [Amerindian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigenous_peoples_in_Brazil "Indigenous peoples in Brazil") people (0.1%).[[33]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-:0-34)

In 2010, the city had 428,893 opposite-sex couples and 1,090 [same-sex couples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same-sex_couple "Same-sex couple"). The population of Belo Horizonte was 53.1% female and 46.9% male.[[34]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-autogenerated3-35)

The Metropolitan Region of Belo Horizonte, called [Greater Belo Horizonte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Belo_Horizonte "Greater Belo Horizonte"), is the [3rd most populous of Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_largest_cities_in_Brazil "List of largest cities in Brazil"), after only [Greater São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_S%C3%A3o_Paulo "Greater São Paulo") (with 19,672,582 people, first in Brazil and [5th in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_metropolitan_areas_by_population "List of metropolitan areas by population")) and [Greater Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro "Rio de Janeiro") (with 14,387,000 people). The city is the [6th most populous of the country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_largest_cities_in_Brazil "List of largest cities in Brazil").

During the 18th century, Minas Gerais received many [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Brazilian "Portuguese Brazilian") immigrants, mainly from [Northern Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Portugal "Northern Portugal") as well as many enslaved Africans.[[35]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-36) Belo Horizonte has a notable [Italian influence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Brazilian "Italian Brazilian"); around 30% of the city's population have some [Italian origin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Brazilian "Italian Brazilian").[[36]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-37) The [Italian culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_culture "Italian culture") is present in the cuisine, dance, and language.[[37]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-38) People of [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Brazilian "German Brazilian"), [Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Brazilian "Spanish Brazilian"), [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Brazilian "French Brazilian"), and [Syrian-Lebanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_Brazilian "Arab Brazilian") ancestries also make up sizeable groups.

The [Catholic Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Church "Catholic Church") includes the territory of the municipality of Belo Horizonte and 28 other municipalities in the ecclesiastical jurisdiction of the [Archdiocese of Belo Horizonte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Archdiocese_of_Belo_Horizonte "Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Belo Horizonte"), which has [Divinópolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Divin%C3%B3polis "Roman Catholic Diocese of Divinópolis"), [Luz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Luz "Roman Catholic Diocese of Luz"), [Oliveira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Oliveira "Roman Catholic Diocese of Oliveira") and [Sete Lagoas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Sete_Lagoas "Roman Catholic Diocese of Sete Lagoas") as suffragan dioceses. Some of the churches in Belo Horizonte have great artistic value, especially the [Church of São Francisco de Assis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_Saint_Francis_of_Assisi,_Pampulha "Church of Saint Francis of Assisi, Pampulha"), in [Pampulha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pampulha_(Belo_Horizonte) "Pampulha (Belo Horizonte)"). The church's architectural design was by [Oscar Niemeyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_Niemeyer "Oscar Niemeyer") and its structural calculations were by [Joaquim Cardoso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joaquim_Cardozo "Joaquim Cardozo"). Its interior houses the Stations of the Cross, made up of 14 panels by [Cândido Portinari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candido_Portinari "Candido Portinari"), considered one of his most significant works. The external panels are by Cândido Portinari (figurative panel) and Paulo Werneck (abstract panel). The gardens were designed by [Burle Marx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_Burle_Marx "Roberto Burle Marx"). [Alfredo Ceschiatti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfredo_Ceschiatti "Alfredo Ceschiatti") sculpted the bronze bas-reliefs in the baptistery. Other shrines worth mentioning are the [Basilica of Our Lady of Lourdes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basilica_of_Our_Lady_of_Lourdes,_Belo_Horizonte "Basilica of Our Lady of Lourdes, Belo Horizonte") with its [neo-Gothic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothic_Revival_architecture "Gothic Revival architecture") style, in the Lourdes neighborhood; the [Church of São José](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Jos%C3%A9_Church "São José Church"), in the [hypercenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro,_Belo_Horizonte "Centro, Belo Horizonte") and the Church of São Judas Tadeu, in the Graça neighborhood. The Catholic Church recognizes Our Lady of Boa Viagem as the city's [patron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patron_saint "Patron saint") saint.[[38]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-39)

### Human development

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Belo_Horizonte&action=edit&section=12 "Edit section: Human development")]

[![Image 27](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c0/Praca_da_Liberdade_BH_17_Jan_2019_02.jpg/250px-Praca_da_Liberdade_BH_17_Jan_2019_02.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Praca_da_Liberdade_BH_17_Jan_2019_02.jpg)

[Savassi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savassi "Savassi") is a developed neighbourhood in Belo Horizonte.

In 1993, under mayor Patrus Ananias de Souza, the city started a series of innovations based on its citizens having the "[right to food](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_to_food "Right to food")." These include, for example, creating [farmers' markets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farmers'_market "Farmers' market") in the town to enable direct sales and regularly surveying market prices and posting the results across the city.[[39]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-40) The city's process of [participatory budgeting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Participatory_budgeting "Participatory budgeting") was linked with these innovations, as a result of which the [infant mortality rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infant_mortality_rate "Infant mortality rate") was reduced by 50% in a decade.[[40]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-41)[[41]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-thesis-42)

There is some evidence that these programs have helped support a higher quality of life for the local farmers partnering with the city and that this may be having positive effects on biodiversity in the Atlantic rainforest around the city.[[41]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-thesis-42)[[42]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-43) The city's development of these policies garnered the first "Future Policy Award" in 2009, awarded by the [World Future Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Future_Council "World Future Council"), a group of 50 activists (including [Frances Moore Lappé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frances_Moore_Lapp%C3%A9 "Frances Moore Lappé"), [Vandana Shiva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vandana_Shiva "Vandana Shiva"), [Wes Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wes_Jackson "Wes Jackson"), and [Youssou N'Dour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youssou_N'Dour "Youssou N'Dour")[[43]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-44)) concerned with the development and recognition of policies to promote a just and sustainable future.

The city has undertaken an internationally heralded project called Vila Viva ("Living Village" in Portuguese) that promises to "urbanize" the poorest areas (_[favelas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Favela "Favela")_), relocating families from areas with high risk of floods and landslides but keeping them in the same neighborhood, paving main avenues to allow public transportation, police and postal service to have access. All the work is done with 80% of locals, reducing unemployment and increasing family income.[[44]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-mayor-45)[[45]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-46) Former mayor [Fernando da Mata Pimentel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_da_Mata_Pimentel "Fernando da Mata Pimentel") was nominated for [World Mayor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Mayor "World Mayor") in 2005 on the strength of these and other programs.[[44]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-mayor-45)

## Economy

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Belo_Horizonte&action=edit&section=13 "Edit section: Economy")]

Main articles: [Economy of Belo Horizonte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economy_of_Belo_Horizonte "Economy of Belo Horizonte") and [Economy of Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economy_of_Brazil "Economy of Brazil")

[![Image 28](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/06/Usiminasmonumento.JPG/250px-Usiminasmonumento.JPG?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Usiminasmonumento.JPG)

[![Image 29](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/1a/Pra%C3%A7a_ABC_-_panoramio_%28cropped%29.jpg/250px-Pra%C3%A7a_ABC_-_panoramio_%28cropped%29.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Pra%C3%A7a_ABC_-_panoramio_(cropped).jpg)

Business district 

Belo Horizonte receives large numbers of visitors, as it is in the Brazilian main economic axis, exerting influence even on other states. Multinational and Brazilian companies, such as Google, [Deloitte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deloitte "Deloitte"), [Thoughtworks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thoughtworks "Thoughtworks"), [Localiza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Localiza "Localiza") and [Oi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oi_(mobile_phone_network) "Oi (mobile phone network)"), maintain offices in the city. The service sector plays a very important role in the economy of Belo Horizonte, being responsible for 85% of the city's Gross Domestic Product (GDP), with other industry making up most of the remaining 15%. Belo Horizonte has a developed industrial sector, being traditionally a hub of the Brazilian [steel-making](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siderurgy "Siderurgy") and [metallurgical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metallurgy "Metallurgy") industries, as the state of Minas Gerais has always been very rich in [minerals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minerals "Minerals"), specifically iron ore.

Belo Horizonte is the distribution and processing centre of a rich agricultural and mining region and the nucleus of a burgeoning industrial complex. Production is centred on steel, steel products, [automobiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automobile "Automobile"), and textiles. Gold, [manganese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manganese "Manganese"), and [gemstones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemstone "Gemstone") mined in the surrounding region are processed in the city.[[46]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-47) The main industrial district of the city was set during the 1940s in [Contagem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contagem "Contagem"), a part of greater Belo Horizonte. Multinational companies like [FIAT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIAT "FIAT") (which opened its plant in Betim in 1974), [Arcelor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcelor "Arcelor"), and [Toshiba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toshiba "Toshiba") have subsidiaries in the region, along with textiles like Group Rachelle Textil, Ematex and Cedro Textil, [cosmetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmetics "Cosmetics"), food, [chemicals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemicals "Chemicals"), [pharmaceuticals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharmaceuticals "Pharmaceuticals"), [furnishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Furnishing "Furnishing") and [refractory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refractory "Refractory") companies.

Among the companies headquartered in the city are steel producer Açominas (held by [Gerdau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerdau "Gerdau"), one of the largest multinationals originated in Brazil); [Usiminas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Usiminas "Usiminas"); Belgo-Mineira (held by [Arcelor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcelor "Arcelor")); [Acesita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acesita "Acesita") (partially held by Arcelor); mobile communication [Vivo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vivo_S.A. "Vivo S.A."); and [Telecom Italia Mobile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telecom_Italia_Mobile "Telecom Italia Mobile"), Dasein Executive Search, executive coaching company, as well as the [NYSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NYSE "NYSE")-listed electrical company [CEMIG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CEMIG "CEMIG"). Leading steel product makers Sumitomo Metals of Japan and Vallourec of France also have plans to construct an integrated steel works on the outskirts of the city.

There are also a large number of small enterprises in the technology sector with regional to nationwide success, particularly in the fields of [computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computing "Computing") and biotechnology. Because of both governmental and private funding in the diversification of its economy, the city has become an international reference in Information Technology and [Biotechnology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biotechnology "Biotechnology"), and is also cited because of the advanced corporate and university research in [Biodiesel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biodiesel "Biodiesel") fuel. The number of jobs in the Information sector has been growing at annual rates above 50%. The Belo Horizonte Metropolitan Area, composed of 33 cities under the capital's direct influence, is home to 16% of the country's biotechnology companies, with annual sales of over R$550 million.[[47]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-48)

Projects in these fields are likely to expand because of integration between universities, the oil company [Petrobras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrobras "Petrobras") and the [Brazilian Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Government "Brazilian Government"). One of the largest events that ever took place in the city, the [Inter-American Development Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inter-American_Development_Bank "Inter-American Development Bank") meeting, occurred in 2005 and attracted people from all over the world. For a long time Belo Horizonte was marked by the predominance of its [industrial sector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_sector "Industrial sector"), but from the 1990s there has been a constant expansion of the [service sector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Service_sector "Service sector") economy, particularly in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science "Computer science"), [biotechnology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biotechnology "Biotechnology"), [business tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_tourism "Business tourism"), fashion and the jewelry-making. The city is considered to be a strategic leader in the [Brazilian economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economy_of_Brazil "Economy of Brazil"). The move towards [business tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_tourism "Business tourism") transformed the capital into a national hub for this segment of the [tourist industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourist_industry "Tourist industry").

### Airports

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Belo_Horizonte&action=edit&section=18 "Edit section: Airports")]

Belo Horizonte is served by two airports:

*   [Tancredo Neves/Confins International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tancredo_Neves_International_Airport "Tancredo Neves International Airport"), dedicated to domestic and international traffic. It is located in the municipalities of [Lagoa Santa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagoa_Santa,_Minas_Gerais "Lagoa Santa, Minas Gerais") and [Confins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confins "Confins"), 38 kilometres (24 mi) from Belo Horizonte, and was opened in January 1984. Plans for gradual expansion to meet growing demand had been already drawn up from the airport's inception. The airport has one of the lowest rates of shutdown for bad weather in the country. It ran at limited capacity until 2005, when a large proportion of Pampulha Airport air traffic was transferred to Confins. There are direct international flights to/from [Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buenos_Aires "Buenos Aires"), [Fort Lauderdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Lauderdale "Fort Lauderdale"), Lisbon, [Orlando](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando,_Florida "Orlando, Florida"), Panama City, and [Santiago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago "Santiago");

### Highways

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Belo_Horizonte&action=edit&section=19 "Edit section: Highways")]

[![Image 36](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/47/Rodovia_em_Belo_Horizonte.jpg/250px-Rodovia_em_Belo_Horizonte.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Rodovia_em_Belo_Horizonte.jpg)

The city is connected to the rest of Minas Gerais state and the country by a number of roadways. [Minas Gerais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minas_Gerais "Minas Gerais") has the country's largest federal highway network.[[52]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-53)

*   [BR-040](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Highway_System#BR-040 "Brazilian Highway System") connects Belo Horizonte to [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro "Rio de Janeiro") (going south) and [Brasília](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bras%C3%ADlia "Brasília") (going northwest). It also links other cities in the state, such as [Juiz de Fora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juiz_de_Fora "Juiz de Fora"), [Conselheiro Lafaiete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conselheiro_Lafaiete "Conselheiro Lafaiete"), [Barbacena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbacena "Barbacena"), [Sete Lagoas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sete_Lagoas "Sete Lagoas"), and [Paracatu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracatu,_Minas_Gerais "Paracatu, Minas Gerais").
*   [BR-262](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BR-262 "BR-262") begins in [Mato Grosso do Sul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mato_Grosso_do_Sul "Mato Grosso do Sul") and ends in [Espírito Santo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esp%C3%ADrito_Santo "Espírito Santo"), crossing [Minas Gerais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minas_Gerais "Minas Gerais") from west to east. It links Belo Horizonte to [Pará de Minas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Par%C3%A1_de_Minas "Pará de Minas"), [Araxá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arax%C3%A1 "Araxá"), [Manhuaçu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhua%C3%A7u "Manhuaçu"), [Uberaba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uberaba "Uberaba"), [Governador Valadares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governador_Valadares "Governador Valadares"), and [Vitória](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vit%C3%B3ria,_Brazil "Vitória, Brazil"), the capital of [Espírito Santo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esp%C3%ADrito_Santo "Espírito Santo") state.
*   [BR-381](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodovia_Fern%C3%A3o_Dias "Rodovia Fernão Dias") is an important federal highway. It connects Belo Horizonte to [São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Paulo "São Paulo").
*   [MG-010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MG-010 "MG-010") is a state highway that connects the capital to the [Tancredo Neves International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tancredo_Neves_International_Airport "Tancredo Neves International Airport"), itself located in the municipalities of Confins and Lagoa Santa, which are part of the metropolitan area of Belo Horizonte. Starting in 2005, several flights were transferred from the [Pampulha Regional Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pampulha_Regional_Airport "Pampulha Regional Airport") to the international airport. To improve access to the international airport, MG-010 is being expanded (effectively duplicating its lanes).[[53]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-54)

The city is also served by other minor roads such as state highways MG-020, MG-050, MG-030, and MG-433. There is also an East-West Express Way, which goes from the city to the nearby industrial centres of [Contagem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contagem "Contagem") and [Betim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betim "Betim") (together having a population of ca. 900,000), and Anel Rodoviário, a kind of "beltway" – indeed it is not circumferential, but connects many highways, such as the federal (BR-ones) so it is not necessary for a large number of cars and trucks to pass through the city centre. Many of these roads are in poor condition, but in the last years many revitalization and rebuilding projects have been started.

### Bus system

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Belo_Horizonte&action=edit&section=20 "Edit section: Bus system")]

The bus system has a large number of bus lines going through all parts in the city, and is administered by _BHTRANS_. Among the upcoming projects are the expansion of the integration between bus lines and the [metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapid_transit "Rapid transit"), with integrated stations, many already in use. And the construction of bus corridors, with lanes and bus stops exclusively for the bus lines. Keeping buses from traffic congestions, making the trips more viable for commuters.

### Railways

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Belo_Horizonte&action=edit&section=21 "Edit section: Railways")]

[Belo Horizonte Metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte_Metro "Belo Horizonte Metro") or MetroBH started operating at the end of the 1970s. There is one line, with 19 stations, from Vilarinho to Eldorado Station, in [Contagem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contagem "Contagem"), transporting over 160,000 people daily. The current projects of expansion include Line 2, linking the existing Calafate Station to the region of Barreiro. And Line 3, from the city's main bus terminal to São Inácio de Loyola, [Savassi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savassi "Savassi") economical district. Line 2 is planned to be overground (similar to the current line) and Line 3 is planned to be underground, passing through the city's financial centre, Praça Sete and Afonso Pena Avenue. Also, Line 1 is planned to be extended to Novo Eldorado Station.[[54]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-55)

For long distance, Belo Horizonte is served by the [Vitória-Minas Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vit%C3%B3ria-Minas_Railway "Vitória-Minas Railway"). There are daily passenger trips connecting Belo Horizonte to [Cariacica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cariacica "Cariacica"), in the metropolitan region of [Vitória, Espírito Santo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vit%C3%B3ria,_Esp%C3%ADrito_Santo "Vitória, Espírito Santo"). On its route, the railway also connects Belo Horizonte to several locations on Eastern Minas Gerais, such as [Governador Valadares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governador_Valadares "Governador Valadares"), [João Monlevade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jo%C3%A3o_Monlevade "João Monlevade") and the cities of the [Vale do Aço Metropolitan Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vale_do_A%C3%A7o_Metropolitan_Region "Vale do Aço Metropolitan Region").

### Belo Horizonte Public Transportation statistics

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Belo_Horizonte&action=edit&section=22 "Edit section: Belo Horizonte Public Transportation statistics")]

The average amount of time people spend commuting with public transit in Belo Horizonte, for example to and from work, on a weekday is 85 min. 26% of public transit riders, ride for more than 2 hours every day. The average amount of time people wait at a stop or station for public transit is 23 min, while 50% of riders wait for over 20 minutes on average every day. The average distance people usually ride in a single trip with public transit is 8.7 km, while 19% travel for over 12 km in a single direction.[[55]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-56)

### Music

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Belo_Horizonte&action=edit&section=24 "Edit section: Music")]

[![Image 37](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a9/Sala_Minas_Gerais_02.jpg/250px-Sala_Minas_Gerais_02.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Sala_Minas_Gerais_02.jpg)

[Sala Minas Gerais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sala_Minas_Gerais "Sala Minas Gerais"), at the [Centro de Cultura Presidente Itamar Franco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro_de_Cultura_Presidente_Itamar_Franco "Centro de Cultura Presidente Itamar Franco"), is the house of the Minas Gerais Philharmonic Orchestra.

[Clube da Esquina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clube_da_Esquina "Clube da Esquina") is one of the most important musical movements in the musical history of Brazil. It originated in the mid-1960s, and since then its members have been hugely influential in Brazilian and even international music, some like Milton Nascimento and Toninho Horta achieving worldwide acclaim. Other people involved in the movement include musicians, songwriters, composers, conductors and lyricists, such as Tavinho Moura, [Wagner Tiso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wagner_Tiso "Wagner Tiso"), [Andersen Viana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andersen_Viana "Andersen Viana"), [Milton Nascimento](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milton_Nascimento "Milton Nascimento"), [Lô Borges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%B4_Borges "Lô Borges"), [Beto Guedes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beto_Guedes "Beto Guedes"), Flávio Venturini, [Toninho Horta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toninho_Horta "Toninho Horta"), [Márcio Borges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A1rcio_Borges "Márcio Borges") and [Fernando Brant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Brant "Fernando Brant"), among others.

The band [Uakti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uakti_(band) "Uakti (band)"), known for performing with self-built [musical instruments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musical_instrument "Musical instrument"), originated in Belo Horizonte under the influence of Walter Smetak and the Composition School from Bahia. Also, several nationally famous rock groups have been founded in Belo Horizonte, including [Jota Quest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jota_Quest "Jota Quest"), [Pato Fu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pato_Fu "Pato Fu"), [Skank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skank_(band) "Skank (band)"), [14 Bis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/14_Bis_(Band) "14 Bis (Band)") and Tianastácia. In later years, Belo Horizonte has been more frequently included in Brazilian tours of foreign mainstream and independent acts.

Belo Horizonte is also known as the Brazilian Capital of [Metal music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metal_music "Metal music"), hence the huge number of heavy metal bands (and the likes) founded there, especially in the 1980s. Most importantly, Overdose, the first metal band from BH and one of the first to gain prominence in Brazil; [Sepultura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sepultura "Sepultura"), the world's best known Brazilian metal band; and [Sarcófago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarc%C3%B3fago "Sarcófago"), one of the founders of modern [black metal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_metal "Black metal"). The [contemporary Christian music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contemporary_Christian_music "Contemporary Christian music") band [Diante do Trono](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diante_do_Trono "Diante do Trono"), is also of Belo Horizonte. A short instrumental song by American band [Earth, Wind & Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth,_Wind_&_Fire "Earth, Wind & Fire") is named after the city on their album [Now, Then & Forever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Now,_Then_&_Forever "Now, Then & Forever").

Other genres are also popular, such as [Sertanejo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sertanejo_music "Sertanejo music") music, and it also has its own [Funk carioca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funk_carioca "Funk carioca") scene, being extremely popular among youngsters.

### Museums

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Belo_Horizonte&action=edit&section=25 "Edit section: Museums")]

[![Image 38](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/db/Museu_de_Arte_da_Pampulha_-_BH.jpg/250px-Museu_de_Arte_da_Pampulha_-_BH.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Museu_de_Arte_da_Pampulha_-_BH.jpg)

Belo Horizonte features a number of museums including the Mineiro Museum, the Abílio Barreto Historic Museum, Arts and Workmanship Museum, a Natural History Museum and the UFMG [Botanic Gardens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botanic_Garden "Botanic Garden"), a telephone museum, the Pampulha Art Museum, the Professor Taylor Gramke Mineralogy Museum, and the UFMG Conservatory. The puppet theatre group Giramundo was established here in 1970, and continues to maintain a puppetry museum hosting a collection of their creations.

[![Image 39](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/22/Casa_Kubitschek_frente_01.jpg/250px-Casa_Kubitschek_frente_01.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Casa_Kubitschek_frente_01.jpg)

[Kubitschek Residence Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kubitschek_Residence_Museum "Kubitschek Residence Museum")

[![Image 40](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9c/Memorial_Minas_Gerais_Vale.jpg/250px-Memorial_Minas_Gerais_Vale.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Memorial_Minas_Gerais_Vale.jpg)

Minas Gerais Vale Memorial

The Circuito Cultural Praça da Liberdade, located in the central region of Belo Horizonte, is the largest cultural circuit in Brazil. In all, there are eleven functioning museums and cultural spaces: Arquivo Público Mineiro (Minas Gerais Public Archive), Biblioteca Pública Estadual Luiz de Bessa (Luiz de Bessa State Public Library), Cefar Liberdade, Centro de Arte Popular Cemig (Cemig Center of Popular Art), Centro Cultural Banco do Brasil (Culture Center Bank of Brazil), Espaço do Conhecimento UFMG (UFMG Knowledge Space), Horizonte Sebrae – Casa da Economia Criativa (Sebrae Horizon – The House of Creative Economy), Memorial Minas Gerais Vale (Minas Gerais Memorial), Museu das Minas e do Metal (Mines and Metal Museum), Museu Mineiro (Minas Gerais Museum) and [Palácio da Liberdade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pal%C3%A1cio_da_Liberdade "Palácio da Liberdade") (Liberty Palace). Besides these, another three spaces are already in the process of being implemented: the Casa Fiat de Cultura (Fiat Culture House), CENA and Oi Futuro. The proposal, according to the Circuit manager, Cristiana Kumaira, is to strengthen the circuit in the world cultural context. "We are already on this path and are being careful to ensure that the activities, services and assistance fulfill the needs and expectations of both the local population and the tourists who come to Belo Horizonte from around the world. The Circuit is establishing itself as one more source of pride for the people of Minas Gerais," she stresses.

[![Image 41](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/93/AvenidaAfonsoPena_e_PalacioDasArtes.jpg/250px-AvenidaAfonsoPena_e_PalacioDasArtes.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:AvenidaAfonsoPena_e_PalacioDasArtes.jpg)

Palace of Arts Museum

Inaugurated in 2010, the Circuito Cultural Praça da Liberdade was created with the goal of exploring [cultural diversity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_diversity "Cultural diversity"), with interactive options open to the public, in an area of great symbolic, historical and architectural value for Belo Horizonte. The opportunity came with the transference of the Minas Gerais Government headquarters to the Cidade Administrativa (Administrative City), in Serra Verde. After they had been adapted, the old department buildings opened their doors and began to house museums and [cultural spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_space "Cultural space"). The Circuito Cultural Praça da Liberdade is co-managed by the Instituto Sérgio Magnani (Institute) since June 2012, through a partnership signed with the Minas Gerais Government, and the museums/spaces are mostly run by private companies, which carry out investments in heritage recovery and building maintenance. According to Kumaira, this public-private partnership model allows large companies to participate and effectively contribute to the cultural advance of the city. "Beyond their fields of activities, the partners invest in the implantation and maintenance of museums, learning spaces, exhibition halls and shows, as well as memory centers that consolidate the history of Minas Gerais, presenting it either for free or at affordable prices," she adds. [[56]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-57)

The Natural History Museum and Botanical Garden holds a notable piece of [folk art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk_art "Folk art"), the Nativity of Pipiripau. Created during the 20th century, the craftsman Raimundo Machado, synchronized 586 figures, distributed in 45 scenes, which tell the story of life and death of Jesus, mixed with its variety of arts and [crafts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crafts "Crafts").[[57]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-58) With 600,000 sq. m. of green area, the [UFMG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidade_Federal_de_Minas_Gerais "Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais") Museum of Natural History and Botanic Garden (MHN-JB) is a ecological space that enables visitors to experience nature in a multidisciplinary way. For 30 years, the mission of the MHN-JB has been to do research, to educate, and to meet the community's demand for service. It covers the areas of [anthropology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropology "Anthropology"), [archeology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archeology "Archeology"), Environmental Education, [Natural History](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_History "Natural History"), [Mineralogy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mineralogy "Mineralogy"), and [Paleontology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paleontology "Paleontology"). It has an Ecological Amphitheater, a Free Art atelier, a [greenhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenhouse "Greenhouse"), and an Interactive Room. One of its traditional exhibitions is the Pipiripau Nativity Crèche.

Palácio das Artes, inaugurated in 1970, comprises three theaters, three [art galleries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_galleries "Art galleries"), a movie theater, a bookstore, a coffee shop and photography exhibition space. The building was designed by Oscar Niemeyer and it also houses the Minas Gerais Handcraft Center. The Pampulha Art Museum is located at the Pampulha Lake in Belo Horizonte in a building that originally housed the Pampulha Casino. The building was designed by Oscar Niemeyer, commissioned by the then-mayor and future president of Brazil Juscelino Kubitschek in the early 1940s, with external grounds by landscaper [Roberto Burle Marx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_Burle_Marx "Roberto Burle Marx"). The building was the first project of Oscar Niemeyer, opened as a casino, and closed in 1946. In 1957, it was re-opened as the Art Museum. His design was influenced by the principles of Le Corbusier. The gardens of [Burle Marx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burle_Marx "Burle Marx") are a tribute to the tropical green. There are three sculptures, by Ceschiatti, Zamoiski and José Pedrosa. In 1996, it won new multimedia rooms, library, café bar, a souvenir shop and technical infrastructure. The MAP has an impressive collection of 1,600 works.[[58]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-BH_GUIDE-59)

### Architecture

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Belo_Horizonte&action=edit&section=26 "Edit section: Architecture")]

[![Image 42](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e8/Oscar_Niemeyer%27s_Church_of_St_Francis_in_Belo_Horizonte2.jpg/250px-Oscar_Niemeyer%27s_Church_of_St_Francis_in_Belo_Horizonte2.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Oscar_Niemeyer's_Church_of_St_Francis_in_Belo_Horizonte2.jpg)

São Francisco de Assis Church in Pampulha Park

Belo Horizonte has several significant cultural landmarks, many of them situated in the [Pampulha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pampulha_(Belo_Horizonte) "Pampulha (Belo Horizonte)") district, where there are notable examples of Brazilian [contemporary architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contemporary_architecture "Contemporary architecture").[[59]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-60) Under the leadership of the then mayor of city, [Juscelino Kubitschek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juscelino_Kubitschek "Juscelino Kubitschek"), architects and artists such as [Oscar Niemeyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_Niemeyer "Oscar Niemeyer"), landscaper [Burle Marx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burle_Marx "Burle Marx"), and painter [Candido Portinari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candido_Portinari "Candido Portinari"), started a type of modern architecture and art here that was greatly developed later with the construction of Brazil's new capital, [Brasília](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bras%C3%ADlia "Brasília"), also led by the now president Juscelino (aka JK). It is at the [Pampulha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pampulha_(Belo_Horizonte) "Pampulha (Belo Horizonte)") complex that one can see the routes of this new architecture movement. The modern and protomodern architecture can also be seen all over Belo Horizonte, either in emblematic 1950s buildings such as Edificio Acaiaca, Conjunto JK, Hotel Amazonas[[60]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-61) and former Hotel Excelsior,[[61]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belo_Horizonte#cite_note-62) all reflecting the modernity culture of the first planned capital of Brazil.

The Pampulha Park area includes one of the largest [soccer stadiums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soccer_stadium "Soccer stadium") in the world, the [Mineirão](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mineir%C3%A3o "Mineirão") stadium, and the [São Francisco de Assis Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_Saint_Francis_of_Assisi,_Pampulha "Church of Saint Francis of Assisi, Pampulha"), widely known as Igreja da Pampulha, designed by Brazilian [Modernist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_movement "Modern movement") architect [Oscar Niemeyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_Niemeyer "Oscar Niemeyer"). In Pampulha there is also the [Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidade_Federal_de_Minas_Gerais "Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais") campus, whose buildings themselves are important contributions to the city's architecture. Other notable buildings include the Mesbla and Niemeyer buildings.
