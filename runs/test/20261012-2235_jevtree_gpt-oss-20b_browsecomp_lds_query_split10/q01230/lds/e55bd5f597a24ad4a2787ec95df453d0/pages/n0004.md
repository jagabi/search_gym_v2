### Description

The cultural and event space, located in Praça Sete, was inaugurated in 1932, in the heart of the capital. It houses the Terraço Brasil, Museographic and Gallery Houses, Chamber Theatre and the Grande Theatro Unimed-BH. It was the first building in the city influenced by the Art Deco style, designed by the architect Alberto Murgel.

A landmark in Belo Horizonte's provincial architecture, the building was inspired by French architecture, with well-defined geometric volumes, minimal ornamentation, stained glass windows made of iron and hammered glass, and facades clad in stone powder. The construction was also a pioneer in the use of reinforced concrete, imported from England. For several years, the Cine Theatro Brasil was the tallest building in Belo Horizonte, inspiring people to visit the rooftop terrace, from where one could see the entire Serra do Curral mountain range.

Currently, the center is a historical and cultural landmark of Belo Horizonte, and it showcases a program of theatrical plays, films, music, stand-up comedy, exhibitions, and various other art forms in all its exhibition spaces. In addition, the space can also be rented for private events, such as cocktail parties and corporate events, large-scale parties and receptions, and other events for small, medium, or large audiences.

It is important to check the attractions' websites and social media for the health protocols adopted as safety measures for the public.

*** The operating hours described refer to the administration. Theaters and cultural spaces operate according to their schedules and programming.**
