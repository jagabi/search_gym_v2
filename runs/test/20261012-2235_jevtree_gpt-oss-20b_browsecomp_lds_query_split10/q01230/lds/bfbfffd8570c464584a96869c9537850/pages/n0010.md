## What Around Us is about

#### Around Us is a free service for finding tourist places around you.

Around Us is a free online interactive travel guide that allows you to discover tourist spots near you or explore them through an immersive, simple, and fun world map. Around Us lists nearly 20 million points of interest worldwide. With this brand new app, you can find out the most popular places to visit in each region of the world or find specific and unique points of interest through our some 16,000 filters! Whether you are a fan of culture, sports, cuisine, or something else, Around Us is accessible for free and without registration. You can always create an account to participate or save your past or future destinations as favorites and eventually build your itineraries and book your entire experience online.
