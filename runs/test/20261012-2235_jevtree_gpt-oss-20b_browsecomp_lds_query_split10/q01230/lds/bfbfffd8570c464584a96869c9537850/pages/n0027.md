# **Pampulha art museum****,**Art museum in Pampulha district, Belo Horizonte, Brazil

Audio guide· About one minute Sign in to listen to the audio guide

The Pampulha Art Museum is a curved modernist structure with smooth marble surfaces and gleaming steel elements positioned alongside an artificial lake. The site includes a garden with outdoor sculptures, walking paths, a library, and a café.

The building was originally constructed as a casino between 1942 and 1943, then transformed into an art museum in 1957 after the national prohibition of gambling. This conversion established it as a significant venue for preserving and displaying Brazilian art.

The place takes its name from the artificial reservoir nearby, which forms the backdrop for the entire ensemble. Visitors experience how the art collection is displayed within a setting where nature and creative work are meant to coexist.

The museum sits in a quiet area by the water, where visitors should plan to spend time exploring the garden and pathways. You can easily spend several hours here enjoying both the art collection and the surroundings.

The building was one of the early major works by renowned architect Oscar Niemeyer and displays his distinctive preference for curved forms. The way the structure integrates with the surrounding landscape was a novel design approach for its time.
