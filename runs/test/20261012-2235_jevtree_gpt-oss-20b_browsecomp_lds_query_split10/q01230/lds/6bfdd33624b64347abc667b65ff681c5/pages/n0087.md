## Base Statistics

The [congestion data Excel spreadsheet](https://tti.tamu.edu/documents/umr/data/complete-data-2025-umr-by-tti.xlsx) (2.0MB) contains the base statistics for the [101 urban areas](https://mobility.tamu.edu/umr/data-and-trends/urban-areas-in-the-study/#urban-areas) from 1982 to 2024. The spreadsheet also includes individual 2017 to 2024 congestion statistics for [393 other urban areas](https://mobility.tamu.edu/umr/data-and-trends/urban-areas-in-the-study/#other-urban-areas). The new observed [destination access area statistics](https://tti.tamu.edu/documents/umr/data/complete-access-data-2025-umr-by-tti.xlsx) (1.5MB) for 2024 are also available in Excel format.
