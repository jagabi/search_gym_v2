# Cine Theatro Brasil Vallourec

Avenida Amazonas, 315, downtown, at the meeting point of Praça Sete and Belo Horizonte's Hypercenter.

In the heart of Belo Horizonte, this Art Deco landmark turns a walk through Praça Sete into an encounter with theater, music, film and urban memory.

Opened in 1932 and reopened as a cultural center in 2013, Cine Theatro Brasil Vallourec brings together Grande Theatro Unimed-BH, Teatro de Câmara, galleries on the upper floors and Terraço Brasil. Its protected architecture and changing program make the building an arts stop in the pulse of downtown.

🤔 Why does it matter?

A performance here reveals how an architectural landmark can remain alive: the experience brings together an active stage, preserved details from the 1930s and the everyday energy of the most central part of Minas Gerais's capital.

## 📍 What to see

*   •Grande Theatro Unimed-BH, with a proscenium stage, box seats and 1,000 seats
*   •Teatro de Câmara, a ground-floor auditorium for up to 200 people with restored armchairs
*   •Fifth- and sixth-floor galleries, including the glass walkway
*   •Terraço Brasil on the seventh floor, with 932 square meters (10,030 sq ft)

## 🎒 Things to do

*   •Choose a concert, play, film or special session from the current program
*   •Observe Angelo Biggi's geometric Art Deco paintings in Grande Theatro
*   •Visit exhibitions open to the public when the galleries are operating
*   •Extend the outing to Mercado Central or along Avenida Afonso Pena

## ✨ Recommended experiences

*   •Performance night: arrive early, take in the surroundings of Praça Sete, and round out the evening with dinner downtown or in Lourdes.
*   •Architecture itinerary: pair a guided tour, when available, with a reading of the building's Art Deco volumes and the landmarks along Afonso Pena.
*   •Compact cultural outing: attend a session at Teatro de Câmara and set aside time to see an exhibition in the galleries, if one is open that day.

## 📅 Best time to visit

The Cine can be visited year-round; the ideal date depends more on the desired program than on the tourism season.

✅ Best time: Dates with a performance, film, exhibition or visit of interest confirmed on the official calendar.

🟡 Shoulder season: Weekdays with cultural programming and lower demand for highly sought-after sessions.

🔴 Less recommended: There is no fixed period; avoid making the trip without checking the calendar and whether the event will take place.

📝 Notes: Holidays, the times of each presentation and any guided visits require advance confirmation.

*   •At its opening, the auditorium could hold about 2,000 people.
*   •The building's design follows the curve of its corner lot.
*   •The original reinforced-concrete structure remains legible in the upper galleries.

## 🤔 Did you know?

*   •For a time, it was one of Belo Horizonte's tallest buildings and charged admission for the public to go up to the terrace.
*   •Two hundred armchairs from the first project were restored and installed in Grande Theatro's upper seating area.
*   •The interior preserves decorative panels inspired by Marajoara art, signed by Angelo Biggi.

## 📜 History and context

Designed by architect Angelo Alberto Murgel, with structural calculations by Emilio Baumgart, the eight-story building opened on July 14, 1932. Its state heritage listing was approved in 1999, and it underwent restoration from 2006 to 2013, when it again welcomed the public with new cultural spaces.

## 🌍 Global importance

Cine Theatro Brasil expresses, on a Brazilian scale, an Art Deco vocabulary that circulated internationally from the 1920s through the 1940s. Its continued presence in the heart of Minas Gerais's capital shows how conserving a cultural asset can support contemporary uses without erasing its identity.
