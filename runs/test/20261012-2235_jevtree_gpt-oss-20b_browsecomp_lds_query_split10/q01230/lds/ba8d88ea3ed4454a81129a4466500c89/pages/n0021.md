Wiki Loves Monuments (WLM) is a public photo competition around cultural heritage monuments, organized by Wikimedia chapters and groups. In 2010 [it was organised](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Wiki_Loves_Monuments_2010 "Commons:Wiki Loves Monuments 2010") in the Netherlands, and was followed by a Europe-wide [2011 edition](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Wiki_Loves_Monuments_2011 "Commons:Wiki Loves Monuments 2011"). Ever since [2012](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Wiki_Loves_Monuments_2012 "Commons:Wiki Loves Monuments 2012") the competition is organized annually in countries across the world! This is the biggest photography competition in the world and we hope to be able to count on massive participation again this year.

The aim of the contest is to ask the general public—readers and users of Wikipedia, photographers, hobbyists, etc.—to take pictures of cultural heritage monuments and upload them to Wikimedia Commons for use on Wikipedia and other Wikimedia projects.

Read more about how you can participate in the photo contest on [the website of the contest](http://www.wikilovesmonuments.org/).

If you want to express your interest to be kept up to date next year, please leave your contact details at [Commons:Wiki Loves Monuments 2018/Participating countries](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Wiki_Loves_Monuments_2018/Participating_countries "Commons:Wiki Loves Monuments 2018/Participating countries") and [subscribe to the wlm-announce mailing list](https://lists.wikimedia.org/mailman/listinfo/wlm-announce).

### Is my country participating in 2018?

Maybe! Check [the updated participants list](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Wiki_Loves_Monuments_2018/Participating_countries "Commons:Wiki Loves Monuments 2018/Participating countries") to find out!

### Why is my country not participating in 2018?

Wiki Loves Monuments is organized in a federative manner, which means **the organization depends on volunteers in each country.** Enthusiastic local teams get in contact with heritage organizations and authorities, create monuments lists in Wikipedia, contact partners and sponsors, and motivate the general public to participate.
