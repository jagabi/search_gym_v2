Where the city appears

Nine decades ago, a building appeared in the center of the capital of Minas Gerais, bringing with it the marks of a pioneering spirit that can still be seen today. Cine Theatro Brasil, now known as Cine Theatro Brasil Vallourec, was inaugurated on July 14, 1932 as one of the tallest buildings in the city (people paid a ticket to go up to the terrace to see Serra do Curral), the largest cinema in the Brazil and the first construction in Belo Horizonte in the Art Deco style (inspired for buildings such as the Post Office and City Hall).   
  
 To mark the 90th anniversary of Cine Theatro Brasil Vallourec, the space prepared a whole day of free activities. The attractions start at 8 am, with the Banda da Guarda Municipal, at the main entrance of Cine Brasil, in Praça Sete, and continue until 9 pm, with a performing arts performance at the Grand Theater. To remember the heyday of what was once the largest exhibition hall in the country, cinema sessions at the Teatro de Câmara with great classics: The Boy (Charles Chaplin), Scarface (Howard Hawks), M, The Vampire of Dusseldorf (Fritz Lang) and Devil’s Feast (Alfred Hitchcock). There will also be guided tours with the Educativo team for those who want to know a little more behind the scenes of the cultural center that moves the heart of the city.   
  
 “_We invite the public to occupy the Cine and its surroundings, enjoying attractions that not only rescue part of our history but also reinforce the quality of our programming and the concern of this space in democratizing access to culture. Cine is a meeting point where the city presents itself, and this is where we want to bring people together_“, invites Sandra Campos, manager of Cine Brasil.

**Closing**  
 To close the program for the 90th anniversary of Cine Brasil, on 07/29 the public will have more an opportunity to attend the Música de Cinema concert, with the Symphony Orchestra of Minas Gerais (OSMG). Conducted by Sérgio Gomes, the repertoire includes soundtracks from the Harry Potter and STAR WARS sagas, in addition to the feature films Indiana Jones, Superman, Jurassic Park and Schindler’s List. The concerts also count with the simultaneous display of images from some movies. The presentation marks the partnership of the  
 Cine with Fundação Clóvis Salgado.
