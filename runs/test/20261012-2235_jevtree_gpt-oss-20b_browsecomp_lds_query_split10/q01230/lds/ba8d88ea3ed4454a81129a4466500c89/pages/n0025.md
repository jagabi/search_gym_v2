# Structures and Large-Scale Projects

*   [Summary](https://structurae.net/en/structures/)
*   [Added](https://structurae.net/en/structures/new)
*   [Modified](https://structurae.net/en/structures/modified)
*   [Illustrated](https://structurae.net/en/structures/illustrated)

The engineering structures included in Structurae are first and foremost those which are necessary to establish lines of communication or transport, such as bridges, tunnels, dams, etc. But Structurae is also interested in those structures in which the civil or structural engineering aspect is very significant be it through the structure itself or for the way it was built.

Such rides can be fixed or mobile, though only the former are included in Structurae.](https://structurae.net/en/structures/amusement-rides)
