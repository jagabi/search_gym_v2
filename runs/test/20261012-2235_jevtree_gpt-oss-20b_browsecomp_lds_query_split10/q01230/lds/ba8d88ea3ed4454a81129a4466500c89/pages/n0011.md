[Expansion of Museo de Bellas Artes de Bilbao Complete](https://world-architects.com/en/architecture-news/headlines/foster-partners-bilbao-museum-expansion)

 Five years after winning an international competition, Foster + Partners and architect Luis María Uriarte have completed the expansion of the Museo de Bellas Artes de Bilbao (Bilbao Fine Arts Museum). The new structure, named Agravitas, sits above the existing buildings, increases the museum’s floor area by 60 percent, and restores the original 1945 entrance.
