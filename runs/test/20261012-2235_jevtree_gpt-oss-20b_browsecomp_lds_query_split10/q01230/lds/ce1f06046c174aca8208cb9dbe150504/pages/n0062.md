SORTIE ROADMAP

# Features _&_ Roadmap

Sortie is being built as a planning-first travel app. Our goal isn't to become a toolbox containing every travel utility imaginable. We're focused on making the whole planning journey work beautifully.

Discover**→**Save**→**Build**→**Organise**→**Book**→**Travel

This page separates what Sortie already supports from the V2 improvements and genuinely new capabilities we're building next.

Roadmap dates are target windows rather than guaranteed release dates. Priorities may move as we learn from users, uncover technical issues, or find better ways to solve a problem.

Last updated: October 2026

MAJOR NEXT PRIORITY · PLANNED

## Sortie, in your pocket.

A dedicated phone app focused primarily on the on-trip experience—not simply responsive web work. The initial product will centre on viewing, navigating, bookings, documents, on-trip actions, and offline resilience.

Planning often happens on a larger screen. Travelling does not. The responsive web application will remain the richer planning surface initially, while the native app focuses on the journey itself.

## How we prioritise

The roadmap follows the product loop: protect the foundation, make the trip useful in motion, then connect the pieces with care.

**1. Plan**Make the core itinerary workflow clear, useful, and controllable.

**2. Go mobile**Build the phone experience travellers need while actually travelling.

**3. Improve**Deepen destinations, places, and itinerary quality as the product grows.

**4. Connect**Discover, Collections, Trips and Bookings.

**5. Assist**Planning intelligence without removing user control.

**6. Travel deeper**Offline, documents, live adjustments and native travel features.

**7. Expand**Collaboration, commercial features and public content.

## The long-term product

Sortie already has most of its major nouns:

Destinations · Places · Collections · Trips · Segments · Days · Stops · Bookings

The next phase is about building better verbs between them:

Discover something  
→ save it  
→ add it to a trip  
→ put it on the right day  
→ attach the booking  
→ use it while travelling  
→ carry the whole thing in your pocket
