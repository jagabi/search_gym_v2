## Explore North America

North America combines dense city neighborhoods with longer travel legs, so itinerary structure matters before you start filling days.

Why North America works in Sortie

North America works best as a sequence of city personalities: dense historic cores, food-led neighborhoods, and landscape escapes that need honest transfer time. Current live entry points include New York, Chicago, Montreal, San Francisco.

Choose one city base before adding a national-park or coastal leg; the distances are part of the route design.Use neighborhood clustering for urban days and reserve a full day for the excursion that changes the trip's scale.Build around weather and seasonal events, especially when a city is paired with a mountain, lake, or Atlantic coast stop.
