Why Europe works in Sortie

Europe works best when you think in connected city pairs, not isolated weekends. Rail links, short hops, and dense culture make it ideal for layered itineraries. Plan this region by choosing one anchor city first, then layering nearby countries or secondary cities only when the pacing stays realistic. Strong entry points include Barcelona, London, Paris, Rome.

Start with country clusters that naturally pair together: Italy, France, Spain are the easiest places to build coherent multi-city routes.Start from a surfaced itinerary, then adjust day pace and neighborhood focus instead of building from a blank list.For multi-city trips, keep transfers deliberate so the trip stays city-first instead of transit-heavy.
