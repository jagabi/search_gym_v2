Detroit FAQ
## Things travellers usually want to know before Detroit.

How many days do you need in Detroit?
Three full days cover the central anchors, Corktown, and one excursion. Add a fourth if Ann Arbor is central to the trip.

Where should I stay in Detroit?
Downtown is the easiest first base; Corktown suits travellers who want more local food and evening texture.

What should I book ahead?
Reserve timed entry for Motown Museum when required, then keep one flexible day for weather and The Henry Ford.

Is Detroit easy to combine with another destination?
Yes, but protect at least two full days here before adding a longer transfer. Belle Isle is the more natural regional extension.

What is the biggest planning mistake?
Treating Detroit as a list of disconnected sights. Group Motown Museum, Detroit Institute of Arts, and Midtown into a geographic day.

What kind of traveller will enjoy Detroit?
It suits travellers who want music history, industrial scale, and a compelling creative comeback, with enough structure to keep the route clear and enough space to notice local life.
