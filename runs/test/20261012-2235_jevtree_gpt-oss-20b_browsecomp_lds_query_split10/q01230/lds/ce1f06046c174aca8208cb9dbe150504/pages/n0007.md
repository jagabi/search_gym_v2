Why Sortie exists
## Generic trip planners break down  
exactly when the trip gets _interesting_.

WHAT USUALLY HAPPENS

You open a spreadsheet or a notes app. It works fine for a weekend. Add a second city and a hotel handoff and you're already managing three separate documents.

WHY IT GETS MESSY

Travel context disappears. Hotel check-ins float loose. Moving one stop means manually fixing five other things. The plan becomes fragile exactly when you need it to be solid.

WHAT SORTIE CHANGES

Segments, hotels, travel rows, and day structure stay grouped correctly. Move things around without losing the plot. The more complex the trip, the more useful it gets.

How it works

FAQ
## A few things people ask.

What is Sortie?
Sortie is a structured travel planning app built for real trips, especially the ones that become messy fast. It helps you organise multi-city routes, save places, manage hotel handoffs, and turn scattered travel research into a coherent itinerary.

Is Sortie free to use?
Yes. Free includes unlimited manually planned trips, each up to 3 locations and 14 days. You can save places, organise travel days, and attach supported booking details.

What does Sortie Pro unlock?
Sortie Pro is built for more complex travel planning. It unlocks:

*   more than 3 locations per trip
*   longer itineraries
*   Plan a Trip, Fill a Day, and Optimise a Day
*   PDF export

It is designed for travellers managing bigger routes, longer journeys, and more moving parts.

Can I still plan trips manually?
Absolutely. You stay in control of the trip structure, dates, stops, pacing, and saved places. Automated planning tools are available on Pro, while Free lets you build the itinerary yourself.

Is Sortie just another AI itinerary generator?
No. Sortie is designed around structured travel planning rather than disposable AI-generated schedules. The focus is on helping travellers build coherent trips that evolve over time, especially across multiple cities or longer journeys.

Does Sortie work for multi-city trips?
Yes. Multi-city planning is one of the core reasons Sortie exists. The app is designed to handle route structure, hotel changes, travel days, saved planning context, and itinerary organisation across larger trips.

Can I add a public itinerary to my trip?
Yes. Trip Stitching lets you add a public Sortie itinerary to one of your trips and continue editing the result as a native itinerary. Free limits still apply per trip.

Can I plan a trip with someone else?
Yes. Share a view-only link, or choose Plan together to invite one collaborator to edit and build the same itinerary with you.

Can I use Sortie before booking everything?
That’s usually the best time to use it. Many travellers start by saving places, comparing routes, exploring destination ideas, and gradually shaping the trip before hotels and transport are finalised.

Does Sortie work offline?
Yes. You can save an offline snapshot of a trip so your itinerary stays accessible on travel days, international trips, unreliable connections, and while moving between cities.

Can I browse public itineraries and destination guides?
Yes. Sortie includes destination pages and public itineraries designed to help travellers discover routes, pacing ideas, neighbourhoods, and planning inspiration before building their own trip.

Who is Sortie best for?
Sortie is best for travellers planning trips that are becoming too complicated for notes apps, browser tabs, spreadsheets, or generic itinerary generators. It is especially useful for longer trips, multi-city journeys, and travellers who want more structure without losing flexibility.
