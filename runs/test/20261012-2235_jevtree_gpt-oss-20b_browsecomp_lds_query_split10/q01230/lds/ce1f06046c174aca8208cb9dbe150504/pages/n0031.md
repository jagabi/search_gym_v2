WHY RECIFE
## Recife makes atlantic beaches part of the itinerary.

Recife is best understood through Atlantic beaches, frevo, and a Pernambuco capital full of layered history. Let Recife Antigo orient the trip, add time in Boa Viagem, and reserve a deliberate day for Olinda rather than spreading the route thin.

Recife FAQ
## Things travellers usually want to know before Recife.

How many days do you need in Recife?
Three full days cover the central anchors, Boa Viagem, and one excursion. Add a fourth if Olinda is central to the trip.

Where should I stay in Recife?
Recife Antigo is the easiest first base; Boa Viagem suits travellers who want more local food and evening texture.

What should I book ahead?
Reserve timed entry for Marco Zero when required, then keep one flexible day for weather and Porto de Galinhas.

Is Recife easy to combine with another destination?
Yes, but protect at least two full days here before adding a longer transfer. Itamaracá is the more natural regional extension.

What is the biggest planning mistake?
Treating Recife as disconnected sights. Group Marco Zero, Instituto Ricardo Brennand, and Casa Forte into a geographic day.

What kind of traveller will enjoy Recife?
It suits travellers who want Atlantic beaches, frevo, and a Pernambuco capital full of layered history, with enough structure to keep the route clear and enough space to notice local life.
