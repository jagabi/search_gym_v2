Ilhéus FAQ
## Things travellers usually want to know before Ilhéus.

How many days do you need in Ilhéus?
Three full days cover the central anchors, Praia dos Milionários, and one excursion. Add a fourth if Itacaré is central to the trip.

Where should I stay in Ilhéus?
Historic Centre is the easiest first base; Praia dos Milionários suits travellers who want more local food and evening texture.

What should I book ahead?
Reserve timed entry for Jorge Amado House when required, then keep one flexible day for weather and Cocoa farms.

Is Ilhéus easy to combine with another destination?
Yes, but protect at least two full days here before adding a longer transfer. Serra do Conduru is the more natural regional extension.

What is the biggest planning mistake?
Treating Ilhéus as a list of disconnected sights. Group Jorge Amado House, São Sebastião Cathedral, and Pontal into a geographic day.

What kind of traveller will enjoy Ilhéus?
It suits travellers who want cocoa country, literary history, and Bahia's humid Atlantic coast, with enough structure to keep the route clear and enough space to notice local life.
