FAQ

Can I use Sortie for free?
Yes. Free includes unlimited trips, up to 3 locations and 14 days per trip, manual planning, flights, bookings, travel days, and transfers.

How many trips can I create?
As many as you like. The Free limits apply per trip: up to 3 locations and 14 days.

Can I plan multi-city trips?
Yes. Free supports real multi-city trips with up to 3 locations per trip. Pro adds larger and longer trips plus Plan a Trip, Fill a Day, and Optimise a Day.

Are public itineraries included?
Yes. Public itineraries and destination guides are available to browse on Free. Pro adds larger trips, longer itineraries, automated planning, and PDF export.

Can I stitch a public itinerary into a Free trip?
Yes. Trip Stitching is available on Free while the resulting trip stays within 3 locations and 14 days. Pro removes those trip-size limits.

Can I plan a trip with someone else?
Yes. Shared Itineraries let you invite one collaborator to edit and build the same trip with you. Share this trip remains available for view-only links.

Can I cancel Pro?
Yes. You can cancel Pro at any time. Your trips stay in Sortie, and plan changes apply at the end of the billing period.

Is export or offline included?
PDF export is part of Pro. Every plan can save a read-only trip copy on the device for offline access.
