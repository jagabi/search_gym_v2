Cine Theatro Brasil: historic Art Deco building in Belo Horizonte

Titles are generated by AI from Meta

Cine Theatro Brasil, currently called Cine Theatro Brasil Vallourec, is a historic building in Belo Horizonte, Brazil built in 1932 at Praça Sete de Setembro to function as the main cinema and largest theater in the city. It is a large 11-story building whose façade at a curvilinear angle marks the corner of Avenida Amazonas and Rua Carijós. Designed by the architect Alberto Murgel, it is one of the precursors of reinforced concrete and has a stone powder façade, typical finish of the art deco architectural style.
