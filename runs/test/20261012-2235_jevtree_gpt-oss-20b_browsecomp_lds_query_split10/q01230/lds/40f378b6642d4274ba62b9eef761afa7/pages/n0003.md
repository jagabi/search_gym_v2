Cine Theatro Brazil Vallourec is a modern cultural center. Located in the heart of the city, Praça Sete, Cine Theatro Brazil Vallourec combines tradition and avant-garde and has spaces to host different types of events. It hosts concerts, plays, dances, exhibitions, congresses and social events on a weekly basis.
