## In short What to expect

Step into a musical journey from Sinatra’s effortless swing to Armstrong’s unforgettable voice and trumpet. Their music comes alive once again in a heartfelt live tribute.

The “Entrada Social” ticket is a ticket category that anyone can buy, with no proof of eligibility required. It includes a R$10 donation to Instituto Hatus, a non-profit organization, already included in the ticket price.

## FAQs Got questions? We’ve got answers

Where can I find my ticket?

You can find your ticket in the 'Tickets' section of the Fever app (you can download it from the Apple App Store or Google Play). Please remember to log in with the same login method used to make the purchase. You will also find your QR code in your purchase confirmation email.

Can I change my ticket?

Subject to availability, you can change the date and/or time up to 48 hours in advance. To make the change, please contact us and include the ticket ID associated with your purchase. No refunds are permitted under any circumstances. For more information, you can consult our T&Cs.

How long does the experience last?

The experience lasts approximately 60 minutes.

What are the different ticket types?

Zone A and Zone B. Each zone offers Inteira (full price), Meia-entrada (half price, for those entitled by law) and Entrada Social tickets. Seating is assigned on a first come, first served basis within each zone.

Can I buy tickets at the venue?

Tickets are not sold on site; they can only be purchased in the Fever App or on the Fever website.

Is there an age requirement for the event?

This experience is for guests aged 18 and over. Valid ID may be requested.

Can I sit next to my friends?

Yes, if you arrive together and have tickets in the same area, you will be seated together. We recommend arriving early so we can do our best to accommodate you.

Can I book a ticket for my caretaker?

Guests requiring assistance throughout this experience are entitled to apply for one free pass for their personal assistant/support worker.

What can I expect from the event?

A soulful live music experience in an intimate, atmospheric venue, where world-class musicians bring jazz to life through saxophones, trumpets, vocals, and spontaneous improvisation.
