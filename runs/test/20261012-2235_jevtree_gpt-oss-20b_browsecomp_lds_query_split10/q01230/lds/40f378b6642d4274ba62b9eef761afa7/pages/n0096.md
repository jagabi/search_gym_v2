## Takeaways

Whether you're stitching together clips from a weekend trip, syncing a transition to a trending sound or showing off a recipe in 30 seconds flat, Instagram Reels is the place to do it. Reels let you create short, entertaining videos with music, effects and creative tools built right into the app.

This guide walks you through everything: what Reels are, how long they can be, how to record and edit your first one, how to share it with the right people and how to collaborate with others using Remix and Collab.

## What are Instagram Reels?

Instagram Reels are short-form videos that can include music, audio, effects, text and more. They live on your profile in a dedicated Reels tab and can also appear in the main feed, the Explore page and the Reels tab at the bottom of the app.

Think of reels as your creative canvas for video. Unlike Stories, which disappear after 24 hours, reels stay on your profile permanently (unless you choose to delete them). They're designed to be discovered, which means people who don't follow you yet can find your reels through Explore and recommendations.

Whether you're posting a 10-second clip with a trending song or a 90-second mini-vlog, reels gives you a dedicated space to create and be discovered. Explore more reels features [here](https://about.instagram.com/features/reels).

## How long can Instagram Reels be?

Reels can be anywhere from 3 seconds to 3 minutes long. Here's a quick breakdown:

• **3 to 15 seconds:** Great for quick transitions, trends and punchy one-liners.

• **15 to 60 seconds:** The sweet spot for tutorials, day-in-my-life clips and most creative formats.

• **60 seconds to 3 minutes:** Best for longer storytelling, walkthroughs or detailed how-tos.

When you open the Reels camera, you'll see duration options at the bottom of the screen. Tap to choose your max length before you start recording.

![Image 4](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=1625405689193228&version=1790608210&transcode_extension=webp)

**Tip:** If you're just getting started, try a 15-second reel. Shorter videos are easier to edit and tend to hold attention all the way through. You can always go longer once you're comfortable with the tools.

## Creating and posting your first reel

Making a reel takes just a few steps. Here's how to create one from scratch:

1. Open Instagram and tap the **+** icon at the bottom of the screen, then select **Reel.**

2. Choose your clip length by tapping the duration option on the left side.

3. **Record** by holding the capture button, or tap the gallery icon in the bottom left to upload clips from your camera roll.

4. Add multiple clips by recording again or uploading more. They'll stitch together automatically.

5. When you're done capturing, tap the **arrow** to move to the editing screen.

6. Add music, text, effects and stickers (more on each of these below).

7. Tap **Next** to reach the share screen.

8. Write a caption, add a cover image, choose your audience and tap **Share.**

![Image 5](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=933932796456944&version=1790608210&transcode_extension=webp)

**Tip:** You can also create a reel entirely from photos. Upload multiple photos from your camera roll and they'll play as a slideshow. Add music and you've got a photo reel in seconds.

If you want to speed up the process, try using [Reels templates](https://about.instagram.com/blog/tips-and-tricks/instagram-reels-tips-tricks). Templates let you drop your own clips into a pre-made structure with timing and transitions already set.

## Editing your reels like a pro

The editing screen is where your reel really comes together. You'll find tools for audio, text, effects and pacing all in one place. If you want even more creative control, you can also use the [Edits app](https://creators.instagram.com/edits/) to build your reel with a more detailed timeline, layered audio and frame-by-frame precision before posting.

### Adding music and audio

Music is often what makes a reel feel complete. A great track can set the mood, drive the pacing and make your video more memorable. 

To add a track:

1. Tap the **music note** icon at the top of the editing screen.

2. Search for a song by name, browse trending tracks or explore curated playlists.

3. Select your track, then drag the audio waveform to choose which part of the song plays.

4. Adjust the volume slider to balance your original audio with the music.

You can also record a voiceover by tapping the **microphone icon** after filming. This is great for narrating tutorials, adding commentary or telling a story over your clips.

For more ideas, check out how to [add music to your reels](https://about.instagram.com/blog/tips-and-tricks/how-to-add-music-to-instagram-reels).

### Adding text overlays

Text overlays let you put words directly on screen at the exact moment you want them to appear. They're great for adding context, calling out a key detail or creating a hook that stops someone mid-scroll. 

To add text:

1. Tap the **Aa** text icon on the editing screen.

2. Type your message, then choose a font style, color and alignment.

3. Pinch and drag to resize and position your text anywhere on screen.

4. Use the timeline at the bottom to set when text appears and disappears.

You can add multiple text layers to a single reel, each timed to show up at different moments. Try stacking a question in the first frame and an answer a few seconds later to build curiosity.

![Image 6](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=1554504929233318&version=1790608210&transcode_extension=webp)

Tip: A strong opening line of text in the first frame can be just as powerful as a great thumbnail. Think of it as your reel's headline.

### Adding captions and subtitles

Captions are different from text overlays. Instead of placing custom words on screen manually, captions automatically transcribe whatever is being said in your reel and display the words in sync with the audio. They make your reel accessible to people who are watching without sound or who are deaf or hard of hearing.

To add captions:

1. After recording or uploading your clips, tap the **Stickers** icon on the editing screen.

2. Select the **Captions** sticker.

3. Instagram will automatically generate subtitles from the audio in your reel.

4. Wait for the transcription to process, then review the text for accuracy.

5. Tap any word to edit it if the auto-generated text needs fixing.

6. Choose a caption style (font, color and background) to match the look of your reel.

7. Pinch and drag to reposition the captions on screen

![Image 7](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=2090649684887304&version=1790608210&transcode_extension=webp)

**Tip:** Even if you've already added text overlays, captions are worth including. Text overlays highlight key moments you choose, while captions make your entire spoken audio visible. The two features work well together and serve different purposes.

### AR effects and filters

Effects can transform the look and feel of your reel with one tap. To add them:

1. Tap the **sparkle icon** (effects) before you record or in the editing screen.

2. Browse by category: trending, appearance, world, funny and more.

3. Tap an effect to preview it, then tap the **bookmark icon** to save it for later.

You can also layer effects on specific clips rather than the entire reel. Just select the clip in your timeline, then apply the effect.

![Image 8](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=2541995872940813&version=1790608210&transcode_extension=webp)

### Speed, timer and align tools.

These tools give you more control over your recording:

• **Speed:** Change the recording speed (0.5x for slow-mo, 2x or 3x for fast-forward) before you hit record. Great for transitions and time-lapses.

• **Timer:** Set a countdown (3 or 10 seconds) so you can put your phone down and get in frame. You can also set a recording duration so it stops automatically.

• **Align:** Shows a transparent overlay of your last clip's final frame. Use this to line up transitions seamlessly between takes.

**Tip:** The align tool is what creators use for those smooth outfit-change or location-switch transitions. Film your first clip, tap align, then position yourself in the same spot before recording the next one.

## Sharing your reels to the feed and Explore page

Once you've edited your reel, tap Next to reach the share screen. Here you'll set up how and where your reel appears:

• **Caption:** Write something that adds context, asks a question or includes relevant hashtags.

• **Cover image:** Choose a frame from the reel or upload a custom cover. This is what shows on your profile grid.

• **Tag people and locations:** Helps your reel reach more viewers and show up in location-based searches.

• **Share to Feed:** Toggle this on to have your reel appear in your main grid alongside your photos and carousels.

![Image 9](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=28179640145027994&version=1790608210&transcode_extension=webp)

Reels that perform well can appear on the **Explore page**, where millions of people discover new accounts and videos every day. This is one of the biggest differences between Reels and Stories. Reels are built for reach beyond your existing followers. To give your reel the best chance of being recommended, keep it high-quality, original and engaging from the first second.

Want to understand what goes into recommendations? You can [learn how the reels algorithm works](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/reels-algorithm-control). You can also [schedule your content](https://help.instagram.com/439971288310029/) in advance if you want to plan your posting time without being on your phone.

## Collaborating with Remix and Collab

Reels aren't just a solo thing. Two features make it easy to create with others: **Remix** lets you record your reel alongside someone else's. Your video appears side-by-side or in a green-screen layout with the original. It's great for reactions, duets, challenges and adding your take to a trending reel.

To Remix a reel:

1. Find a reel you want to respond to.

2. Tap the **three dots** (**•••**) menu.

3. Select **Remix this reel.**

4. Choose your layout (side-by-side, green screen, or split screen) and record your response.

Learn more about the Remix feature and how to get started.

Collab lets you co-author a reel with another account. The reel appears on both profiles and shares likes, comments and views. It's perfect for joint projects, friend features or collaborative challenges. To invite a collaborator, tag them on the share screen and select.

## Frequently asked questions

#### How do I make a reel with pictures and music on Instagram?

Open Reels, tap the gallery icon and select multiple photos from your camera roll. They'll arrange as a slideshow. Then tap the music icon to add a track. Adjust the timing of each photo in the timeline and share.

#### How do I make a reel from my camera roll?

Tap the **+** icon, select reel, then tap the gallery icon in the bottom left. Choose one or more videos or photos from your library. You can trim, reorder and add music or effects just like you would with freshly recorded clips.

#### How do I create my own reel?

Start by tapping the + button and selecting reel. Record clips, upload from your camera roll or use a template. Edit with music, text and effects, then tap Share. The whole process takes just a few minutes once you get the hang of it.

#### Can I edit a reel after posting?

You can edit the caption, cover image, tags and location after posting. To change the video itself, you'll need to delete the reel and re-upload an edited version.

#### How do I add trending audio to my reel?

When you see a reel with a song you like, tap the audio name at the bottom. From there you can save the audio for later or tap **Use audio** to start a new reel with that track already loaded.

#### What is the Edits app and how does it work with Reels?

Edits is Instagram's standalone video editing app, built for people who want more precision when creating reels. It gives you a detailed timeline, the ability to layer multiple audio tracks, keyframe animations and frame-by-frame control. When you're done editing, you can export directly to Instagram and post as a reel. If you've outgrown the in-app editor or want to spend more time crafting your video before sharing, Edits is a great next step. Download it from the App Store or Google Play and sign in with your Instagram account to get started.

The best way to get comfortable is to just start. Open the Reels camera, try a template, add a song you love and see where it takes you.
