# Candlelight: Tribute to Coldplay

4.8(1086) 

⭐ _**Candlelight** concerts bring the magic of the live music experience to the most sublime venues in Belo Horizonte. Get your tickets and rediscover the songs of Coldplay at Cine Theatro Brasil Vallourec illuminated by a sea of candles!_

_This is a tribute concert. It has no affiliation with, endorsement by or sponsorship from the artist, their estate or their official representatives._

**General Info**  
📅 Date and time: select your dates/times directly from the ticket selector  
⏳ Duration: 60 minutes  
**🚫 No entry will be permitted after the scheduled start time of the concert. Arrive early and secure the best spot in the zone of your choice**  
📍 Location: Cine Theatro Brasil  
👤 Age requirement: from 8 years old. Children under 16 must be accompanied by an adult  
♿ Accessibility: the venue is wheelchair accessible  
❓ You can consult the FAQ for this event [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vQjyGadYd2JWwROztk0YQOzAtdv8klCtwGKS_Qd0RibruAPzdu-QMqxWL4L3M5LPA/pub)  
🪑 Seats at the event are only allocated on a first-come, first-served basis and on the type of ticket purchased  
🎫 Half-price tickets will be available to students, seniors (60+), people with disabilities (PWD) and companions, young people under 21, low-income young people and teachers in the state public network and municipal teaching networks  
👉 See all the details about half-price tickets [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vS7l8WxtYgWpgfN7-q1HlA4Q9euMApwxS87S49TtuF0c9eqqbFW9KwBXNGrcxnlJIVbaRAZTdpvn7EV/pub)  
🕯️ If you want to book a private concert or buy tickets for a large group (+30 people), click [here](https://candlelightexperience.com/groups/pt-br/)
