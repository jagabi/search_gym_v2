Minnesota Legislators Past & Present is the only comprehensive list of legislators who have served in the Minnesota Legislature since territorial times. Find biographical information on members plus details on each session in which they served. Direct links to bills authored are available in recent years. For all years, committee membership and leadership positions are included.

While there is information on each legislator, this is a work in progress. [Do you have legislator information to add?](https://www.leg.mn.gov/leg/comments.aspx)

Is the search you need missing? Library staff members can create custom searches of the database to answer specialized requests. [Contact a librarian](https://www.leg.mn.gov/leg/comments.aspx).
