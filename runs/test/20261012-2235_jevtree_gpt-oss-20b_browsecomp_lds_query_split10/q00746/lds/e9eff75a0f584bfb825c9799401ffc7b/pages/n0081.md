# Research

# Research

### We find ways to make Wikimedia better.

![Image 5: Wikimedia Conference 2018](https://wikimediafoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/Wikimedia_Conference_2018_%E2%80%93_290-e1533254857491.jpg?w=1024)

Our goal is to make Wikimedia sites more useful for people seeking knowledge across the globe. Research helps us live up to our mission. We turn research questions into opportunities to empower new readers and contributors. We use research-backed insights to inform new technologies, products, and programs. We partner with researchers around the world to better understand knowledge making and sharing.

## Building a positive community culture

![Image 6](https://wikimediafoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Wikimedia_Conference_2017_%E2%80%93_130-e1565971700509.jpg?w=600&h=338&crop=1)
Harassment is a pervasive issue on the internet—even on Wikipedia. We’re working to[understand and combat harassment](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Community_health_initiative)in Wikimedia projects and discussion spaces.

## Improving sources and citations

![Image 7](https://wikimediafoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/The_Leeds_Library_interior_2-1-e1565971708296.jpg?w=600&h=338&crop=1)
Verifiable citations are critical to open knowledge projects like Wikipedia. We are[building an open repository of sources](https://research.wikimedia.org/knowledge-integrity.html)to make it easier to manage citations.

## Understanding reader behavior

![Image 8](https://wikimediafoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Wikimedia_Conference_2018_%E2%80%93_291-e1565971674397.jpg?w=600&h=338&crop=1)
Every second 6,000 people visit Wikipedia. We want to know why. Our research aims to[understand their motivations and needs](https://research.wikimedia.org/knowledge-gaps.html)so we can identify opportunities to improve.

## Increasing editor diversity

![Image 9](https://wikimediafoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Engrossed_in_editing_during_Women_in_art_editathon_IMG_5858-e1565971717433.jpg?w=600&h=338&crop=1)
Wikimedia is a global movement but participation has been easier for some users than others. We are partnering with communities to understand why. With everyone’s help we can[increase editor diversity](https://research.wikimedia.org/foundational.html)across our projects.

## Helping new editors

![Image 10](https://wikimediafoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Content_Translation_demo-e1565971734716.jpg?w=600&h=338&crop=1)
Learning how to contribute to Wikimedia projects can be complex. We worked with new editors in South Korea and the Czech Republic to[understand why they edit](https://wikimediafoundation.org/2017/10/30/new-editor-experiences-midsized-wikipedias/)and how we could make it easier.

## Protecting the open internet

![Image 11](https://wikimediafoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/SarpSozdinler_WeMissTurkey_A3_Poster.png?w=600&h=338&crop=1)
The law should allow internet platforms to stay out of editorial decisions so that people can share and speak freely.[We research how policies affect the way information is shared online](https://wikimediafoundation.org/2017/07/12/intermediary-liability-protections-research-initiative/).
