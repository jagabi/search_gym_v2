# Search Courthouse News

Tips for getting started:

*   Put quotes around groups of words or phrases. For example: "Broward County" or "Jim Smith"
*   AND is the default operator. For example: president Biden will look for "president" AND "Biden"
*   Use OR to search for one term or another. For example: Florida OR elections
*   Searches are not case sensitive
