### Iowa State University leads efforts to prevent contaminant exposure for firefighters

Iowa State University researchers and partners are working to learn about how modern fires impact firefighters’ health due to toxic contaminants on their gear and to find practical solutions to keep them safe. 

    *   [Read the Iowa Capital Dispatch story](https://iowacapitaldispatch.com/2026/09/21/iowa-state-university-leads-efforts-to-prevent-contaminant-exposure-for-firefighters/)

*   
### Iowa State recognized for support of military-affiliated students

Brady Talley, director of the Military-Affiliated Student Center, and Michael Casson, an Iowa State student and Iowa National Guard member, talk about the support and services offered through Iowa State's Military-Affiliated Student Center to help students succeed. 

    *   [See the WOI-TV interview](https://www.weareiowa.com/video/entertainment/places/around-town/iowa-state-university-named-a-top-ranked-military-school-whats-behind-that-success/524-06bd2b6d-8703-4103-9dbd-ae0e8588c666)

*   
### Iowa State University lab examines muscle and bone deterioration in space and on Earth

Researchers at Iowa State University have earned national recognition for their work connecting the impact of space on astronauts to potential treatments for muscle loss here on Earth. 

    *   [Read the Iowa Capital Dispatch story](https://iowacapitaldispatch.com/2026/07/17/iowa-state-university-lab-examines-muscle-and-bone-deterioration-in-space-and-on-earth/)

*   
### Iowa State researchers study menopause’s impact on brain health

Researchers at Iowa State University have launched a study to explore how menopause impacts brain and vascular aging, aiming to address women’s long-term health risks. 

    *   [See the KCCI-TV story](https://www.kcci.com/article/iowa-state-brava-study-menopause-alzheimers-dementia-vascular-aging/71693955)

*   
### ISU study points to AI’s place in the classroom

A recent Iowa State University study found that students using AI as part of the writing process were often forced to use more thought and had to focus more on brainstorming and refining their own ideas and the end product. 

    *   [See the WHO-TV story](https://who13.com/news/ai-writing-process-students/)

*   
### Iowa State scientists unravel the history of cotton domestication

Cotton, the world's most profitable nonfood crop, is used more than any other natural fiber. Known for its comfort and durability, it has been utilized since antiquity in fabrics and ​other goods. Four species are grown commercially, but one is dominant, accounting for about 90% of global production. 

    *   [Read the Reuters story](https://www.reuters.com/science/scientists-unravel-history-cotton-domestication-2026-05-18/)
