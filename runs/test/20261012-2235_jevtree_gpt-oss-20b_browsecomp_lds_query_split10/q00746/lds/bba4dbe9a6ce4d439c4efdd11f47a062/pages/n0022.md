Trade Updates

Aug 11, 2025

President Trump issues an executive order extending the pause on the April 2 tariffs on Chinese goods until November 10.

4,000+everyday products using corn as an ingredient  
Corn is a renewable feedstock found in thousands of everyday products, from packaging and clothing to adhesives, biobased manufacturing materials, personal care products, and innovative biobased chemicals.  
  
 Source: Iowa Corn

6.6 Billion Bushels  
Corn-based biomanufacturing has significant potential to replace fossil-derived chemicals and materials. Capturing a 10% share of the global plastics market could result in 6.6 bushels of corn demand.

18 Farm Bills  
The Farm Bill was first passed in 1933 as part of the New Deal. Since that time, Congress has passed 18 farm bills, greatly expanding the legislation’s reach.

12 Titles  
The most recent farm bill includes 12 titles ranging from commodities, conservation, trade, nutrition, rural development, research, energy to crop insurance.
