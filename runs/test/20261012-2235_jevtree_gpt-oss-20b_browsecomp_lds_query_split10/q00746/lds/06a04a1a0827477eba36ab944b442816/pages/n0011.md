 If you are sure you [understand](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Understand "Understand") everything that is going on, you are hopelessly [confused](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Confused "Confused").

**[Walter Frederick "Fritz" Mondale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter%20Frederick%20%22Fritz%22%20Mondale "w:Walter Frederick \"Fritz\" Mondale")** ([5 January](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/5_January "5 January")[1928](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/1928 "1928") – [19 April](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/19_April "19 April")[2021](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/2021 "2021")) was an American politician, diplomat, and lawyer who served as the 42nd [vice president of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice%20President%20of%20the%20United%20States "w:Vice President of the United States") from 1977 to 1981 under President [Jimmy Carter](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Jimmy_Carter "Jimmy Carter"). A U.S. senator from Minnesota from 1964 to 1976, he was the [Democratic Party](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Democratic_Party "Democratic Party")'s nominee in the 1984 presidential election, but lost to incumbent [Ronald Reagan](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Ronald_Reagan "Ronald Reagan") in an Electoral College and popular vote landslide. His vice presidential nominee, U.S. Representative [Geraldine Ferraro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geraldine%20Ferraro "w:Geraldine Ferraro") from New York, was the first female vice-presidential nominee of any major party in U.S. history.

## Quotes

[[edit](https://en.wikiquote.org/w/index.php?title=Walter_Mondale&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Quotes")]

*   If you are sure you understand everything that is going on, you are hopelessly confused. 
    *   As quoted by [Ann Landers](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Ann_Landers "Ann Landers"), in _The Poughkeepsie Journal_ (26 March 1978); cited as "Mondale's Law" in _The Book of Laws_ (1979) by Harold Faber, p. 13

*   He taught us how to live, and finally he taught us how to die. 
    *   Walter Mondale paying his final respects to [Hubert Humphrey](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Hubert_Humphrey "Hubert Humphrey") at his funeral.[[1]](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Walter_Mondale#cite_note-1)
