The **ambassador of the United States of America to Japan** ([Japanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_language "Japanese language"): 駐日本国アメリカ合衆国大使, [Hepburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hepburn_romanization "Hepburn romanization"): _Chū Nihonkoku Amerika Gasshūkoku Taishi_) is the [ambassador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambassadors_of_the_United_States "Ambassadors of the United States") from the [United States of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_of_America "United States of America") to [Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan "Japan").

## History

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=List_of_ambassadors_of_the_United_States_to_Japan&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")]

Beginning in 1854 with the [use of gunboat diplomacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convention_of_Kanagawa "Convention of Kanagawa") by [Commodore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commodore_(United_States) "Commodore (United States)")[Matthew C. Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_C._Perry "Matthew C. Perry"), the U.S. has maintained [diplomatic relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplomatic_relations "Diplomatic relations") with Japan, except for the ten-year period between the [attack on Pearl Harbor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attack_on_Pearl_Harbor "Attack on Pearl Harbor") in 1941 (and the subsequent [declaration of war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Declaration_of_war_by_the_United_States "Declaration of war by the United States") on Japan by the United States) and the signing of the [Treaty of San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_San_Francisco "Treaty of San Francisco"), which normalized relations between the United States and Japan. The United States maintains an [embassy in Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassy_of_the_United_States_in_Tokyo "Embassy of the United States in Tokyo"), with [consulates-general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consul_(representative) "Consul (representative)") in [Osaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osaka "Osaka"), [Nagoya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagoya "Nagoya"), [Sapporo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapporo "Sapporo"), [Fukuoka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fukuoka "Fukuoka"), and [Naha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naha "Naha").

Due to the significance of the [relations between the two countries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan%E2%80%93United_States_relations "Japan–United States relations") in recent years on trade and defense, with Japan being described by the United States State Department as "the cornerstone of the U.S. security interests in Asia," [[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Ambassador_to_Japan#cite_note-USStateDept-1) the post has been held by many significant American politicians, including [Mike Mansfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Mansfield "Mike Mansfield"), [Walter Mondale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Mondale "Walter Mondale"), [Tom Foley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Foley "Tom Foley"), [Howard Baker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Baker "Howard Baker"), and [Rahm Emanuel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rahm_Emanuel "Rahm Emanuel").
