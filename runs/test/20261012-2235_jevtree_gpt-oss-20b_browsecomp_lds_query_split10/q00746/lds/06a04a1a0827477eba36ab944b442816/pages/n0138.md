From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

**[Hubert Humphrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hubert_Humphrey "Hubert Humphrey")** (1911–1978) was the 38th vice president of the United States.

**Hubert Humphrey** may also refer to:

*   [Skip Humphrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skip_Humphrey "Skip Humphrey") (Hubert H. Humphrey III, born 1942), Minnesotan politician, son of Hubert Humphrey
*   [Hubert Humphrey (businessman)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hubert_Humphrey_(businessman) "Hubert Humphrey (businessman)") (born 1942), American businessman, financial executive, and insurance broker
