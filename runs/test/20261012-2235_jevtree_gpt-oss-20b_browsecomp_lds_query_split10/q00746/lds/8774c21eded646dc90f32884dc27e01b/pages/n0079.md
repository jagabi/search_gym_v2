## Corn Harvested Acres by State: Top 11

The information below documents the number of corn acres harvested in each state from 2019 to 2000. This information is an important factor in determining [annual corn production in each state](https://www.cropprophet.com/us-corn-production-by-state/). The total US corn harvested acres (and [corn planted acres](https://www.cropprophet.com/corn-planted-acres-by-state-the-top-11/)) by year are shown below. This type of information is used by CropProphet, a corn, soybean, and winter wheat yield and production forecasting system. Our [weather-based model](https://www.cropprophet.com/corn-yield-forecast-model/) updates daily and is designed for agriculture futures market participants.[Risk-free trials are available](https://info.prescientweather.com/try-cropprophet-yield-forecast-system).
