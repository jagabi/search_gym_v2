# Faces of Farming

Have you ever wondered who exactly is responsible for growing and raising the food we enjoy every day? It’s most likely a family. More than 97% of farms in Iowa are family-owned and operated. While technology and efficiencies impact agriculture like every industry, family continues to be the core of the farm. Explore the families and the deep commitment they have to growing corn and caring for their farms and families.
