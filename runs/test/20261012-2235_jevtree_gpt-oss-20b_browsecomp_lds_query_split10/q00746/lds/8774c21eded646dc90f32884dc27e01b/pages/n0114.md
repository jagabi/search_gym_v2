# Iowa Corn Quarterly Update

The Iowa Corn Quarterly Update shares farmer insights on key projects and priorities of the Iowa Corn Promotion Board (ICPB) and Iowa Corn Growers Association (ICGA), and what they mean for long-term corn grower profitability.

Please accept cookies to access this content

### Iowa Corn 350 Powered by Ethanol: Fueling the Cars on the Track

_Featuring Iowa Corn Promotion Board Chair Joe Roberts and Iowa Speedway President Eric Peterson_  

The Iowa Corn 350 Powered by Ethanol highlights the connection between Iowa corn farmers and ethanol, from the corn grown on Iowa farms to the fuel powering the race. The conversation explores how ethanol creates demand for Iowa-grown corn, adds value to the crop and why NASCAR chooses to power their vehicles with ethanol.

Please accept cookies to access this content

### Iowa Corn Day at the Iowa State Fair: Celebrating Iowa Corn and Its Impact

_Featuring Iowa Corn Promotion Board President Paul Gieselman and Washington County farmer Kerri Bell_  

They discuss Iowa Corn Day at the Iowa State Fair and how it showcases the many ways Iowa corn is grown for more. From connecting with fairgoers and celebrating Iowa farmers to highlighting corn-fed meat, ethanol and the many products made from corn, Iowa Corn brings the story of Iowa’s corn farmers to the fair.

Please accept cookies to access this content

### Annual Grassroots Summit: Bringing Iowa Farmers’ Voices to the Table

_Featuring Iowa Corn Growers Association President Steve Kuiper and Linn County farmer Jordan Balderston_  

The two discuss how the Annual Grassroots Summit brings Iowa corn farmers from across the state together to discuss policies and help shape Iowa Corn’s policy book. Farmers are able to share their perspectives and experiences, while also recognizing that priorities and challenges can look different across the state while working together to make their voices heard.

Please accept cookies to access this content

### National Waterways Conference: Keeping Iowa Corn Moving

_Featuring Iowa Corn Promotion Board District 9 Director Dave Vandenberg and Iowa Corn Government Relations Manager Ian Latham_  

They highlight why investing in our nation’s waterways matters to Iowa farmers. Reliable rivers are essential for moving Iowa-grown corn to markets around the world, supporting agriculture, trade and the rural communities that depend on it.
