# Iowa Corn Growers Association Board

The Iowa Corn Growers Association (ICGA) is comprised of 12 peer-elected farmers that serve as the collective voice for 7,000 corn farmer members, lobbying on agricultural issues at the state and federal level. Elections are held each year to select leadership and representatives from each of the nine crop reporting districts in the state.
