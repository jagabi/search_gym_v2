# Corn Planted Acres by State: The Top 11

Corn[Jan Dutton](https://www.cropprophet.com/about-us/Jan-bio/)04/11/2020

## Corn Planted Acres by Year

The USDA NASS makes a wealth of data available, including corn planted acres information.In fact, much of the historical data used to create the CropProphet corn, soybean, and winter wheat yield and production forecasts come from the [USDA NASS](https://www.cropprophet.com/usda-nass-monthly-update/).

As discussed in other posts about [corn yield forecasting](https://www.cropprophet.com/corn-yield-forecast-model/) and [US corn production](https://www.cropprophet.com/us-corn-production-by-state/), corn production is a function of both planted acres and yield.In this post, we provide a history of the rankings of corn acres planted by state from 2000 to 2022. The USDA reports there was 88.4 million corn acres in 2022, a decrease from the 93.2 million corn planted acres the year before.The corn planted acres graphic below shows the decrease in planted acres. Iowa, with 12.9 million acres, is the top state for corn acres planted in 2022 or 14.6% of the national total corn planted acres.

[![Image 3: US Corn Planted and Harvested Acres since 1960 (up to 2023)](https://www.cropprophet.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/National_Corn_PlantedAcresx.png)](https://www.cropprophet.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/National_Corn_PlantedAcresx.png)US corn planted and harvested acres since 1960

The information below provides the ranking of corn-planted acres by state for each year from 2000 to 2021.
