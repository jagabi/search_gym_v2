# Highway History

![Image 5: painting of old cars](https://highways.dot.gov/sites/fhwa.dot.gov/files/inline-images/history_cover_0.jpg)

## [Interstate System](https://highways.dot.gov/highway-history/interstate-system)

From its launch under President Eisenhower in 1956, the Greatest Public Works Project in History has transformed the Nation.

## [Federal-Aid Legislation](https://highways.dot.gov/highway-history/federal-aid-legislation)

The laws that shaped the Federal-aid highway program were affected by the times in which they were created.

## [History of FHWA](https://highways.dot.gov/highway-history/history-fhwa)

Tracing its start to the Bicycle Craze of the 1890s, FHWA has helped make the U.S. the most mobile country in history

## [General](https://highways.dot.gov/highway-history/general-highway-history)

Read about travel in the 19th century, the named trails and U.S. highways, "The Road to Civil Rights," the origins of NEPA, and more.
