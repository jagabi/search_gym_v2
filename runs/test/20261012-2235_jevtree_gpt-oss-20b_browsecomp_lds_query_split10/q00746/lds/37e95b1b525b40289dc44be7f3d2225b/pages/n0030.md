About This Course

## About This Course

This convenient online course covers all of the presidents and vice presidents from American history. Inside the course, you'll find expertly-taught lessons that examine important facts and accomplishments related to America's presidents and vice presidents from the 18th century to today.

The course comes with interactive lesson quizzes, chapter exams and a final assessment to help you retain your knowledge. Printable lesson transcripts are available, and you can easily access these lessons at any time that fits your schedule. The course material is also accessible on any device that has an Internet connection, which helps you study at school, at home, at work or wherever you happen to be.

### Course Uses

Students, teachers, parents and independent learners can all benefit from this course.

*   Students in a history or social studies class can use this course to supplement their textbook, study for exams or catch up on missed class information. 
*   Teachers who are looking for a fun way to enhance their American president and vice president lessons can easily incorporate the course's features into their classrooms. 
*   Independent learners who want a simple way to improve their knowledge of U.S. presidents and vice presidents can access the course whenever and wherever it's convenient. 
*   Parents can use this course as a fun tutoring solution for their students.
