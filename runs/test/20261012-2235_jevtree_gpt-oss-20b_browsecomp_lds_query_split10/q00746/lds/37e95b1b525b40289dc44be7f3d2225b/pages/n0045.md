American politician

**Elijah Hamlin** (March 29, 1800 – July 16, 1872) was an American lawyer, businessman, politician and historian. He served in both houses of the Maine state legislature and as mayor of Bangor.

Elijah Livermore Hamlin was born in [Livermore, Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livermore,_Maine "Livermore, Maine") on July 29, 1800, and was the older brother of future [Vice President of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_President_of_the_United_States "Vice President of the United States")[Hannibal Hamlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin "Hannibal Hamlin"). He graduated from [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University "Brown University"), studied law, and was admitted to the bar.

After practicing in [Waterford, Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterford,_Maine "Waterford, Maine"),[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elijah_Hamlin#cite_note-:0-1) Hamlin moved to [Bangor, Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangor,_Maine "Bangor, Maine"). He served in the [Maine House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maine_House_of_Representatives "Maine House of Representatives") in 1830–1832 and 1847–1848, and in the [Maine State Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maine_State_Senate "Maine State Senate") in 1837 and 1858–1859.

He ran unsuccessfully for [Governor of Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Maine "Governor of Maine")[in 1848](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1848_Maine_gubernatorial_election "1848 Maine gubernatorial election") and [1849](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1849_Maine_gubernatorial_election "1849 Maine gubernatorial election") as a [Whig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whig_Party_(United_States) "Whig Party (United States)"). From 1851 to 1853 he served as mayor of Bangor.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elijah_Hamlin#cite_note-2)[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elijah_Hamlin#cite_note-3)

Hamlin died in Bangor on July 16, 1872, and was buried at Mount Hope Cemetery in Bangor.
