**Hannibal Hamlin** (August 27, 1809 – July 4, 1891) was an American politician and diplomat who was the 15th [vice president of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_president_of_the_United_States "Vice president of the United States"), serving from 1861 to 1865, during President [Abraham Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Lincoln "Abraham Lincoln")'s first term. He was the first [Republican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republican_Party_(United_States) "Republican Party (United States)") vice president.

As an attorney from [Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maine "Maine"), Hamlin began his political career as a [Democrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_(United_States) "Democratic Party (United States)") in the Maine House of Representatives before being elected twice to the [United States House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_House_of_Representatives "United States House of Representatives"), and then to the [United States Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Senate "United States Senate"). With his strong [abolitionist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abolitionist "Abolitionist") views, he left the Democratic Party for the newly formed Republican Party in 1856. In the [1860 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1860_United_States_presidential_election "1860 United States presidential election"), Hamlin balanced the successful Republican ticket as a New Englander partnered with the Midwesterner Lincoln. Although not a close friend of the president, he lent loyal support to his key projects such as the [Emancipation Proclamation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emancipation_Proclamation "Emancipation Proclamation") during the [American Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War "American Civil War").

In the [1864 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1864_United_States_presidential_election "1864 United States presidential election"), Hamlin was replaced as vice-presidential nominee by [Andrew Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Johnson "Andrew Johnson"), a [Southern Democrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Democrats "Southern Democrats") from [Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennessee "Tennessee") who remained loyal to the union, and chosen for his appeal to [Southern Unionists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Unionists "Southern Unionists"). After being appointed Collector of the Port of Boston, Hamlin was elected to two more terms in the Senate, and finally served as [U.S. Minister to Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ambassadors_of_the_United_States_to_Spain "List of ambassadors of the United States to Spain") before retiring in 1882.

## Early life

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Hannibal_Hamlin&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Early life")]

[![Image 8](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/69/Younger_Hannibal_Hamlin.jpg/250px-Younger_Hannibal_Hamlin.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Younger_Hannibal_Hamlin.jpg)

Hamlin c.late 1840s

Hamlin was born to Cyrus Hamlin and Anna ([née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birth_name#Maiden_and_married_names "Birth name")Livermore) in [Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris,_Maine "Paris, Maine") (now in [Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maine "Maine"), then a part of [Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts "Massachusetts")). He was a sixth-generation descendant of English colonist James Hamlin, who had settled in [Barnstable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnstable,_Massachusetts "Barnstable, Massachusetts"), part of the [Plymouth Colony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plymouth_Colony "Plymouth Colony") in 1639.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-1) He was a grandnephew of U.S. Senator [Samuel Livermore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Livermore "Samuel Livermore") II[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-lifeandtimes-2) of New Hampshire.

According to folklore, Hamlin's life was saved when he was an infant by a [Native American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Americans_in_the_United_States "Native Americans in the United States")[medicine woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medicine_man "Medicine man") named [Molly Ockett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molly_Ockett "Molly Ockett"). Hamlin was gravely ill, and Ockett prescribed that he be given warm cow's milk, after which he recovered.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-:0-3)

Hamlin attended the district schools and [Hebron Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebron_Academy "Hebron Academy") and later managed his father's farm. From 1827 to 1830 he published the _Oxford Jeffersonian_ newspaper in partnership with [Horatio King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horatio_King "Horatio King").[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-4)

He studied law with the firm headed by [Samuel Fessenden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Fessenden "Samuel Fessenden"),[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-5) was admitted to the bar in 1833, and began practicing in [Hampden, Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hampden,_Maine "Hampden, Maine"), where he lived until 1848.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-6)

## Personal life

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Hannibal_Hamlin&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Personal life")]

Hamlin married Sarah Jane Emery of Paris Hill in 1833. Her father was Stephen Emery, who was appointed as [Maine's Attorney General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maine_Attorney_General "Maine Attorney General") from 1839 to 1840.[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-7) Hamlin and Sarah had four children: George, [Charles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Hamlin_(general) "Charles Hamlin (general)"), [Cyrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyrus_Hamlin_(general) "Cyrus Hamlin (general)"), and Sarah.[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-8)

His wife died in 1855. The next year, Hamlin married Sarah's half-sister, [Ellen Vesta Emery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellen_Vesta_Emery "Ellen Vesta Emery"). They had two children together: [Hannibal E.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_E._Hamlin "Hannibal E. Hamlin") and Frank. Ellen Hamlin died in 1925.[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-9)

## Political beginnings

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Hannibal_Hamlin&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Political beginnings")]

Hamlin's political career began in 1835 when he was elected to the [Maine House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maine_House_of_Representatives "Maine House of Representatives"). Appointed a [Major](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_(United_States) "Major (United States)") on the staff of Governor [John Fairfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Fairfield "John Fairfield"), he served with the militia in the bloodless [Aroostook War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aroostook_War "Aroostook War") of 1839. He facilitated negotiations between Fairfield and Lieutenant Governor [John Harvey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Harvey_(British_Army_officer) "John Harvey (British Army officer)") of [New Brunswick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Brunswick "New Brunswick"), which helped reduce tensions and make possible the [Webster–Ashburton Treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Webster%E2%80%93Ashburton_Treaty "Webster–Ashburton Treaty"), which ended the war.[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-10)

Hamlin unsuccessfully ran for the [United States House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_House_of_Representatives "United States House of Representatives") in 1840 and left the State House in 1841. He was later elected to two terms in the [United States House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_House_of_Representatives "United States House of Representatives"), serving from 1843 to 1847. He was elected by the state legislature to fill a [U.S. Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Senate "U.S. Senate") vacancy in 1848 and to a full term in 1851. A [Democrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_(United_States) "Democratic Party (United States)") at the beginning of his career, Hamlin supported the presidential candidacy of [Franklin Pierce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_Pierce "Franklin Pierce") in 1852.[_[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation\_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")_]

From the very beginning of his service in Congress, Hamlin was a prominent opponent of the extension of slavery. He was a conspicuous supporter of the [Wilmot Proviso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilmot_Proviso "Wilmot Proviso") and spoke against the [Compromise of 1850](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compromise_of_1850 "Compromise of 1850"). In 1854, Hamlin strongly opposed the passage of the [Kansas–Nebraska Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas%E2%80%93Nebraska_Act "Kansas–Nebraska Act"), which repealed the [Missouri Compromise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missouri_Compromise "Missouri Compromise"). After the [Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_(United_States) "Democratic Party (United States)") endorsed that repeal at the [1856 Democratic National Convention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1856_Democratic_National_Convention "1856 Democratic National Convention"), on June 12, 1856, he withdrew from the Democratic Party and joined the newly organized [Republican Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_United_States_Republican_Party "History of the United States Republican Party"), causing a national sensation.[_[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation\_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")_]

The Republicans nominated Hamlin for [governor of Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Maine "Governor of Maine") the same year. He won the election by a large margin and was inaugurated on January 8, 1857. In the latter part of February 1857, however, he resigned the governorship.[_[why?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Please\_clarify "Wikipedia:Please clarify")_] He returned to the [United States Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Senate "United States Senate"), serving from 1857 to January 1861.[_[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation\_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")_]

## Vice presidency (1861–1865)

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Hannibal_Hamlin&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Vice presidency (1861–1865)")]

[![Image 9](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/ea/Hamlin_button_1860.jpg/250px-Hamlin_button_1860.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Hamlin_button_1860.jpg)

1860 election campaign button for [Abraham Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Lincoln "Abraham Lincoln") and Hannibal Hamlin. The other side of the button has Lincoln's portrait.

Hamlin was nominated by the Republican Party for [Vice President of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_President_of_the_United_States "Vice President of the United States") in the [1860 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1860_United_States_presidential_election "1860 United States presidential election") on a ticket with former Representative [Abraham Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Lincoln "Abraham Lincoln"), the presidential nominee.[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-11) Given that Lincoln was a former Whig from Illinois, a former Democrat from Maine as the vice presidential nominee helped to balance the ticket. Hamlin could also persuade other anti-slavery Democrats that joining the Republican Party was the only way to ensure slavery's demise.[[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-12)

Hamlin and Lincoln were not close personally, but had a good working relationship. At the time, the vice president was part of the legislative branch in his role as president of the Senate and did not attend cabinet meetings; Hamlin did not regularly visit the White House. [Mary Todd Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Todd_Lincoln "Mary Todd Lincoln") and Hamlin disliked each other. For his part, Hamlin complained, "I am only a fifth wheel of a coach and can do little for my friends."[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-13)

He had little influence in the Lincoln administration, although he urged both the [Emancipation Proclamation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emancipation_Proclamation "Emancipation Proclamation")[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-14) and the arming of [Black Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Americans "Black Americans").[[15]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-15) He strongly supported the appointment of [Joseph Hooker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Hooker "Joseph Hooker") as commander of the [Army of the Potomac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_of_the_Potomac "Army of the Potomac"),[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-16) which failed at the [Battle of Chancellorsville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Chancellorsville "Battle of Chancellorsville").[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-17)

Beginning in 1860, Hamlin was a member of Company A of the [Maine State Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maine_State_Guard "Maine State Guard"), a [militia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Militia_(United_States) "Militia (United States)") unit.[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-18) When the company was called up in the summer of 1864, militia leaders informed Hamlin that because of his position as vice president, he did not have to take part in the muster. He opted to serve, arguing that he could set an example by doing the duty expected of any citizen, and the only concession made because of his office was that he was quartered with the officers. He reported to [Fort McClary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_McClary "Fort McClary"), in [Kittery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kittery,_Maine "Kittery, Maine"), in July, initially taking part in routine assignments including guard duty, and later taking over as company cook. He was promoted to [corporal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporal#United_States "Corporal") during his service and mustered out with the rest of his unit in mid-September.[[19]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-19)[[20]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-20)[[21]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-21)

In 1862, Hamlin presided over the [impeachment trial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_impeachment_trial_in_the_United_States "Federal impeachment trial in the United States") in the Senate of Judge [West Hughes Humphreys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Hughes_Humphreys "West Hughes Humphreys").[[22]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-22)

In June 1864, the Republicans and [War Democrats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_Democrat "War Democrat") joined to form the [National Union Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Union_Party_(United_States) "National Union Party (United States)"). Although Lincoln was renominated, War Democrat [Andrew Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Johnson "Andrew Johnson") of Tennessee was named to replace Hamlin as Lincoln's running mate. Lincoln was seeking to broaden his base of support and was also looking ahead to Southern [Reconstruction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reconstruction_era_of_the_United_States "Reconstruction era of the United States"), at which Johnson had proven himself adept as military governor of occupied Tennessee. Hamlin, by contrast, was an ally of the Northern "[Radical Republicans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radical_Republicans "Radical Republicans")" (who later [impeached Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impeachment_of_Andrew_Johnson "Impeachment of Andrew Johnson")). Lincoln and Johnson were elected in November 1864, and Hamlin's term expired on March 4, 1865. Hamlin swore Johnson in as vice president, and was a witness to Johnson's [incoherent inaugural speech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Johnson's_drunk_vice-presidential_inaugural_address "Andrew Johnson's drunk vice-presidential inaugural address").

Although Hamlin narrowly missed becoming president, his vice presidency ushered in a half-century of sustained national influence for the [Maine Republican Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maine_Republican_Party "Maine Republican Party"). In the period 1861–1911, Maine Republicans occupied the offices of vice president, [Secretary of the Treasury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Secretary_of_the_Treasury "United States Secretary of the Treasury") (twice), [Secretary of State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Secretary_of_State "United States Secretary of State"), [President pro tempore of the United States Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_pro_tempore_of_the_United_States_Senate "President pro tempore of the United States Senate"), and [Speaker of the United States House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_of_the_United_States_House_of_Representatives "Speaker of the United States House of Representatives") (twice), and fielded a presidential nominee in [James G. Blaine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_G._Blaine "James G. Blaine"), a level of influence in national politics unmatched by subsequent Maine political delegations.

## Post-vice presidency (1865–1891)

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Hannibal_Hamlin&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Post-vice presidency (1865–1891)")]

After leaving the vice presidency, Hamlin served briefly as [Collector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_Custom_House "Boston Custom House") of the [Port of Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Boston "Port of Boston"). Appointed to the post by Johnson, he resigned in protest over Johnson's [Reconstruction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reconstruction_Era "Reconstruction Era") policy and accompanying efforts to build a political following loyal to him after he had been repudiated by the Republicans. Republicans had supported Johnson as part of the National Union ticket during the war, but opposed him after he became president and his position on Reconstruction deviated from theirs.[[23]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-23)

Not content with private life, Hamlin returned to the U.S. Senate in 1869 to serve two more terms before declining to run for reelection in 1880 because of an ailing heart. His last duty as a public servant came in 1881 when [Secretary of State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Secretary_of_State "United States Secretary of State")[James G. Blaine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_G._Blaine "James G. Blaine") convinced President [James A. Garfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_A._Garfield "James A. Garfield") to name Hamlin as [United States Ambassador to Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Ambassador_to_Spain "United States Ambassador to Spain"). Hamlin received the appointment on June 30, 1881, and held the post until October 17, 1882.

Upon returning from Spain, Hamlin retired from public life to his home in [Bangor, Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangor,_Maine "Bangor, Maine"), which he had purchased in 1851. The [Hannibal Hamlin House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin_House "Hannibal Hamlin House")—as it is known today—is in central Bangor at 15 5th Street. Incorporating [Victorian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victorian_architecture "Victorian architecture"), [Italianate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italianate_architecture "Italianate architecture"), and [Mansard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mansard_roof "Mansard roof")-style architecture, the mansion was posted to the [National Register of Historic Places](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Register_of_Historic_Places "National Register of Historic Places") in 1979.[[24]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-24)

On October 4, 1865, Hamlin was elected as a Third Class Companion in the Pennsylvania Commandery of the [Military Order of the Loyal Legion of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_Order_of_the_Loyal_Legion_of_the_United_States "Military Order of the Loyal Legion of the United States") (MOLLUS). Third Class was the MOLLUS division created to recognize civilians who had contributed outstanding service to the Union during the war. He was assigned MOLLUS insignia number 47.

## Death

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Hannibal_Hamlin&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Death")]

[![Image 10](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c3/Hannibal_Hamlin_Gravesite.jpg/250px-Hannibal_Hamlin_Gravesite.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Hannibal_Hamlin_Gravesite.jpg)

Hamlin's grave

On July 4, 1891, Hamlin collapsed and fell unconscious while playing cards at the Tarratine Club he founded in downtown Bangor, and died there a few hours later, at the age of 81.[[25]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-25) He was buried in the Hamlin family plot at [Mount Hope Cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Hope_Cemetery_(Bangor,_Maine) "Mount Hope Cemetery (Bangor, Maine)") in Bangor. He outlived six of his successors in the vice presidency ([Andrew Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Johnson "Andrew Johnson"), [Schuyler Colfax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schuyler_Colfax "Schuyler Colfax"), [Henry Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Wilson "Henry Wilson"), [William A. Wheeler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_A._Wheeler "William A. Wheeler"), [Chester A. Arthur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chester_A._Arthur "Chester A. Arthur"), and [Thomas A. Hendricks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_A._Hendricks "Thomas A. Hendricks")), more than any other U.S. vice president. Following [John Adams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Adams "John Adams") and [Thomas Jefferson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Jefferson "Thomas Jefferson"), he was also the third American Vice President to die on Independence Day.

He is buried in the Mount Hope Cemetery in [Bangor, Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangor,_Maine "Bangor, Maine").

## Family

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Hannibal_Hamlin&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Family")]

Hamlin had four sons and one daughter who grew to adulthood: [Charles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Hamlin_(general) "Charles Hamlin (general)"), [Cyrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyrus_Hamlin_(general) "Cyrus Hamlin (general)"), [Hannibal Emery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_E._Hamlin "Hannibal E. Hamlin"), Frank, and Sarah. Charles and Cyrus served in the Union forces during the [Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War "American Civil War"), both becoming generals, Charles by [brevet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brevet_(military) "Brevet (military)"). Cyrus was among the first Union officers to argue for the enlistment of black troops and commanded a brigade of freedmen in the [Siege of Port Hudson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Port_Hudson "Siege of Port Hudson"). Charles and Sarah were present at [Ford's Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford's_Theater "Ford's Theater") the night of [Lincoln's assassination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Lincoln_assassination "Abraham Lincoln assassination"). Hannibal Emery Hamlin was [Maine Attorney General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maine_Attorney_General "Maine Attorney General") from 1905 to 1908. Hannibal Hamlin's great-granddaughter [Sally Hamlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sally_Hamlin "Sally Hamlin") was a child actor who made many [spoken word](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spoken_word "Spoken word") recordings for the [Victor Talking Machine Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Talking_Machine_Company "Victor Talking Machine Company") in the early 20th century.

Hannibal's older brother, Elijah Livermore Hamlin, was president of the Mutual Fire Insurance Co. of Bangor and the Bangor Institution for Savings.[[26]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-26) He was twice an unsuccessful candidate for [governor of Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Maine "Governor of Maine") in the late 1840s and served as mayor of Bangor in 1851–1852. The brothers were members of different political parties (Hannibal a Democrat, and Elijah a Whig) before both became Republicans in the late 1850s.[[27]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-Elijahobit-27)

Hannibal's nephew (Elijah's son) Augustus Choate Hamlin was a physician, artist, mineralogist, author, and historian. He was also mayor of Bangor in 1877–1878, and a founding member of the Bangor Historical Society.[[28]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-28)

Augustus served as a surgeon in the [2nd Maine Volunteer Infantry Regiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2nd_Maine_Volunteer_Infantry_Regiment "2nd Maine Volunteer Infantry Regiment") during the Civil War, eventually becoming a U.S. Army Medical Inspector, and later the Surgeon General of Maine. He wrote books about [Andersonville Prison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andersonville_Prison "Andersonville Prison") and the [Battle of Chancellorsville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Chancellorsville "Battle of Chancellorsville").[[29]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-Chancellorsville-29) Hannibal's grand-nephew (Elijah's grandson) Isaiah K. Stetson was Speaker of the [Maine House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maine_House_of_Representatives "Maine House of Representatives") in 1899–1900,[[30]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-Speakers-30) and owned a large company in Bangor that manufactured and shipped lumber and ice and ran a shipyard and marine railway.[[31]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-31)

Hannibal's first cousin [Cyrus Hamlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyrus_Hamlin_(missionary) "Cyrus Hamlin (missionary)"), who was a graduate of the [Bangor Theological Seminary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangor_Theological_Seminary "Bangor Theological Seminary"), became a missionary in Turkey, where he founded [Robert College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_College "Robert College"). He later became president of [Middlebury College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middlebury_College "Middlebury College") in Vermont. His son, [A. D. F. Hamlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Dwight_Foster_Hamlin "Alfred Dwight Foster Hamlin"), Hannibal's first cousin once removed, became a professor of architecture at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University "Columbia University") and a noted architectural historian. There are biographies of Hamlin by his grandson Charles E. Hamlin (1899, reprinted 1971) and by H. Draper Hunt (1969).[[32]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-32)

## Honors and legacy

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Hannibal_Hamlin&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Honors and legacy")]

[![Image 11](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/ef/Hannibal_Hamlin_statue%2C_Bangor%2C_ME_IMG_2073.JPG/250px-Hannibal_Hamlin_statue%2C_Bangor%2C_ME_IMG_2073.JPG?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Hannibal_Hamlin_statue,_Bangor,_ME_IMG_2073.JPG)

Sculptor [Charles Tefft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Tefft "Charles Tefft") of [Brewer, Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brewer,_Maine "Brewer, Maine"), created this bronze statue of Hannibal Hamlin, which was dedicated in 1927 in downtown [Bangor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangor,_Maine "Bangor, Maine").

[Hamlin County, South Dakota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamlin_County,_South_Dakota "Hamlin County, South Dakota") is named in his honor, as are [Hamlin, Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamlin,_Kansas "Hamlin, Kansas"); [Hamlin, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamlin,_New_York "Hamlin, New York"); [Hamlin, West Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamlin,_West_Virginia "Hamlin, West Virginia"); [Hamlin Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamlin_Township,_Mason_County,_Michigan "Hamlin Township, Mason County, Michigan"); [Hamlin Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamlin_Lake "Hamlin Lake") in [Mason County, Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mason_County,_Michigan "Mason County, Michigan"); [Hamlin Peak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamlin_Peak "Hamlin Peak"), a mountain in [Piscataquis County, Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piscataquis_County,_Maine "Piscataquis County, Maine"); and [Hamlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamlin,_Maine "Hamlin, Maine"), a small Maine village that is a U.S.–Canada border crossing with [Grand Falls, New Brunswick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Falls,_New_Brunswick "Grand Falls, New Brunswick"). There are statues in Hamlin's likeness in the [United States Capitol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Capitol "United States Capitol") and a public park ([Norumbega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norumbega "Norumbega") Mall) in Bangor, Maine.[[33]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-33)

There is also a building on the [University of Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maine "University of Maine") Campus, in [Orono](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orono,_Maine "Orono, Maine"), named Hannibal Hamlin Hall. A fire broke out there on February 13, 1944, in which two students died and one was severely injured. The building was later rebuilt. Hannibal Hamlin Memorial Library is next to his birthplace in [Paris, Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris,_Maine "Paris, Maine").[[34]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-34)

The Hampden Maine Historical Society exhibits a restoration of his first law office at its Kinsley House Museum grounds.

[Hamlin's house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin_House "Hannibal Hamlin House") in [Bangor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangor,_Maine "Bangor, Maine") subsequently housed the presidents of the adjacent [Bangor Theological Seminary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangor_Theological_Seminary "Bangor Theological Seminary"). It is listed on the [National Register of Historic Places](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Register_of_Historic_Places "National Register of Historic Places"), as is Hamlin's birthplace in [Paris, Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris,_Maine "Paris, Maine") (as part of the [Paris Hill Historic District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Hill_Historic_District "Paris Hill Historic District")).[[35]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-35)

Hamlin Park in Chicago is named in his honor.[[36]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-36)

## In popular culture

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Hannibal_Hamlin&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: In popular culture")]

Hamlin appears briefly in three [alternate history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternate_history "Alternate history") writings by [Harry Turtledove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Turtledove "Harry Turtledove"): _[The Guns of the South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Guns\_of\_the\_South "The Guns of the South")_, _[Must and Shall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counting\_Up,\_Counting\_Down#Must\_and\_Shall "Counting Up, Counting Down")_, and _[How Few Remain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/How\_Few\_Remain "How Few Remain")_.[[37]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-37)[[38]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-38)[[39]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-39)

In _[Fallout 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fallout\_3 "Fallout 3")_, a 2008 [action role-playing game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action_role-playing_game "Action role-playing game"), a quest called "Head of State" involves returning the [Lincoln Memorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Memorial "Lincoln Memorial") statue's head to its pedestal. The leader of the group trying to return the head is named Hannibal Hamlin.[[40]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin#cite_note-40)
