From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

**[Hannibal Hamlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_Hamlin "Hannibal Hamlin")** (1809–1891) was a U.S. senator from Maine from 1869 to 1881. **Senator Hamlin** may also refer to:

*   [Elijah Hamlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elijah_Hamlin "Elijah Hamlin") (1800–1872), Maine State Senate
*   [Hannibal E. Hamlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal_E._Hamlin "Hannibal E. Hamlin") (1858–1938), Maine State Senate
*   [John Hamlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hamlin "John Hamlin") (1800–1876), Illinois State Senate
