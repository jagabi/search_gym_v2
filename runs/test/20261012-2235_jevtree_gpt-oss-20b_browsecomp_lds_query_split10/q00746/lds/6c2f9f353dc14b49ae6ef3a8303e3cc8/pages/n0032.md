The attribution gap

## Analytics can't see the full picture

When visitors decline consent or arrive through offline channels — podcasts, word of mouth, events — tracking pixels lose the signal entirely. You see traffic, but not where it came from.

*   Consent decline rates across the EU average 40–54%, leaving a significant share of traffic unattributed.
*   Dark social channels — referrals, podcasts, offline conversations — never appear in your analytics dashboard.
*   Without knowing which channels work, marketing budgets get allocated based on incomplete data.

## Frequently asked questions

##### Does the widget collect personal data?

No. Each response stores only the selected answer, the page path, device type, and timestamp. No visitor identifiers, IP addresses, or cookies are recorded.

##### Does it show to visitors who have declined consent?

Yes. The widget displays to all visitors regardless of consent status, because it sets no cookies and collects no personal data.

##### Will it affect my existing cookie banner or privacy settings?

No. Audience Survey operates independently of your consent configuration. Your existing banner and privacy settings remain unchanged.

##### I don't have Cookiebot™ yet. Can I still use the Audience Survey?

Audience Survey requires an active Cookiebot™ account. Start with Cookiebot™ and add Audience Survey once your account is set up.

##### Can I run multiple surveys on the same domain?

No, currently only one Audience Survey configuration can be set up per domain. This limit applies regardless of whether a survey is active or paused. To activate another survey, the old one needs to be deleted completely.

##### What happens to my data if I delete a survey?

Deleting a survey removes the configuration and all associated data permanently. If you want to stop a survey temporarily without losing data, use the pause function instead.

##### Is the configuration attached to a domain or a domain group?

Configurations are attached to individual domains, not domain groups. If you have multiple domains, you'll need to configure Audience Survey separately for each.

##### How long does it take for the survey to go live after I activate it?

After activating or making changes to your survey configuration, it can take up to 20 minutes for the survey to become visible to website visitors. This delay is due to caching. The same applies when pausing or deactivating a survey, the change won't take effect immediately.
