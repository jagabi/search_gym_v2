Privacy Policy

At Dreamdata, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal

information. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when

you visit our website, use our services, or interact with us in any way.

This policy applies if you are a Dreamdata customer or subscriber, or if you are just

visiting our website and materials.

If you have a Dreamdata account (free or paid subscription), Dreamdata.io ApS acts

as the data controller of personal data about you and your use of Dreamdata (such

as the email address you used to create the account and payment information). But

Dreamdata.io is the data processor of personal data about your customers,

prospects, or any other information you provide to the Dreamdata application.

Dreamdata Inc. complies with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (“EU-U.S. DPF”),

the UK Extension to the EU-U.S. DPF, and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework

(“Swiss-U.S. DPF”) (collectively, “DPFs”) as set forth by the U.S. Department of

Commerce, the Data Privacy Framework when information is shared from the Danish

entity Dreamdata.io ApS to the US entity Dreamdata Inc. Dreamdata Inc. is

committed to the Principles and adheres to the Principles for all personal data

received from the EU. For more detail regarding DPF and Dreamdata’s privacy

practices, see the detail provided in this document. The DPF portions apply only in

cases where the PII of an EU, Swiss, or UK resident is processed by a Dreamdata

employee in the US.

Collected Information

Log Data When you visit our website, we automatically collect standard data

provided by your web browser. This may include your IP address,

browser type, pages visited and when, URL clickstreams, and more.

Personal Information We may ask for personal information such as your name, email,

phone number, work address, and payment information. We only

collect this data when you interact with our services, such as signing

up for an account or subscribing to our newsletter.

We only request this information when you interact with our

services—like signing up for an account, making use of our platform

features, paying for access, or subscribing to our newsletter. We only

use collected personal information in the context of providing or

improving these services.

Business Information We also collect business data generated through your use of our

platform, including transaction records, user profiles, and analytics

data.

How We Collect Information

From the first moment you interact with Dreamdata, we are collecting data.

Sometimes you provide us with data (such as by creating an account or signing up

for a webinar or newsletter). Other times we collect data about you, either

automatically based on your consent settings, refer to our Cookie Policy for more

information. We may also collect information from other sources, like publicly

available websites or from a trusted data supplier to obtain relevant business contact

information. We use trusted vendors who have obtained appropriate consent or legal

bases for obtaining that information.

You have the right to refuse our request for information, although this may limit the

services we can provide. You can also adjust your cookie preferences in your

browser and on our website to limit the type of information that is collected about

you, but refusing certain cookies may limit the services we can provide.

Use of Information

We use both identifying and non-identifying information to understand how our

visitors use our services and to improve their experience. Your personal information

is primarily used to grant you access to our services, provide customer support,

process payments, and provide updates about our website and services.

We may use anonymized or aggregated information to improve Dreamdata’s services

and product offerings, but if you are a customer, the data you submit to Dreamdata is

never used to train or improve another customer’s data. We may also use

anonymized and aggregated data in website and customer usage reports or

benchmarking data.

Dreamdata’s accountability for PII it receives under the DPFs and subsequent

transfers to a third party is described in the EU-U.S. DPF Principles. In particular,

Dreamdata remains responsible and liable under the DPFs if third-party agents that it

engages to process the PII on its behalf do so in a manner inconsistent with the

Principles, unless Dreamdata proves that it is not responsible for the event giving

rise to the damage.

We may use your personal information to contact you regarding updates and/or

promotional content. You can always use the “unsubscribe” instructions in

communications to control the content you receive from us.

Data Processing and Storage

The personal information we collect is stored and processed in the European Union,

or where we or our partners, affiliates and third-party providers maintain facilities. We

only transfer data within jurisdictions subject to data protection laws that reflect our

commitment to protecting the privacy of our users.

We only retain personal information and business data for as long as necessary to

provide a service, or to improve our services in future. While we retain this data, we

will protect it within commercially acceptable means to prevent loss and theft, as

well as unauthorised access, disclosure, copying, use or modification. That said, we

advise that no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and

cannot guarantee absolute data security.
