**William Palfrey** (1741–1780) was an [American Patriot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patriot_(American_Revolution) "Patriot (American Revolution)").

## Early life

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=William_Palfrey&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Early life")]

William Palfrey was born February 24, 1741, in [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston "Boston"), [Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts "Massachusetts").

## Freemasonry

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=William_Palfrey&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Freemasonry")]

In 1769, Palfrey was Substitute Master of the Lodge of St. Andrew, a masonic lodge warranted by the [Grand Lodge of Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Lodge_of_Scotland "Grand Lodge of Scotland") in 1756. Palfrey was Substitute Master when the Master was [Joseph Warren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Warren "Joseph Warren") and the lodge Secretary was [Paul Revere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Revere "Paul Revere"). Member, St. Andrew's Lodge at Boston: EA, December 5, 1760; FC, December 18, 1760; MM, January 27, 1761, and later served as Junior Warden and Senior Warden of this Lodge. Palfrey, with seven others, petitioned the Massachusetts Grand Lodge on May 11, 1770, for new lodge under the title of The Massachusetts Lodge. The charter was granted May 13, 1770, and Palfrey's name is the second one on the charter. He is on record as SW of this lodge on June 1, 1770; elected WM December 3, 1770, and again, December 18, 1778. Elected Grand Secretary of the Massachusetts Grand Lodge December 4, 1778. "It was in all probability through the influence of William Palfrey, Grand Secretary, that the charter (The Massachusetts Lodge) was granted."[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Palfrey_#cite_note-:0-1)[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Palfrey_#cite_note-2)

## Career

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=William_Palfrey&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Career")]

Working as [John Hancock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hancock "John Hancock")'s chief clerk, he was active in the movements that preceded the [American Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolution "American Revolution"), and visited England in 1771. During the War of Independence, he served as an [aide-de-camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aide-de-camp "Aide-de-camp") to [George Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington "George Washington") in March and April 1776, after which Hancock arranged to have him appointed [paymaster-general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paymaster-General_of_the_United_States_Army "Paymaster-General of the United States Army") in the [Continental Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_Army "Continental Army"), with the rank of lieutenant-colonel. In November 1780, he was appointed consul-general in [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France "France") by a unanimous vote of Congress, and embarked in a ship for that country, which was never heard of after she had left the capes. He was succeeded as paymaster-general by [John Pierce Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Pierce_Jr. "John Pierce Jr.")[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Palfrey_#cite_note-Ledger-3)

## Disappearance

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=William_Palfrey&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Disappearance")]

Lost at sea in December, 1780, while on special business to France by order of the Continental Congress.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Palfrey_#cite_note-4) His assignment was "consul to reside in France" and "he sailed for his post on the _[Shillala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shillala?action=edit&redlink=1 "Shillala (page does not exist)")_, an armed ship of 16 guns. The vessel was never heard from after it passed the [Delaware capes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delaware_capes "Delaware capes")."[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Palfrey_#cite_note-:0-1)

## Legacy

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=William_Palfrey&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Legacy")]

His grandson [John G. Palfrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_G._Palfrey "John G. Palfrey") was a [U.S. Representative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Representative "U.S. Representative") from Massachusetts.
