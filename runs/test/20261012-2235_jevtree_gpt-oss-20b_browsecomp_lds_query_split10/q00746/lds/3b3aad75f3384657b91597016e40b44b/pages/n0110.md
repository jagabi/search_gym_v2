### WHY STUDY ABROAD?

*   **Global Perspective:** Gain a deeper understanding of diverse cultures and global issues.
*   **Career Advantage:** Enhance your resume with international experience and cultural competence.
*   **Networking:** Connect with global leaders, professionals, and peers.
*   **Personal Growth:** Develop adaptability, independence, and cross-cultural communication skills.
*   **Academic Excellence:** Study under expert faculty with a global focus.
*   **Immersive Learning:** Participate in internships, service-learning, and research opportunities abroad.
*   **Language Proficiency:** Improve or acquire new language skills through immersion.
*   **Leadership Skills:** Build leadership capabilities through international projects and collaborations.
*   **Cultural Exchange:** Experience living in a different culture, broadening your worldview.
*   **Lifelong Impact:** Create lasting memories and relationships that influence your personal and professional life.

### HOW TO APPLY

The Global Education office staff is available to advise you on how an off campus program can fit into your academic plan. We strongly suggest attending our office hours (every Monday and Thursday 2-4pm) or setting up an appointment to discuss your options. You can use your financial aid (merit- and need-based awards) while enrolled in a semester-long Westmont program. In order to attend a third-party program, please submit the "Leave of Absence for Study Abroad" application through our office. Financial aid is not available for Mayterm and summer programs, but study abroad scholarships are available (see scholarship information below). The deadline to submit an application for all Mayterm/Summer 2027 programs is **October 16, 2025**.

## FACULTY-LED MAYTERM AND SUMMER PROGRAMS

![Image 14: ecuador-1268 - Blake Victor Kent](https://www.westmont.edu/sites/default/files/styles/scale_1280/public/ecuador-1268%20-%20Blake%20Victor%20Kent.jpg?itok=vR3hD3Ea)

###### **MAYTERM 2027**

#### [Ecuador and the Galápagos Islands](https://www.westmont.edu/study-abroad/ecuador-and-galapagos-islands)

*   Backpack a volcano, SCUBA dive, hike, and surf in the Galapagos Islands! Explore waterfalls and rivers in the Amazon and tour the world famous Charles Darwin Research Station.
*   Explore some of the most biodiverse regions in the world, including cloud forests, steamy jungles, mountainous highlands, and epic marine reserves, while examining human/environment interaction at the individual and structural levels.

![Image 15: westmont in mexico crop](https://www.westmont.edu/sites/default/files/westmontinmexico_0.jpg)

###### **MAYTERM 2027**

#### [Mexico](https://www.westmont.edu/global-education/mayterm-mexico "Mayterm in Mexico")

*   Join us for a truly immersive experience in the colonial city of Querétaro, a World Heritage Site. Live with a host family, gain Spanish fluency, and explore the art and culture of Mexico with locals.
*   Hike along rivers and through canyons in the Sierra Gorda Biosphere Reserve, climb the pyramids of Teotihuacan, visit Frida Kahlo’s house in Mexico City, and hike up the world’s third-largest monolith in Bernal.

![Image 16: Vienna, Austria by @dylu on Unsplash](https://www.westmont.edu/sites/default/files/styles/scale_1280/public/jacek-dylag-5SjAaqqCCmY-unsplash%201.png?itok=OPJ3Go5T)

###### **SUMMER 2027**

#### [Psychology and Psychotherapy from Berlin to Vienna](https://www.westmont.edu/study-abroad/psychology-and-psychotherapy-berlin-vienna)

*   Explore and study in Berlin (historic capital of Germany and the site of Gestalt psychology), Leipzig (site of the first experimental psychology laboratory), Regensburg (UNESCO heritage city), and Vienna (the birthplace of psychotherapy).
*   Gain insight into the minds of Sigmund Freud, Alfred Adler, and Viktor Frankl – three prominent psychotherapists and founders of classic psychological theories – as you explore their homes and museums.

![Image 17: Oxford, England by @nilslindner on Unsplash](https://www.westmont.edu/sites/default/files/styles/scale_1280/public/nils-lindner-2fPT0OpMvRY-unsplash%201.png?itok=zFN3F7CJ)

###### **MAYTERM 2027**

#### [Literature and Theatre England Mayterm](https://www.westmont.edu/academics/global-education/england)

*   Join us for transformative, immersive learning from the West End stages of London to Shakespeare's birthplace in Stratford-upon-Avon, from the dreaming spires of Oxford to the wild beauty of the Lake District.
*   This program offers cohort-based learning as we study historical, social, and religious foundations of English literature and drama, see plays performed, and speak with members of England’s vibrant theatre culture.

### SCHOLARSHIPS

###### **SCHOLARSHIP**

#### The Donna Dillon Manning and Larry Horner Study Abroad Scholarship

* * *

This is a need-based scholarship, designed to alleviate the financial burden associated with global programs. These scholarships, governed by Westmont College policies and procedures, support students with demonstrated financial need who have been selected to participate in approved study abroad opportunities. The financial awards specifically target expenses such as airfare, program fees, and other related costs that extend beyond the standard tuition, room/board charges, and fees. Students attending any Westmont -led semester-long, Mayterm, or summer program are eligible to apply. This includes summer affiliate programs (as long as they are attending through Westmont). Recipients will be selected based on academic merit and financial need.

[**Click to Apply**](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeFqtmRVjMlZJ4zkdqdfrvRey4ol7xpeJh54U1QWvfdhtuYnA/viewform "Scholarship form")

###### **SCHOLARSHIP**

#### Rose Marie Springer Diversity Scholarship

* * *

This scholarship was established by [Westmont in San Francisco](https://www.westmont.edu/san-francisco "Westmont in San Francisco") (then “San Francisco Urban Program”) in 2005 to honor [Rose Marie “Rusty” Springer](https://www.westmont.edu/50-year-urban-adventure) who served at the Program for 20 years. The purpose of this scholarship is to provide a continuing source of support to individuals interested in pursuing work in diversity and reconciliation, whether that is socio-economic, religious, gender, racial, or other, by awarding scholarships to college students who participate at Westmont in San Francisco. Scholarships will be awarded on the basis of merit to students who embody the qualities that are eminently seen in the life of Rusty; those of tolerance, sensitivity, grace, mercy, justice and compassion.
