## **Guns Like Lightning**

**Hampton Smith**

This little-known unit drawn from across Minnesota—celebrated as a hard-fighting, well-led “band of brothers”—persevered through trials and tragedy in the battle to save the Union and end slavery.

[Learn more](https://shop.mnhs.org/products/guns-like-lightning-the-1st-minnesota-light-artillery-in-the-civil-war)

[![Image 21: Book cover for Minnesota Hoops by Marc Hugunin and Stew Thornley with photo of Paige Bueckers](https://www.mnhs.org/hs-fs/hubfs/HUGUNIN_9781681343495.jpg?width=600&height=600&name=HUGUNIN_9781681343495.jpg)](https://shop.mnhs.org/products/minnesota-hoops-revised-edition-the-state-of-basketball)

## Minnesota Hoops

**Marc Hugunin and Stew Thornley**

Minnesota’s basketball tradition rivals other sports, including hockey, with breakout stars and winning teams making headlines. This revised edition proves the case for Minnesota as the state of basketball.

[Learn more](https://shop.mnhs.org/products/minnesota-hoops-revised-edition-the-state-of-basketball)

[![Image 22: Book cover of Perseverance: A Memoir by Mark Dayton with a foreword by Hillary Clinton](https://www.mnhs.org/hs-fs/hubfs/DAYTON_9781681343396.jpg?width=600&height=600&name=DAYTON_9781681343396.jpg)](https://shop.mnhs.org/products/perseverance-a-memoir)

## Perseverance: A Memoir

**Mark Dayton**

**Foreword by Hillary Rodham Clinton**

This entertaining, insightful, and revealing account of a man born into social prominence who dedicated his life to public service provides a candid look behind the curtain at contemporary politics in the state and the nation.

[Learn more](https://shop.mnhs.org/products/perseverance-a-memoir)

[![Image 23: Book cover of children's book titled Blue Bearies by Brenda Child with illustrations by Jonathan Thunder and translations by Gordon Jourdain](https://www.mnhs.org/hs-fs/hubfs/CHILDTHUNDER_9781681343389.jpg?width=600&height=600&name=CHILDTHUNDER_9781681343389.jpg)](https://shop.mnhs.org/products/blue-bearies)

## Blue Bearies

**Story by Brenda Child**

**Illustrations by Jonathan Thunder**

**Translations by Gordon Jourdain**

In this follow-up to the bestselling _Bowwow Powwow_, Windy Girl and her classmates learn to appreciate our bear relatives and the wisdom that comes from observing their actions and antics in various northland scenes.

[Learn more](https://shop.mnhs.org/products/blue-bearies)

[![Image 24: Book cover for Walking My Ancestors' Path by Sam Zimmerman](https://www.mnhs.org/hs-fs/hubfs/ZIMMERMAN_9781681343440.jpg?width=600&height=600&name=ZIMMERMAN_9781681343440.jpg)](https://shop.mnhs.org/products/walking-my-ancestors-path-a-collection-of-paintings-and-stories)

## Walking My Ancestors' Path

****Sam Zimmerman / Zhaawanoogiizhik****

This compilation commemorates animal spirits ranging from bees to rainbow trout, from hummingbirds to wolves, and is illuminated with the vibrant flora and vivid hues of Minnesota’s beloved northland.

[Learn more](https://shop.mnhs.org/products/walking-my-ancestors-path-a-collection-of-paintings-and-stories)

[![Image 25: Book cover for The Red Wing Way by Dave Kenney](https://www.mnhs.org/hs-fs/hubfs/KENNEY_9781681342917.jpg?width=600&height=600&name=KENNEY_9781681342917.jpg)](https://shop.mnhs.org/products/the-red-wing-way-from-main-street-to-global-legacy)

## The Red Wing Way

**Dave Kenney**

Rare archival images and profiles of legendary figures chronicle pivotal moments in the history of the Red Wing Shoe Company, offering an all-access pass inside a Minnesota original that grew into an international player.

[Learn more](https://shop.mnhs.org/products/the-red-wing-way-from-main-street-to-global-legacy)

[![Image 26: Book cover for End of the Road Cookbook by Crystal Schlueter](https://www.mnhs.org/hs-fs/hubfs/SCHLUETER_9781681343419.jpg?width=600&height=600&name=SCHLUETER_9781681343419.jpg)](https://shop.mnhs.org/products/end-of-the-road-cookbook-traditional-favorites-from-the-iron-range)

## End of the Road Cookbook

**Crystal Schlueter**

Communities near the Boundary Waters in northern Minnesota—at the “end of the road”—offer diverse recipes and foodways. Columnist Crystal Schlueter has compiled a hearty selection that will delight the palate.

[Learn more](https://shop.mnhs.org/products/end-of-the-road-cookbook-traditional-favorites-from-the-iron-range)

[![Image 27: Book cover for The Babysitter Tree by Molly Beth Griffin with illustrations by Jennifer A. Bell](https://www.mnhs.org/hs-fs/hubfs/GRIFFINBELL_9781681343433.jpg?width=600&height=600&name=GRIFFINBELL_9781681343433.jpg)](https://shop.mnhs.org/products/the-babysitter-tree)

## The Babysitter Tree

**Story by Molly Beth Griffin**

**Illustrations by Jennifer A. Bell**

Young black bears tumble and spar, rest and recharge, and—most importantly—find safety and security in the shaded sanctuary of a white pine tree.

[Learn More](https://shop.mnhs.org/products/the-babysitter-tree)

## The Spirit of Fearless Living

**Kao Choua Vue**

An unfiltered, deeply personal account of war and survival as told by the author’s mother—a Hmong woman who endured bombings, famine, and forced displacement during the US Secret War in Laos.
