### To learn, To serve, To lead, To connect, To give, To explore.

Westmont College alumni stories highlight the inspiring journeys of graduates who are making a difference in their communities and careers around the world. Through powerful testimonials, our alumni share how Westmont’s Christ-centered education, rigorous academics, and vibrant campus life prepared them for meaningful work, leadership, and service. Explore these stories to see the lasting impact of a Westmont education and the diverse paths our alumni pursue.
