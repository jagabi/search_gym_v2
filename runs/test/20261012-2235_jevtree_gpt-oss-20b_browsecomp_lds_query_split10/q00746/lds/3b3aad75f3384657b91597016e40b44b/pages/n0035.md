# Digital Newspapers at MNHS

The Minnesota Historical Society holds the largest single collection of Minnesota newspapers, with publication dates ranging from 1849 to the present day.

Here’s a list of current and future online collections and resources.

## [Minnesota Digital Newspaper Hub](https://www.mnhs.org/newspapers/hub)

The Minnesota Digital Newspaper Hub is a searchable website from the Minnesota Historical Society that makes millions of pages of Minnesota newspapers available online. The Hub contains geographically and culturally diverse newspapers published between 1849 and today, with new content added regularly. Due to potential copyright restrictions, most issues published after 1977 can only be accessed from the [Gale Family Library](https://www.mnhs.org/library) at the Minnesota History Center. [Explore the Newspaper Hub](https://www.mnhs.org/newspapers/hub).

![Image 10](https://www.mnhs.org/sites/default/files/node/2257/newsboys.jpg?width=288&height=138&name=newsboys.jpg)

## [Minnesota Digital Newspaper Hub - Pioneer Press Collection](https://newspaperhub.mnhs.org/)

The St. Paul Pioneer Press 1861-1961 digital newspaper collection is a free, searchable archive of digitized issues of the St. Paul Pioneer Press and related titles, published between 1861 and 1961. [Search the newspapers](https://newspaperhub.mnhs.org/).

Digitization of the St. Paul Pioneer Press 1861-1961 was made possible by the Legacy Amendment's Arts and Cultural Heritage Fund through the vote of Minnesotans on Nov. 4, 2008.

![Image 11: Newsboys, St. Paul Pioneer Press, 1923](https://www.mnhs.org/sites/default/files/2025-08/pioneerpress_0.png?width=390&height=253&name=pioneerpress_0.png)

## [Swedish American Newspapers](https://www.mnhs.org/newspapers/swedishamerican)

Hundreds of Swedish-language newspapers were published in North America from 1850 onward, catering to the continent's Swedish immigrant communities. This free, searchable archive contains over 1.3 million digitized newspaper pages from Swedish American newspaper titles published across the United States and Canada between 1851 and 2007. [Search, view, and read](https://www.mnhs.org/newspapers/swedishamerican) the newspapers, and learn more about Swedish America through essays, photographs and recommendations for further reading.

![Image 12](https://www.mnhs.org/sites/default/files/newspapers/swedish-kids3.jpg?width=288&height=180&name=swedish-kids3.jpg)

## [National Digital Newspaper Program](https://www.mnhs.org/newspapers/ndnp)

In 2007, 2009, 2011, 2015, 2017, 2019, and 2022 the Minnesota Historical Society received two-year grants from the National Endowment for the Humanities (NEH) to participate in the National Digital Newspaper Program (NDNP). This program is a partnership between the NEH and the Library of Congress to build a national digital resource of newspapers published between 1690 and 1963. [Learn more](https://www.mnhs.org/newspapers/ndnp).

[Find out more](https://www.mnhs.org/library/learn/collections/newspapers) about newspapers at the Minnesota Historical Society, including those which have not yet been digitized.

Historical American newspaper pages from 1690-1963 can be found at the Library of Congress [Chronicling America site](http://chroniclingamerica.loc.gov/).
