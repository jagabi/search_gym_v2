# _WESTMONT_ Magazine

This issue of _Westmont Magazine_ celebrates the unifying values that continue to shape the college’s mission, community and impact around the world. From transformative educational experiences and innovative academic programs to stories of faith, leadership and service, these pages highlight the many ways Westmont prepares thoughtful scholars, grateful servants and faithful leaders. As the college expands its reach through new initiatives, global engagement and community partnerships, the enduring commitment to Christ-centered learning remains at the heart of every endeavor.

![Image 2: m](https://www.westmont.edu/sites/default/files/the-new-york-public-library-L3D6oyQO6Do-unsplash.jpg)

## Featured Stories

This issue’s featured stories invite readers to engage with timely and thought-provoking topics that connect faith, culture and civic life. From the spiritual practices of solitude and silence to reflections on patriotism, democracy and the enduring significance of the Declaration of Independence at 250, these articles encourage deeper consideration of the values and convictions that shape individuals, communities and the common good.

## Athletics

Westmont Athletics continues to exemplify excellence both on the field and in the classroom.
