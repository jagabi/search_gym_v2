# Research Tools

The Gale Family Library provides resources and tools for distance and on-site research.

[![Image 13: Library and archives catalog.](https://www.mnhs.org/hs-fs/hubfs/library_rt_archives_700x470.jpg?width=700&height=470&name=library_rt_archives_700x470.jpg) ## Library and archives catalog](https://mnpals-mhs.primo.exlibrisgroup.com/discovery/search?sortby=rank&vid=01MNPALS_MHS:MHS&lang=en)

The MNHS library and archives catalog searches books, archival collections, maps, newspapers, and much more.

[![Image 14: Frederick McKinley Jones standing in front of a Werner Transportation refrigerated truck](https://www.mnhs.org/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20sitepage%20images/library_rt_collections_700x470.jpg?width=700&height=470&name=library_rt_collections_700x470.jpg) ## Collections online](https://www.mnhs.org/search/collections)

Search for photographs, three-dimensional and archaeological objects, artwork, oral histories, moving images, maps, and more.

[![Image 15: A stack of various vital records documents.](https://www.mnhs.org/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20sitepage%20images/library_rt_records_700x470.jpg?width=700&height=470&name=library_rt_records_700x470.jpg) ## Minnesota People Records Search](https://www.mnhs.org/search/people)

A useful starting point for genealogists. Searches birth, death, state census, veterans’ grave, and Gold Star Roll records.

[![Image 16: A photograph of a storage room with tall shelves of archival boxes](https://www.mnhs.org/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20sitepage%20images/library_rt_findingaid_700x470.jpg?width=700&height=470&name=library_rt_findingaid_700x470.jpg) ## Archival collection finding aids](https://libguides.mnhs.org/searchfindingaids?_gl=1*f8f198*_gcl_au*MTU4NzAxMTM5OS4xNzU1MTMyMjYz)

Finding aids are under maintenance and may be temporarily unavailable throughout the month of July. [Click here](https://libguides.mnhs.org/searchfindingaids) for other ways we can help.

[![Image 17: Many newspapers, including &quot;Anishinaabeg Today,&quot; arranged on top of one another](https://www.mnhs.org/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20sitepage%20images/library_rt_newspapers_700x470.jpg?width=700&height=470&name=library_rt_newspapers_700x470.jpg) ## Minnesota Digital Newspaper Hub](https://www.mnhs.org/newspapers/hub)

Geographically and culturally diverse Minnesota newspapers published between 1856 and today. Due to copyright law, most newspapers published after 1922 can be accessed only from the Gale Family Library at the Minnesota History Center.

[![Image 18: A collage of Minnesota History Magazine covers](https://www.mnhs.org/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20sitepage%20images/library_rt_mnhistory_700x470.jpg?width=700&height=470&name=library_rt_mnhistory_700x470.jpg) ## _Minnesota History_ magazine index](https://mnhs.gitlab.io/archive/minnesotahistory/index/index.html)

_Minnesota History_ magazine provides history lovers with intriguing stories about Minnesota’s past in full-color, richly illustrated print and digital formats.
