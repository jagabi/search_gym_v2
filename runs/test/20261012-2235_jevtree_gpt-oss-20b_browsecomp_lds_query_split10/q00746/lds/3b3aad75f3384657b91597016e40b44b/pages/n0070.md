# Publications

AFSA's publications are among our most important tools to inform, educate and engage.

#### [_The Foreign Service Journal_ – Diplomacy and Development from the Practitioner's Perspective](https://afsa.org/fsj)

[![Image 2](https://afsa.org/sites/default/files/fsjCoversMontage.jpg)](https://afsa.org/fsj)

Our most visible outreach tool is our flagship magazine, _The Foreign Service Journal_. With a circulation of 18,000, the _FSJ_ not only goes to every member of AFSA but also to a large group of outside audiences – including all foreign embassies and key offices on Capitol Hill. With six issues a year, the _FSJ_ is our best way of putting the amazing work of the Foreign Service in front of those who most need to know about it. [Click here to read more about _The Foreign Service Journal_](https://afsa.org/fsj).

#### [_AFSA News_ – Reporting on the Work of the Association and Union](https://afsa.org/afsa-news)

[![Image 3](https://afsa.org/sites/default/files/AfsaNewsLogo.png)](https://afsa.org/afsa-news)

AFSA also publishes _AFSA News_, the association/union newsletter which is included monthly in the _FSJ_. This is where we tell you what’s going on at AFSA – what are we working on, what events and programs are coming up, what you need to know as a member of AFSA. Similarly, the _AFSA Newsletter_ is a bi-monthly publication aimed mostly at our retirees, with a focus on benefits and federal issues.

#### [Foreign Service Books](https://afsa.org/foreign-service-books)

![Image 4](https://afsa.org/sites/default/files/fsBooksMontage2024.jpg)

FS Books is our publishing arm, which already has a worldwide bestseller under its belt. [_Inside a U.S. Embassy: Diplomacy at Work_](https://afsa.org/inside-us-embassy) is now in its third edition and has sold over 200,000 copies worldwide. There is no better primer on the Foreign Service. Anyone considering a diplomatic career should have a copy of this book. In 2024, in time for the Foreign Service and AFSA dual centennials, FS Books published the second edition of _[The Voice of the Foreign Service: A History of the American Foreign Service Association at 100](https://afsa.org/voice-foreign-service)_ by Harry Kopp.

#### [Other Publications](https://afsa.org/afsa-publications-resources)

![Image 5](https://afsa.org/sites/default/files/otherPublications.jpg)

Our other publications include our indispensable annual tax guide for members of the Foreign Service, the AFSA annual report, our semiannual education supplement with information tailored to Foreign Service families, and the annual retiree directory.
