[Overview](https://www.mnhs.org/explore)

    *   [**Minnesota People Records** Discover materials associated with a particular person or name. Includes birth, death, census, veterans graves, and Gold Star Roll records.](https://www.mnhs.org/search/people)
    *   [**Search Collections** Search photographs, artifacts, maps and atlases, oral histories, works of art, sound recordings and much more from MNHS Collections.](https://www.mnhs.org/library/research)
    *   [**Gale Family Library/Research Help** Start your research here. Tools, library and archival collection guides and information on distance and in-person library services.](https://www.mnhs.org/library)
    *   [**Digital Newspapers** Access to millions of digital newspaper pages through the Minnesota Digital Newspaper Hub.](https://www.mnhs.org/newspapers)
    *   [**MNHS Press** Browse MNHS books, and explore MNopedia, our online Minnesota encyclopedia.](https://www.mnhs.org/mnhspress)
    *   [**Minnesota History magazine** A lively, richly illustrated magazine published quarterly. Purchase recent issues and explore the archive.](https://mnhs.gitlab.io/archive/minnesotahistory/)
    *   [**Minnesota Unraveled** Journey into our state’s past with the new podcast from the Minnesota Historical Society](https://www3.mnhs.org/unraveled)
    *   [**MNopedia** Minnesota’s online encyclopedia with entries about significant people, places, events and things in Minnesota history.](https://www.mnhs.org/mnopedia)
    *   [**Preservation** Learn how we preserve and protect our state’s objects, documents, and places.](https://www.mnhs.org/preservation)
    *   [**Archaeology** Learn about the Minnesota Historical Society’s archaeology collections, with more than 950,000 artifacts dating back about 11,500 years.](https://www.mnhs.org/library/learn/collections/archaeology)

    *   [**National History Day** Students choose a topic on an annual theme, then research and present papers, exhibits, performances, documentaries and websites.](https://www.mnhs.org/historyday)

    *   [**Teaching Native American History & Culture** Resources developed to broaden your knowledge and engage students.](https://www.mnhs.org/education/native-american)
    *   [**Field Trips** Transport your students to hands-on history destinations.](https://www.mnhs.org/fieldtrips)
    *   [**Teacher Memberships** Discounted admission for educators.](https://www.mnhs.org/support/membership#discounts)
    *   [**6th Grade History Pass** Minnesota 6th graders are granted FREE admission to MNHS historic sites and museums for one year. Teachers and parents may order them now!](https://www.mnhs.org/historypass)
