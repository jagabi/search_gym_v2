# Welcome to Lake Mallalieu

Nestled in the heart of Hudson, Wisconsin, Lake Mallalieu is a cherished community landmark where natural beauty and recreation come together. Just steps from the St. Croix River, the lake offers residents and visitors opportunities for boating, fishing, wildlife viewing, and relaxation along its scenic shores. The Lake Mallalieu Association is dedicated to preserving the health, beauty, and vitality of the lake for generations to come, while fostering a strong sense of community among those who enjoy its waters.

![Image 3](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/96a55d_a90bd7e8032d4f9a9e0315c2771b39fc~mv2.jpg/v1/fill/w_311,h_178,al_c,lg_1,q_80,enc_avif,quality_auto/96a55d_a90bd7e8032d4f9a9e0315c2771b39fc~mv2.jpg)

Membership in the Lake Mallalieu Association is open to all who own property on, or reside within one mile of Lake Mallalieu and whose annual membership dues are paid.
