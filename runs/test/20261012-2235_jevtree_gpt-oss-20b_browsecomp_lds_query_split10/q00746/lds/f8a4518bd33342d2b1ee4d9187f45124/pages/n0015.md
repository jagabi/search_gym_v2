# About Us

Meet our Board

The Lake Mallalieu Association board is made up of dedicated volunteers who share a deep commitment to preserving the health and beauty of our lake. Each member generously contributes their time, talents, and energy to support our community, lead stewardship efforts, and ensure Lake Mallalieu remains a vibrant resource for all to enjoy.
