PUBLISHED: August 10, 2014 at 2:57 AM EDT | UPDATED: January 9, 2019 at 7:54 PM EST

A 25-year-old Manhattan woman was found dead in Riverside Park surrounded by pills and beer cans early Saturday.

Anne Wiswell was discovered by a dog walker under a tree in the park near W. 99th St. and Riverside Drive about 6:40 a.m., cops said.

She was clothed and had no visible signs of trauma.

“They had the whole area cordoned off,” said Sue, a 71-year-old gardener in the park who declined to give her last name.

[Video 2](https://www.nydailynews.com/2014/08/10/woman-25-found-dead-in-riverside-park-2/)

“I asked, ‘did somebody die?’ They said there was a woman laying on her back.

“I’m sad. It’s always sad whenever someone dies.”

Locals were stunned by the discovery.

“I never see any crimes here. You never really see anything. I love coming down here,” said Jennifer Garner, 43, who was out walking her dog.

Wiswell lived on Riverside Drive, about a block from where she was found.

The city medical examiner will conduct an autopsy to determine how she died.

Parkgoers recalled an earlier unsettling death.

Two years ago, a man’s body was found hanging from a tree about six blocks away in Riverside Park in an apparent suicide. His body was found near W. 115 St. on July 8, 2012.
