# Paulette Stenseth

## Nov 24, 1945 — Aug 28, 2015

 Paulette Stenseth, 69, Grand Forks, died Friday, August 28, 2015 at home. Paulette Sylvia Bossoletti the daughter of Paul and Sylvia (Land) Bossoletti was born on November 24, 1945 at Pembina, North Dakota. She moved with her family to Grand Forks where they lived on Riverside Drive. She attended St. Michael's School and graduated from St. James Academy in 1963. Paulette Bossoletti and David L. Stenseth were united in marriage on April 24, 1965 in Grand Forks. In her teens and early twenties she worked for Roller's Office Supply, The Beanery, and various other part time jobs. Her main focus became raising her family, and following her children and grandchildren in all of their events and activities. Paulette enjoyed time at Pine Lake, Minnesota, her flower gardens, shopping for flowers and crafting. She later used her creativity, working a part-time position at Ballons by D. Paulette is survived by her children, Stephen (Jennifer) Stenseth, Thomas (Tracy) Stenseth, Mitchell (Kim) Stenseth, and Kirissa (Larry) Iverson, all of Grand Forks; 8 grandchildren, Ashley (Andy), Brenton, Jeremy, Haleigh, Brandon, Cole, Karley, and Morgan; great-granddaughter, Ellason; a brother, Tom Bossoletti, Grand Forks; sister, Mary Jo Bossoletti, Cape Cod, MA. She was preceded in death by her husband David, who died July 11, 2005, an infant son, Michael David, her parents, and a brother, John. Mass of Christian Burial: 10:30 a.m. Wednesday, September 2, 2015 in St. Michael's Catholic Church, 520 N. 5th St. Grand Forks. Visitation: will be from 5 to 7 p.m. with a 7:00 p.m. Prayer Service Tuesday, September 1, 2015 in Amundson Funeral Home, 2975 S. 42nd St., Grand Forks and one hour prior to the service in the church. Interment will be in Calvary North Cemetery, Grand Forks.
