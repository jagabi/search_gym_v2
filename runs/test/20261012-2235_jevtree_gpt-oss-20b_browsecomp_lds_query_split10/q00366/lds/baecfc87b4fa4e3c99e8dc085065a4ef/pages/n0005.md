_**Untold: The Rise and Fall of AND1**_ is a 2022 American [Netflix original](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lists_of_Netflix_original_films "Lists of Netflix original films")[documentary film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Documentary_film "Documentary film") directed by [Kevin Wilson Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Wilson_Jr. "Kevin Wilson Jr.")[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Untold:_The_Rise_and_Fall_of_AND1#cite_note-1)

## Summary

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Untold:_The_Rise_and_Fall_of_AND1&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Summary")]

The film is the seventh installment in the nine-part [_Untold_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Untold_(Netflix) "Untold (Netflix)") documentary film series. Its story follows the rise and fall of the American sportswear brand [AND1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AND1 "AND1").[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Untold:_The_Rise_and_Fall_of_AND1#cite_note-2) It was released on August 23, 2022.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Untold:_The_Rise_and_Fall_of_AND1#cite_note-3)
