# About Khalhon

Khalhon reframes golf as culture, not performance gear. Founded in Seoul, the brand makes apparel that belongs equally at the 9th hole, the gallery opening, and the after-party — designed for the late-twenty- and thirty-something creative professional who treats the fairway as just another place to dress with intention.

The collections sit at the intersection of sport, streetwear, and quiet luxury — clean silhouettes, considered materials, and a refusal to lean on tradition for credibility. We work with collaborators who share that sensibility, most recently Sean Wotherspoon of Round Two on a capsule that ties the brand’s range together.

Designed in Seoul. Worn worldwide.

## Designed in Seoul, worn worldwide.

The brand grew out of Seoul’s golf scene in the mid-2020s. Every piece still starts at a desk in Hannam-dong, then moves through specialty retail and direct customers across North America, Asia, and Europe.
