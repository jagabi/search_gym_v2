## Best Serious Player Only Midsoles

All of Serious Player Only’s shoes feature **drop-in midsole cushioning setups.**The**Player 1 and the Player 1 Plus feature a triple-density foam setup that combines an Evanlon foam carrier,**which is a basic EVA foam similar to Lunarlon, **with forefoot and heel units of Serious Player Only’s Pocon and Gum foams**. This is the “Strong” drop-in. The Player 1 Plus comes with the same midsole, but it also comes with an additional midsole as well.

The second**“Quick” midsole is constructed with just Evanlon**and is an even more low profile and reactive setup. It is also the only midsole that comes with the Game 1. The Game 1 also has a thin traditional EVA midsole integrated into the “shell” of the shoe for added cushioning and comfort.

**SPO Quick Drop-in Midsole**

The Quick 2.0 midsole is an**ATPU foam-based cushioning system**. Compared to the original Quick midsole, this one is bouncier, more responsive, and significantly more durable. What makes ATPU foam special? It’s a**supercritical foaming process using clean CO2 technology**, which not only enhances energy return but also makes the material more eco-friendly. Unlike traditional EVA setups, this midsole doesn’t break down as quickly, ensuring that your ride remains consistent even after months of use. If you’ve tried SPO’s Explosive midsole from previous models, you’ll find that Quick 2.0 offers a more balanced feel—plush without being overly soft, and responsive without feeling too firm. It’s a great middle-ground for guards and wings who need**impact protection**without sacrificing court feel. It’s our favorite SPO midsole to date. _Price: $60_
