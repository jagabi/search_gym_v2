## Boniface KIPROP - Uganda - 4th in the 10,000m at Olympic Games & World Championships.

**Boniface is pictured above (red vest) during the final of the men's 10,000m at the**

**2005 World Championships in Helsinki. Kiprop finished just outside the medals in**

**fourth place, as indeed he did at the previous years Olympics, in a seasons best time**

**of 27:10.98. Kenenisa Bekele (262) won the gold, Sileshi Sihine (274) the silver and**

**Moses Mosop (the athlete behind Boniface) the bronze.**
