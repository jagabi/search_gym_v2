### Did you know?

In the days when female competitors had to undergo gender verification, only one woman was ever exempted from that. At the 1976 Montreal Olympics, [HRH Princess Anne](https://www.olympedia.org/athletes/11440), who competed in equestrian, was not required to undergo testing.
