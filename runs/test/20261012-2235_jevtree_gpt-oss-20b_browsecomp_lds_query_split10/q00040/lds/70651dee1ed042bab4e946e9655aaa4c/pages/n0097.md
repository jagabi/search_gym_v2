### Did you know?

After [Toni Sailer](https://www.olympedia.org/athletes/97080), [Jean-Claude Killy](https://www.olympedia.org/athletes/87932) became the second person to win all three alpine skiing events in a single Olympics. His victory in the slalom was controversial, as [Karl Schranz](https://www.olympedia.org/athletes/97312) was disqualified under mysterious circumstances.
