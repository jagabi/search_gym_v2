**Berhane Adere Debala** ([Ge'ez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ge'ez_alphabet "Ge'ez alphabet"): ብርሀኔ አደሬ born 21 July 1973)[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berhane_Adere#cite_note-WAprofile-1) is a retired Ethiopian [long-distance runner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long-distance_runner "Long-distance runner") who specialised in the [10,000 metres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10,000_metres "10,000 metres") and [half marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Half_marathon "Half marathon"). She won the gold medal in the 10,000 m at the [2003 World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_World_Championships_in_Athletics "2003 World Championships in Athletics") and silver medals in the event at the [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_World_Championships_in_Athletics "2001 World Championships in Athletics") and [2005 World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_World_Championships_in_Athletics "2005 World Championships in Athletics"). Berhane claimed gold and silver for the [3,000 metres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3000_metres "3000 metres") at the [2003](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_IAAF_World_Indoor_Championships "2003 IAAF World Indoor Championships") and [2004 World Indoor Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_IAAF_World_Indoor_Championships "2004 IAAF World Indoor Championships"), respectively. Her medal in 2003 was Ethiopia's first world indoor medal in a women's event.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berhane_Adere#cite_note-WAbio-2) At the half marathon, she was the world champion in [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships "2002 IAAF World Half Marathon Championships"), took silver in [2003](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships "2003 IAAF World Half Marathon Championships") and bronze in [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships "2001 IAAF World Half Marathon Championships"). She won the [Chicago Marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Marathon "Chicago Marathon") in [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Chicago_Marathon "2006 Chicago Marathon") and [2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Chicago_Marathon "2007 Chicago Marathon").

Berhane held the world indoor record for the 3,000 m, set in 2002, which was the first sub-8:30 mark run by a woman indoors and the first world indoor record in a distance event by an Ethiopian woman. She also held the world indoor record in the 5,000 m, set in 2004.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berhane_Adere#cite_note-WAbio-2)

Berhane works for [UNICEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNICEF "UNICEF") as a [goodwill ambassador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodwill_ambassador "Goodwill ambassador") for [girls' education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girls'_education "Girls' education").

## Career

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Berhane_Adere&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Career")]

Berhane held the African record for the [10,000 metres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10,000_metres "10,000 metres") in a time of 30:04.18, set at the [2003 World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_World_Championships_in_Athletics "2003 World Championships in Athletics"), where she won gold. Her record was broken at the [2008 Beijing Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletics_at_the_2008_Summer_Olympics "Athletics at the 2008 Summer Olympics") by [Tirunesh Dibaba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tirunesh_Dibaba "Tirunesh Dibaba"), who became the first African woman to run under 30 minutes in the event.[_[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation\_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")_]

She won the [2006 Chicago Marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Chicago_Marathon "2006 Chicago Marathon") with a personal best time of 2:20:42. She won the [Chicago Marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Marathon "Chicago Marathon") again in [2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Chicago_Marathon "2007 Chicago Marathon")[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berhane_Adere#cite_note-3) and the [Dubai Marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubai_Marathon "Dubai Marathon") on 18 January 2008.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berhane_Adere#cite_note-4) Berhane also claimed victory in the 2007 [Ras Al Khaimah Half Marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ras_Al_Khaimah_Half_Marathon "Ras Al Khaimah Half Marathon").[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berhane_Adere#cite_note-WAprofile-1)

She was the top female finisher in the 2010 [Rock ‘n’ Roll Mardi Gras Marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_%E2%80%98n%E2%80%99_Roll_Mardi_Gras_Marathon "Rock ‘n’ Roll Mardi Gras Marathon"), in which she recorded the fastest ever [half marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Half_marathon "Half marathon") on American soil with a time of 1:07:52, breaking her previous best by over 25 seconds.[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berhane_Adere#cite_note-5) At age 36, that should also be the Masters world record.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berhane_Adere#cite_note-6) She saw off a challenge from [Ana Dulce Félix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana_Dulce_F%C3%A9lix "Ana Dulce Félix") to win at the [Great North Run](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_North_Run "Great North Run") in September, completing the half marathon in 1:08:49.[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berhane_Adere#cite_note-7) In 2012, she had two races, both in October in Britain, where she was runner-up at the [Great Birmingham Run](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Birmingham_Run "Great Birmingham Run") and third at the [Great South Run](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_South_Run "Great South Run").[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berhane_Adere#cite_note-8)
