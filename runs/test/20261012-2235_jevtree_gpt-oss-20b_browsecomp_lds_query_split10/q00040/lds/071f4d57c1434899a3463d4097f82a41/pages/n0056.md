Biennial international road running competition

The **World Athletics Road Running Championships** is an international [road running](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_running "Road running") competition organized by [World Athletics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Athletics "World Athletics"). The current format features three distances: the [half marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Half_marathon "Half marathon"), [5k](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5K_run "5K run"), and [mile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mile_run "Mile run"). The half marathon includes a team race, with athletes scoring for their national teams.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships#cite_note-1)

The name and format of the event have changed several times over its history. From 1992 to 2010, the championships were held annually with the half marathon as the sole race distance.[[a]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships#cite_note-2) From 2010 to 2020 the championships were held biennially. In 2023 the 5k and mile distances were introduced. The [coronavirus pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic "COVID-19 pandemic") and funding issues resulted in the cancellation of the 2022[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships#cite_note-3) and 2025[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships#cite_note-4) editions, respectively. The most recent edition was held in [Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_World_Athletics_Road_Running_Championships "2026 World Athletics Road Running Championships") in September 2026.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships#cite_note-5)

The competition replaced the female-only [IAAF World Women's Road Race Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAAF_World_Women's_Road_Race_Championships "IAAF World Women's Road Race Championships"), which was held annually from 1983 to 1991.

## History

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=World_Athletics_Road_Running_Championships&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: History")]

The IAAF World Half Marathon Championships was first held in 1992. It comprised three races: the men's race, the women's race and the junior men's race. Furthermore, a team competition was held in each category, with the winners being decided by combining the performances of a country's top three finishers. The country with the lowest aggregate time won the team competition. The junior men's race was held in only the first and second editions, and was removed from the programme from 1994 onwards.

The competition went largely unchanged until 2006, when the competition was renamed as the **IAAF World Road Running Championships**. Aside from the name change, the significant difference was the distance of the race, changing from a [half marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Half_marathon "Half marathon") to a [20 kilometres road race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/20K_run "20K run"). The 20 km race featured only at the [2006 edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_IAAF_World_Road_Running_Championships "2006 IAAF World Road Running Championships"), and the half marathon distance returned for the [2007 World Road Running Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_IAAF_World_Road_Running_Championships "2007 IAAF World Road Running Championships").

On 29 November 2007, the IAAF announced that the name of the competition would revert to its original title of the IAAF World Half Marathon Championships, beginning with the [2008 IAAF World Half Marathon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships "2008 IAAF World Half Marathon Championships") in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro "Rio de Janeiro"), Brazil on 12 October 2008.[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships#cite_note-11)[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships#cite_note-12)

This competition is not to be confused with the [IAAF World Women's Road Race Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAAF_World_Women's_Road_Race_Championships "IAAF World Women's Road Race Championships") which were run from 1983 to 1991, or the [IAAF World Road Relay Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAAF_World_Road_Relay_Championships "IAAF World Road Relay Championships") which took place between 1992 and 1998.

The 2020 edition of the competition was postponed due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic "COVID-19 pandemic").[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships#cite_note-13) Although it was held later that year, many countries, including Australia, Canada, Japan, New Zealand, and the U.S., declined to participate.[[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships#cite_note-2022.postponement.2-14)

Similarly, the 2022 edition of the competition was postponed twice before being cancelled due to the pandemic.[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships#cite_note-2022.cancellation-15)[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships#cite_note-2022.postponement.1-16)[[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships#cite_note-2022.postponement.2-14) As a result of the cancellation, the World Athletics Council decided to award the 2027 World Athletics Road Running Championships to Yangzhou, the city originally scheduled to host the 2022 competition.[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships#cite_note-2022.cancellation-15)

## Competition format

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=World_Athletics_Road_Running_Championships&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Competition format")]

The competition is generally held every October. Runners compete on public roads which have been closed off to traffic specifically for the event. Prize money varies from [US$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US$ "US$")30,000 to US$3000.[_[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation\_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")_]
