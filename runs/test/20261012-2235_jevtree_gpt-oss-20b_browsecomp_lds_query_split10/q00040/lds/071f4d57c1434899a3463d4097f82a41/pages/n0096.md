**Maria Conceição da Costa Ferreira** (born 13 March 1962 in [Aveleda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aveleda_(Braga) "Aveleda (Braga)")) is a retired [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portugal "Portugal") long-distance runner.

She won the [1993 IAAF World Half Marathon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships "1993 IAAF World Half Marathon Championships"), and finished sixth in [10,000 metres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10,000_metres "10,000 metres") at the [1993 World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_World_Championships_in_Athletics "1993 World Championships in Athletics").
