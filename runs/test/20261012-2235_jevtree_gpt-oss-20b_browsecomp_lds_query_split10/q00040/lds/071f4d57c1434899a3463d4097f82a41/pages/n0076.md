The **2nd [IAAF World Half Marathon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships "IAAF World Half Marathon Championships")** was held on October 3, 1993, in [Brussels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brussels "Brussels"), [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium "Belgium"). A total of 254 athletes, 133 men, 86 women and 35 juniors, from 49 countries took part. Complete results were published.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships#cite_note-iaaf_01-1)
