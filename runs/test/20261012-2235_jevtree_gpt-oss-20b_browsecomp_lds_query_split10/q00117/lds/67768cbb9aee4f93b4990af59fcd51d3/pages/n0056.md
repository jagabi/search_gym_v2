Great news for internet researchers is the new[https://web-beta.archive.org](https://web-beta.archive.org/) You are now able to battle “Error 404” or “Page not Found” better than ever. Please check the seven tips I have for you. Share this with fellow researchers and add comments for any other findings.

1.   **Less guessing**

Hardest part of [@internetarchive](http://twitter.com/internetarchive) was to predict what the link is of a website that is gone. Now you can just type and pick your candidate(s).

Press enter or click to view image in full size

![Image 3](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*zrtoctP__qsSMg9pVc05yQ.jpeg)

**2. Use a wildcard**

With (name *) you can see different variations of a website name in [@internetarchive](http://twitter.com/internetarchive) now , as shown here in pic.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

![Image 4](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*EHrIqB5Wj-uFwfbju5ygAg.jpeg)

**3. Search for names**

Type a name of a person in new [@internetarchive](http://twitter.com/internetarchive) to get related websites incl. hate sites [http://web-beta.archive.org/#/search/”Hillary%20Clinton”](http://web-beta.archive.org/#/search/%E2%80%9DHillary%20Clinton%E2%80%9D) Now watch carefully. @internetarchive says it doesn’t support keyword searching yet, but it DOES find sites about Clinton that don’t have Clinton in it, like [againsthillary.com](http://againsthillary.com/)

Press enter or click to view image in full size

![Image 5](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*_CMT3ot396pxLt4f_n7XeQ.jpeg)

**4. Physical addresses**

Again, Internet Archive says it doesn’t allow keyword search yet. Strangely enough, I was able to type in**real, physical addresses**and got a website with that address in it, as shown in the example. That is extremely powerful stuff I think. Type in a REAL, physical address in new [@internetarchive](http://twitter.com/internetarchive) yourself and let me know if it works as good as it worked for me.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

![Image 6](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*aPPcifPprGqBYSO1TmDE7w.jpeg)

**7. Do the Site: Search**

Perphaps you know that in Google, with site:int you only search for sites that have to do with bilateral and multilateral agreements. Suddenly I can find out which organisations according to that int-definition should be knowledgable. In other words: you can use @internetarchive to get current insights. Great!

**Key Features**

Predict names or part of names of websites  
It’s not just an archive, you can find stuff that is relevant now  
Better video search  
Some keyword searches, especially addresses, seem to work although @internetarchive says it isn’t working
