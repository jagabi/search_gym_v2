### Vote in our Poll on what should happen with the Minister's Tree House

 We have posted a poll asking readers about what they would like to see happen to the Minister's Tree House on Beehive Lane in Crossville. It is also known as the World's Largest Tree House as well.  
  

[![Image 2](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEikyBpkyR4ElFl6C0pnm3_4hNnQP9NYbpRjDYiqWnZzaxPJ-m8QRiQLhXCr0trdq1NOqkaZXEzPDtaVWkU35437iZoAhGl_o_ah5ycxwLGj2fZyYL2nlv6wXmFSOBZtHMdxKvkYkEepFN8/s400/29060828_2037417443249928_5909960936649542067_o.jpg)](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEikyBpkyR4ElFl6C0pnm3_4hNnQP9NYbpRjDYiqWnZzaxPJ-m8QRiQLhXCr0trdq1NOqkaZXEzPDtaVWkU35437iZoAhGl_o_ah5ycxwLGj2fZyYL2nlv6wXmFSOBZtHMdxKvkYkEepFN8/s1600/29060828_2037417443249928_5909960936649542067_o.jpg)

**_Minister's Treehouse_**

  
 The property has been put up for sale and some have said on social media that the city and/or county should buy the property and fix the structure to allow visitors again. The property is listed at $1.5 million and it is not known what repair of the tree house would cost.
