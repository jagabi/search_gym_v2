## About

The Mountain House isn't just a restaurant; it's a dining experience tucked among the redwoods of Kings Mountain in Woodside California. A winding mountain road must be traveled to reach the destination nestled beneath some of California's tallest trees. The rustic cabin feels storybook-like, as if it were built for a family of gnomes rather than hungry diners. Inside, the windowed ceiling dining room shows towering redwoods offering a humbling reminder of just how small we are in the presence of nature.

Getting there is part of the adventure. No matter which direction visitors arrive from, the drive gradually transports them from the pace of everyday life to something slower, quieter, and more timeless. Between the forest, the atmosphere, and the setting, every meal comes with a reminder of just how beautiful Northern California can be.

The food is as special as the surroundings. The region's farm-to-table style is evident, and the ever-changing menu celebrates hyper-seasonal ingredients and inspired dishes. The proteins are thoughtfully sourced, and it's exciting to see menus featuring quail, venison, and trout, to name a few. Attentive service with immaculate attention to detail elevates the experience, creating a meal that lingers in your memory long after the drive back down the mountain.
