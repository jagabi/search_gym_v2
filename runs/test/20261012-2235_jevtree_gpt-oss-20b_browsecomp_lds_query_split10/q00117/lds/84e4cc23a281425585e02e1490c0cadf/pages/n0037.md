The Ketch Agent Network is the orchestration layer that powers every product in the Ketch platform, continuously gathering the context privacy teams need instead of chasing by hand: regulations, policies, contracts, data repositories, collection points, access controls, and Ketch configuration.

[Explore Ketch Agent Network](https://www.ketch.com/platform/agent-network)

Laws, enforcement actions, and settlement terms — synthesized as they land, mapped to where you operate. The regulatory baseline every decision gets measured against.

The version of your program that exists on paper. Policies, compliance documentation, and stated commitments — what you've told the world you do with data.

### Operational reality

Live activity across your applications, code, configurations, and data systems. The reality layer — what's collecting, what's flowing, what's firing, and what's leaving — whether anyone documented it or not.
