# Law Commission – Homepage

## **Reforming the law**

The Law Commission is an independent organisation. We were created in 1965 to keep the law of England and Wales under review and recommend reform where needed.

Find out more [about us.](https://lawcom.gov.uk/about-us/)

## Current projects

![Image 2](https://cdn.websitebuilder.service.justice.gov.uk/uploads/sites/54/2025/01/people-coffee-meeting-team-7096-scaled.jpg)

Our law reform work focuses on changes that deliver real benefits to people, organisations and institutions.

Take a look at the wide range of [projects we’re working on currently](https://lawcom.gov.uk/current-projects/).

### Latest projects

[Accessibility of transport for disabled people](https://lawcom.gov.uk/project/accessibility-of-transport-for-disabled-people/)

This review will consider the laws governing the accessibility of transport for disabled people in England and Wales and the wider frameworks in which they are contained.

[View project](https://lawcom.gov.uk/project/accessibility-of-transport-for-disabled-people/)

[Contempt of court](https://lawcom.gov.uk/project/contempt-of-court/)

The Law Commission has been asked by the Government to review the law on contempt of court and consider reform to improve its effectiveness, consistency and coherence.

[View project](https://lawcom.gov.uk/project/contempt-of-court/)

[Disabled children’s social care](https://lawcom.gov.uk/project/disabled-childrens-social-care/)

The Law Commission has completed its review of disabled children’s social care law and published its final report.

[View project](https://lawcom.gov.uk/project/disabled-childrens-social-care/)

[View all projects](https://lawcom.gov.uk/projects/)
