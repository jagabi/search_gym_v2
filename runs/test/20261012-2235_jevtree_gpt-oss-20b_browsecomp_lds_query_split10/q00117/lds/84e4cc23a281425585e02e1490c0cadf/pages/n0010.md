# Fort Cherry High School prom in jeopardy following Noah's Event Venue closure

## The school was supposed to hold their prom at Noah's in May, but the abrupt closure left them out of thousands of dollars.

**CANONSBURG, Pa. —**
Students at Fort Cherry High School are scrambling to find another location for their prom after [the sudden and abrupt closure of their venue](https://www.wtae.com/article/two-local-event-centers-wedding-venues-abruptly-close-forcing-couples-to-change-plans/30818464).

The school is the latest victim of the closure of Noah's Event Venue after they filed for bankruptcy. The school's prom committee, which has about 20 students, raised thousands of dollars to hold their big event on May 15.

Advertisement

"We've been fundraising since summer," said Katie Nemec, prom committee member. "This was supposed to be our biggest prom ever and now we have none of it."

Nemec said they signed a $5700 contract in 2019 to hold their prom at the venue on May 15.

"We gave them over $3,000 which may not seem like a lot to other people, but for us, a small rural school, that's all we have basically," she said.

The venue has more than 30 locations across the country, and the closure has canceled about 2,800 events at 28 venues across the county at a cost of more than $7 million, according to the company's bankruptcy attorney.

Pittsburgh's Action News 4 spoke with Noah's bankruptcy attorney, Ken Cannon. He said the company filed for bankruptcy last year. On Friday, a bankruptcy judge ruled that the company should not be marketing for future events or accepting payments. The judge did not believe Noah's had a viable reorganization plan.

[![Image 27: Pittsburgh-area venues offering to help after sudden closure of two wedding venues ](https://kubrick.htvapps.com/htv-prod-media.s3.amazonaws.com/images/vlcsnap-2020-02-07-21h00m16s554-1581183661.png?crop=1.00xw%3A1.00xh%3B0%2C0&resize=136%3A*) LIST: Western Pennsylvania venues offering to help after sudden closure of two wedding venues](https://www.wtae.com/article/list-local-venues-offering-to-help-after-sudden-closure-of-two-wedding-venues/30823769)

Nemec said the last contact they had with Noah's was on Thursday, and there was no indication there were any problems. She found out they shut down from other prom committee members on Saturday, and never heard a word from Noah's.

"They deposited our checks this morning, and we have no money and we have no prom," she said.

Pittsburgh's Action News 4 reached out to Noah's multiple times for comment but hasn't heard back.

Nemec said they're starting a GoFundMe page, and the community, several venues and vendors have reached out to help.

"At this point, we have nothing," she said. "So we can't even really start to look at other venues or switch in and around the dates or anything because we have no money for a down payment. So we can't do anything at this point.

"I'm extremely disappointed about it," she said. "I'm trying to stay hopeful because, you know, we're still going to have a prom. We're going to have our senior prom. But it's not everything we had hoped for and everything we had been planning for."
