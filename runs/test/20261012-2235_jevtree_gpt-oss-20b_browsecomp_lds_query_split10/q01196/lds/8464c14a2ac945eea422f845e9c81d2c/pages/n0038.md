## Sveriges Riksbank Prize in Economics Sciences in Memory of Alfred Nobel 2026

### [Microfoundations deliver macro insights](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2026/press-release/)

Economic development is characterised by alternating downturns and upturns. Stimulating or slowing down the economy at the right time can provide stability and allow us to avoid drawn-out recessions or runaway inflation. Robert E. Hall’s and Michael Woodford’s research has generated a paradigm shift in stabilisation policy. New Keynesian models, a result of their research, allow us to explain and quantify how stabilisation policy affects business cycles and are now used by central banks and finance ministries around the world.

[Press release](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2026/press-release/)[Popular information: Microfoundations deliver macro insights](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economics/2026/popular-information/)[Scientific background: Microeconomic foundations of macroeconomic stabilization](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economics/2026/advanced-information/)

### [The 2026 economic sciences laureates](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2026/summary/)

The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences has decided to award the Sveriges Riksbank Prize in Economic Sciences in Memory of Alfred Nobel 2026 to [Robert E. Hall](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economics/2026/hall/facts/) and [Michael Woodford](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2026/woodford/facts/) “for microeconomic foundations of macroeconomic stabilisation.”

They demonstrated how economic models could be reconstructed to revitalise stabilisation policy. Rather than basing the models on statistical links between macroeconomic variables, such as inflation, production and consumption, they chose to build them on a microeconomic foundation. This encompasses an understanding for how the total mass of individual households and businesses affect the economy through their actions.

## Nobel Peace Prize 2026

### [Peace requires justice](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/peace/2026/press-release/)

At a time of existential challenges – including more wars and conflicts than the world has seen in a long time – international law is no longer just a supplement to peace and security. It is an absolute necessity.

In historic international court cases, Navi Pillay has shown that legal measures can help prevent acts of war and violence. She has also contributed to strengthen the institutions of international law. Her independence, expertise and steadfastness have made her one of the most respected international jurists of our time.

## Nobel Prize in Literature 2026

### [Anne Carson](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/literature/2026/summary/)

The Nobel Prize in Literature 2026 has been awarded to the Canadian author [Anne Carson](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/literature/2026/carson/facts/) “for her bold and inventive oeuvre that, in playful dialogue with the classical tradition, has created new forms for contemporary literature.”

Anne Carson has, throughout her entire career, combined poetry writing with translation work and teaching at various universities. Almost all her literary production has centred upon the intersection between the philologist’s obsession with words and the poet’s desire for new meaning. She has herself identified the etymology of words as the starting point for all her work.

## Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2026

### [They made chemistry choose a mirror image](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/2026/press-release/)

Some molecules, such as amino acids, exist as two variants that are each other’s mirror image. However, living organisms only contain one of these mirror images. How chemical asymmetry such as this could have emerged was long a mystery to chemists. Henri B. Kagan and Kenso Soai have been awarded the Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2026 for discovering a solution to this puzzle.

## Nobel Prize in Physics 2026

### [Elusive particles from the universe captured at the South Pole](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2026/press-release/)

Francis Halzen realised that ice at the South Pole could be used to track particles known as neutrinos. His vision and scientific leadership have been fundamental for the IceCube Neutrino Observatory – a cubic kilometre of ice that is equipped with light sensors. Using IceCube, researchers can capture neutrinos from extremely energy-rich processes in the distant universe.

## Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine 2026

### [Light-seeking algae gave us a switch for nerve cells](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/medicine/2026/press-release/)

To understand how the brain forms memories, feelings and behaviours has long been a dream for researchers. Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel have been awarded the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for discoveries leading to optogenetics, which makes it possible to switch on, or off, the activity of individual nerve cells in a living brain. This method is now being used in laboratories around the world to reveal the brain’s mysteries.

[Press release](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/medicine/2026/press-release/)[Popular information: A light-sensitive algal protein energised neuroscience](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/medicine/2026/popular-information/)

# [The man behind the prize In his last will, Alfred Nobel specified that the bulk of his fortune should be used for prizes to “those who, during the preceding year, shall have conferred the greatest benefit to humankind.”](https://www.nobelprize.org/alfred-nobel)

### [A home for the unique legacy of Alfred Nobel](https://www.nobelprize.org/nobel-center/)

The first design of the Nobel Center project is here – a public building in Stockholm, Sweden for science, literature and peace. It will be an international symbol of knowledge, focusing on the Nobel Prize laureates’ stories and groundbreaking achievements. Read more about the project.

[![Image 74: Susumu Tonegawa](blob:http://localhost/aef335eb562a1da6957ce25a574b4aa1)](https://www.nobelprize.org/nobel-center/)

View from the water along the quay. The Nobel Center consists of four interconnected volumes that relate to the buildings of Södermalm and the size and height of the buildings in Gamla stan, on the other side of the water. The choice to use a wooden frame and reclaimed bricks reduces the impact on the environment. Bricks have characterised the architecture of Stockholm for centuries. The red shade is found in Stockholm’s classic brick buildings, such as City Hall.
