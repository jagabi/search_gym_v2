# Wikimedia Projects

The Wikimedia Foundation supports hundreds of thousands of people around the world in creating the largest free knowledge projects in history.The work of volunteers helps millions of people around the globe discover information, contribute knowledge, and share it with others no matter their bandwidth.

## Reference

### Wikipedia

**All the world’s knowledge**

[Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org/)is a free, collaborative encyclopedia written in over 300 languages by volunteers around the world.

### Wikibooks

**E-book textbooks and annotated texts**

[Wikibooks](https://www.wikibooks.org/)aims to build a collection of free e-book resources including textbooks, annotated texts, instructional guides, and manuals.

### Wiktionary

**A dictionary for over 170 languages**

[Wiktionary](https://www.wiktionary.org/)is a free multilingual dictionary. The project aims to describe all words of all languages. It includes language resources such as a thesaurus, rhyme guide, and language statistics.

### Wikiquote

**Find quotes across your favorite books, movies, authors and more**

[Wikiquote](https://www.wikiquote.org/)is an online collection of sourced quotations from notable people and creative works in over 75 languages. It also includes proverbs, mnemonics and slogans.

## Collections

### Wikimedia Commons

**114 million images, photographs, videos and music files and counting**

[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Main_Page)is the world’s largest free-to-use library of illustrations, photos, drawings, videos, and music.

### Wikisource

**The free library**

[Wikisource](https://wikisource.org/)is a library of freely-licensed source texts and historical documents, including poetry, government documents, constitutions of many countries, and general literature.

### Wikiversity

**Access learning resources, projects and research at any level of study**

[Wikiversity](https://www.wikiversity.org/)is devoted to learning resources, learning projects, and research for use in all levels, types, and styles of education.

### Wikispecies

**The free species directory**

[Wikispecies](https://species.wikimedia.org/)is a species database for taxonomy that covers living and fossil representatives of Animalia, Plantae, Fungi, Bacteria, Archaea, Protista and all other forms of life.

## Technology

### Wikidata

**The database of structured information collaboratively edited**

[Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Main_Page)acts as central storage of structured data for Wikimedia projects. Structuring data into a machine-readable format makes it easier to view, search, edit, curate, use and re-use files.

### Wikifunctions

**The library of code functions anyone can edit**

[Wikifunctions](https://www.wikifunctions.org/)allows everyone to collaboratively create and maintain code functions that can be used to answer questions, such as how many days have passed between two dates, or the distance between two cities.

### MediaWiki

**The software platform that makes Wikipedia possible**

[MediaWiki](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/MediaWiki)is free and open source wiki software that anyone can use and develop. It is the platform on which Wikimedia projects are built.

## Guides

### Wikivoyage

**The ultimate travel guide**

[Wikivoyage](https://www.wikivoyage.org/)aims to create the world’s largest free, complete and up-to-date worldwide travel guide.

## Collaboration

### Meta-Wiki

**Project coordination software tool for global collaboration**

[Meta-Wiki](https://meta.wikimedia.org/)is a project used as a central hub for various coordination and organization tasks, such as discussions affecting multiple wikis or planning upcoming events.
