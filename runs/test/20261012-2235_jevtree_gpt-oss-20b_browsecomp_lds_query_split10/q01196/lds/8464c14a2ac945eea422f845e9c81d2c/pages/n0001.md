**Clockwise from top to bottom left-right:**
*   A protester at [Occupy Wall Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupy_Wall_Street "Occupy Wall Street"), which begins the [Occupy movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupy_movement "Occupy movement");
*   Protests against Libyan dictator [Muammar Gaddafi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muammar_Gaddafi "Muammar Gaddafi"), [killed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassination_of_Muammar_Gaddafi "Assassination of Muammar Gaddafi") in October;
*   Celebrating the [independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_South_Sudanese_independence_referendum "2011 South Sudanese independence referendum") of [South Sudan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Sudan "South Sudan"), the world's newest country;
*   [Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_T%C5%8Dhoku_earthquake_and_tsunami "2011 Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami") devastates Eastern Japan and kills nearly 20,000 people while initiating a [major nuclear accident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fukushima_nuclear_accident "Fukushima nuclear accident"), becoming the most expensive natural disaster on record;
*   [Attacks in Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Norway_attacks "2011 Norway attacks") kill 77 and mark the rise of [Right-wing terrorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right-wing_terrorism "Right-wing terrorism") across the west;
*   U.S. national security team gathered in the [White House Situation Room](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_House_Situation_Room "White House Situation Room") to monitor progress of [Operation Neptune Spear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Neptune_Spear "Operation Neptune Spear"), resulting in the death of [Al-Qaeda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Qaeda "Al-Qaeda") leader [Osama bin Laden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osama_bin_Laden "Osama bin Laden");
*   A [drought in East Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_East_Africa_drought "2011 East Africa drought") kills 50,000–260,000 people;
*   A block from _[Minecraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minecraft "Minecraft")_, released this year, which later becomes the world's [best-selling video game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_best-selling_video_games "List of best-selling video games");
*   Anti-government protests known as the "[Arab Spring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_Spring "Arab Spring")" spread across the [Middle East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_East "Middle East") and [North Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Africa "North Africa"), sparking revolutions in [Tunisia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunisian_revolution "Tunisian revolution"), [Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Egyptian_revolution "2011 Egyptian revolution"), [Libya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libyan_civil_war_(2011) "Libyan civil war (2011)"), [Yemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemeni_revolution "Yemeni revolution"), and [Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_revolution "Syrian revolution").

**2011** ([MMXI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_numerals "Roman numerals")) was a [common year starting on Saturday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_year_starting_on_Saturday "Common year starting on Saturday") of the [Gregorian calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregorian_calendar "Gregorian calendar"), the 2011th year of the [Common Era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Era "Common Era") (CE) and _[Anno Domini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anno\_Domini "Anno Domini")_ (AD) designations, the 11th year of the [3rd millennium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3rd_millennium "3rd millennium")and the [21st century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21st_century "21st century"), and the 2nd year of the [2010s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010s "2010s") decade.

Calendar year

The year marked the start of [a series of protests and revolutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_Spring "Arab Spring") throughout the Arab world advocating for democracy, reform, and economic recovery, later leading to the depositions of world leaders in [Tunisia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunisian_revolution "Tunisian revolution"), [Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Egyptian_revolution "2011 Egyptian revolution"), and [Yemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemeni_revolution "Yemeni revolution"), and in some cases sparking civil wars such as the [Syrian civil war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_civil_war "Syrian civil war") and the [first Libyan civil war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libyan_civil_war_(2011) "Libyan civil war (2011)"), the latter giving way to the [second Libyan civil war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libyan_civil_war_(2014%E2%80%932020) "Libyan civil war (2014–2020)"). The year also saw the rise of the [Occupy movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupy_movement "Occupy movement"), including [Occupy Wall Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupy_Wall_Street "Occupy Wall Street"), a protest movement against [social](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_equality "Social equality") and [economic inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_inequality "Economic inequality"), corporate influence, and the financial system that [spread across numerous countries worldwide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Occupy_movement_protest_locations "List of Occupy movement protest locations").

U.S. Navy SEALs [killed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_Osama_bin_Laden "Killing of Osama bin Laden") al-Qaeda leader and terrorist [Osama bin Laden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osama_bin_Laden "Osama bin Laden") in his [compound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osama_bin_Laden's_compound_in_Abbottabad "Osama bin Laden's compound in Abbottabad") in Pakistan on May 2. The [Curiosity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curiosity_(rover) "Curiosity (rover)") rover, which was to land on Mars in August of the following year, launched from Cape Canaveral on November 26. In December, North Korean leader [Kim Jong Il](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Jong_Il "Kim Jong Il"), who had been the supreme leader of North Korea since the death of his father [Kim Il Sung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Il_Sung "Kim Il Sung") in 1994, [died](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_and_state_funeral_of_Kim_Jong_Il "Death and state funeral of Kim Jong Il") while traveling by train to a place outside Pyongyang. He was succeeded by his son [Kim Jong Un](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Jong_Un "Kim Jong Un").

2011 was designated as:

*   [International Year of Forests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Year_of_Forests "International Year of Forests")
*   [International Year of Chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Year_of_Chemistry "International Year of Chemistry")[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011#cite_note-1)
*   [International Year for People of African Descent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Year_for_People_of_African_Descent "International Year for People of African Descent")

In 2011, the nation of [Samoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samoa "Samoa") only had 364 days as it moved across the International Date Line skipping [December 30](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/December_30 "December 30"), 2011; it is now 24 hours ahead of American Samoa.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011#cite_note-2)[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011#cite_note-3)

*   [March 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_5 "March 5")– Egyptian protestors storm the [State Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Security_Investigations_Service "State Security Investigations Service") headquarters in the [Nasr City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasr_City "Nasr City") district of Cairo.

*   [March 11](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_11 "March 11")– A 9.1-magnitude [earthquake and subsequent tsunami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_T%C5%8Dhoku_earthquake_and_tsunami "2011 Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami") hit the east of Japan, killing 19,759 people and leaving another 2,553 missing. Tsunami warnings are issued in 50 countries and territories. [Emergencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fukushima_Daiichi_nuclear_disaster "Fukushima Daiichi nuclear disaster") are declared at four nuclear power plants affected by the quake.[[20]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011#cite_note-20) As a result of the March 11 earthquake and tsunami, multiple plants at the [Fukushima Daiichi Nuclear Power Plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fukushima_Daiichi_Nuclear_Power_Plant "Fukushima Daiichi Nuclear Power Plant") were damaged, several workers injured, and [contaminants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fukushima_nuclear_disaster#Releases_of_radioactive_contamination "Fukushima nuclear disaster") were released into the environment.

    *   Egypt’s [Ministry of Interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Interior_(Egypt) "Ministry of Interior (Egypt)") dissolves the widely feared and hated [State Security Investigations Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Security_Investigations_Service "State Security Investigations Service") accused of human rights abuses and announced the establishment of the new [National Security Agency will replace it and take over its internal security duties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_Agency_(Egypt) "National Security Agency (Egypt)").
    *   [Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamad_bin_Isa_Al_Khalifa "Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa"), [King of Bahrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_Bahrain "King of Bahrain"), declares a three-month [state of emergency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_emergency "State of emergency") as troops from the [Gulf Co-operation Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_Co-operation_Council "Gulf Co-operation Council") are sent to quell the [civil unrest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahraini_uprising_(2011%E2%80%93present) "Bahraini uprising (2011–present)").[[21]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011#cite_note-BBCBahr15Mar-21)[[22]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011#cite_note-22)
    *   [Protests breakout across Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_uprising_phase_of_the_Syrian_Civil_War "Civil uprising phase of the Syrian Civil War") demanding democratic reforms, resignation of President [Bashar al-Assad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bashar_al-Assad "Bashar al-Assad"), and release of those imprisoned for the March 6 Daraa protest.[[23]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011#cite_note-23) The government responds by killing hundreds of protesters and laying siege to various cities, beginning the [Syrian Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_Civil_War "Syrian Civil War"), which lasts until Assad’s overthrow in [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024 "2024").[[24]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011#cite_note-24)

*   [March 17](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_17 "March 17")– The [United Nations Security Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council "United Nations Security Council") votes 10–0 to create a [no-fly zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libyan_no-fly_zone "Libyan no-fly zone") over Libya in response to allegations of government aggression against civilians.[[25]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011#cite_note-25)
*   [March 19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_19 "March 19")– In light of continuing attacks on Libyan rebels by forces in support of leader [Muammar Gaddafi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muammar_Gaddafi "Muammar Gaddafi"),[[26]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011#cite_note-26)[military intervention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_military_intervention_in_Libya "2011 military intervention in Libya") authorized under [UNSCR 1973](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council_Resolution_1973 "United Nations Security Council Resolution 1973") begins as French fighter jets make reconnaissance flights over Libya.[[27]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011#cite_note-27)
