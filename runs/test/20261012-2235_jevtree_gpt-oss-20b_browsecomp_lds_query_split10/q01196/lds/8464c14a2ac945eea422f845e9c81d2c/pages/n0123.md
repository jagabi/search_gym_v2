# events & Museums

## Celebrating knowledge across the world

Our public events, held across the world, celebrate and share achievements awarded the Nobel Prize in science, literature and peace.

Our museums, situated in Stockholm and Oslo, invite visitors to engage with the Nobel Prize and its values through a world-class array of exhibitions, educational programmes, lectures and dialogues.

We create contexts that inspire people to think, act and challenge each other, and encourage cross disciplinary dialogue in issues that are crucial to our world and our future. We look forward to seeing you there.

## In brief

*   The [Nobel Prize Museum](https://nobelprizemuseum.se/) is in Stockholm, Sweden

*   The [Nobel Peace Center](https://www.nobelpeacecenter.org/en) is in Oslo, Norway

*   Our international events take place in person around the world and online

## Event concepts

### [Nobel Week Dialogue](https://www.nobelprize.org/nobel-week-dialogue/)

Laureates and thought leaders from various disciplines come together in Sweden during Nobel Week to discuss global issues.

[![Image 36: 2020 Nobel Week Dialogue](blob:http://localhost/aef335eb562a1da6957ce25a574b4aa1)](https://www.nobelprize.org/nobel-week-dialogue/)

### [Nobel Prize Dialogue](https://www.nobelprize.org/nobel-prize-dialogue/)

Nobel Prize laureates, scientists and policy makers discuss global issues in this series of conversations held internationally.

[![Image 38: 20220913_144_DSC00573.jpg](blob:http://localhost/aef335eb562a1da6957ce25a574b4aa1)](https://www.nobelprize.org/nobel-prize-dialogue/)

### [Nobel Prize lectures](https://www.nobelprize.org/streams-during-nobel-week-2024/)

The Nobel Prize laureates give lectures describing the history and background of their prize-awarded work.

[![Image 40](blob:http://localhost/aef335eb562a1da6957ce25a574b4aa1)](https://www.nobelprize.org/streams-during-nobel-week-2024/)

### [Nobel Prize Concert](https://www.nobelprize.org/nobel-prize-concert/)

Classical music featuring internationally-renowned musicians and conductors held annually to honour the year’s Nobel Prize laureates.

[![Image 42: Nobel Prize Concert 2025](blob:http://localhost/aef335eb562a1da6957ce25a574b4aa1)](https://www.nobelprize.org/nobel-prize-concert/)

### [Nobel Prize Inspiration Initiative](https://www.nobelprize.org/nobel-prize-inspiration-initiative/)

Laureates visit universities and research centres around the world to share their stories and connect with an audience of young scientists.

[![Image 44: 2018_NPII_Spain (13)](blob:http://localhost/aef335eb562a1da6957ce25a574b4aa1)](https://www.nobelprize.org/nobel-prize-inspiration-initiative/)

### [Nobel Prize Summit](https://www.nobelprize.org/events/nobel-prize-summit/2023/)

Nobel Prize laureates join youth from around the world for this three day event combining keynotes, discussion and performances.

[![Image 46: NPTS 042 _DSC8382](blob:http://localhost/aef335eb562a1da6957ce25a574b4aa1)](https://www.nobelprize.org/events/nobel-prize-summit/2023/)

### [Nobel Peace Prize Forum](https://www.nobelpeaceprize.org/research/nobel-peace-prize-forum/)

Issues of international peace and security are reflected upon at this interdisciplinary discussion during Nobel Week in Oslo.

[![Image 48](blob:http://localhost/aef335eb562a1da6957ce25a574b4aa1)](https://www.nobelpeaceprize.org/research/nobel-peace-prize-forum/)
