# Datasets

Pew Research Center makes the case-level microdata for much of its research available to the public for secondary analysis after a period of time. These datasets are listed below by collection date. A listing of our[American Trends Panel datasets](https://www.pewresearch.org/american-trends-panel-datasets/)and[religion datasets](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/religion-datasets/)are also available. See[this post](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2021/10/22/how-to-access-pew-research-center-survey-data/)for more information on how to use our datasets and contact us at[info@pewresearch.org](mailto:info@pewresearch.org)with any questions.

To download datasets, please[log in](https://www.pewresearch.org/profile/)or[create a free account](https://www.pewresearch.org/profile/registration/).
