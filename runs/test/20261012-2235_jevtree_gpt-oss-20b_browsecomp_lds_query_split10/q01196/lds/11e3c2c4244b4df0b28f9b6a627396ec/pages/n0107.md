FAQ'S

## [What are the voting and result dates for the 2026 Kerala Assembly elections?](https://www.indiatoday.in/elections/assembly/kerala/results#accordion-1)

According to the schedule announced by the Election Commission, polling in Kerala will be conducted in a single phase. Voting will take place on April 9, while the counting of votes and declaration of results will take place on May 4.

## [Where can the Kerala Assembly Election 2026 results be viewed?](https://www.indiatoday.in/elections/assembly/kerala/results#accordion-2)

The Kerala Assembly Election 2026 results will be available with live updates on the Election Commission of India (ECI)’s official website and on the India Today News platform.

## [Will the previous (2021) Kerala Assembly Election results also be available on this page?](https://www.indiatoday.in/elections/assembly/kerala/results#accordion-3)

Yes, for comparison, data and trends from the previous election will also be made available.

## [How does the result process work for the Kerala Assembly Election 2026?](https://www.indiatoday.in/elections/assembly/kerala/results#accordion-4)

Votes are counted in multiple rounds separately for each Assembly constituency.

## [What kind of data is available along with the election results?](https://www.indiatoday.in/elections/assembly/kerala/results#accordion-5)

Details include the winning candidates, their margin of victory, and the overall voter turnout percentage.

## [Who officially announces the election results?](https://www.indiatoday.in/elections/assembly/kerala/results#accordion-6)

The Election Commission of India.
