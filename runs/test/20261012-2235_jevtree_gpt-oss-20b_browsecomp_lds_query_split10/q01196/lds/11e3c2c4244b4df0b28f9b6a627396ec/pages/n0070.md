The [Lok Sabha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lok_Sabha "Lok Sabha"), the Lower House of the [Parliament of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_India "Parliament of India"), is made up of [Members of Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_Parliament,_Lok_Sabha "Member of Parliament, Lok Sabha") ([MPs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_Parliament_(India) "Member of Parliament (India)")). Each member of parliament represents a single geographic constituency.

The maximum size of the Lok Sabha as outlined in the [Constitution of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_India "Constitution of India") is 550 members, made up of up to 530 members representing people of [28 states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_India "States of India") and 20 members representing people of [8 union territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_territories_of_India "Union territories of India") based on their population. There are currently 543 constituencies in the Lok Sabha.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_constituencies_of_the_Lok_Sabha#cite_note-1)

## Delimitation of constituencies

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=List_of_constituencies_of_the_Lok_Sabha&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Delimitation of constituencies")]

Under the [Delimitation Act of 2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delimitation_Act_of_2002 "Delimitation Act of 2002"), the [Delimitation Commission of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delimitation_Commission_of_India "Delimitation Commission of India") has redefined the list of parliamentary constituencies, their constituent assembly segments and reservation status (whether reserved for [Scheduled castes (SC)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scheduled_castes_and_scheduled_tribes "Scheduled castes and scheduled tribes") candidates or [Scheduled tribes (ST)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scheduled_castes_and_scheduled_tribes "Scheduled castes and scheduled tribes") candidates or unreserved). [2008 Karnataka Legislative Assembly election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Karnataka_Legislative_Assembly_election "2008 Karnataka Legislative Assembly election"), which took place in May 2008, was the first state election to use newly demarcated assembly constituencies.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_constituencies_of_the_Lok_Sabha#cite_note-2) Consequently, all assembly elections scheduled in 2008, viz. in the states of Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, NCT of Delhi, Mizoram and Rajasthan were based on newly defined assembly constituencies.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_constituencies_of_the_Lok_Sabha#cite_note-3)

The size and shape of the Parliamentary and Assembly Constituencies are determined, according to section 4 of the [Representation of the People Act, 1951](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Representation_of_the_People_Act,_1951 "Representation of the People Act, 1951"), by an independent Delimitation Commission. Under [a constitutional amendment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forty-second_Amendment_of_the_Constitution_of_India "Forty-second Amendment of the Constitution of India") of 1976, delimitation was suspended until after the census of 2001.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_constituencies_of_the_Lok_Sabha#cite_note-4) However, certain amendments to the Constitution made in 2001 and 2003 have, while putting a freeze on the total number of existing seats as allocated to various States in the House of the People and the State Legislative Assemblies based on the 1971 census until the first census to be taken after the year 2026,[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_constituencies_of_the_Lok_Sabha#cite_note-:0-5) provided that each State shall be delimited into territorial Parliamentary and Assembly Constituencies based on [2001 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Census_of_India "2001 Census of India") and the extent of such constituencies as delimited shall remain frozen until the first census to be taken after the year 2026.[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_constituencies_of_the_Lok_Sabha#cite_note-:0-5) The number of seats to be reserved for SC/ ST shall be re-worked based on the 2001 census. The constituency shall be delimited in a manner that the population of each Parliamentary and Assembly Constituency in a State so far as practicable be the same throughout the State.[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_constituencies_of_the_Lok_Sabha#cite_note-:0-5)

## Summary

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=List_of_constituencies_of_the_Lok_Sabha&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

The Delimitation Commission, set up under the [Delimitation Act, 2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delimitation_Act "Delimitation Act"), was entrusted with the task of readjusting all parliamentary and assembly constituencies in the country in all the states of India, except the state of Jammu and Kashmir, based on population ascertained in 2001 Census. The government of India promulgated an Ordinance amending the Delimitation Act, of 2002 nullifies the Final Order of the Delimitation Commission for the state of Jharkhand. Later on, the Government passed four separate Orders under Sec 10 A of the Delimitation Act, 2002, deferring the delimitation exercise in the four North Eastern states of Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur and Nagaland. The following table indicates the total number of seats and the number of seats reserved for SC and ST statewise.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_constituencies_of_the_Lok_Sabha#cite_note-6)

Between 1952 and 2020, [two seats were reserved](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Indian_reserved_seats_in_the_Lok_Sabha "Anglo-Indian reserved seats in the Lok Sabha") in the [Lok Sabha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lok_Sabha "Lok Sabha") for members of the [Anglo-Indian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Indian "Anglo-Indian") community. They were nominated by the [President of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_India "President of India") on the advice of the [Government of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_India "Government of India"). In 2020, it was abolished under the [104th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Hundred_and_Fourth_Amendment_of_the_Constitution_of_India#:~:text=The_One_Hundred_and_Fourth,_Lok_Sabha_and_State_Legislative "One Hundred and Fourth Amendment of the Constitution of India").[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_constituencies_of_the_Lok_Sabha#cite_note-7)[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_constituencies_of_the_Lok_Sabha#cite_note-8)
