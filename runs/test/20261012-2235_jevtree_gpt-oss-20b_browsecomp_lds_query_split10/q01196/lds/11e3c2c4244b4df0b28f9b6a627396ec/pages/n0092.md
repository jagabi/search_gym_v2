Kerala Assembly Election FAQs

## [How many total seats are there in the Kerala Assembly?](https://www.indiatoday.in/elections/assembly/kerala/constituency-list#accordion-1)

The Kerala Assembly (Vidhan Sabha) has a total of **140 seats**. Public representatives are elected directly.

## [When will the Kerala Assembly Elections 2026 be held?](https://www.indiatoday.in/elections/assembly/kerala/constituency-list#accordion-2)

Voting for the Kerala Assembly Elections 2026 will take place on **April 9**, while the counting of votes will be held on **May 4**.

## [How many phases were the Kerala Assembly elections held in 2021?](https://www.indiatoday.in/elections/assembly/kerala/constituency-list#accordion-3)

The Kerala Assembly elections 2021 were conducted in **1 phase**.

## [When will Kerala election results be announced?](https://www.indiatoday.in/elections/assembly/kerala/constituency-list#accordion-4)

The Kerala Assembly election results will be out in the **first half of 2026**.

## [Which parties are mainly participating in the Kerala Assembly elections 2026?](https://www.indiatoday.in/elections/assembly/kerala/constituency-list#accordion-5)

The main parties in the Kerala Assembly elections 2026 are the **Left Democratic Front (LDF)**, **United Democratic Front (UDF)**, and the **Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP)**, along with a few other regional parties.

## [What was the voter turnout in the Kerala Assembly elections 2021?](https://www.indiatoday.in/elections/assembly/kerala/constituency-list#accordion-6)

According to available data, the overall voter turnout in the Kerala Assembly elections 2021 was 74.06%.

## [What is the SIR (Special Intensive Revision) process?](https://www.indiatoday.in/elections/assembly/kerala/constituency-list#accordion-7)

SIR, or Special Intensive Revision, is a special process by the Election Commission through which names are added, removed, or corrected in the voter list.

## [How can I find the name of the winning candidate in my area?](https://www.indiatoday.in/elections/assembly/kerala/constituency-list#accordion-8)

You can check the name of the winning candidate in your area on the official website of the Election Commission: [https://results.eci.gov.in](https://results.eci.gov.in/). You can also check it on the India Today website: [https://www.indiatoday.in/elections/assembly/kerala](https://www.indiatoday.in/elections/assembly/kerala)
