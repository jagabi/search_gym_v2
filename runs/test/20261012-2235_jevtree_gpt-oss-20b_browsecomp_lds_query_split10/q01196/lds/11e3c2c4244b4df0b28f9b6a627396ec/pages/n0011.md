## [Highlights: BJP wins by over 75,000 votes, Humayun Kabir's AJUP comes second](https://www.indiatoday.in/elections/assembly/story/rejinagar-by-election-results-2026-live-updates-bypoll-vote-counting-3012908-2026-10-09 "Highlights: BJP wins by over 75,000 votes, Humayun Kabir's AJUP comes second")

The Suvendu Adhikari-led BJP government faces its first electoral test since coming to power in West Bengal. The bypoll was necessitated after Aam Janata Unnayan Party (AJUP) chief Humayun Kabir vacated the seat. His son, Golam Nabi Azad, is contesting the election this time. The 2027 Assembly elections will present distinct political challenges in Uttarakhand, Punjab, Goa and Manipur, with the BJP defending power in three states, the AAP seeking a second term in Punjab and the Congress looking to regain ground across several key contests.

In Uttarakhand, Chief Minister Pushkar Singh Dhami will lead the BJP's bid for a third consecutive term in the 70-member Assembly. The BJP is seeking to break the state's pattern of alternating power between the BJP and Congress. Development, the Uniform Civil Code and governance will be central to its campaign, while Congress is banking on anti-incumbency, particularly in the hill districts.

In Punjab, Chief Minister Bhagwant Mann will seek a second term for the AAP after its sweeping 2022 victory, when it won 92 of 117 seats. Employment, farmers' concerns, welfare, law and order and state finances are likely to shape the contest. Congress remains the main challenger, while the SAD is rebuilding and the BJP is expanding its presence.

In Goa, Chief Minister Pramod Sawant will lead the BJP into another closely watched contest. The party has remained in power since 2012 and won 20 of the 40 seats in 2022. Its strength later rose to 28 after eight Congress MLAs crossed over. Alliances and defections are likely to remain important factors.

In Manipur, Chief Minister Yumnam Khemchand Singh faces the challenge of leading the BJP into an election shaped by the aftermath of prolonged ethnic turmoil. The BJP-led NDA holds 51 of 60 seats. Security, rehabilitation, displacement, inter-community relations and restoring normal life are likely to dominate the campaign.
