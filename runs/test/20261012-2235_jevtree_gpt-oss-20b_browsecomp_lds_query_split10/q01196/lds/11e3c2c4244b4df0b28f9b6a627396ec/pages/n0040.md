**[Thiruvananthapuram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiruvananthapuram "Thiruvananthapuram") Parliamentary (Lok Sabha) Constituency** is one of the 20 parliamentary constituencies in Kerala, India. It is located in [Thiruvananthapuram district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiruvananthapuram_district "Thiruvananthapuram district") and encompasses the major part of [Thiruvananthapuram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiruvananthapuram "Thiruvananthapuram") city, the capital of [Kerala State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerala "Kerala").

The incumbent MP of this constituency, [Shashi Tharoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shashi_Tharoor "Shashi Tharoor") of the [Indian National Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Congress "Indian National Congress"), has been elected on 4 consecutive occasions: 2009, 2014, 2019 and 2024. He is the longest serving Member of Parliament to have represented the constituency.

## Demographics

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Thiruvananthapuram_Lok_Sabha_constituency&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Demographics")]

This constituency is dominated By [Nair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nair "Nair") and [Nadar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nadar_(caste) "Nadar (caste)").[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiruvananthapuram_(Lok_Sabha_constituency)#cite_note-2) As per the electoral roll for the 2024 polls, the total electorate is of 14,03,281 people including 7,27,469 women, 6,75,771 men and 41 transgender people.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiruvananthapuram_(Lok_Sabha_constituency)#cite_note-3) Out of total electorate, 26% is rural and 74% is urban. The Scheduled castes (SC) and Scheduled tribes (ST) is 9.82% and 0.45%, respectively of total electorate.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiruvananthapuram_(Lok_Sabha_constituency)#cite_note-4) The constituency also has highest proportion of [Nadars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nadar_(caste) "Nadar (caste)") and [Converted Christians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conversion_to_Christianity "Conversion to Christianity") in Kerala.[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiruvananthapuram_(Lok_Sabha_constituency)#cite_note-5)[Nairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nair "Nair") dominates the urban segments of [Kazhakootam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazhakootam_Assembly_constituency "Kazhakootam Assembly constituency"),[Nemom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nemom_Assembly_constituency "Nemom Assembly constituency"),[Vattiyoorkavu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vattiyoorkavu_Assembly_constituency "Vattiyoorkavu Assembly constituency") and [Thiruvananthapuram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiruvananthapuram_Assembly_constituency "Thiruvananthapuram Assembly constituency") While the [Nadars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nadar_(caste) "Nadar (caste)") are Concentrated on [Neyyattinkara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neyyattinkara_Assembly_constituency "Neyyattinkara Assembly constituency"),[Parassala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parassala_Assembly_constituency "Parassala Assembly constituency"),[Kovalam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kovalam_Assembly_constituency "Kovalam Assembly constituency").

As per the voter list published by the Election commission for the 2019 Lok Sabha elections, there were 13,34,665 voters in the electorate and 1077 polling stations in this constituency.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiruvananthapuram_(Lok_Sabha_constituency)#cite_note-ceo.kerala.gov.in-6) The voter turnout for the 2019 Lok Sabha election was 73.45% where as it was 68.63% and 65.74% in the 2014 and 2009 elections respectively.[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiruvananthapuram_(Lok_Sabha_constituency)#cite_note-7)
