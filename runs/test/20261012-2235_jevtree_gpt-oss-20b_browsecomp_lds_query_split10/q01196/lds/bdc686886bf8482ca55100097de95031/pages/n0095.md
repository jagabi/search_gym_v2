## Built around your whole life

Give Muse a goal or task and it handles the rest, from saving money and improving your health to smart shopping and helping you strengthen your relationships.

### Every Muse user shares their own computer in the cloud with their Muse.

Muse Secure VM is a persistent, isolated Linux computer with a full browser that you and your agent can use to complete tasks, allowing you to intervene at any point. It's your computer and your browser, with the storage, CPU, and memory to handle real work.

![Image 8: Floating browser, search, map, calendar, and confirmation panels](https://l.facebook.com/images/landing/browser.webp)![Image 9: Floating browser, search, map, calendar, and confirmation panels](https://l.facebook.com/images/landing/browser-dark.webp)

### Designed around the way you already communicate

Chat with your agent in the same way you message with other people, using the Muse app on mobile, Mac, or directly in WhatsApp. There's no learning curve, just tell Muse what you want to get done.

![Image 10: A chat on a phone where the agent has filled out a school form, shown beside a laptop](https://l.facebook.com/images/landing/messaging.webp)![Image 11: A chat on a phone where the agent has filled out a school form, shown beside a laptop](https://l.facebook.com/images/landing/messaging-dark.webp)

### Stay in control of what your agent does

Approve actions your agent takes on your behalf before they happen, like sending emails and making purchases. See a complete audit trail of everything your agent has done (and what it's planning to do).

![Image 12: Floating purchase, message, activity, permission, and decision controls](https://l.facebook.com/images/landing/approvals.webp)![Image 13: Floating purchase, message, activity, permission, and decision controls](https://l.facebook.com/images/landing/approvals-dark.webp)

### Your data is kept secure and private

Save your logins to a secure credential store Muse can't see, with 1Password integration coming soon. When shopping, a one-time card number is generated at checkout so your real card stays hidden from the merchant and your agent. Eligible purchases are covered by [Link's purchase protections](https://link.com/protections), a first for AI agents, and your conversations are not shared with Meta's ad systems.

![Image 14: A vault, privacy shield, masked credential, security control, and virtual card](https://l.facebook.com/images/landing/secure-storage.webp)![Image 15: A vault, privacy shield, masked credential, security control, and virtual card](https://l.facebook.com/images/landing/secure-storage-dark.webp)

### Proactively helps with your goals and suggests ideas

Muse helps you stay on top of things, takes tasks and projects off your plate, and turns long-term goals into real progress. It learns from conversations, reflects on what matters and gets sharper, coming back with ideas built around what you actually need, ready to act on them under your direction.

![Image 16: Floating checklist, calendar, route, progress, and idea panels](https://l.facebook.com/images/landing/goals.webp)![Image 17: Floating checklist, calendar, route, progress, and idea panels](https://l.facebook.com/images/landing/goals-dark.webp)

### Connects to your apps and builds its own tools

Connect the apps you use daily, like email, calendar, and Instagram. You decide what your agent can see and what it can do. If a task needs a tool that doesn't exist, your agent builds it for you.

![Image 18: Connected app, messaging, media, cloud, calendar, and automation tiles](https://l.facebook.com/images/landing/connectors.webp)
