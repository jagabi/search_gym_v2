## വെടിപ്പായിട്ടുള്ള ലേഖനങ്ങളുടെ ശേഖരണം

### അവലോകന ലേഖനങ്ങൾ

Overview articles summarize in prose a broad topic like [biology](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology "Biology"), and also have illustrations and links to subtopics like [cell biology](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_biology "Cell biology"), biographies like [Carl Linnaeus](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Linnaeus "Carl Linnaeus"), and other related articles like [Human Genome Project](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Genome_Project "Human Genome Project").

*   [Portal:Contents/Overviews](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E0%B4%95%E0%B4%B5%E0%B4%BE%E0%B4%9F%E0%B4%82:Contents/Overviews "കവാടം:Contents/Overviews") lists overview articles from covered areas of knowledge in a single page.

### അതിർ താളുകൾ

Outline pages have [trees](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tree_structure?action=edit&redlink=1 "Tree structure (ഇതുവരെ എഴുതപ്പെട്ടിട്ടില്ല)") of topics in an [outline format](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outline_(list)?action=edit&redlink=1 "Outline (list) (ഇതുവരെ എഴുതപ്പെട്ടിട്ടില്ല)"), which in turn are linked to further outlines and articles providing more detail. Outlines show how important subtopics relate to each other based on how they are arranged in the tree, and they are useful as a more condensed, non-prose alternative to overview articles.

*   [Portal:Contents/Outlines](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E0%B4%95%E0%B4%B5%E0%B4%BE%E0%B4%9F%E0%B4%82:Contents/Outlines?action=edit&redlink=1 "കവാടം:Contents/Outlines (ഇതുവരെ എഴുതപ്പെട്ടിട്ടില്ല)") is a comprehensive list of "Outline of __" pages, organized by subject. It is itself an outline, that links (almost) exclusively to other outlines.
*   [Outline of academic disciplines](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outline_of_academic_disciplines?action=edit&redlink=1 "Outline of academic disciplines (ഇതുവരെ എഴുതപ്പെട്ടിട്ടില്ല)") covers subjects studied in college or university, and provides links to prose overview articles and their corresponding outlines.
*   [Outline of knowledge](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outline_of_knowledge?action=edit&redlink=1 "Outline of knowledge (ഇതുവരെ എഴുതപ്പെട്ടിട്ടില്ല)") is the top-level outline, its subject being the broadest one of all. It is the ancestor of all other outlines, and they branch out from it, in successive levels.

### പട്ടിക താളുകൾ

List pages enumerate items of a particular type, such as the [List of sovereign states](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_sovereign_states "List of sovereign states") or [List of South Africans](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_South_Africans?action=edit&redlink=1 "List of South Africans (ഇതുവരെ എഴുതപ്പെട്ടിട്ടില്ല)"). Wikipedia has "lists of lists" when there are too many items to fit on a single page, when the items can sorted in different ways, or as a way of navigating lists on a topic (for example [Lists of countries and territories](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lists_of_countries_and_territories?action=edit&redlink=1 "Lists of countries and territories (ഇതുവരെ എഴുതപ്പെട്ടിട്ടില്ല)") or [Lists of people](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lists_of_people?action=edit&redlink=1 "Lists of people (ഇതുവരെ എഴുതപ്പെട്ടിട്ടില്ല)")). There are several ways to find lists:

*   [Portal:Contents/Lists](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E0%B4%95%E0%B4%B5%E0%B4%BE%E0%B4%9F%E0%B4%82:Contents/Lists?action=edit&redlink=1 "കവാടം:Contents/Lists (ഇതുവരെ എഴുതപ്പെട്ടിട്ടില്ല)") – A limited single-page collection of lists and lists of lists
*   [List of lists of lists](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_lists_of_lists?action=edit&redlink=1 "List of lists of lists (ഇതുവരെ എഴുതപ്പെട്ടിട്ടില്ല)") – A broad single-page collection of lists of lists
*   [Category:Lists](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E0%B4%B5%E0%B5%BC%E0%B4%97%E0%B5%8D%E0%B4%97%E0%B4%82:Lists?action=edit&redlink=1 "വർഗ്ഗം:Lists (ഇതുവരെ എഴുതപ്പെട്ടിട്ടില്ല)") – Browse lists comprehensively via the multi-page Wikipedia category system

## ലേഖനങ്ങളുടെ ശേഖരങ്ങൾ

### ശേഖരണം ചിട്ട

Wikipedia's [collection of category pages](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E0%B4%B5%E0%B4%BF%E0%B4%95%E0%B5%8D%E0%B4%95%E0%B4%BF%E0%B4%AA%E0%B5%80%E0%B4%A1%E0%B4%BF%E0%B4%AF:Categorization "വിക്കിപീഡിയ:Categorization") is a classified index system. It is automatically generated from category tags at the bottoms of articles and most other pages. Nearly all of the articles available so far on the website can be found through these subject indexes.

If you are simply looking to browse articles by topic, there are three top-level pages to choose from:

*   [Category:Main topic classifications](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E0%B4%B5%E0%B5%BC%E0%B4%97%E0%B5%8D%E0%B4%97%E0%B4%82:Main_topic_classifications?action=edit&redlink=1 "വർഗ്ഗം:Main topic classifications (ഇതുവരെ എഴുതപ്പെട്ടിട്ടില്ല)")– probably what you are looking for: Arts, History, Technology, etc.
*   [Portal:Contents/Categories](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E0%B4%95%E0%B4%B5%E0%B4%BE%E0%B4%9F%E0%B4%82:Contents/Categories?action=edit&redlink=1 "കവാടം:Contents/Categories (ഇതുവരെ എഴുതപ്പെട്ടിട്ടില്ല)")– a hand-crafted list of first- and second-level topic categories

For biographies, see [Category:People](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E0%B4%B5%E0%B5%BC%E0%B4%97%E0%B5%8D%E0%B4%97%E0%B4%82:People?action=edit&redlink=1 "വർഗ്ഗം:People (ഇതുവരെ എഴുതപ്പെട്ടിട്ടില്ല)").

[Category:Contents](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E0%B4%B5%E0%B5%BC%E0%B4%97%E0%B5%8D%E0%B4%97%E0%B4%82:Contents?action=edit&redlink=1 "വർഗ്ഗം:Contents (ഇതുവരെ എഴുതപ്പെട്ടിട്ടില്ല)") is technically at the top of the category hierarchy, but contains many categories useful to editors but not readers. [Special:Categories](https://ml.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E0%B4%AA%E0%B5%8D%E0%B4%B0%E0%B4%A4%E0%B5%8D%E0%B4%AF%E0%B5%87%E0%B4%95%E0%B4%82:%E0%B4%B5%E0%B5%BC%E0%B4%97%E0%B5%8D%E0%B4%97%E0%B4%99%E0%B5%8D%E0%B4%99%E0%B5%BE "പ്രത്യേകം:വർഗ്ഗങ്ങൾ") lists every category alphabetically.
