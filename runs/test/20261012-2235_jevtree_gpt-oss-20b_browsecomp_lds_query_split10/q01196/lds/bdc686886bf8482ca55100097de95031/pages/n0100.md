## About Wikipedia

This is the front page of the **[Simple English Wikipedia](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple_English_Wikipedia "Simple English Wikipedia")**. _Wikipedias_ are places where people work together to write [encyclopedias](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encyclopedia "Encyclopedia") in different languages. We use [Simple English](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Simple_English_Wikipedia#Simple_English "Wikipedia:Simple English Wikipedia") words and [grammar](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammar "Grammar") here. The Simple English Wikipedia is for everyone, such as children and adults who are learning English. There are 285,613 articles on the Simple English Wikipedia. All of the pages are [free to use](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_content "Open content"). They have all been published under both the _[Creative Commons Attribution/Share-Alike License 4.0 International License](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Text\_of\_the\_Creative\_Commons\_Attribution-ShareAlike\_4.0\_International\_License "Wikipedia:Text of the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License")_ and the _[GNU Free Documentation License](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU\_Free\_Documentation\_License "GNU Free Documentation License")_. **You can help here!** You may change these pages and make new pages. Read the [help pages](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Contents "Help:Contents") and other [good pages](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Useful "Wikipedia:Useful") to learn how to [write pages here](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:How_to_write_Simple_English_articles "Wikipedia:How to write Simple English articles"). If you need help, you may ask questions at [Simple talk](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Simple_talk "Wikipedia:Simple talk").

* * *

When writing articles here:

*   **Use [Basic English](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_English "Basic English") words and shorter sentences.** This allows people to understand complex terms or phrases.
*   **Write good pages.** The best encyclopedia pages have useful, well-written information.
*   **Use the pages to learn and teach.** These pages can help people learn English. You can also use them to [make a new Wikipedia](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/How%20to%20start%20a%20new%20Wikipedia "meta:How to start a new Wikipedia") to help other people.
*   **Simple does not mean short.** Writing in Simple English means that simple words are used. It does not mean readers want basic information. Articles do not have to be short to be simple; expand articles, add details, but use basic [vocabulary](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vocabulary "Vocabulary").
*   **Be bold!** Your article does not have to be perfect because other editors will fix it and make it better. And most importantly, do not be afraid to start and make articles better yourself.

Map of the storm on October 10

The **[1910 Cuba hurricane](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/1910_Cuba_hurricane "1910 Cuba hurricane")** was said to be one of the worst [tropical cyclones](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_cyclones "Tropical cyclones") that has ever hit [Cuba](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuba "Cuba"). The storm formed in the southern [Caribbean Sea](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caribbean_Sea "Caribbean Sea") on October 9, 1910. It grew stronger as it moved northwest. It then made [landfall](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landfall_(meteorology) "Landfall (meteorology)") on the western end of Cuba. The storm made a loop over open water, and then began moving towards the [United States](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States "United States"). After crossing [Florida](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida "Florida"), the storm moved near the rest of the southeastern United States and passed out to sea.

Because of the storm's loop, some reports said it was actually two hurricanes. In Cuba, the storm was one of the worst disasters in the island's history. There was bad damage and thousands of people lost their homes. In Florida, the storm also caused damage and caused [flooding](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flooding "Flooding") in some areas. It is not known exactly how much damage the storm caused. However, losses in [Havana](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Havana "Havana"), Cuba were over [$](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Dollar "United States Dollar")1 million. At least 100 people died in Cuba alone.

[![Image 9: The west entrance of the Wheeling Tunnel](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/36/WB_on_I-70_at_the_Wheeling_Tunnel.jpg/120px-WB_on_I-70_at_the_Wheeling_Tunnel.jpg?utm_source=simple.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:WB_on_I-70_at_the_Wheeling_Tunnel.jpg "The west entrance of the Wheeling Tunnel")

The west entrance of the Wheeling Tunnel

The **[Wheeling Tunnel](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheeling_Tunnel "Wheeling Tunnel")** is a pair of [tunnels](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunnel "Tunnel") in [Wheeling](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheeling,_West_Virginia "Wheeling, West Virginia"), [Ohio County](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_County,_West_Virginia "Ohio County, West Virginia"), [West Virginia](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Virginia "West Virginia"). The tunnels allow [Interstate 70](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_70 "Interstate 70") and [U.S. Route 250](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_250 "U.S. Route 250") to go through Wheeling Hill. Each tunnel has two [lanes](https://simple.wiktionary.org/wiki/lane "wikt:lane") of each road. Opened in 1966, the Wheeling Tunnel cost [$](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Dollar "United States Dollar")6.9 million to build. It was rebuilt between 2007 and 2010 for $13.7 million.
