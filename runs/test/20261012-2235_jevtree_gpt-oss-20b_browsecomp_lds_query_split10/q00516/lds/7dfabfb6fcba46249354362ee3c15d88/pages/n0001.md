In 2004, the romance film Love in Thoughts, starring him, was released, for which he won the Audience Award for Best Actor at the European Film Awards. In 2009, the crime film Inglourious Basterds, in which he appeared, was released in North America. Evaluation

Almost all the roles played by Daniel Brühl carry an idealistic hue. His babyface appearance seems to permanently etch this trace of idealism onto his features, which has caused considerable trouble for Brühl, who wishes to broaden his acting range.

Just as people often see the beauty and grandeur of dreams while overlooking their dark side, Daniel Brühl's dark side finds little room for expression in the German film industry. Even in the most somber-toned horror films, he has never truly attempted to play the "sinister and despicable scoundrel" he has long desired to portray. Whether as the protagonist in Triangle or in a minor role in The Intruders, his characters are still fighting, only the adversaries have changed to demons or executioners. If audiences connect Brühl's roles, they will discover the growth trajectory of a typical dreamer—he forever lives in a youthful manner. (Review from Movie World)
