The **British Academy Film Awards** (the **BAFTAs** or **BAFTA Awards**; officially the **EE British Academy Film Awards** since [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/66th_British_Academy_Film_Awards "66th British Academy Film Awards")) is an annual [film award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_award "Film award") show hosted by the [British Academy of Film and Television Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Academy_of_Film_and_Television_Arts "British Academy of Film and Television Arts") (BAFTA) to honour the best [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinema_of_the_United_Kingdom "Cinema of the United Kingdom") and [international](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_cinema "World cinema") film contributions. The ceremony was [first held](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1st_British_Academy_Film_Awards "1st British Academy Film Awards") in London at [Odeon Luxe Leicester Square Cinema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odeon_Luxe_Leicester_Square "Odeon Luxe Leicester Square"), then the [Royal Opera House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Opera_House "Royal Opera House") from [2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/60th_British_Academy_Film_Awards "60th British Academy Film Awards") to [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/69th_British_Academy_Film_Awards "69th British Academy Film Awards"). The event was held at the [Royal Albert Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Albert_Hall "Royal Albert Hall") from [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/70th_British_Academy_Film_Awards "70th British Academy Film Awards") to [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/75th_British_Academy_Film_Awards "75th British Academy Film Awards"), before moving to the [Royal Festival Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Festival_Hall "Royal Festival Hall") for [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/76th_British_Academy_Film_Awards "76th British Academy Film Awards"). The statue awarded to recipients depicts a theatrical mask. The BAFTA Awards are recognised internationally as the highest honour in British cinema, equivalent to the [BAFTA TV Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Academy_Television_Awards "British Academy Television Awards") for [television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television_in_the_United_Kingdom "Television in the United Kingdom"), [Laurence Olivier Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Olivier_Awards "Laurence Olivier Awards") for [theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theatre_of_the_United_Kingdom "Theatre of the United Kingdom"), and the [BRIT Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brit_Awards "Brit Awards") for [music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_of_the_United_Kingdom "Music of the United Kingdom"). The BAFTA Film Awards are considered equivalent to the [Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Awards "Academy Awards").

The first BAFTA Awards ceremony was held in 1949, and the ceremony was first broadcast on the [BBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC "BBC") in [1956](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9th_British_Academy_Film_Awards "9th British Academy Film Awards") with [Vivien Leigh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vivien_Leigh "Vivien Leigh") as the host. The ceremony was initially held in April or May; since [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/54th_British_Academy_Film_Awards "54th British Academy Film Awards"), it typically takes place in February. The BAFTAs have been sponsored by [EE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EE_(telecommunications) "EE (telecommunications)") since 2013.

## History

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=British_Academy_Film_Awards&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")]

[![Image 6](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7b/Society_of_Film_and_Television_Arts_award%2C_V%26A_East_Storehouse.jpg/250px-Society_of_Film_and_Television_Arts_award%2C_V%26A_East_Storehouse.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Society_of_Film_and_Television_Arts_award,_V&A_East_Storehouse.jpg)

Society of Film and Television Arts award from 1970

[![Image 7](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/44/BAFTA_Special_Lifetime_Achievement_Award%2C_V%26A_East_Storehouse.jpg/250px-BAFTA_Special_Lifetime_Achievement_Award%2C_V%26A_East_Storehouse.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:BAFTA_Special_Lifetime_Achievement_Award,_V&A_East_Storehouse.jpg)

BAFTA special lifetime achievement award presented to Ronnie Barker

The [British Academy of Film and Television Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Academy_of_Film_and_Television_Arts "British Academy of Film and Television Arts") (BAFTA) was founded in 1947 as The British Film Academy, by [David Lean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Lean "David Lean"), [Alexander Korda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Korda "Alexander Korda"), [Carol Reed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carol_Reed "Carol Reed"), [Charles Laughton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Laughton "Charles Laughton"), [Roger Manvell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Manvell "Roger Manvell"), [Laurence Olivier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Olivier "Laurence Olivier"), [Emeric Pressburger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeric_Pressburger "Emeric Pressburger"), [Michael Powell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Powell "Michael Powell"), [Michael Balcon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Balcon "Michael Balcon"), and other major figures of the British film industry.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-1)[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-2) In 1958, the Academy merged with The Guild of Television Producers and Directors to form The Society of Film and Television, which eventually became The British Academy of Film and Television Arts in 1976.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-3)

The stated charitable purpose of BAFTA is to "support, develop and promote the art forms of the moving image, by identifying and rewarding excellence, inspiring practitioners, and benefiting the public". In addition to high-profile awards ceremonies, BAFTA runs a year-round programme of educational events, including film screenings and tribute evenings. BAFTA is supported by a membership of about 6,000 people from the film, television, and video game industries.

The Academy's awards are in the form of a theatrical mask designed by American sculptor [Mitzi Cunliffe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitzi_Cunliffe "Mitzi Cunliffe"), in response to a commission from the Guild of Television Producers in 1955.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-4)

[Prince Philip, Duke of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Philip,_Duke_of_Edinburgh "Prince Philip, Duke of Edinburgh") was the host of the ceremony as the first president of BAFTA in 1960, 1963, and 1965.[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-5)[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-6)[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-7)

[Princess Anne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Anne "Princess Anne") presented the first Fellowship Award to [Alfred Hitchcock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Hitchcock "Alfred Hitchcock") in 1971,[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-8)[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-9) and subsequently attended the ceremony several times as President of BAFTA between 1973 and 2001.[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-10)[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-11)[[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-12)

## Annual ceremony

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=British_Academy_Film_Awards&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Annual ceremony")]

[![Image 8](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/73/British_Academy_Film_Awards.jpg/250px-British_Academy_Film_Awards.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:British_Academy_Film_Awards.jpg)

The location for the [2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/64th_British_Academy_Film_Awards "64th British Academy Film Awards") ceremony

The ceremony previously took place in April or May, but since 2001 it has been held in February in order to precede the [Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Awards "Academy Awards"). Most of the awards are open to all nationalities, though there are awards for Outstanding British Film and Outstanding Debut by a British Writer, Producer or Director. Only UK films are eligible for the categories of The British Short Film and British Short Animation awards.

During each annual ceremony, BAFTA pauses _[in memoriam](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/in%20memoriam "wikt:in memoriam")_ to pay tribute to those in the industry who have died over the past 12 months, showcasing a montage of images accompanied by music.

[William, Prince of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William,_Prince_of_Wales "William, Prince of Wales") attended the ceremony for the first time in 2010 after being named president of BAFTA, and has attended frequently since, including together with [Catherine, Princess of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine,_Princess_of_Wales "Catherine, Princess of Wales").[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-13)[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-14) He presented the [BAFTA Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Fellowship "BAFTA Fellowship") award to [Mel Brooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mel_Brooks "Mel Brooks") in 2017 and [Kathleen Kennedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathleen_Kennedy_(producer) "Kathleen Kennedy (producer)") in 2020.[[15]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-15)[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-16)

### Broadcast

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=British_Academy_Film_Awards&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Broadcast")]

Historically, the Awards ceremony was broadcast on British television on a same-day delay, and later distributed internationally. The first broadcast was on the [BBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC "BBC") in 1956, with [Vivien Leigh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vivien_Leigh "Vivien Leigh") (who would present an award to her ex husband Sir [Laurence Olivier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Olivier "Laurence Olivier")) as the host.[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-17) It has been broadcast in colour since 1970. In the US the show was previously broadcast with a delay on [BBC America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_America "BBC America") until 2021, after which it has broadcast on streaming service [BritBox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BritBox "BritBox").[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-18)[[19]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-19)[[20]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-20) In 2023, the BAFTA Film Awards included a live telecast for its major award categories during the culmination of the ceremony.[[21]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-21)

### Location

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=British_Academy_Film_Awards&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Location")]

The award ceremony is held in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London "London"). From 2000 to 2007, the ceremonies took place at the flagship [Odeon cinema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odeon_Luxe_Leicester_Square "Odeon Luxe Leicester Square") in [Leicester Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leicester_Square "Leicester Square"). Between 2008 and 2016, the ceremonies took place at the Royal Opera House. The 70th Awards in 2017, and subsequent ceremonies up to the 75th Awards in 2022, were held at the Royal Albert Hall.[[22]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-22)[[23]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-23)

For the [76th British Academy Film Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/76th_British_Academy_Film_Awards "76th British Academy Film Awards") in 2023, it was announced that the ceremony would be moved to the Royal Festival Hall as part of a new multi-year deal between BAFTA and the [Southbank Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southbank_Centre "Southbank Centre"), bringing the Film Awards in-line with the [British Academy Television Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Academy_Television_Awards "British Academy Television Awards") and [British Academy Games Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Academy_Games_Awards "British Academy Games Awards"), which were already held there.[[24]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-24)

### Sponsorship

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=British_Academy_Film_Awards&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Sponsorship")]

Until 2012, the mobile telephone network [Orange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_UK "Orange UK") sponsored the awards. Orange's parent company, [EE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EE_Limited "EE Limited"), took over the sponsorship of the event from 2013.[[25]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-25)

#### Directors nominated for multiple films in the same year

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=British_Academy_Film_Awards&action=edit&section=14 "Edit section: Directors nominated for multiple films in the same year")]

The only director to receive dual nominations in the same year at the BAFTA Film Awards was [Steven Soderbergh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Soderbergh "Steven Soderbergh") for _[Erin Brockovich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erin\_Brockovich\_(film) "Erin Brockovich (film)")_ and _[Traffic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traffic\_(2000\_film) "Traffic (2000 film)")_ in 2000. Additionally, [Sidney Lumet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidney_Lumet "Sidney Lumet") received one joint nomination for _[Murder on the Orient Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder\_on\_the\_Orient\_Express\_(1974\_film) "Murder on the Orient Express (1974 film)")_ and _[Serpico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serpico "Serpico")_ in 1974.

### Other

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=British_Academy_Film_Awards&action=edit&section=15 "Edit section: Other")]

*   **Most awards won by a single film**
    *   _[Butch Cassidy and the Sundance Kid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butch\_Cassidy\_and\_the\_Sundance\_Kid "Butch Cassidy and the Sundance Kid")_ (1969), with 9 wins.

*   **Most nominations received by a single film**
    *   _[Gandhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gandhi\_(film) "Gandhi (film)")_ (1982), with 16 nominations.

*   **Most awards won by a non English-language film**
    *   _[All Quiet on the Western Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All\_Quiet\_on\_the\_Western\_Front\_(2022\_film) "All Quiet on the Western Front (2022 film)")_ (2022), with 7 wins.

*   **Most nominations received by a non English-language film**
    *   _[Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crouching\_Tiger,\_Hidden\_Dragon "Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon")_ (2000) and _[All Quiet on the Western Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All\_Quiet\_on\_the\_Western\_Front\_(2022\_film) "All Quiet on the Western Front (2022 film)")_ (2022), with 14 nominations each.

*   **Most nominations received by an animated film**
    *   _[Shrek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrek "Shrek")_ (2001), with 6 nominations.

*   **First non-English language film to win Best Film**
    *   _[Bicycle Thieves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicycle\_Thieves "Bicycle Thieves")_ in 1949.

*   **First non-English language films to be nominated for Best Film**
    *   _[Four Steps in the Clouds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four\_Steps\_in\_the\_Clouds "Four Steps in the Clouds")_, _[Monsieur Vincent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monsieur\_Vincent "Monsieur Vincent")_ and _[Paisan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paisan "Paisan")_ in 1948.

*   **First animated film to be nominated for Best Film**
    *   _[Shrek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrek "Shrek")_ (2001)[[a]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-28)

*   **First animated film to win any screenplay categories**
    *   _[Shrek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrek "Shrek")_ (2001), with [Best Adapted Screenplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award_for_Best_Adapted_Screenplay "BAFTA Award for Best Adapted Screenplay")[[b]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award#cite_note-screenplay-29)

*   **Largest sweep (winning awards in every nominated category)**
    *   _[A Man for All Seasons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A\_Man\_for\_All\_Seasons\_(1966\_film) "A Man for All Seasons (1966 film)")_ (1966), with 7 wins.

*   **Most nominations without winning an award**
    *   _[Women in Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women\_in\_Love\_(film) "Women in Love (film)")_ (1969), _[Finding Neverland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finding\_Neverland\_(film) "Finding Neverland (film)")_ (2004), and _[Marty Supreme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marty\_Supreme "Marty Supreme")_ (2025), with 11 nominations each.

*   **Most total nominations and awards for a person**
    *   [Woody Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Allen "Woody Allen") received 24 nominations and won 10 awards.

*   **Most nominations and awards for a person in a single year**
    *   [Alfonso Cuarón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfonso_Cuar%C3%B3n "Alfonso Cuarón") received six nominations and won four awards in [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/72nd_British_Academy_Film_Awards "72nd British Academy Film Awards").

*   **Most nominations for a woman in a single year**
    *   [Chloé Zhao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chlo%C3%A9_Zhao "Chloé Zhao") received four nominations in [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/74th_British_Academy_Film_Awards "74th British Academy Film Awards").

*   **Most total awards for a woman**
    *   [Judi Dench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judi_Dench "Judi Dench") and [Catherine Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_Martin_(designer) "Catherine Martin (designer)") won six awards each.

*   **Most total nominations for a woman**
    *   [Sandy Powell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandy_Powell_(costume_designer) "Sandy Powell (costume designer)") received 16 nominations.

*   **Highest "perfect score"**
    *   Composer [Ennio Morricone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ennio_Morricone "Ennio Morricone") has six nominations and six wins.

*   **Only person to receive every nomination in a category**
    *   [Jocelyn Rickards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jocelyn_Rickards "Jocelyn Rickards") received both nominations for Best British Costume Design – Black and White in [1967](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21st_British_Academy_Film_Awards "21st British Academy Film Awards"), winning for _[Mademoiselle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mademoiselle\_(1966\_film) "Mademoiselle (1966 film)")_.

*   **Only actor to win a BAFTA for portraying a real BAFTA winner**
    *   [Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett "Cate Blanchett") won Best Actress in a Supporting Role for portraying [Katharine Hepburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katharine_Hepburn "Katharine Hepburn") in _[The Aviator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Aviator\_(2004\_film) "The Aviator (2004 film)")_ (2004). 
        *   NOTE: [Robert Downey Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr. "Robert Downey Jr.") won Best Actor in a Leading Role for portraying [Charlie Chaplin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Chaplin "Charlie Chaplin") in _[Chaplin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaplin\_(film) "Chaplin (film)")_ (1992). Chaplin received the [BAFTA Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Fellowship "BAFTA Fellowship"), an honorary award, but was never nominated for a competitive BAFTA.

*   **Only actor to be nominated for a voice-only performance**
    *   [Eddie Murphy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eddie_Murphy "Eddie Murphy") nominated for Best Actor in a Supporting Role for voicing [Donkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donkey_(Shrek) "Donkey (Shrek)") in _[Shrek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrek "Shrek")_ (2001).

*   **Oldest person to win an award**
    *   [James Ivory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Ivory "James Ivory"), age 89 (Best Adapted Screenplay,_[Call Me by Your Name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call\_Me\_by\_Your\_Name\_(film) "Call Me by Your Name (film)")_, 2017).

*   **Youngest person to win an award**
    *   [Jodie Foster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jodie_Foster "Jodie Foster"), age 13 (Best Actress in a Supporting Role and Most Promising Newcomer to Leading Film Roles,_[Bugsy Malone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bugsy\_Malone "Bugsy Malone")_ / _[Taxi Driver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taxi\_Driver "Taxi Driver")_, 1976).
