National Spanish Heritage Month Wikipedia editing campaign, September–October 2026

**404: Historia Not Found** is a month-long editing campaign on English and Spanish Wikipedia, run by [WikiLatinos](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/WikiLatinos "m:WikiLatinos") during Latine Heritage Month. Participants pick a trailblazer or a milestone from five theme lists, write expand or improve the lede of the article, cite it, and earn [barnstars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Barnstars "Wikipedia:Barnstars") as they go.

The campaign runs **September 15 – October 15, 2026**.

## Why this campaign

Latinos have shaped this country in every field there is. We have won court cases that changed who counts as an American. We have won Nobel Prizes. We have served in Congress and in combat. We have built the music, the food, and the television people take for granted. And when you look us up, the record is thin or the page is blank.

## How to participate

1.   **Sign up** on this campaign page (see [page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Event:404:_Historia_Not_Found#Participants) below). You'll need a [Wikipedia account](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special:CreateAccount "Special:CreateAccount"), which takes a minute to make.
2.   **Pick an article** from one of the five theme lists below, or bring your own subject.
3.   **Write a new article, expand an existing one or edit the lede section**, to at least 3,000 bytes, with reliable sources. A shortlist of five articles per theme will also be flagged to expand the lede.
4.   **Earn a barnstar** at four completed articles — awarded as you go, not saved for the end.

## The five themes

_For the complete list, please check out [this spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/19sLojWIh9iyfek7VpkqWmC\_2xMkYPqpkHzkliTHtET8/edit?gid=1622583747#gid=1622583747)_

Themes, not regions: every list covers the whole hemisphere, including Mexican, Puerto Rican, Cuban, Dominican, Central American, South American, Haitian, and Brazilian subjects, and Black, Indigenous, and Asian-descended Latinos throughout. Each theme carries a list of subjects, each with a link and one line on what's missing. You can also bring your own subject.
