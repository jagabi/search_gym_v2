# SALVADOR

_Manuel HUERGA_

*   2006
*   Un Certain Regard

![Image 7](https://img.festival-cannes.com/eyJidWNrZXQiOiJtZWRpYSIsImtleSI6InVwbG9hZHNcLzIwMjNcLzAzXC8xOTY2MS5qcGciLCJlZGl0cyI6eyJyZXNpemUiOnsid2lkdGgiOjMyMCwiaGVpZ2h0Ijo0MzAsImZpdCI6ImNvdmVyIn19fQ==)

On 2 March 1974, the young militant of the Movimiento Ibérico de Liberación (Iberian Liberation Movement), Salvador Puig Antich, became the last political prisoner to be executed in Spain by the garrotte.

This is his story and that of the desperate attempts of his family, colleagues and lawyers to avoid his execution.

 Directed by : Manuel HUERGA  
 Year of production: 2006  
 Country: Spain, United Kingdom  
 Durée : 138 minutes
