_**City of God – 10 Years Later**_ ([Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language "Portuguese language"): _Cidade de Deus: 10 Anos Depois_) is a 2013 [Brazilian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil "Brazil")[documentary film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Documentary_film "Documentary film") directed by Cavi Borges and Luciano Vidigal.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_God_%E2%80%93_10_Years_Later#cite_note-2)[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_God_%E2%80%93_10_Years_Later#cite_note-3)

The documentary shows what has changed in the lives of the actors from the 2002 [Fernando Meirelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Meirelles "Fernando Meirelles") film _[City of God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City\_of\_God\_(2002\_film) "City of God (2002 film)")_. The actors who portrayed Dadinho/Lil' Zé, Bené/Benny, Angélica, and Mané Galinha/Knockout Ned all participated in the documentary ten years after starring in the earlier film.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_God_%E2%80%93_10_Years_Later#cite_note-4)

## Production

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=City_of_God_%E2%80%93_10_Years_Later&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Production")]

Director Cavi Borges contacted [Luciano Vidigal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luciano_Vidigal?action=edit&redlink=1 "Luciano Vidigal (page does not exist)") and suggested a documentary about _[City of God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City\_of\_God\_(2002\_film) "City of God (2002 film)")_. Although they invited [Fernando Meirelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Meirelles "Fernando Meirelles") to produce the documentary, he declined and said that his involvement could limit their perspective; however, he was an important part of their research. They found 50 people involved in the original film and chose to use 18.[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_God_%E2%80%93_10_Years_Later#cite_note-5) The actors were paid $90 for their interviews.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_God_%E2%80%93_10_Years_Later#cite_note-csm-1) In order to raise money, the filmmakers used crowdfunding and threw parties.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_God_%E2%80%93_10_Years_Later#cite_note-thr-interview-6) In the community, news of a retrospective was not universally accepted, as some people disliked the original film for its violence. However, Borges wanted to change international perceptions of the area, which he says _City of God_ colored.[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_God_%E2%80%93_10_Years_Later#cite_note-7)

## Release

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=City_of_God_%E2%80%93_10_Years_Later&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Release")]

_City of God – 10 Years Later_ premiered at the [Festival do Rio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festival_do_Rio "Festival do Rio") in October 2013.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_God_%E2%80%93_10_Years_Later#cite_note-csm-1) Due to unrelated protests, the original showing was canceled, and it was delayed.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_God_%E2%80%93_10_Years_Later#cite_note-thr-interview-6) Its international premiere was at the [Miami International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami_International_Film_Festival "Miami International Film Festival") in March 2014.[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_God_%E2%80%93_10_Years_Later#cite_note-8)

## Reception

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=City_of_God_%E2%80%93_10_Years_Later&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Reception")]

The _[Miami New Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami\_New\_Times "Miami New Times")_ gave the documentary a mixed review, stating that "Fans of the original film will find plenty to enjoy, but as a standalone documentary about the effect of a hit movie on the poor community that birthed it, it comes up somewhat short."[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_God_%E2%80%93_10_Years_Later#cite_note-9)
