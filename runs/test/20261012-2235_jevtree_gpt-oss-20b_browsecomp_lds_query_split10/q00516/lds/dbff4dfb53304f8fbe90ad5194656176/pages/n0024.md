*   Actress
*   Producer

[](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0174225/bio/?ref_=nm_ov_bio_sm)

Conceição Senna was born in 1937 in Valente - Bahia. She was an actress and producer, known for [Chega de Saudade (2007)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0977642/?ref_=nm_ov_bio_lk), [Iracema: Uma Transa Amazônica (1975)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0126968/?ref_=nm_ov_bio_lk) and [Abrigo Nuclear (1981)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0194640/?ref_=nm_ov_bio_lk). She was married to [Orlando Senna](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0784395/?ref_=nm_ov_bio_lk). She died on 27 May 2020 in Rio de Janeiro, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.
