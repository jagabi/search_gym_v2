**Egízio Antônio Calloni** (born 6 December 1961) is a Brazilian actor, best known for _[Os Dias Eram Assim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Os\_Dias\_Eram\_Assim "Os Dias Eram Assim")_ (2017), _[Dois Irmãos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dois\_Irm%C3%A3os\_(miniseries) "Dois Irmãos (miniseries)")_ (2017), _[Brazilian Western](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian\_Western "Brazilian Western")_ (2013) and he also dubbed [Garfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garfield_(character) "Garfield (character)") in the [first film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garfield:_The_Movie "Garfield: The Movie") and the [second film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garfield:_A_Tail_of_Two_Kitties "Garfield: A Tail of Two Kitties").[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ant%C3%B4nio_Calloni#cite_note-1)[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ant%C3%B4nio_Calloni#cite_note-2)[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ant%C3%B4nio_Calloni#cite_note-3)[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ant%C3%B4nio_Calloni#cite_note-4)[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ant%C3%B4nio_Calloni#cite_note-5)
