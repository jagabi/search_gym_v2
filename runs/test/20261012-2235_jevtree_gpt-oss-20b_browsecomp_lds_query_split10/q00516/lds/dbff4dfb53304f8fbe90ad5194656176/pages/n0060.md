## Literature

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=City_of_God&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Literature")]

*   _[The City of God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_City\_of\_God "The City of God")_ (Latin: _De civitate Dei_), a 5th-century book by St. Augustine of Hippo
*   [_City of God_ (Cuadros work)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_God_(Cuadros_work) "City of God (Cuadros work)"), a 1994 collection of short stories and poems by Gil Cuadros
*   [_City of God_ (Holland novel)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_God_(Holland_novel) "City of God (Holland novel)"), a 1979 historical novel by Cecelia Holland
*   [_City of God_ (Lins novel)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_God_(Lins_novel) "City of God (Lins novel)") (Portuguese: _Cidade de Deus_), a 1997 novel by Paulo Lins set in the Rio de Janeiro neighborhood _Cidade de Deus_
*   _City of God_, a 2000 novel by [E. L. Doctorow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E._L._Doctorow "E. L. Doctorow")

## Places

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=City_of_God&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Places")]

*   [Cidade de Deus (Osasco)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cidade_de_Deus_(Osasco) "Cidade de Deus (Osasco)"), the Banco Bradesco headquarters in Osasco, São Paulo, Brazil
*   [Cidade de Deus, Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cidade_de_Deus,_Rio_de_Janeiro "Cidade de Deus, Rio de Janeiro"), a neighborhood of Rio de Janeiro
*   [Elohim City, Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elohim_City,_Oklahoma "Elohim City, Oklahoma"), Christian Identity community

## Film

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=City_of_God&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Film")]

*   [_City of God_ (2002 film)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_God_(2002_film) "City of God (2002 film)") (Portuguese: _Cidade de Deus_), a 2002 Brazilian film based on Lins' novel 
    *   _[City of God – 10 Years Later](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City\_of\_God\_%E2%80%93\_10\_Years\_Later "City of God – 10 Years Later")_, a 2012 Brazilian documentary about the 2002 film

*   [_City of God_ (2011 film)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_God_(2011_film) "City of God (2011 film)"), an Indian film

## Other arts and entertainment

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=City_of_God&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Other arts and entertainment")]

*   _[City of God: The Fight Rages On](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City\_of\_God:\_The\_Fight\_Rages\_On "City of God: The Fight Rages On")_, a 2024 Brazilian television series set two decades after the events of the 2002 film
*   _[City of the Gods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City\_of\_the\_Gods "City of the Gods")_, a 1987 adventure module for the _Dungeons & Dragons_ role-playing game
*   [City of Gods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Gods "City of Gods"), a 2022 song by Fivio Foreign, Kanye West, and Alicia Keys

## See also

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=City_of_God&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: See also")]

*   [Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerusalem "Jerusalem"), Israel, according to Psalm 46 in the Old Testament
*   [New Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Jerusalem "New Jerusalem"), a concept in the Abrahamic religions and Zionism
*   [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome "Rome"), since the diocese of the city is known as the Holy See and the Pope is the bishop of Rome 
    *   [Vatican City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vatican_City "Vatican City"), a sovereign state and an enclave within Rome

*   Theopolis or [Antioch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antioch "Antioch"), an ancient city in what is now Turkey called "the cradle of Christianity"
