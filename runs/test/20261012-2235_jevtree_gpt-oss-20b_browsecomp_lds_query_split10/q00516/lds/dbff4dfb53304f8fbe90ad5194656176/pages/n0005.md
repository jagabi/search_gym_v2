From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Brazilian television series

For the Brazilian film originally titled _Era uma Vez…_, see [Once Upon a Time in Rio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Once_Upon_a_Time_in_Rio "Once Upon a Time in Rio").

_**Era Uma Vez…**_ (English title: _**Once Upon a Time**_) is a Brazilian [telenovela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telenovela "Telenovela") produced by [Estúdios Globo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Est%C3%BAdios_Globo "Estúdios Globo") and aired by [Globo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TV_Globo "TV Globo") from March 30 to October 2, 1998, in 161 chapters, replacing _[Anjo Mau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anjo\_Mau\_(1997\_TV\_series) "Anjo Mau (1997 TV series)")_ and being replaced by _Pecado Capital_. It is written by Walther Negrão and directed by Jorge Fernando.
