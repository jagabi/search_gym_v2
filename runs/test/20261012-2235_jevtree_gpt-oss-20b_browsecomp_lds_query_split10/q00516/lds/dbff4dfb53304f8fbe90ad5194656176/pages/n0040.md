**Thiago dos Santos Martins**[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-:0-1) (born 19 September 1988) is a Brazilian actor and [singer-songwriter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singer-songwriter "Singer-songwriter").

## Career

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Thiago_Martins_(actor)&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Career")]

[![Image 7](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/89/Thiago_Martins_2006.jpg/250px-Thiago_Martins_2006.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Thiago_Martins_2006.jpg)

Martins in 2008

Martins was born on 19 September 1988 in [Vidigal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vidigal,_Rio_de_Janeiro "Vidigal, Rio de Janeiro"), a neighborhood of [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro "Rio de Janeiro").[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-:0-1) He had a difficult childhood, living with her mother and a brother in conditions of extreme [poverty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_in_Brazil "Poverty in Brazil") after his father abandoned the family.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-:1-2) Martins found a male [role model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Role_model "Role model") in his older brother Carlos André, who nearly died after being shot in the [_favela_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Favela "Favela") they lived in in 2002.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-:1-2) An avid fan of [football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football "Association football"), Martins trained with a team in [Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portugal "Portugal") and hoped to pursue a professional career in football when he was called for an important job as an actor: the 2004 telenovela _[Da Cor do Pecado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Da\_Cor\_do\_Pecado "Da Cor do Pecado")_.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-:1-2)

While he had started an acting career in theater in 1998, Martins got his first role in television at a [Christmas special](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christmas_special "Christmas special") by [Xuxa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xuxa "Xuxa") in 1999. From that moment his career opportunities increased, appearing in the highly acclaimed Brazilian film [_City of God_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_God_(2002_film) "City of God (2002 film)") (2002) and television series like _[Cidade dos Homens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cidade\_dos\_Homens "Cidade dos Homens")_ (2002) and _Da Cor do Pecado_ (2004).[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-3)[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-4)[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-5) After the success of his initial career, Martins continued working in important productions, including _[Belíssima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bel%C3%ADssima "Belíssima")_, _[Desejo Proibido](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desejo\_Proibido "Desejo Proibido")_, _[Insensato Coração](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insensato\_Cora%C3%A7%C3%A3o "Insensato Coração")_, [_Avenida Brasil_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avenida_Brasil_(TV_series) "Avenida Brasil (TV series)"), _[Flor do Caribe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flor\_do\_Caribe "Flor do Caribe")_, _[Império](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imp%C3%A9rio "Império")_, [_Babilônia_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babil%C3%B4nia_(TV_series) "Babilônia (TV series)"), _[Pega Pega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pega\_Pega "Pega Pega")_, and _[Amor de Mãe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amor\_de\_M%C3%A3e "Amor de Mãe")_.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-:2-6)[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-:3-7) In the 2020s, Martins has taken part in the TV series _[City of God: The Fight Rages On](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City\_of\_God:\_The\_Fight\_Rages\_On "City of God: The Fight Rages On")_ and the telenovelas _[Família é Tudo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fam%C3%ADlia\_%C3%A9\_Tudo "Família é Tudo")_ and [_Vale Tudo_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vale_Tudo_(2025_TV_series) "Vale Tudo (2025 TV series)").[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-:1-2)[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-8)[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-9)[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-:2-6)[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-10)

In cinema, aside from _City of God_, others of Martins' credits include _[Xuxa Gêmeas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xuxa\_G%C3%AAmeas "Xuxa Gêmeas")_ (2006), _[Once Upon a Time in Rio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Once\_Upon\_a\_Time\_in\_Rio "Once Upon a Time in Rio")_ (2008), _[Flordelis: Basta uma Palavra para Mudar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flordelis:\_Basta\_uma\_Palavra\_para\_Mudar "Flordelis: Basta uma Palavra para Mudar")_ (2009), _[The Silver Cliff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Silver\_Cliff "The Silver Cliff")_ (2011) and _[City of God – 10 Years Later](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City\_of\_God\_%E2%80%93\_10\_Years\_Later "City of God – 10 Years Later")_ (2013).[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-:1-2)[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-11)[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-:3-7)[[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-12)[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-13)

## Personal life

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Thiago_Martins_(actor)&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Personal life")]

Martins has been in a relationship with several well-known Brazilian actresses, including [Roberta Rodrigues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberta_Rodrigues "Roberta Rodrigues"), [Fernanda Paes Leme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernanda_Paes_Leme "Fernanda Paes Leme"), [Paloma Bernardi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paloma_Bernardi "Paloma Bernardi"), and model Talita Nogueira.[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-:3-7)[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Martins_(actor)#cite_note-:2-6)
