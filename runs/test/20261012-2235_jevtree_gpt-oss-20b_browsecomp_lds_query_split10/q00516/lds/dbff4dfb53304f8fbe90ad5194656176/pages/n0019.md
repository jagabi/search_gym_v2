Edna de Cássia was born on 31 July 1959 in Belém, Pará, Brazil. She is an actress, known for [Iracema: Uma Transa Amazônica (1975)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0126968/?ref_=nm_ov_bio_lk), [Era Uma Vez Iracema (2005)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt13417174/?ref_=nm_ov_bio_lk) and [Idade da Àgua (2018)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt13415520/?ref_=nm_ov_bio_lk).
