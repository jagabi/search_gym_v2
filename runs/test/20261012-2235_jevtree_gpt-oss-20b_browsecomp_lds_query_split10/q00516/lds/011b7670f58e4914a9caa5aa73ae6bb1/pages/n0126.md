# Trivia

## Schindler's List

Jump to Edit

When survivor [Ludmila Page](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm1059189/?ref_=tttrv_tr) was introduced to [Ralph Fiennes](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000146/?ref_=tttrv_tr) on the set, she began shaking uncontrollably, as he reminded her too much of the real [Amon Göth](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm7061784/?ref_=tttrv_tr).

Helpful•2.9K 21

[Steven Spielberg](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000229/?ref_=tttrv_tr) initially intended to make the film in Polish and German with English subtitles, but rethought the idea because he felt he wouldn't be able to accurately assess performances in unfamiliar languages.

Helpful•1.1K 7

At his insistence (citing that it would be "blood money"), all royalties and residuals from this movie that would normally have gone to [Steven Spielberg](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000229/?ref_=tttrv_tr) instead are given to the Shoah Foundation, which records and preserves written and videotaped testimonies from survivors of genocide worldwide, including the Holocaust.

Helpful•1.9K 17

[Steven Spielberg](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000229/?ref_=tttrv_tr) was able to get permission to film inside Auschwitz, but chose not to, out of respect for the victims, so the scenes of the death camp were filmed outside the gates on a set constructed in a mirror image of the real location on the other side.

Helpful•1.6K 13

When [Steven Spielberg](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000229/?ref_=tttrv_tr) first showed [John Williams](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0002354/?ref_=tttrv_tr) a cut of this movie, Williams was so moved he had to take a walk outside for several minutes to collect himself. Upon his return, he told Spielberg he deserved a better composer. Spielberg replied, "I know, but they're all dead."

## [Cameo](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0108052/trivia/?ref_=tt_ov_ql_3#cameo)

[Branko Lustig](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0527322/?ref_=tttrv_tr): Nightclub maître d'hotel in [Oskar Schindler](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0771861/?ref_=tttrv_tr)'s first scene. Lustig is one of this movie's producers and a Holocaust survivor (upon receiving his Oscar, he recited his serial number, A3317).
