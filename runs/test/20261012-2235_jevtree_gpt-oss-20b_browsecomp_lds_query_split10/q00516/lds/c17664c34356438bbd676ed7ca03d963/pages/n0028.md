Find what you’re looking for faster with generative AI

AI Overviews provide a snapshot of key information about a topic or question with links so you can easily explore more on the web.

When your question has questions, ask it all in one go

AI Overviews makes it easier than ever for people to find the information they need and discover the richness of the web.

Currently available in over 120 countries and territories, and 11 languages.

Ask questions in any way

Whether you’re using text, voice, or an image, you'll get relevant, reliable AI responses with links to dig deeper.

Currently available for English searches in the US
