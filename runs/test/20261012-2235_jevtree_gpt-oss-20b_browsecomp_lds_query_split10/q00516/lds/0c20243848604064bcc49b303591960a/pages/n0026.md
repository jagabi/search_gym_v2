Brazilian film director (1940–2026)

**Orlando Sales de Senna** (April 25, 1940 – June 9, 2026) was a Brazilian film director.

[![Image 5](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/68/Guia-Chapada-Diamantina-Orlando-Senna-Foto-Jorge-Alfredo.jpg/250px-Guia-Chapada-Diamantina-Orlando-Senna-Foto-Jorge-Alfredo.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Guia-Chapada-Diamantina-Orlando-Senna-Foto-Jorge-Alfredo.jpg)

Orlando Senna

## Life and career

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Orlando_Senna&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Life and career")]

Senna acted as co-director of the 1975 film _[Iracema: Uma Transa Amazônica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iracema:\_Uma\_Transa\_Amaz%C3%B4nica "Iracema: Uma Transa Amazônica")_, together with [Jorge Bodanzky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jorge_Bodanzky?action=edit&redlink=1 "Jorge Bodanzky (page does not exist)"), which was censored during the [Brazilian military regime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_dictatorship_in_Brazil "Military dictatorship in Brazil").[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_Senna#cite_note-1) He was linked to the [Cinema Novo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinema_Novo "Cinema Novo") movement.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_Senna#cite_note-2)[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_Senna#cite_note-3)

Senna died on June 9, 2026, at the age of 86.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_Senna#cite_note-4)
