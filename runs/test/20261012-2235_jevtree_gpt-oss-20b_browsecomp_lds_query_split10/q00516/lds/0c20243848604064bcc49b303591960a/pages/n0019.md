### [The Devils](https://www.filmlinc.org/films/the-devils/)
Newly restored 35mm print plays through October 22 only

Ken Russell’s infamous adaptation of Aldous Huxley’s novel, about the apparent possession of a group of nuns after one (Vanessa Redgrave) accuses a priest (Oliver Reed) of collaborating with the devil, is at long last available in its full, uncensored splendor.

[Get Tickets](https://www.filmlinc.org/films/the-devils/)

Film

### [Once Upon a Time in Harlem](https://www.filmlinc.org/films/once-upon-a-time-in-harlem/)
Opens Friday

In 1972, legendary filmmaker William Greaves gathered an extraordinary group of artists, writers, actors, musicians, and thinkers of the Harlem Renaissance. After more than 50 years, his son David Greaves has completed his breathtaking miracle of a movie.

[Get Tickets](https://www.filmlinc.org/films/once-upon-a-time-in-harlem/)

Film

### [Fatherland](https://www.filmlinc.org/films/fatherland/)
Opens October 23

Oscar-nominated director Paweł Pawlikowski (_Cold War_, NYFF56) won Best Director at Cannes for his most potent film yet, following novelist Thomas Mann (Hanns Zischler) and his daughter Erika Mann (Sandra Hüller) on a road trip across Germany in the aftermath of World War II. Poland’s entry for Best International Feature Film at the 2027 Academy Awards.

[Get Tickets](https://www.filmlinc.org/films/fatherland/)

[![Image 29](https://www.filmlinc.org/cdn-cgi/image/width=800%2Cformat=auto%2Cquality=85/https://wp.filmlinc.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Possible-Love_NYFF64_01.jpeg)](https://www.filmlinc.org/films/possible-love/)

Film

### [Possible Love](https://www.filmlinc.org/films/possible-love/)
Opens October 23

The lives of two married couples from different social strata collide in Lee Chang-dong’s brilliantly unfolding narrative about economic precarity, in which a laid-off factory worker and his wife become the camera subjects for a documentary filmmaker. Winner of the Silver Lion at the 2026 Venice Film Festival and South Korea’s entry for Best International Feature Film at the 2027 Academy Awards.

[Get Tickets](https://www.filmlinc.org/films/possible-love/)

Film

### [Bitter Christmas](https://www.filmlinc.org/films/bitter-christmas/)
Opens November 13

The incomparable Pedro Almodóvar explores the artist’s tenuous relationship with fiction and reality in this film about an actress writing a script inspired by the lives of those around her—which is itself the invention of an Almodóvar-like filmmaker.

[Get Tickets](https://www.filmlinc.org/films/bitter-christmas/)

Film

### [Minotaur](https://www.filmlinc.org/films/minotaur/)
Opens November 20

Winner of the Grand Prix at Cannes, Russian filmmaker Andrey Zvyagintsev’s riveting, razor-sharp tale of personal, patriarchal, and governmental corruption follows a wealthy CEO who begins to suspect his wife is having an extramarital affair. France’s entry for Best International Feature Film at the 2027 Academy Awards.

[Get Tickets](https://www.filmlinc.org/films/minotaur/)
