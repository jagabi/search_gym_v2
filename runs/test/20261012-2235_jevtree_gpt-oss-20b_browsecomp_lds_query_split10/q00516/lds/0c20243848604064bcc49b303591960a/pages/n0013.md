Orlando Senna was born on 25 April 1940 in Afrânio Peixoto, Bahia, Brazil. He was a writer and director, known for [Iracema: Uma Transa Amazônica (1975)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0126968/?ref_=nm_ov_bio_lk), [Diamante bruto (1978)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0137464/?ref_=nm_ov_bio_lk) and [Longe do Paraíso (2020)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt10217460/?ref_=nm_ov_bio_lk). He was married to [Conceição Senna](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0174225/?ref_=nm_ov_bio_lk). He died on 9 June 2026 in Rio de Janeiro, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.
