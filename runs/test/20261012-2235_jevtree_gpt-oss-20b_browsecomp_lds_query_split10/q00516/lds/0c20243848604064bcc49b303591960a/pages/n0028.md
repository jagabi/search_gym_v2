**Paulo César de Campos Velho**, better known as **Paulo César Pereio** (19 October 1940 – 12 May 2024), was a Brazilian actor.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paulo_C%C3%A9sar_Pereio#cite_note-1)

## Activism

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Paulo_C%C3%A9sar_Pereio&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Activism")]

Pereio was known for being an ardent [atheist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atheism "Atheism") and [communist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communism "Communism") who was heavily opposed to the statue of [Christ the Redeemer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christ_the_Redeemer_(statue) "Christ the Redeemer (statue)"), stating that it violates Brazil's secular constitution and ruins the beauty of the mountain. From the late 20th century he gathered signatures for its removal.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paulo_C%C3%A9sar_Pereio#cite_note-2)

## Death

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Paulo_C%C3%A9sar_Pereio&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Death")]

Pereio died on 12 May 2024, at the age of 83.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paulo_C%C3%A9sar_Pereio#cite_note-3)
