Jorge Bodanzky was born on 22 December 1942 in São Paulo, São Paulo, Brazil. He is a cinematographer and director, known for [Iracema: Uma Transa Amazônica (1975)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0126968/?ref_=nm_ov_bio_lk), [Jakobine (1978)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0077764/?ref_=nm_ov_bio_lk) and [O Terceiro Milênio (1982)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0162938/?ref_=nm_ov_bio_lk).
