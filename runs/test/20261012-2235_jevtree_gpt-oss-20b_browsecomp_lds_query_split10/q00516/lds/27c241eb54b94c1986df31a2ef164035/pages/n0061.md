# About the Rosslyn BID

Funded by a special tax paid by property owners within the BID boundaries, the Rosslyn BID sets the standard for the future of urban living and working. We enhance Rosslyn’s appeal by providing services that enhance, grow, and maintain the community, making Rosslyn a thriving destination for professionals, businesses, residents, and visitors alike.

## Rosslyn BID Boundaries

The Rosslyn Business Improvement District (BID) is a 17-block mixed-use, urban center in the heart of Arlington, Virginia, just minutes from Washington, D.C. As a vibrant and connected hub, the Rosslyn BID enhances and maintains the neighborhood through a public-private partnership with Arlington County.
