# Find your Chinese zodiac sign!

_The information below is courtesy of the Chinese Embassy._

The Chinese zodiac, or shengxiao, refers to the circle of 12 animals that measure the cycles of time. Signs or animals are determined by the lunar year in which you were born.

Different people born under each zodiac sign are believed to have different personalities that represent animals of the year. You can determine your animal sign by referring to the year you were born. Check out the real animal in your heart and the personalities they represent!

## **Rat**

**Birth year:**1912, 1924, 1936, 1948, 1960, 1972, 1984, 1996, 2008, 2020

_Intelligent, adaptable, quick-witted, charming, artistic, sociable_

## **Ox**
