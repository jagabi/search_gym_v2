# Explore Rosslyn

Rosslyn has something to pique every curiosity, offering a vibrant mix of dining, attractions, and modern amenities — all set against stunning views of D.C. and the Potomac River. From business to leisure with the perfect blend of energy, convenience, and community, you can reach new heights in Rosslyn!

## Find Your Next Adventure

Dining

From food halls and fast-casual to happy hour and date night dining, Rosslyn has a variety of cuisines to satisfy any craving.

[Grab a bite](https://www.rosslynva.org/explore/dining)

Lodging

Rosslyn is an ideal base for your visit to Arlington and the D.C. area. Whether you're seeking luxury or budget-friendly accommodations, there's something to suit every traveler.

[Book your stay](https://www.rosslynva.org/explore/lodging)

Retail and Services

With everyday essentials like a post office, grocery stores and pharmacies, plus specialty service spas and medical offices, Rosslyn offers convenient access to everything you need — all within walking distance.

[Explore services](https://www.rosslynva.org/explore/retail-and-services)

Living Here

Experience a dynamic urban lifestyle with diverse residential options just minutes from world-class amenities, blending city convenience with community charm.

[Find your home](https://www.rosslynva.org/explore/living-here)

Parks and Attractions

With historic landmarks, green space, public art, and high walkability, there’s ample opportunity to learn and explore as you stroll Rosslyn.

[Discover Rosslyn](https://www.rosslynva.org/explore/parks-and-cultural-attractions)
