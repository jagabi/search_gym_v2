# Kūkala Nūhou Home

**Kūkala Nūhou**, meaning “_to announce or herald the news_”, is UH Hilo’s communications platform, showcasing stories about our academic programs, innovative research, campus news, events, and key messages from leadership.

Our Kūkala Nūhou **e-Newsletter** is a great way to stay connected.
