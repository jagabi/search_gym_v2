# Employment Opportunities

## **Associate Marketing Manager**

[Apply Here](https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/R9C8VLK)

**Location:**Rosslyn, Virginia

**Work Schedule:**Full-time, hybrid (3 days per week in office).Occasional evening and weekend hours may be required to support Rosslyn BID events.

**Position Overview**  
The Associate Marketing Manager plays a key role in advancing our brand, marketing initiatives, and community impact. Reporting directly to the Director of Marketing and Communications, this position supports the planning, development, and execution of integrated marketing, events, and communications activities, working closely with the Digital Content Coordinator and cross-functional team members.

This role manages day-to-day marketing activities and serves as a primary point of contact for vendors, partners, and community stakeholders to support brand success. The Associate Marketing Manager evaluates campaign performance and is responsible for implementing projects aligned with organizational goals. The ideal candidate is a strong writer, a detail-oriented project manager, and a collaborative team member who can balance multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.

### **Key Responsibilities**

**Content & Campaign Management**

*   Manage the organization’s content calendar in coordination with the Digital Content Coordinator, ensuring timely and strategic delivery across channels. 
*   Support media and promotional planning for marketing campaigns, including timelines, assets, and performance tracking. 
*   Write and/or edit articles, blog posts, newsletter features, and website copy using AP Style. 
*   Develop campaign and promotional copy, collaborating with the Digital Content Coordinator — who leads social and newsletter copywriting — to refine messaging for tone and brand alignment, contribute integrated content ideas, and provide on-site social coverage as needed.
*   Help coordinate targeted e-blasts and segmented email campaigns for stakeholders and partners. 
*   Analyze campaign performance and audience data to guide decision-making and recommend refinements to increase engagement and impact.

**Brand & Creative**

*   Assist in managing brand assets, ensuring consistent application of visual identity across materials. 
*   Coordinate creative requests and graphic design needs; produce basic creative assets using Canva or Adobe Creative Suite when necessary.
*   Partner with team members and external vendors to support event promotions, branding, and on-site activations.

**Digital & Web Experience**

*   Oversee website content updates and user experience enhancements, collaborating with internal stakeholders and external partners.
*   Monitor and report on website analytics and search engine performance (SEO), recommending improvements to increase traffic and engagement.
*   Provide guidance and support to junior team members, including offering feedback on content development, timelines, and creative execution.

**Cross-Team Collaboration and Communications**

*   Work closely with the Events Coordinator to support event marketing, promotions, and integrated activation plans.
*   Serve as a key point of contact and collaborate with external partners, stakeholders, vendors, and internal clients to support marketing and communications efforts, including reports, presentations,emails,and other collateral needs that serve the broader neighborhood and community, delivering strong customer service and representing the brand with professionalism.

### **Qualifications & Skills**

*   3–5 years of experience in marketing, communications, content management, or related field.
*   Strong writing and editing skills with foundational knowledge of AP Style.
*   Excellent project management and organizational skills; ability to manage multiple projects and deadlines simultaneously.
*   Detail-oriented, proactive, and responsive with strong time-management abilities.
*   Ability to work independently as well as collaboratively across teams.
*   Service-oriented mindset and strong interpersonal communication skills.
*   Basic design skills using Canva and/or Adobe Creative Suite;experience designing slide decks or presentations.
*   Experience with website management platforms and familiarity with analytics and SEO best practices preferred.
*   Comfortable adapting quickly to evolving priorities and community needs.

### **Work Style & Culture**

This role is ideal for someone who enjoys creative problem-solving, values collaboration, and is energized by opportunities to contribute to a dynamic and growing urban community. The Associate Marketing Manager should bring curiosity, initiative, and enthusiasm for supporting meaningful engagement and exceptional experiences in the Rosslyn neighborhood.We offer a highly competitive salary complemented by a comprehensive benefits package.
