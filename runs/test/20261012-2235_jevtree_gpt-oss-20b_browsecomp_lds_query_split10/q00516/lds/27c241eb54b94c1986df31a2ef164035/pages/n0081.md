The charts above list the Moon’s astrological place for each day in the months of October and November 2026. This is determined by where the Moon is located at 12:00 P.M. ET along the ecliptic. The [ecliptic](https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/plane-of-ecliptic/), simply put, is the plane of Earth’s orbit around the Sun. It’s broken up into twelve 30° sections named for the zodiac constellations that had been within them more than 2,000 years ago. However, the actual constellations (from our perspective on Earth) have since shifted location and may no longer precisely align with these 30° sections. Learn more about the [ecliptic plane.](https://www.almanac.com/what-age-aquarius)

## What Is a Moon Sign?

You are probably more familiar with your monthly zodiac sign, your “Sun Sign.” The Sun takes around a month to transit through a zodiac sign, which is why each month is assigned a sign (Aries, Taurus, etc.). [Which zodiac sign are you?](https://www.almanac.com/tool/what-is-my-zodiac-sign)

The Moon also travels through each [zodiac sign](https://www.almanac.com/12-astrology-zodiac-signs), though much more quickly. It usually takes less than 3 days. We refer to the sign of the zodiac that the Moon is transiting through as the “Moon sign.”

### What Does the Moon Sign Mean?

Like the Sun, the Moon also plays a role in our lives. From controlling the tides with its gravitational force to tracking time as a calendar of the heavens, the Moon has been a driving force for many.

## How Does the Moon’s Sign Affect You?

Ancient astrologers made the connection that the Moon’s position in the zodiac influenced a specific part of the body, starting at the beginning of the zodiac cycle, with Aries being attributed to the head, moving down the body to the feet (Pisces). The [Man of Signs](https://www.almanac.com/man-signs-zodiac-man) is a visual depiction of this, allowing users to know which areas of their body may require special care and more attention during certain times of the month.

By aligning your life with the Moon’s astrological position, you can also enhance your gardening practices, plan activities and events (such as [setting eggs](https://www.almanac.com/setting-chicken-eggs-moons-sign)), and optimize your daily routines (see [our best days for activities](https://www.almanac.com/bestdays/timetable)).
