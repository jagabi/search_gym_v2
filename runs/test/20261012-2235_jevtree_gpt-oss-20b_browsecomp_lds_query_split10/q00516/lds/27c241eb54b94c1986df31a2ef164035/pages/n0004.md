## Year of the Goat || The Five Elements: Which Type of Goat Are You?

In Chinese astrology, each zodiac sign is further shaped by one of the Five Elements 五行 (wǔ xíng): Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water. These cycle every 60 years, so two people born in different Goat years can have quite different personalities.

**Wood Goat 木羊 (mù yáng), 1955, 2015**  
The most sociable of all Goats. Wood Goats are generous, warm-hearted and excellent at building community. They’re natural peacekeepers who genuinely care about fairness. The risk? They can overextend themselves trying to please everyone.

**Fire Goat 火羊 (huǒ yáng), 1967, 2027**  
Fire Goats have more drive and ambition than the sign’s reputation suggests. They’re passionate, expressive and charismatic. They chase their creative visions hard, but they need emotional support from those around them to sustain momentum.

**Earth Goat 土羊 (tǔ yáng), 1979**  
Calm, steady and practical. Earth Goats are the most self-sufficient of the group. They’re less swayed by others’ opinions and more likely to quietly build something lasting. Family security matters deeply to them.

**Metal Goat 金羊 (jīn yáng), 1931, 1991**  
Metal Goats have a determined streak that surprises people. Behind the gentle exterior is real conviction. They can be stubborn when they believe they’re right, and they hold high standards, for themselves and for others.

**Water Goat 水羊 (shuǐ yáng), 1943, 2003**  
The most intuitive and empathetic Goat. Water Goats are sensitive to mood and atmosphere, sometimes to the point of anxiety. They’re deeply creative and thrive in nurturing environments. They need to be careful not to let others take advantage of their giving nature.

## Year of the Goat || Goat, Sheep or Ram? The 羊 Question

Here’s something that confuses people right away: is it the Year of the Goat, the Year of the Sheep, or the Year of the Ram?

The honest answer: all three. The Chinese character 羊 (yáng) covers the whole family, goats, sheep, and rams. Unlike in English, Chinese doesn’t force you to choose between them.

When the zodiac was formalised, the animal simply represented 羊, a creature that was central to agriculture across ancient China. Different regions and translators have landed on different English equivalents over the centuries.

In mainland China today, 山羊 (shānyáng, literally ‘mountain goat’) is the image most commonly associated with the sign. But you’ll see ‘Sheep’ used just as frequently in older texts and among Chinese diaspora communities, particularly those with Cantonese roots.

For what it’s worth, we use ‘Goat’ here because it’s the most widely recognised term in contemporary English-language discussions of the zodiac. But if your grandmother calls it the Year of the Sheep, she’s not wrong. 🐑

It’s not uncommon for parents around the world to choose a baby name based on its meaning. The same is true in China, only here parents may _also_ choose their children’s names based on their zodiac sign’s auspicious characters.

## Year Of The Goat || Chinese Dishes Named After the Goat

Like in many other countries, lamb is a popular meat found in many dishes. As a ‘warm’ food, you’ll mainly find it in delicacies from colder regions, such as those in the north. Here are some to look out for and try.  
  
**Lamb Skewers  羊肉串  (yáng ròu chuàn)**

![Image 13](https://ltl-school.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Lamb-Skewer-in-Chinese-Year-of-the-Goat-in-Chinese-Zodiac-1.jpg)
Lamb skewers are a popular street food dish in China, particularly in Beijing and other northern regions.

_Chunks of tender lamb meat are marinated in a delicious mixture of spices and then grilled over an open flame._

They are typically served hot and seasoned with cumin, chilli powder, and salt, and best eaten on small roadside tables.

**Lamb Soup  羊肉汤  (yáng ròu tāng)**

Lamb soup is a hearty dish commonly found in regions with a colder climate. It consists of tender lamb meat simmered in a flavorful broth with vegetables, such as carrots, potatoes, and onions. Perfect for a cold winter’s day!

**Stir-Fried Lamb with Noodles  羊肉炒面  (yáng ròu chǎo miàn)**

Stir-fried lamb with noodles is a savoury and satisfying dish from Xinjiang, in northwestern China. Thinly sliced lamb meat is stir-fried with onions, bell peppers, and spices, then tossed with chewy hand-pulled noodles.

**You’ll find this in Lanzhou Noodle shop**s (兰州拉面店 Lánzhōu lāmiàn diàn) across the country.

### Year Of The Goat || The Influence of The Chinese Zodiac On A Larger Scale

An individual’s decision of who to marry or how to name their child might seem insignificant in the grand scheme of things.

But when enough of China’s 1.3 billion people make them, small individual choices can have a _huge_ real-world impact on things as important as the population.

> And because the Year of the Goat is considered an unlucky year to have children, many Chinese parents will actively avoid falling pregnant around this time.

So unlike the Year of the Dragon, when birth rates rise rapidly, the Year of the Goat sees a sharp decline. This may sound silly, but it has a lasting effect on Chinese society, from the number of kids in a school year to the demand for national services.

### Year of the Goat || FAQs

When is the next Year of the Goat?

The Chinese zodiac calendar is 12 years long, which means the next Year of the Horse will be 2027. Previous years included **​​**​​​​1919, 1931, 1943, 1955, 1967, 1979, 1991, 2003, 2015.

What is my Chinese zodiac sign?

Use [our Zodiac calculator](https://ltl-school.com/chinese-zodiac-calculator/) to find out!

What signs is the Goat most compatible with?

People born in the Year of the Goat are romantically compatible with Horses, Rabbits, Pigs.

Is the Year of the Goat lucky?

No. The Year of the Goat is considered unlucky in Chinese culture due to the fact that goats are not natural leaders. They are thought to have bad luck in love and life in general.

Why Is the Goat Considered the “Unluckiest” Chinese Zodiac?

The idea that the Goat (羊) is unlucky largely comes from an old saying: **十羊九不全 (shí yáng jiǔ bù quán)** – literally “out of ten goats, nine are not complete.” Over time, this phrase became linked to people born in the Year of the Goat, suggesting that most Goat-year births would face hardship or an “imperfect” life.

What is the Year of the Goat personality like?

People born in the Year of the Goat are calm, gentle and kind hearted. They care deeply about the people around them and will do their best to keep others happy. This can sometimes mean they follow the crowd and try hard to please people.

What comes after the year of the Goat?

The Year of the Monkey follows the Year of the Goat.

Will 2026 be a good year for Goats?

2026 is the Year of the Horse, and for Goats it’s a mixed but generally positive year. Horse and Goat share compatible energy in the Chinese zodiac, so relationships and creative pursuits are the strongest areas. Career progress is possible but requires effort, and Goats are advised to avoid large speculative investments in the second half of the year and to build in extra rest to cope with the Horse year’s fast pace.
