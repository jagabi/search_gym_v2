# Rosslyn BID Staff

A small but dynamic team, the Rosslyn Business Improvement District (BID) staff members are agile and creative in their tireless pursuit of making Rosslyn great for those who live, work, and play in the neighborhood. Learn more about[Rosslyn BID programming](https://www.rosslynva.org/about/rosslyn-bid)[](https://www.rosslynva.org/about/contact/employment).

![Image 2: Mary-Claire Burick](https://img.ctykit.com/cdn/va-rosslyn/images/tr:w-900,h-900,fo-auto/mcb_headshot_web_600x600.jpg)

Mary-Claire Burick

President

As the Rosslyn BID’s president, Mary-Claire works with local businesses, residents, and government officials on the county, state, and federal level to ensure Rosslyn flourishes as an urban center. In her role, she helps to position businesses for success by building engagement, offering highly curated resources and leading change.

Previously, Mary-Claire was President of MC Strategy, a consulting firm helping leaders build healthy and effective organizations. She has also served as Vice President of Operations for Allbritton Communications, leading ABC-7, NewsChannel 8, and POLITICO. Earlier in her career, she helped launch the Fox News Channel, where she served as Operations Manager.

Currently, she serves on the board of directors for the NOVA Chamber of Commerce, the International Downtown Association (IDA), and the Greater Washington Board of Trade; she is a past board member of the Arlington Chamber of Commerce. Most recently, she was presented with the 2023 IDA Inspired Leader Award, and was previously named to the Washington Business Journal’s Power 100 Class of 2022 and the Women Who Mean Business Class of 2011. Outside of the office, you’ll likely find Mary-Claire out on the water and enjoying the sunshine any chance she gets!

![Image 3: Victor Carcamo](https://img.ctykit.com/cdn/va-rosslyn/images/tr:w-900,h-900,fo-auto/vc.png)

Victor Carcamo

Vice President, Operations & Public Safety

[vcarcamo@rosslynva.org](mailto:vcarcamo@rosslynva.org)

Victor is the vice president of operations and public for the Rosslyn BID. He oversees an integrated operations program that combines public safety, cleanliness, homeless outreach, and beautification—an intentionally holistic operations program to enhance Rosslyn.He also serves as the main point of contact for property owners and managers as well as the BID liaison for residents.

Victor has more than 15 years of experience in customer service and management in Southern California and Northern Virginia. Previously, he worked as an Operations Manager for Block by Block and managed the daily work of the BID’s Ambassadors, who welcome people to Rosslyn by keeping the streets clean and helping people find their way, whether they are going to an event, looking for parking or seeking restaurant recommendations.

Victor attended California State University in Los Angeles where he studied physical education on a soccer scholarship and completed a recording engineer program at the Los Angeles Film School. He is a former professional soccer player (Lulea FC in Sweden) and also an ACE certified personal trainer and has lead the BID’s popular bootcamp exercise classes in Gateway Park for the last seven years. He is bilingual in English and Spanish. When Victor is not at work you can find him at Gold’s Gym lifting weights, playing soccer, or looking for a new place to eat tacos!

![Image 4: Alli Henry](https://img.ctykit.com/cdn/va-rosslyn/images/tr:w-900,h-900,fo-auto/ah.png)

Alli Henry

Senior Community Planning Director

[ahenry@rosslynva.org](mailto:ahenry@rosslynva.org)

Alli Henry is the senior community planning director at the Rosslyn BID. In her role, Alli collaborates with diverse Arlington County partners and neighborhood stakeholders to support the BID’s community planning efforts, including site plan and development projects, public space enhancements and placemaking initiatives. As an engaged Arlington resident, Alli is guided by her people-first planning approach and passion for creating more socially inviting and connected communities.

Prior to joining the BID, Alli worked for Arlington County Government’s Transportation Division (DOT) as a Community Engagement Specialist. As a member of DOT’s Leadership Team, her work focused on advancing the County’s Smart Growth vision and multimodal transportation options designed for all ages and abilities. With over ten years of private and public sector experience, Alli also worked for Arlington County’s Commuter Service Division as WalkArlington’s Program Manager and Arlington Transportation Partner’s Multifamily Residential Development Manager.

Alli has a B.A. in Communications and Public Relations from George Mason University, and a Master's in Regional and Community Planning from the University of British Columbia in Vancouver, Canada.

![Image 5: Jenna Sobray](https://img.ctykit.com/cdn/va-rosslyn/images/tr:w-900,h-900,fo-auto/js.png)

Jenna Sobray

Marketing & Communications Director

[jsobray@rosslynva.org](mailto:jsobray@rosslynva.org)

Jenna Sobray currently serves as the marketing and communications director for the Rosslyn BID. In this role, she leads efforts to promote Rosslyn using creative strategies to elevate the brand, drive awareness and boost community engagement. Jenna oversees a team and multiple vendors, managing marketing, PR, digital, web, and social media activities.

With over 15 years of experience, Jenna is a forward-thinking leader dedicated to engaging diverse stakeholders through innovative marketing strategies and storytelling. Her marketing and communications background spans creative direction, research, PR strategy, event marketing, program management, and brand architecture.

Previously, Jenna headed the marketing team at the NFL Players Association, overseeing integrated marketing strategies for NFL Players Inc. While there she created cohesive campaigns across channels, launched ambassador programs and unique brands, and developed strategic media partnerships. At Personify,an association management software company,she pioneered a content marketing strategy across web, social, and video channels, enhancing demand generation and brand awareness.

A graduate of Virginia Tech, Jenna's passion for bringing brand stories to life is rooted in her background in communications and theatre arts.

![Image 6: Laura Doolan](https://img.ctykit.com/cdn/va-rosslyn/images/tr:w-900,h-900,fo-auto/ld.png)

Laura Doolan

Senior Finance & Human Resources Manager

[ldoolan@rosslynva.org](mailto:ldoolan@rosslynva.org)

As the senior finance and human resources manager, Laura manages the BID’s bookkeeping, as she prepares and sends checks, assists with auditing, and prepares financial reports as needed. On a daily basis, Laura helps us all by organizing operations and streamlining processes to keep the office running smoothly. With a desk front and center, Laura’s is the first smiling face you’ll see when you enter the BID’s office and is there to help direct you with any questions or needs.

Laura has extensive experience as an executive assistant, office manager, and bookkeeper. Not to mention her three years on tour as a travel coordinator for Riverdance, the famous traveling traditional Irish music and dance show!

Originally from a seaside town near Dublin, Ireland, Laura still feels strong ties to her Irish roots. Currently, she lives in Alexandria with her husband and two children. When not in the office, you can find Laura enjoying family time while watching her favorite soccer team, Everton, play on TV.

![Image 7: Solomon McKenzie](https://img.ctykit.com/cdn/va-rosslyn/images/tr:w-900,h-900,fo-auto/sm.png)

Solomon McKenzie

Placemaking & Public Space Manager

[smckenzie@rosslynva.org](mailto:smckenzie@rosslynva.org)

Solomon is the placemaking and public space manager for the Rosslyn BID where he designs and implements placemaking projects including but not limited to events setup, neighborhood beautification, space activation, and a variety of pilot projects.

Solomon brings a blend of technical expertise and creative problem-solving to placemaking and design. With a Bachelor’s degree in Biomedical Engineering from the Rochester Institute of Technology and a Master’s degree in Community Planning, Public Policy, Architecture, and Design from the College of Charleston, Solomon combines analytical precision with planning knowledge to create data-centric design solutions within the Rosslyn Community.

His previous professional experience includes being a data analyst for innovative drug creation and associated clinical trials and as a consultant at an urban planning and design firm, where he focused on crafting design solutions for underdeveloped neighborhoods. Solomon has lead and contributed to many projects including the 2025-2030 Rhode Island State Economic Development Strategy, the DC Public Realm Study Plan, and the Downtown Raleigh Retail Plan.

![Image 8: Jackie Evans](https://img.ctykit.com/cdn/va-rosslyn/images/tr:w-900,h-900,fo-auto/image-14-1.jpg)

Jackie Evans

Associate Marketing Manager

[jevans@rosslynva.org](mailto:jevans@rosslynva.org)

Jackie Evans is the Associate Marketing Manager for the Rosslyn BID, supporting integrated marketing and content efforts that build neighborhood awareness while helping manage the BID’s brand and promote community activities and initiatives.In her role, she oversees advertising and content strategy, manages the BID’s brand identity, and develops integrated marketing campaigns that drive engagement and elevate Rosslyn's visibility.

Jackie brings a background in destination marketing, economic development, and urban planning to the marketing and events team. Prior to joining the BID, she managed digital content for the Arlington Convention and Visitors Service, the County’s Destination Marketing Organization, where she helped drive visitation to Arlington while frequently spotlighting Rosslyn. Before relocating to Northern Virginia, Jackie held marketing roles with local governments and nonprofit organizations in and around Richmond, Virginia,and also worked as a freelancer for the Virginia Chapter of the American Planning Association.She earned a Bachelor’s degree in Urban and Regional Studies with a minor in Psychology from Virginia Commonwealth University.

As a Northern Virginia native, Jackie is a devoted fan of the Washington Capitals and D.C. sports. Outside of work, you can find her at a sporting event, at the gym, spending time with her cats, Dorito and Ruffles, or playing tourist in her own town with her husband around the DMV.

![Image 9: Christina Carlston](https://img.ctykit.com/cdn/va-rosslyn/images/tr:w-900,h-900,fo-auto/cc.png)

Christina Carlston

Event Planner

[ccarlston@rosslynva.org](mailto:ccarlston@rosslynva.org)

Christina is the event planner for the Rosslyn BID, supporting the coordination and execution of the BID’s 100+ events each year building community and creating experiences for those visiting or staying in the neighborhood.

Before her role with the BID, Christina held previous event positions for Breaux Vineyards and Creighton Farms executing a variety of public and private events. She also worked for the major league soccer team, D.C. United, as an Account Executive and supported the launch of their USL affiliate, Loudoun United F.C.

A graduate and collegiate athlete of the University of Mary Washington, Christina received a Bachelor of Arts in Communications and Digital Studies with a minor in Sport Management while playing on the university’s women’s soccer team. She additionally graduated from the Virginia Commonwealth University with a master's degree in Sport Leadership from the internationally acclaimed program, the Center for Sport Leadership.

Christina is a Leesburg, Virginia, native; when offline, you can find her exploring the many wineries Loudoun County has to offer, catching a professional or collegiate sporting event, or spending time with her family and friends.

![Image 10: Malaika Ravindran](https://img.ctykit.com/cdn/va-rosslyn/images/tr:w-900,h-900,fo-auto/mr.png)

Malaika Ravindran

Digital Content Coordinator

[mravindran@rosslynva.org](mailto:mravindran@rosslynva.org)

Malaika is the digital content coordinator at the Rosslyn BID, which includes writing the weekly newsletter and managing the BID’s social media channels, helping to promote Rosslyn events, campaigns, and businesses.

Before working at the BID, Malaika started her career as a junior copywriter at design agency Instrument, and worked as a marketing and social media freelancer for consulting company Meytier, PR and marketing agency Kahani Digital, and news website Urban Asian.

Malaika graduated from D.C.-based American University in 2022, with a B.A. in Journalism and a minor in Political Science, along with an Advanced Leadership certificate. Originally from Basking Ridge, New Jersey, Malaika has been in the DMV area for the past five years, and outside of work, she enjoys perusing through local thrift stores, reading at home, and dancing.

![Image 11: Margo Johnson](https://img.ctykit.com/cdn/va-rosslyn/images/tr:w-900,h-900,fo-auto/untitled-design-15.png)

Margo Johnson

Manager, Strategic Initiatives & Executive Operations

[mjohnson@rosslynva.org](mailto:mjohnson@rosslynva.org)

Margo Johnson is the Manager of Strategic Initiatives & Executive Operations at the Rosslyn BID, where she works closely with the President, leadership team, Board of Directors, and community stakeholders to advance key organizational priorities. Through cross-functional collaboration, relationship building, and project leadership, she helps drive efforts that strengthen operations, foster stakeholder engagement, and support Rosslyn's evolving business community and long-term success.

Margo joined the Rosslyn BID after several years in mortgage lending and commercial real estate, where she developed a strong foundation in relationship management, business development, and client service. Her diverse professional background, combined with a passion for building connections and solving complex challenges, shapes her approach to supporting strategic initiatives, organizational growth, and engagement across the Rosslyn community.

A University of Kentucky alum, she earned her Bachelor of Science in Business Administration with concentrations in Marketing and Management, graduating summa cum laude. Margo is an avid Wildcats fan, especially during March Madness! Outside the office, Margo enjoys discovering new foodie spots around the DMV, spending time with her husband and their cat, Tux, fitness training, and testing out new baking recipes.
