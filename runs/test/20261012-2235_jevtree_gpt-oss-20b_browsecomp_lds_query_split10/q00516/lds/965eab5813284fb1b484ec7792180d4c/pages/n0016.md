Bill Condon was born on 22 October 1955 in New York City, New York, USA. He is a director and writer, known for [Dreamgirls (2006)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0443489/?ref_=nm_ov_bio_lk), [Gods and Monsters (1998)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0120684/?ref_=nm_ov_bio_lk) and [Kinsey (2004)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0362269/?ref_=nm_ov_bio_lk).
