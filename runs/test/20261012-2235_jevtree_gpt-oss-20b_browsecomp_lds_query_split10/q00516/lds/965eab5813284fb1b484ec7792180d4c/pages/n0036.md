_**The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn – Part 1**_ is a 2011 American [romantic fantasy film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romantic_fantasy_film "Romantic fantasy film") directed by [Bill Condon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Condon "Bill Condon") from a screenplay by [Melissa Rosenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melissa_Rosenberg "Melissa Rosenberg"), based on the 2008 novel _[Breaking Dawn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breaking\_Dawn "Breaking Dawn")_ by [Stephenie Meyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephenie_Meyer "Stephenie Meyer").[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-McIntyre-5) It is the sequel to _[The Twilight Saga: Eclipse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Twilight\_Saga:\_Eclipse "The Twilight Saga: Eclipse")_ (2010) and the fourth installment in [_The Twilight Saga_ film series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga_(film_series) "The Twilight Saga (film series)"). The film stars [Kristen Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristen_Stewart "Kristen Stewart"), [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson "Robert Pattinson"), and [Taylor Lautner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Lautner "Taylor Lautner"), reprising their roles as [Bella Swan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bella_Swan "Bella Swan"), [Edward Cullen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Cullen "Edward Cullen"), and [Jacob Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Black "Jacob Black"), respectively. In the film, Bella and Edward marry and become pregnant with a half-human, half-vampire child, which causes the Cullen family to become a target of Jacob and his Wolf Pack.

[Summit Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summit_Entertainment "Summit Entertainment") announced that _Breaking Dawn_ would be adapted into a two-part film on June 10, 2010. [Principal photography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_photography "Principal photography") for both parts began on November 1, 2010, and wrapped on April 22, 2011. The first part was shot in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro "Rio de Janeiro"), Brazil; [Baton Rouge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baton_Rouge "Baton Rouge"), Louisiana; and [Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver "Vancouver"), Canada.

_The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn – Part 1_ premiered at the [Rome Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome_Film_Festival "Rome Film Festival") on October 30, 2011, and was theatrically released in the United States on November 18, by Summit Entertainment.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-BDP2-6) The film received mostly negative reviews from critics with criticism aimed at pacing, screenplay and performances. It grossed $732 million worldwide, becoming the [fourth-highest-grossing film of 2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_in_film#Highest-grossing_films "2011 in film").[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-7) A sequel, _[Breaking Dawn – Part 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Twilight\_Saga:\_Breaking\_Dawn\_%E2%80%93\_Part\_2 "The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn – Part 2")_, was released in 2012.

## Plot

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Plot")]

Several months after the events of the [previous film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Eclipse "The Twilight Saga: Eclipse"), human [Bella Swan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bella_Swan "Bella Swan") and vampire [Edward Cullen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Cullen "Edward Cullen") are getting married. During the reception, [werewolf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Werewolf "Werewolf")[Jacob Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Black "Jacob Black"), who had left town upon receiving his invitation to the wedding, returns, much to Bella's delight. They share a dance in the woods, where Bella says that she and Edward plan to [consummate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consummate "Consummate") their marriage on their honeymoon while she is still human. Jacob becomes furious, fearing that such an act could kill her.

After the wedding, Bella and Edward travel to an island off the coast of [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro "Rio de Janeiro") for their honeymoon, and they have sex for the first time. Two weeks later, Bella vomits after waking and realizes her [period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Menstruation "Menstruation") is late. She informs [Carlisle Cullen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlisle_Cullen "Carlisle Cullen"), a medical doctor and Edward's adoptive father, that she believes she is pregnant.

Edward is distraught, as it is highly unlikely a human could survive giving birth to a vampire's baby. He encourages Bella to receive an [abortion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abortion "Abortion") from Carlisle. She refuses and convinces Edward's sister [Rosalie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosalie_Hale "Rosalie Hale") to be her bodyguard. The couple flies back home to [Forks, Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forks,_Washington "Forks, Washington"), where Jacob rushes to the Cullens' house to find a pale, underweight, and visibly pregnant Bella. He implores her to terminate the pregnancy immediately, but she refuses.

Jacob storms out of the Cullens' residence and meets in the woods with his packmates, including their leader Sam, who soon feels they should kill Bella to ensure her half-vampire child does not harm anyone. Jacob refuses, and he and several other werewolves separate from the pack.

As Bella's pregnancy progresses, her health declines rapidly. She soon realizes the fetus is craving blood, so she begins drinking human blood obtained by Carlisle from the hospital, improving her health. As a half-vampire, the fetus's development is far more accelerated than that of a human fetus, and thus Bella's pregnancy progresses at an alarming rate.

Edward's resentment for the baby dissolves when he discovers he can read its thoughts. When Bella suddenly goes into labor, Edward, Jacob, and Rosalie begin performing an emergency [C-section](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C-section "C-section") while Carlisle is out obtaining blood. The procedure is excruciatingly painful, and Bella falls unconscious, though she wakes to find she has delivered a healthy daughter, whom she names Renesmee—a combination of her mother's name and Edward's mother's name.

Bella's heart suddenly stops, and Jacob desperately attempts [CPR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CPR "CPR"). Edward injects Bella's heart with his venom in an attempt to transform her into a vampire, but his action seems futile as Bella remains lifeless. A distraught Jacob decides to kill Renesmee for apparently causing Bella's death; however, when he looks into the baby's eyes, he "imprints" on her: a werewolf phenomenon in which one realizes one's soulmate. This prevents his pack from killing her as their most absolute law is not to harm anyone who has been imprinted on.

Over the course of two days, though still unconscious, Bella's injuries heal and her figure returns to normal. Finally, her eyes open and are blood-red, signifying that she has become a vampire.

In a [post-credits scene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-credits_scene "Post-credits scene"), the Volturi, the keepers of vampire law, receive a letter from Carlisle which summarizes Bella and Edward's wedding and her transformation into a vampire. The Volturi bicker about how Carlisle is growing his coven and that the Volturis' dispute with the Cullens is over. However, their leader mentions that the Cullens still have something he wants.

### Development

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Development")]

In early November 2008, [Summit Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summit_Entertainment "Summit Entertainment") obtained the [film adaptation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_adaptation "Film adaptation") rights to the remaining novels in the [_Twilight_ book series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twilight_(novel_series) "Twilight (novel series)"), _[New Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New\_Moon\_(novel) "New Moon (novel)")_, _[Eclipse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eclipse\_(Meyer\_novel) "Eclipse (Meyer novel)")_, and _[Breaking Dawn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breaking\_Dawn "Breaking Dawn")_.[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-TwilightSeriesRights-8) Talks for a _Breaking Dawn_ film started after Summit approved _[The Twilight Saga: New Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Twilight\_Saga:\_New\_Moon "The Twilight Saga: New Moon")_ and _[The Twilight Saga: Eclipse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Twilight\_Saga:\_Eclipse "The Twilight Saga: Eclipse")_, the second and third installments, respectively, in [_The Twilight Saga_ film series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga_(film_series) "The Twilight Saga (film series)").[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Fancon-9)[Wyck Godfrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wyck_Godfrey "Wyck Godfrey"), producer of the previous films in the series, stated in mid-2009 that they had every intention to make the film version of _Breaking Dawn_,[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-10) but [Stephenie Meyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephenie_Meyer "Stephenie Meyer"), author of the series, explained on her website's _Breaking Dawn_ FAQ that if an adaptation were to be created, it would have to be split into two films because "[t]he book is just so long!", saying that she would have made the book shorter if it were possible.[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-MovieFilm-11) She also believed it to be impossible to make a film due to [Renesmee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renesmee_Cullen "Renesmee Cullen"), writing that an actress could not play her because she is a baby that has complete awareness; however, she did acknowledge the film might be possible due to the quickly-advancing technologies.[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-MovieFilm-11) Moreover, because of the mature and explicit nature of the _Breaking Dawn_ novel, fans and critics questioned if the studio would be able to keep a [PG-13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_Picture_Association_of_America_film_rating_system#PG-13 "Motion Picture Association of America film rating system") rating, noting that the movie should not be rated [R](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_Picture_Association_of_America_film_rating_system#R "Motion Picture Association of America film rating system") for the ever-growing fan base.[[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-12)

> I got very nervous. There were, like, 15 people. I had never really auditioned or gone into a job interview in that way since maybe 1988 or '87. I guess I was unprepared for it. […] In this case, they wanted me to talk about their project, which really needed to follow very closely the book. […] I was talking about the book, and really all I was saying was, 'OK, this is great, let's go to it.' That was the pitch. I think they're used to something else. They're used to, for those of you who might audition for film jobs, a 40-minute dissertation with perhaps visual aids and a pep talk about how fantastic this project is going to be. I just didn't know how to do that.

—Gus Van Sant[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-mtv.com-13)

In March 2010, _[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety\_(magazine) "Variety (magazine)")_ reported that Summit Entertainment was considering splitting the 754-page book into two films, along the same lines as [Warner Bros. Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warner_Bros._Pictures "Warner Bros. Pictures")' _[Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry\_Potter\_and\_the\_Deathly\_Hallows\_%E2%80%93\_Part\_1 "Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 1")_ and _[Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry\_Potter\_and\_the\_Deathly\_Hallows\_%E2%80%93\_Part\_2 "Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 2")_.[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Yahoo!-14) Since under contract for only four films, the status of Stewart's, Pattinson's, and Lautner's contracts were in question, making the possibility of a split unlikely.[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Yahoo!-14) Producer Wyck Godfrey stated that all three main cast members were signed onto one _Breaking Dawn_ film.[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-McIntyre-5) In the same month, it was reported that Summit was searching for [Academy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Awards "Academy Awards")-nominated directors to helm the films, with names as [Sofia Coppola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_Coppola "Sofia Coppola"), [Gus Van Sant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gus_Van_Sant "Gus Van Sant"), and [Bill Condon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Condon "Bill Condon") rumored to have been approached to direct.[[15]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-15) On April 28, 2010, Summit announced that Bill Condon would direct _Breaking Dawn_; Wyck Godfrey, [Karen Rosenfelt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karen_Rosenfelt "Karen Rosenfelt"), and author Stephenie Meyer would be producing the film. Condon said, "I'm very excited to get the chance to bring the climax of this saga to life on-screen. As fans of the series know, this is a one-of-a-kind book—and we're hoping to create an equally unique cinematic experience."[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Director4-16) Condon, about Summit approaching him, said, "The very nice folks at Summit… they sent me the novel. I loved it. I quickly imprinted on the material".[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Comic-Con-17) Another reason Condon cited to work on the film was the desire to collaborate with Stewart.[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Movieline-18) Gus Van Sant later explained that [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson "Robert Pattinson") mentioning him as the ideal director for _Breaking Dawn_ was what made him audition for the job and described the audition as "very nerve-wracking". However, after Condon was hired, Sant suggested that the reason behind not getting hired was his style and way of auditioning being different from what the executive producers were used to.[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-mtv.com-13)

On June 10, 2010, Summit officially confirmed that a two-part adaptation of the fourth novel would start filming in November and made clear that all major actors would return for both parts.[[19]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-BD2-19) The first part was released on November 18, 2011, and the second part was released on November 16, 2012.[[20]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-20)

### Pre-production

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Pre-production")]

Since _[Twilight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twilight\_(2008\_film) "Twilight (2008 film)")_ was released, fans and critics speculated whether _Breaking Dawn_ would be adapted into a film considering the adult nature of the book. In March 2010, screenwriter [Melissa Rosenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melissa_Rosenberg "Melissa Rosenberg") spoke about adapting the book saying, "It's the big one, it's gonna be a big challenge, and I guarantee you that not all of the fans will be happy, and I guarantee you some of them will be. You have to give up the ideal of making everybody happy, it's just not gonna happen, but you hope you make the majority happy. Again, for that last book it is about taking that specific character Bella on her journey. It's a big journey, it's a massive change for her, and you hope to realize that."[[21]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Melissa-21) One of the scenes that aroused the speculations that the film would be rated [R](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_Picture_Association_of_America_film_rating_system "Motion Picture Association of America film rating system") is the infamous graphic birth scene in the novel. In August, Rosenberg addressed such speculations saying, "On the fan site, on Facebook, all the comments are "It has to be R rated! You have to show the childbirth! Gore and guts and sex!" For me it's actually more interesting to not see it. You know, you can do childbirth without seeing childbirth … it doesn't mean it's any less evocative of an experience."[[22]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-22) Producer Wyck Godfrey addressed those speculations further saying, "it would be a crime against our audience to go R-rated" as the core fans of the series are below 18 years old, but insisted that the film is based on a mature book, so more progress and sophistication are needed.[[23]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Godfrey-23) To compromise the necessary sophistication in adapting such a mature book and the need for maintaining a PG-13 rating, Rosenberg stated that the scene would be shown from Bella's point of view.[[24]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-24) Godfrey described it saying, "She is looking through the haze, experiencing pain and everything rushing around her. We only see what she sees".[[23]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Godfrey-23)

> You start with, and you end with, what is the emotional journey for these characters. That is the most important thing to capture, that is the only thing to capture. Everything else is up for grabs, but you must take these characters on the same emotional journey that they took in the book, and hence take the audience on the same emotional journey that they took in the book and that's the goal, you hope that you achieve that.

—Melissa Rosenberg, writer of _The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn_, about adapting the book to film[[21]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Melissa-21)

In June, Rosenberg stated in an interview that the decision on where to split the film had not been decided, as she was still in the drafting stage of the scripts.[[25]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Script-25) "I think it comes down to Bella as human and Bella as vampire", she said, hinting at a potential splitting point. She thought that Condon would probably disagree with the statement, explaining that the decision is ultimately up to him.[[25]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Script-25) Later in January 2011, Godfrey confirmed that the _Part 1_ will cover the wedding, honeymoon, pregnancy and birth and ends just before her transformation into a vampire as the filmmakers wanted to "take the audience through the emotional part of Bella's journey as she becomes a vampire". _[Part 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Twilight\_Saga:\_Breaking\_Dawn\_%E2%80%93\_Part\_2 "The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn – Part 2")_ will follow her transformation, the "first exhilarating moments" of her vampire life[[26]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Meyer-26) and the final confrontation with the [Volturi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Twilight_characters "List of Twilight characters"). Godfrey also confirmed that _Part 1_ will follow the book's storyline as it breaks away from Bella and switches into [Jacob](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Black "Jacob Black")'s perspective. "There is a sense that as Bella and the Cullens (Edward's makeshift vampire clan) deal with her pregnancy, the world is still turning outside with Jacob", he explains.[[23]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Godfrey-23) However, in March 2011, Meyer said in interview with _[USA Today](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USA\_Today "USA Today")_ that _Part 1_ will end when Bella opens her eyes as a vampire.[[26]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Meyer-26)

By August 2010, screenwriter [Melissa Rosenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melissa_Rosenberg "Melissa Rosenberg") said that the scripts for _Part 1_ and _2_ were 75 to 85 percent completed. She found the greatest challenge in writing the scripts to be the final sequence of _Part 2_, explaining, "The final battle sequence is a big challenge because it lasts 25 pages. It's almost an entire three-act story in and of itself. You have to track [keep it all in one setting] hundreds of characters. It's an enormous challenge to choreograph on the page and for Bill [Condon] to choreograph on the stage." She had written various drafts of the scene but, at the time, hadn't revised or discussed them with Condon yet. She said, "That's the next big hurdle to sit down with the stunt coordinator and create the ballet. It's a lot of work. I'm exhausted, but we're intent on making them the best scripts yet."[[27]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-27) Godfrey called _Part 2_ "an action film in terms of life-and-death stakes" and said that in _Part 1_ "there are the pangs of newlywed tension that occur that are relatable even in a fantasy film. Marriage is not quite the experience that they thought it was."[[28]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-28) Condon thought of _Part 1_ "as a real companion piece to Catherine Hardwicke's movie". Condon explains, "Like, everything that got set up there gets resolved here. I think you'll find that there are stylistic and other nods to that film."[[29]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-29)

### Filming

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Filming")]

In order to keep the budget on both parts of _Breaking Dawn_ reasonable, even though it is substantially greater than the previous installments in the series, much of the film was shot in and around [Baton Rouge, Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baton_Rouge,_Louisiana "Baton Rouge, Louisiana") and Celtic Studios in Baton Rouge. Shooting in Louisiana provided larger tax credits, which a small studio like Summit Entertainment would find favorable.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-BDP2-6) Summit announced in a press release on July 9, 2010, that filming was to take place in Baton Rouge, [Ucluelet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ucluelet "Ucluelet"), and [Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver "Vancouver"), with the wedding being shot in [Squamish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squamish,_British_Columbia "Squamish, British Columbia")[[30]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-30) and near by [Pemberton, British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pemberton,_British_Columbia "Pemberton, British Columbia").[[31]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-pemberton.ca-31) Both parts would be shot back-to-back as one project.[[32]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Yamato-32) The film would attempt to keep its PG-13 rating, and it would not feature any of the gruesome scenes from the novel[[33]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Budget-33)[[34]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-34) with Kristen Stewart confirming that the birth scene wasn't as grotesque as described in the book and that she didn't "puke up blood", though director Bill Condon said that they shot everything as "powerful and potent as they could". Though there were many reports of the cast in [Whistler, British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whistler,_British_Columbia "Whistler, British Columbia"), none of the actual filming took place in Whistler itself, but to the north and south of the town in nearby Pemberton (north) and Squamish (south). The Stars were housed in Whistler at 4 and 5 star hotels, the crew in Squamish and Pemberton.[[31]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-pemberton.ca-31)[[35]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-35)

[Principal photography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_photography "Principal photography") officially began on November 1, 2010 in Brazil, with locations in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro "Rio de Janeiro") and [Paraty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paraty "Paraty"), Rio de Janeiro.[[36]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-36) The first scenes were shot in the [Lapa District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lapa,_Rio_de_Janeiro "Lapa, Rio de Janeiro") in Rio de Janeiro for one night. A long city block was rented for shooting, and Summit Entertainment paid residents 50 to 500 [reais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_real "Brazilian real") (30 to 300 U.S. dollars) to not allow paparazzi or fans to overlook filming from their windows. Moreover, owners of bars and restaurants were paid 10,000 and 20,000 reais (6,000 to 12,000 U.S. dollars) to stay closed for the evening to eliminate noise and provide a clear street.[[37]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-37)

Shooting then moved to Paraty, Rio de Janeiro where the honeymoon scenes were shot. According to Paraty's Tourism Office, filming took place in the Taquari area, near an unidentified waterfall, and at Mamangua Bay where a mansion is located.[[38]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-38) It rained on every day of shooting.[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Comic-Con-17) In late November, shooting moved to Baton Rouge, Louisiana where most of the indoor scenes were shot on [Raleigh Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raleigh_Studios "Raleigh Studios")[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Comic-Con-17) and in a house. Stewart had to wear heavy make-up to look thin and ghastly to show Bella through a phase of pregnancy where the baby starts breaking her bones.[[39]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Premiere-39) The birth scene took two nights to shoot[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Comic-Con-17) after the cast had a long conversation with Meyer, a midwife, and a doctor to discuss the mechanics of the scene,[[40]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-EW-40) particularly to decide the area where Edward should place his mouth to bite into Bella's placenta if this situation could ever occur in real life.[[39]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Premiere-39) An [animatronic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animatronics "Animatronics") baby was used to film a few scenes of newborn Renesmee.[[41]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-41) The cast and crew spent two months of the filming process shooting in a [green-screen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chroma_key "Chroma key") room on fake snow. Reportedly, a few scenes were also shot in Arsenal Park using green screens.[[42]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-42)

In late February and early March, filming of most of the exterior shots, along with Bella's vampire scenes, occurred in Canada.[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Comic-Con-17) The film's first shooting location in Canada was Vancouver. However, the cast and crew were evacuated from the set due to the tsunami advisories resulting from the 9.0-magnitude [earthquake that hit Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_T%C5%8Dhoku_earthquake_and_tsunami "2011 Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami") on March 11, 2011.[[43]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-43) Toni Atterbury, a spokeswoman for the film, said that "the crew was moved to higher ground" as a safety precaution and "the shoot was delayed for a few hours, but the day's work was accomplished".[[44]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-44) Therefore, filming relocated to Squamish, but a few scenes were shot in the [Orpheum Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orpheum_(Vancouver) "Orpheum (Vancouver)") with the Vancouver police covering all the entrances of the theater.[[45]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-45)

The wedding scene in _Part 1_ was the last scene the cast and crew shot.[[40]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-EW-40) It was also shot under tight security. A helicopter hovered above the set, off-duty police officers surrounded the location, and sheets and umbrellas were used to protect the set from aerial shots being taken.[[46]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-46) Stewart spoke about the wedding at [San Diego Comic-Con](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Diego_Comic-Con "San Diego Comic-Con"), describing it as "insane". She went on to say that it was "secret service style. The crew was incredibly inconvenienced. No cell phone, etc. They wanted to keep the dress secret."[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Comic-Con-17) Concerning the wedding dress, Stewart was locked in a room wearing a [Volturi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Twilight_characters "List of Twilight characters") cloak to cover the dress.[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Comic-Con-17)

Stewart further talked about filming the scene, saying, "I wanted to _run_ down the aisle. I was literally pulling away from Billy Burke. Now it's a trip to watch the wedding scenes. The last shot is Jacob leaving. I called "Cut!" and then Kristen yelled, "Jacob!" and hiked her dress up and started running after him into the woods, saying, "Come back! ### Costume design

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Costume design")]

In October 2010, it was announced that Michael Wilkinson would be the film's costume designer.[[49]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Filmmakers-49) However, Bella's wedding dress was designed by someone other than Wilkinson. Meyer's description of the dress was "a simpler style than the frillier Edwardian stuff. …Elegant white satin, cut on the bias, with long sleeves."[[50]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-50) At first, [Zac Posen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zac_Posen "Zac Posen") was rumored to be the designer of Bella's wedding dress; however, these rumors proved false when Posen [tweeted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitter "Twitter"): "Heard the Bella/Twilight rumor and it's just that. I design for real women like Kristen Stewart and Anna Kendrick".[[51]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-51) In April 2011, Summit announced that [Carolina Herrera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carolina_Herrera_(fashion_designer) "Carolina Herrera (fashion designer)") is the designer of the dress.[[52]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-52) Stewart described the dress as very tight, but still liked it and thought that "it was very pretty".[[53]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-53) Meyer told _[USA Today](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USA\_Today "USA Today")_ that the dress was "an interesting mix" and has a "vintage feel, but at the same time, there's an edge to it".[[26]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Meyer-26)

Concerning Alice's bridesmaid dress, its designer remains unknown, however it was presumably designed by Wilkinson. Ashley Greene described the dress as "magical and beautiful" and spoke about the design process, saying, "We wanted to have all the bridesmaids fit together and also have their own identity. So, we took a little bit of Alice's past and put it into her dress."[[54]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Alice-54) Corsets were added to all the cast's wedding dresses, but were removed during filming because the cast felt uncomfortable wearing them while dancing.[[54]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Alice-54)

[Alfred Angelo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Angelo "Alfred Angelo") has been named the exclusive and official licensed manufacturer of Bella's bridal gown. The gown will be a replica of Bella's wedding dress and marketed under the brand _Twilight Bridal by Alfred Angelo_. It will be revealed in late November 2011, following the release of _Part 1_, and will be available in Alfred Angelo Signature Stores and independent retailers worldwide.[[55]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-55)

### Post-production

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Post-production")]

In October 2010, Condon announced that [Oscar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Awards "Academy Awards")-winning visual effects supervisor John Bruno, along with his team, would helm the visual effects for _Breaking Dawn_, including the effects necessary to show Renesmee in her various stages of life in _Part 2_.[[49]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Filmmakers-49) Later in February, Adam Howard was added to the _Breaking Dawn_ visual effects team to help create the visual effects for Renesmee, due to his notable work on a similar issue in _[The Social Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Social\_Network "The Social Network")_.[[56]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-56) Condon revealed that Mackenzie Foy's face and expressions will be placed digitally on the bodies of the other actresses playing the same character through her various stages of life.[[57]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Friday-57) Condon spoke about the process saying, "Sometimes it was hard because the other actresses were actually just there. It was always going to be just Mackenzie's expressions and things like that, so it was a very specific technical thing that even I was learning as we did it. But I have to say, they were real troopers these girls."[[57]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Friday-57) Furthermore, special effects were also used to illustrate the invisible powers and forces between the vampires in the final battle sequence of _Part 2_.[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Movieline-18)

[Montreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreal "Montreal")-based [Modus FX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modus_FX "Modus FX") created subtle [CG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer-generated_imagery "Computer-generated imagery") effects for _Part 1_. It created stylized effects to emphasize the supernatural capabilities of the main characters without making them too obvious or noticeable.[[58]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-FX-58) A team of 12 artists spent six weeks working on the film. Shots included creating the belly of a pregnant Bella, removing a wrist brace Stewart was wearing in the wedding scene due to an injury, and a variety of subtle cosmetic refinements. Bella's pregnancy was a challenge for Modus; the production team wanted the baby to kick and move around inside her belly, so the artists and cinematographer had to match the camera moves, the lighting, even the [film grain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_grain "Film grain"), along with the subtleties of Stewart's skin.[[58]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-FX-58) On the subject, CG supervisor Martin Pelletier said,

> For this project, we had to be really quick in terms of turn around. We made use of very complex lighting from the set, adding [3D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3D_computer_graphics "3D computer graphics") layers, and a [matchmove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Match_moving "Match moving") of her stomach, to make her look pregnant. We took our time at the start to get the recipe right and that paid off in efficiency once we got going. We were soon able to turn around several shots each day.[[58]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-FX-58)

During the wedding scene, the camera pans around Pattinson and Stewart. Due to a minor wrist injury Stewart had, she was wearing a brace on the day of the shoot. Therefore, Modus was required to create a CG model of the hand and then carefully craft a rig to create natural motions. Once that was finished, every minute movement of the hand had to be matched exactly.[[58]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-FX-58) The rotational panning shot totals 300 frames and called for elaborate camera and object tracking. Modus used subsurface scattering to accurately capture the partial translucence of her skin to make it look more authentic. Pelletier explained that "tracking was particularly challenging, because when they were shooting it, they weren't thinking about it as an effects shot. There was no camera metadata for the sequence." The solution was to do a series of careful manual adjustments until the light sources were correctly replicated on the set.[[58]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-FX-58)

In July, Condon said that the [first cut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_editing "Film editing") of _Part 1_ would be finished in a few weeks, but the visual effects were still in development.[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Movieline-18) Godfrey later stated that they were "very close to locking picture on part one", and that its running time is equal to _[Twilight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twilight\_(2008\_film) "Twilight (2008 film)")_, _[New Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Twilight\_Saga:\_New\_Moon "The Twilight Saga: New Moon")_ and _[Eclipse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Twilight\_Saga:\_Eclipse "The Twilight Saga: Eclipse")_, therefore approximately two hours.[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Movieline-18) A few weeks later, Godfrey announced that he had seen numerous cuts of the film and called it "incredibly powerful already. …It definitely captures what the book captures." Concerning the subject of the [MPAA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_Picture_Association_of_America "Motion Picture Association of America") rating, Godfrey said that the studio does not "have any word yet on the rating", but insisted that it is going to be rated PG-13.[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Movieline-18) Condon discussed the matter further saying, "I think it's a good challenge because the thing that makes something R is literally showing it and if you give yourself that rule: I'm not gonna show, it's not going to be frontal nudity, no one wants that", and added, "we're not going to, again, show splattering blood against the walls but it's gonna be very visceral. It actually becomes a fun challenge to make sure you feel like you have the same experience without having to watch something clinical. I think it makes it better."[[57]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Friday-57)

### Music

On January 14, 2011, it was announced that [Carter Burwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carter_Burwell "Carter Burwell"), composer of the [first film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twilight_(2008_film) "Twilight (2008 film)") in the series, will be returning to score both parts of the final installment.[[59]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-59) The score of _Part 1_ was recorded in [Abbey Road Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbey_Road_Studios "Abbey Road Studios"), London in early September. [Alexandre Desplat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandre_Desplat "Alexandre Desplat") and [Howard Shore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Shore "Howard Shore"), the composers of _[New Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Twilight\_Saga:\_New\_Moon "The Twilight Saga: New Moon")_ and _[Eclipse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Twilight\_Saga:\_Eclipse "The Twilight Saga: Eclipse")_, respectively, happened to be in London at the time of the recording session and stopped by to visit Burwell.[[60]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-60)

_[The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn: Part 1: Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Twilight\_Saga:\_Breaking\_Dawn\_%E2%80%93\_Part\_1\_(soundtrack) "The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn – Part 1 (soundtrack)")_ was released on November 8, 2011.[[61]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-61) In July, Condon said that they are still under negotiations for the soundtrack and have 15 songs to choose from, but no deals have been signed with any artists. [Will Hunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Hunt "Will Hunt"), the drummer of the band, said, "I've been screaming for [new song] 'My Heart Is Broken' to land in that, because I think it would fit the story so well." The lead singer of the band, [Amy Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Lee "Amy Lee"), agreed, adding, "I think that would be awesome, actually." ### Leaks

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1&action=edit&section=12 "Edit section: Leaks")]

When the filming started on November 7, 2010 in the [Lapa District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lapa,_Rio_de_Janeiro "Lapa, Rio de Janeiro") and [Paraty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paraty "Paraty"), [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro_(state) "Rio de Janeiro (state)"), Brazil, leaked set photos and footage videos surfaced online.[[69]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-69)[Summit Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summit_Entertainment "Summit Entertainment") responded to the leaks by removing the photos and videos from YouTube, fansites and gossip websites. On January 13, 2011, scans of a still of Kristen Stewart and Robert Pattinson in one of their honeymoon scenes in an _[Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment\_Weekly "Entertainment Weekly")_ article ran online, prior to the magazine's official release of the still.[[70]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-70) On March 31 and April 1, 2011, a mass leak of a 14-second video and numerous low-quality stills hit the Internet resulting to enthusiastic fan reaction and speculations that the film wouldn't be able to maintain a [PG-13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_Picture_Association_of_America_film_rating_system "Motion Picture Association of America film rating system") rating.[[71]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-71)[Summit Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summit_Entertainment "Summit Entertainment") released an official statement in response to the leaks saying:

> As some of you may know, pictures and screen grabs of _The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn_ as a work in progress have leaked on the internet. We are extremely proud of this film and also extremely heartbroken to see it out there at this stage. The film and these images are not yet ready or in their proper context. They were illegally obtained and their early dissemination is deeply upsetting to the actors, the filmmakers and Summit who are working so hard to bring these movies to fruition to you in November 2011 and November 2012.
> 
> 
> Please, for those who are posting, stop. And please, though the temptation is high, don't view or pass on these images. Wait for the film in its beautiful, finished entirety to thrill you.
> 
> 
> Sincerely,
> 
> 
> Stephenie Meyer, Bill Condon, Wyck Godfrey and Summit Entertainment.[[72]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-72)

Additionally, the first teaser trailer leaked online hours before its debut at the [MTV Movie Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Movie_Awards "MTV Movie Awards").[[73]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-73)

#### Lawsuit

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1&action=edit&section=13 "Edit section: Lawsuit")]

On August 1, 2011, Summit released a [press release](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Press_release "Press release") announcing the identification of some of the alleged people responsible for the leak of images and video from _Breaking Dawn_ on March 31 and April 1 and the decision to take legal action. Summit claimed that the leak came from [Posadas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Posadas,_Misiones "Posadas, Misiones"), [Argentina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentina "Argentina"), but due to the possibility that other people might be involved in the leak, the investigation was still ongoing. The only person Summit named was a woman called Daiana Santia, resident in Posadas, allegedly being involved in the group that stole the images and footage.[[74]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-MTV_Leaks-74) Civil action has been filed in the U.S. and Argentina, while criminal action has also been filed in Argentina.[[74]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-MTV_Leaks-74) Summit hired law firm Keats McFarland & Wilson LLP to search four continents, North America, South America, Europe and Australia, to find the other people behind the leak.[[74]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-MTV_Leaks-74)

Santia hosted a press conference in her town and denied Summit's claims. She claimed that she only saw the images "while surfing the Internet"[[75]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-75) and didn't send them to anyone. She also stated that she didn't log in anywhere to see them and denies having the technical knowledge of [hacking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hacker_(programmer_subculture) "Hacker (programmer subculture)"), specifically saying that she "in no way be considered a "hacker" because [she] has no computer skills other than simple user level".[[76]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Noticine-76) Her attorney confirmed that Santia and her family are considering a counterclaim against Summit due to defamation of character and continual harassment by the film's producers who kept requesting that she would let them check her computer to see if she still has the images on her [hard disk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hard_disk_drive "Hard disk drive"), although she refused more than once claiming that she is innocent and her computer contains personal items. Her attorney called the situation "a harm to privacy and personal right".[[76]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Noticine-76)

Summit's official response to the press conference said, "First and most important this is NOT about greed or the Studio wanting to bully a woman from a small town in Argentina—rather, it is about stolen material that is private and sensitive which was obtained by illegally accessing private/secure servers as well as personal email accounts." Summit gave details about the case stating that the studio has been in contact with Santia since May 2011, but "with no resolution or further good faith efforts on their part, thus the only alternative left was to pursue legal action to ascertain that Ms. Santia no longer holds the images and video in any shape or form".[[77]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Lexicon-77) In the response, Summit announced the following claims:

> Specifically on June 8, 2011, Ms. Santia confessed in the presence of her attorney that she accessed servers and email accounts via a systematic attack—stealing photographs, unfinished images and video footage over several months. Additionally there is indisputable evidence linking her directly to IP addresses that were used in the unauthorized access. Her actions appear to be premeditated and not done on a whim, but rather using technology and tactics that require thought as well as time and skill. Because Ms. Santia decided that she does not want to cooperate, Summit has been unable to settle this matter privately with Ms. Santia and her representatives in Argentina.[[77]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-Lexicon-77)

## Reception

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1&action=edit&section=15 "Edit section: Reception")]

### Box office

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1&action=edit&section=16 "Edit section: Box office")]

_The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn – Part 1_ grossed $281,287,133 in North America and $449,867,136 in other countries, bringing its worldwide total to $731,664,759.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-mojopt1-3) It earned a [_The Twilight Saga_ film series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga_(film_series) "The Twilight Saga (film series)")-best $291.0 million on its worldwide opening weekend, marking the tenth-largest worldwide opening of all time.[[82]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-subers1-82)[[83]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-83) It reached $500 million worldwide in 12 days, a record time for the film series.[[84]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-84) It ranks as the [fourth-highest-grossing film of 2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_in_film#Highest-grossing_films "2011 in film") and the second-highest-grossing film of the film series.[[85]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-85)[[86]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-boxofficemojo1-86)

#### North America

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1&action=edit&section=17 "Edit section: North America")]

_Breaking Dawn – Part 1_ (which opened on November 18, 2011 in 4,061 theaters) was projected to reap at least $140 million in its opening weekend.[[87]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-87) The film earned $30.25 million in midnight showings, which was the second-highest midnight gross ever, at the time, behind _[Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry\_Potter\_and\_the\_Deathly\_Hallows\_%E2%80%93\_Part\_2 "Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 2")_ ($43.5 million) as well as the highest midnight gross of the film series, until it was surpassed by _The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn – Part 2_'s gross of $30.4 million.[[88]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-88) On its opening day, the movie topped the box office with $71.6 million (including midnight showings), which is the fifth-highest opening[[89]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-89) and single-day[[90]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-90) gross of all time.[[91]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-91) On its opening weekend, _Breaking Dawn – Part 1_ claimed first place with $138.1 million, which was the second-highest opening weekend of the film series, at the time, behind _The Twilight Saga: New Moon_ ($142.8 million),[[92]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-boxofficemojo2-92) as well as the fourth-highest November opening ever behind _[The Hunger Games: Catching Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Hunger\_Games:\_Catching\_Fire "The Hunger Games: Catching Fire")_, _New Moon_, and _Breaking Dawn – Part 2_.[[93]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-93) It is also the tenth-highest opening weekend of all time.[[94]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-94) The movie also had the second-best opening weekend of 2011 in North America behind _Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 2_ ($169.2 million).[[95]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-95)

It retained first place on its second three-day weekend, declining 70% to $41.9 million, and earned $61.8 million over the five-day [Thanksgiving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thanksgiving_(United_States) "Thanksgiving (United States)") weekend.[[96]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-96)_Breaking Dawn – Part 1_ remained No. 1 for a third weekend, marking the best third-weekend gross for a _Twilight_ film ($16.5 million)[[97]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-97) and the second film of 2011 to top the weekend box office three times, along with _[The Help](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Help\_(film) "The Help (film)")_.[[98]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-98) Closing on February 23, 2012, with $281.3 miilion, it is the third-highest-grossing movie of 2011.[[99]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-99) It is also the fourth-highest-grossing film in the series, only ahead of the [first film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twilight_(2008_film) "Twilight (2008 film)") ($192.8 million).[[86]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-boxofficemojo1-86)

### Critical response

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1&action=edit&section=19 "Edit section: Critical response")]

_Breaking Dawn – Part 1_ received negative reviews from critics. On review aggregation website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes") 26% of critics (of the 209 counted reviews) gave the film a positive review with an average rating of 4.4/10. The site's consensus reads: "Slow, joyless, and loaded with unintentionally humorous moments, _Breaking Dawn Part 1_ may satisfy the _Twilight_ faithful, but it's strictly for fans of the franchise."[[108]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-108) The review site [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic") gave the film a 45 out of 100, based on reviews from 36 critics.[[109]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-109) It is the lowest-rated installment in the franchise, which was previously _[New Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Twilight\_Saga:\_New\_Moon "The Twilight Saga: New Moon")_. Audiences polled by [CinemaScore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CinemaScore "CinemaScore") gave the film a "B+" grade, the audience was 80% female and 60% over 21 years old. Among females only, the film received an improved "A−" grade.[[92]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-boxofficemojo2-92)

Justin Chang of _[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety\_(magazine) "Variety (magazine)")_ gave the film a negative review, calling the film "disappointing".[[110]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-110) Todd McCarthy of _[The Hollywood Reporter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Hollywood\_Reporter "The Hollywood Reporter")_ also gave the film a negative review, calling _Part 1_ "bloated".[[111]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-111) Brent Simon of _Screen International_ called the film "soapy and melodramatic".[[112]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_1#cite_note-112)[Peter Bradshaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Bradshaw "Peter Bradshaw") of _[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Guardian "The Guardian")_ gave the film one star out of a possible five, and referred to it as the next stage of an "emo-operetta" that "sweeps us away on a new riptide of [mawkish](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/mawkish#English "wiktionary:mawkish")
