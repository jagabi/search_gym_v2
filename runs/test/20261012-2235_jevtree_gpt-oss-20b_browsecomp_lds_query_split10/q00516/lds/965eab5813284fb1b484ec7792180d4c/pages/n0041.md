## [Biography](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0174374/bio/?ref_=nm_ov_ql_1#mini_bio)

*   Bill Condon was born on October 22, 1955 in New York City, New York, USA. He is a director and writer, known for [Dreamgirls (2006)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0443489/?ref_=nmbio_mbio), [Gods and Monsters (1998)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0120684/?ref_=nmbio_mbio) and [Kinsey (2004)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0362269/?ref_=nmbio_mbio).     

## [Trivia](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0174374/bio/?ref_=nm_ov_ql_1#trivia)

*   First film-related job was working for a year in the publicity department at Avco Embassy.   
*   After graduating from Regis High School, an all-male Jesuit school in Manhattan, he studied philosophy at Columbia University.   
*   Directed 5 different performers in Oscar-nominated performances: [Lynn Redgrave](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001655/?ref_=nmbio_trv), [Ian McKellen](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0005212/?ref_=nmbio_trv), [Laura Linney](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001473/?ref_=nmbio_trv), [Eddie Murphy](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000552/?ref_=nmbio_trv) and [Jennifer Hudson](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm1617685/?ref_=nmbio_trv). Hudson won for [Dreamgirls (2006)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0443489/?ref_=nmbio_trv).   
*   Directed two films where [Ian McKellen](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0005212/?ref_=nmbio_trv) played with two actresses he had previously co-starred in different stage productions of [August Strindberg](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0834529/?ref_=nmbio_trv)'s "Dance of Death", both of them directed by [Sean Mathias](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0558773/?ref_=nmbio_trv): [Mr. Holmes (2015)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt3168230/?ref_=nmbio_trv) with [Frances de la Tour](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0209428/?ref_=nmbio_trv) and [The Good Liar (2019)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt5563334/?ref_=nmbio_trv) with [Helen Mirren](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000545/?ref_=nmbio_trv).   
*   His mother attended high school with [Susan Hayward](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001333/?ref_=nmbio_trv).   

## [Quotes](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0174374/bio/?ref_=nm_ov_ql_1#quotes)

*   We were just very surprised and delighted to get the call from (the ratings board) saying that they'd had a long discussion and decided that they'd learned a lot from the screening [of [Kinsey (2004)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0362269/?ref_=nmbio_qu)].   
*   He's somebody who's socially maladroit, he's obsessive, he's a scientist, he's clinical, he's shut off in so many ways. Kinsey as the center of a movie was a big question mark.   
*   "You can imagine that a story that is so much about sex could be much more confrontational. What I wanted to do was create something that was more gentle and done in a classical Hollywood style, which would be true to the period in which it's taking place. You get pretty pictures of Midwestern life in the '30s and '40s, and then, suddenly, there's a clinical close-up of a vagina and a penis -- which are images you're not used to seeing in that context" [on [Kinsey (2004)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0362269/?ref_=nmbio_qu)].   
*   Politics do affect the Academy. The producers of [Life Is Beautiful (1997)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0118799/?ref_=nmbio_qu) had the support of a much bigger studio machine and at the same time Benigni would host private dinners: [Elizabeth Taylor](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000072/?ref_=nmbio_qu), come and meet [Roberto Benigni](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000905/?ref_=nmbio_qu)... you know. And the studio throws a lot of money around promoting him. That sounds like sour grapes, but it's not. It's just the way it is.   
*   The idea of bisexuality, that everyone is somewhere along that sliding scale, is incredibly threatening today. Even politicians, who can hide behind the idea of a gay identity, don't know what to do with the idea that everyone is something in the middle.
