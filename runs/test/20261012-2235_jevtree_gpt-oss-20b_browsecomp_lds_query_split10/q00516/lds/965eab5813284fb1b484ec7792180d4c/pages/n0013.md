**Anthony Dwane Mackie** (born September 23, 1978) is an American actor. He has [appeared](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie_on_screen_and_stage "Anthony Mackie on screen and stage") in [blockbuster films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blockbuster_(entertainment) "Blockbuster (entertainment)") and [independent features](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_film "Independent film"), and has [received accolades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_awards_and_nominations_received_by_Anthony_Mackie "List of awards and nominations received by Anthony Mackie") such as nominations for two [Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmy_Awards "Emmy Awards") and a [Screen Actors Guild Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actor_Awards "Actor Awards").

Mackie was born in [New Orleans, Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans,_Louisiana "New Orleans, Louisiana") and studied acting at [Juilliard School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juilliard_School "Juilliard School"), receiving a [Bachelor of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts "Bachelor of Arts") in drama in 2001. He began his career appearing in a handful of stage productions, and made his film debut in [_8 Mile_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8_Mile_(film) "8 Mile (film)") (2002). While developing his career in films such as _[Million Dollar Baby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Million\_Dollar\_Baby "Million Dollar Baby")_ (2004), [_Half Nelson_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Half_Nelson_(film) "Half Nelson (film)") (2006), [_Notorious_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notorious_(2009_film) "Notorious (2009 film)") (2009), and _[Real Steel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real\_Steel "Real Steel")_ (2011), he garnered critical acclaim and several awards for headlining _[Brother to Brother](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brother\_to\_Brother\_(film) "Brother to Brother (film)")_ (2004) and co-starring in _[The Hurt Locker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Hurt\_Locker "The Hurt Locker")_ (2008).

Mackie gained global stardom for portraying [Sam Wilson / Falcon / Captain America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Wilson_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Sam Wilson (Marvel Cinematic Universe)") in the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe "Marvel Cinematic Universe") (MCU), beginning with _[Captain America: The Winter Soldier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain\_America:\_The\_Winter\_Soldier "Captain America: The Winter Soldier")_ (2014) and including the solo projects _[The Falcon and the Winter Soldier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Falcon\_and\_the\_Winter\_Soldier "The Falcon and the Winter Soldier")_ (2021) and _[Captain America: Brave New World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain\_America:\_Brave\_New\_World "Captain America: Brave New World")_ (2025). He continued to star in mainstream films such as [_Detroit_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit_(film) "Detroit (film)") (2017), [_The Hate U Give_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hate_U_Give_(film) "The Hate U Give (film)") (2018), [_Synchronic_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synchronic_(film) "Synchronic (film)") (2019), and [_Elevation_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elevation_(film) "Elevation (film)") (2024), and in television projects such as [_All the Way_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_the_Way_(2016_film) "All the Way (2016 film)") (2016), _[Altered Carbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altered\_Carbon\_(TV\_series) "Altered Carbon (TV series)")_ (2020), and _[Twisted Metal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twisted\_Metal\_(TV\_series) "Twisted Metal (TV series)")_ (2023–present). He earned Emmy nominations for hosting _[Shark Beach with Anthony Mackie: Gulf Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shark\_Beach\_with\_Anthony\_Mackie:\_Gulf\_Coast "Shark Beach with Anthony Mackie: Gulf Coast")_ (2024) and portraying a fictionalized version of himself in _[The Studio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Studio\_(TV\_series) "The Studio (TV series)")_ (2025).

## Early life

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Anthony_Mackie&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Early life")]

Mackie was born on September 23, 1978,[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-Birthday-1) in [New Orleans, Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans,_Louisiana "New Orleans, Louisiana") to Willie Mackie Sr., a construction worker, and Martha (née Gordon), a stay-at-home mom.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-:0-2)[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-3)[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-:1-4) He has five siblings, including [Calvin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calvin_Mackie "Calvin Mackie"), a motivational speaker and entrepreneur.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-:1-4)[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-5) He aspired to act in his childhood, attending high school at the [New Orleans Center for Creative Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans_Center_for_Creative_Arts "New Orleans Center for Creative Arts") and, after his mother's death, the [University of North Carolina School of the Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_School_of_the_Arts "University of North Carolina School of the Arts").[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-:0-2) At age seventeen, he moved to [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City "New York City") to attend [Juilliard School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juilliard_School "Juilliard School"), graduating in 2001 with a [Bachelor of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts "Bachelor of Arts") in drama.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-:0-2)[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-6)

### 2000s: Early years and breakthrough

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Anthony_Mackie&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: 2000s: Early years and breakthrough")]

In 2002, Mackie worked as an [understudy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Understudy "Understudy") to [Don Cheadle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Cheadle "Don Cheadle") in [Suzan-Lori Parks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzan-Lori_Parks "Suzan-Lori Parks")' play _[Topdog/Underdog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topdog/Underdog "Topdog/Underdog")_ and won an [OBIE Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OBIE_Award "OBIE Award") for his role in [Carl Hancock Rux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Hancock_Rux "Carl Hancock Rux")'s play _[Talk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talk\_(play) "Talk (play)")_. He appeared in the 2002 film _[8 Mile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8\_Mile\_(film) "8 Mile (film)")_ as the main antagonist, Papa Doc. His first starring role in a feature film was the 2004 independent film _[Brother to Brother](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brother\_to\_Brother\_(film) "Brother to Brother (film)")_, where he played Perry, a young artist who struggles to adjust to the world as a gay black man. The following year, Mackie appeared in _[Million Dollar Baby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Million\_Dollar\_Baby "Million Dollar Baby")_, which won the [Academy Award for Best Picture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Picture "Academy Award for Best Picture"), and starred in [Spike Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spike_Lee "Spike Lee")'s _[She Hate Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She\_Hate\_Me "She Hate Me")_. In 2006, Mackie starred in _[Half Nelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Half\_Nelson\_(film) "Half Nelson (film)")_, _[Crossover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossover\_(2006\_film) "Crossover (2006 film)")_, and _[We Are Marshall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We\_Are\_Marshall "We Are Marshall")_.[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-7)[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-8)

In March 2008, Mackie starred in three plays by playwright [August Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_Wilson "August Wilson") at the [John F. Kennedy Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_F._Kennedy_Center "John F. Kennedy Center") for the Performing Arts in Washington DC: _[Ma Rainey's Black Bottom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ma\_Rainey's\_Black\_Bottom "Ma Rainey's Black Bottom")_, _[Fences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fences\_(play) "Fences (play)")_, and _[Jitney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jitney\_(play) "Jitney (play)")_ – all part of "August Wilson's 20th Century", a month-long presentation of ten staged readings of Wilson's "Century Cycle". Mackie has participated several times in the "24-Hour Plays" held in New York City each fall.[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-9) In mid-2009, he played the role of [Pentheus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentheus "Pentheus") in the New York City [Public Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Theater "Public Theater")'s [Shakespeare in the Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shakespeare_in_the_Park "Shakespeare in the Park") production of _[The Bacchae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Bacchae "The Bacchae")_.[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-10) He starred with [Christopher Walken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Walken "Christopher Walken") in _[A Behanding in Spokane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A\_Behanding\_in\_Spokane "A Behanding in Spokane")_ on Broadway in February 2010.[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-11)

Mackie portrayed late American [rapper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapping "Rapping")[Tupac Shakur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tupac_Shakur "Tupac Shakur") in the 2009 film _[Notorious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notorious\_(2009\_film) "Notorious (2009 film)")_. He had previously portrayed Shakur in the play _Up Against the Wind_ in 2001, while attending Juilliard. In 2009, he appeared in _[The Hurt Locker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Hurt\_Locker "The Hurt Locker")_. Mackie also narrated _The Best That Never Was_, a documentary about football player [Marcus Dupree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcus_Dupree "Marcus Dupree").

### 2010s: Marvel and mainstream breakthrough

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Anthony_Mackie&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: 2010s: Marvel and mainstream breakthrough")]

He appeared in the 2011 film _[The Adjustment Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Adjustment\_Bureau "The Adjustment Bureau")_ as Harry Mitchell, a sympathetic member of a shadowy supernatural group that controls human destiny. Mackie co-starred, as [Sam Wilson / Falcon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Wilson_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Sam Wilson (Marvel Cinematic Universe)"), in the [Marvel Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Studios "Marvel Studios")[sequel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequel "Sequel")_[Captain America: The Winter Soldier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain\_America:\_The\_Winter\_Soldier "Captain America: The Winter Soldier")_ (2014).[[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-CaptainATWS-12)[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-13) He reprised the role in multiple [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe "Marvel Cinematic Universe") films over the next several years, including 2015's _[Avengers: Age of Ultron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:\_Age\_of\_Ultron "Avengers: Age of Ultron")_[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-AgeofUltron-14) and _[Ant-Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ant-Man\_(film) "Ant-Man (film)")_, 2016's _[Captain America: Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain\_America:\_Civil\_War "Captain America: Civil War")_, 2018's _[Avengers: Infinity War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:\_Infinity\_War "Avengers: Infinity War")_, and 2019's _[Avengers: Endgame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:\_Endgame "Avengers: Endgame")_.[[15]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-CivilWar-15) Of these, _Avengers: Infinity War_ and _Avengers: Endgame_ rank among the [highest-grossing films of all time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_films "List of highest-grossing films").[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-16)

In 2016, Mackie portrayed [Martin Luther King Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Luther_King_Jr. "Martin Luther King Jr.") in the HBO TV drama _[All the Way](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All\_the\_Way\_(2016\_film) "All the Way (2016 film)")_.[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-17) In 2018, Mackie appeared as gang leader King in _[The Hate U Give](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Hate\_U\_Give\_(film) "The Hate U Give (film)")_, a film adaptation of the bestselling [novel of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hate_U_Give "The Hate U Give").[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-18) In 2019, Mackie had a role in the [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix")[science-fiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_fiction "Science fiction") film, _[IO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IO\_(film) "IO (film)")_.[[19]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-19)

In July 2018, it was announced that Mackie was cast in the role of [Takeshi Kovacs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Takeshi_Kovacs "Takeshi Kovacs") for the second season of Netflix's science-fiction series, _[Altered Carbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altered\_Carbon\_(TV\_series) "Altered Carbon (TV series)")_.[[20]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-20) In March 2019, it was announced that Mackie was cast in the fifth season of Netflix's science-fiction [anthology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthology_series "Anthology series") series, _[Black Mirror](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black\_Mirror "Black Mirror")_.[[21]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-21)

### 2020s: Leading roles

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Anthony_Mackie&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: 2020s: Leading roles")]

The following month, [Disney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney "Disney") confirmed a Marvel television series starring Mackie and [Sebastian Stan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Stan "Sebastian Stan"), called _[The Falcon and the Winter Soldier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Falcon\_and\_the\_Winter\_Soldier "The Falcon and the Winter Soldier")_, would be aired on their upcoming [Disney+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney+ "Disney+") streaming service, debuting on March 19, 2021.[[22]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-22)[[23]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-23) Mackie starred in and produced the science fiction film _[Outside the Wire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outside\_the\_Wire "Outside the Wire")_ which was released by Netflix on January 15, 2021.[[24]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-24) In August 2021, Mackie closed the deal to reprise the role in the fourth _Captain America_ film,[[25]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-25)_[Captain America: Brave New World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain\_America:\_Brave\_New\_World "Captain America: Brave New World")_.[[26]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-26)

In January 2022, it was announced that Mackie will helm the drama film _Spark_, starring Saniyya Sidney, as his directorial debut project.[[27]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-27) In February 2022, he was set to lead action film _Ending Things_ along with [Priyanka Chopra Jonas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priyanka_Chopra_Jonas "Priyanka Chopra Jonas"), directed by Kevin Sullivan[[28]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-28) and star in a live-action series [adaptation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twisted_Metal_(TV_series) "Twisted Metal (TV series)") of the game _[Twisted Metal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twisted\_Metal "Twisted Metal")_ at Peacock.[[29]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-29) In 2022, it was announced Mackie would be attached to a film based on the [1956 Sugar Bowl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1956_Sugar_Bowl "1956 Sugar Bowl") which features his brother's Alma mater [Georgia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Tech "Georgia Tech").[[30]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-30)[[31]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-31) Mackie also appears in the 2024 documentary _[Shark Beach with Anthony Mackie: Gulf Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shark\_Beach\_with\_Anthony\_Mackie:\_Gulf\_Coast "Shark Beach with Anthony Mackie: Gulf Coast")._

Mackie is set to reprise his role as Captain America in the upcoming _Avengers_ movies, _[Avengers: Doomsday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:\_Doomsday "Avengers: Doomsday")_ (2026) and _[Avengers: Secret Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:\_Secret\_Wars "Avengers: Secret Wars")_ (2027).[[32]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-32)

## Personal life

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Anthony_Mackie&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Personal life")]

In 2014, Mackie married his long-time girlfriend and childhood sweetheart Sheletta Chapital. They divorced in 2018. The ex-couple have four sons together and the family lives in [New Orleans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans "New Orleans").[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-:1-4)[[33]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-33) In February 2025, Mackie was asked about his current love life and said he was just focusing on raising his children because: "The idea of dating is really hard. How do you trust someone? How do you put yourself in a position to be vulnerable? How do you know what someone really [wants] from you when all you want is to just be loved and appreciated? What does that look like?”.[[34]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-34)

Mackie opened a bar called NoBar in [Bedford–Stuyvesant, Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bedford%E2%80%93Stuyvesant,_Brooklyn "Bedford–Stuyvesant, Brooklyn") in the summer of 2011.[[35]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-35) He had plans to open a second NoBar in [Williamsburg, Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williamsburg,_Brooklyn "Williamsburg, Brooklyn") in 2013.[[36]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-36)

In November 2013, Mackie was arrested for driving while intoxicated after he was initially stopped by police for having tinted windows, which are illegal in New York. He was pulled over at 1:22 a.m., officers noticed an odor of alcohol and observed that Mackie had bloodshot eyes. He was fined $300, ordered to complete a drunk driving program and banned from the road for 90 days in New York state for the violation.[[37]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-37)

Mackie has [dyslexia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyslexia "Dyslexia").[[38]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie#cite_note-38)
