_**Repentance**_ is a 2013 American [psychological horror](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological_horror "Psychological horror") film written and directed by Philippe Caland, co-written by Shintaro Shimosawa and Yusuf Hassan and starring [Forest Whitaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest_Whitaker "Forest Whitaker"), [Anthony Mackie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie "Anthony Mackie"), [Mike Epps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Epps "Mike Epps"), [Nicole Ari Parker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Ari_Parker "Nicole Ari Parker") and [Sanaa Lathan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanaa_Lathan "Sanaa Lathan"). _Repentance_ is the first major film production of [CodeBlack Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CodeBlack_Entertainment "CodeBlack Entertainment") since CodeBlack's merger with [Lions Gate Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lions_Gate_Entertainment "Lions Gate Entertainment") in May 2012.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Repentance_(2013_film)#cite_note-2) The film had its limited release in [US](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States "United States") theaters on February 28, 2014.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Repentance_(2013_film)#cite_note-3)

## Plot summary

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Repentance_(2013_film)&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Plot summary")]

Years after a drunken car crash that almost took his life, Tommy Carter ([Mackie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie "Anthony Mackie")) has reinvented himself as a therapist/spiritual advisor who advocates a synthesis of world religions and positivity. He has parlayed this vocation into a successful book release that one day draws the attention of Angel Sanchez ([Whitaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest_Whitaker "Forest Whitaker")), a profoundly troubled man fixated on the "untimely" death of his mother. When Carter takes on Sanchez as a personal client in an effort to raise funds for his indebted brother Ben ([Epps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Epps "Mike Epps")), things quickly take a turn for the worse. Angel needs much more than a simple life coach. Plagued by visions of his dead mother, a seance is held by Carter in order for Angel to say goodbye to his mother at which point he suffers a mild breakdown. Subsequently, Carter informs Angel that he can no longer treat him. Angel then incapacitates and captures Carter torturing him over the course of days- to confess his sins so to speak. After capturing Carter's wife and brother it is revealed that it was the brothers who accidentally ran over Angel's mother the night of their accident. Ben then threw her, still alive and crying out for help over a bridge to her death. Upon this revelation Angel untapes Carter, leaving him his gun and walks out. As he walks away from his home with his daughter he sees his smiling mother looking down on him and a gunshot rings out, as Carter narrates that both brothers souls are tainted from what happened with them and Angel's mother that night.

## Release

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Repentance_(2013_film)&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Release")]

_Repentance_ opened on February 28, 2014, in limited release. The film's opening weekend gross was $501,290 in 152 North American theaters (a per theater average of $3,298).[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Repentance_(2013_film)#cite_note-4) The film remained in theaters in limited release for 4 weeks, earning $1,189,612 in its entire run. _Repentance_ was available on Digital HD and [Video on Demand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_on_Demand "Video on Demand") on June 10, 2014, and was released on [Blu-ray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blu-ray "Blu-ray") and [DVD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DVD "DVD") on June 24, 2014.[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Repentance_(2013_film)#cite_note-5)[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Repentance_(2013_film)#cite_note-6)

## Reception

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Repentance_(2013_film)&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Reception")]

_Repentance_ was poorly received by critics. On the [review aggregator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_aggregator "Review aggregator") website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes"), 33% of six critics' reviews are positive, with an average rating of 3.2/10.[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Repentance_(2013_film)#cite_note-Rotten_Tomatoes-7)
