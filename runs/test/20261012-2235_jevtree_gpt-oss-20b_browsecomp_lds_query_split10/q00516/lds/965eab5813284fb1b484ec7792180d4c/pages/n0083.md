[Anthony Mackie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie "Anthony Mackie") is an American actor. The following is his filmography throughout his entire acting career. He has received multiple awards and nominations, including nominations for two [Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmy_Awards "Emmy Awards") and a [Screen Actors Guild Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actor_Awards "Actor Awards").
