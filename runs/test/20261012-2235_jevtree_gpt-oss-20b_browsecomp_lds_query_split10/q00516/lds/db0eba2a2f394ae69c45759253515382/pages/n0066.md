# Best Journalism Movies, Ranked by Tomatometer

* * *

**The Latest:** Academy Award-winner [Aaron Sorkin](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/aaron_sorkin) follows up his celebrated film about Facebook, _[The Social Network](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/the-social-network)_, with another exposé on [Mark Zuckerberg](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/mark_zuckerberg), with [**_The Social Reckoning_**](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/the_social_reckoning). Get your tickets at [Fandango](https://www.fandango.com/the-social-reckoning-2026-246392/movie-overview?cmp=rt_leaderboard&a=13036).

* * *

It’s time to track down your leads, dive into your research, and follow up with your informants. What you’ll find at the end of your investigation is the opportunity of a lifetime, to break the biggest story of the century: **Our guide to the best journalism movies of all time!**

There’s no need for a second pot of coffee, and you can put away that tape recorder you’ve hidden in your jacket. We’ve done all the work for you, uncovering everything from classics, like Bob Woodward and Carl Bernstein’s investigation into the Watergate scandal in [_All the President’s Men_](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/all_the_presidents_men), to hidden gems, such as Ava DuVernay’s examination of caste systems throughout Germany, India, and the United States in her critically acclaimed 2023 film, [_Origin_](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/origin_2023).

We’ve kept our list to narratives, so acclaimed documentaries like [_Citzenfour_](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/citizenfour), [_20 Days in Mariupol_](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/20_days_in_mariupol), and [_City of Ghosts_](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/city_of_ghosts_2017) were left off, but there’s bound to be something here that’ll scratch your investigative itch, whether it’s [_The Insider_](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/insider), _[She Said](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/she\_said)_, or Steven Spielberg’s _[The Post](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/the\_post)_. We rank them from Certified Fresh, followed by Fresh, and then Rotten-rated, but any one of these are sure to expose you to the truth!
