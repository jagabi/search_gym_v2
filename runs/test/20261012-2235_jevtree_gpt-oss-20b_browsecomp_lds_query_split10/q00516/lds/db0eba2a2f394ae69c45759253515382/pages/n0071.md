# Daniel Brühl

Birth Name:

Daniel César Martín Brühl González

Birth Place:

Barcelona, Spain

Profession

Actor, Producer, Director, Writer, Soundtrack

![Image 18: Daniel Brühl](https://www.tvguide.com/a/img/resize/743534bda558bbc8fed47ffbf5a7fa5db7d1b537/catalog/provider/2/13/2-2cbc0d9aceb0eae3a47d63fab9418b29.jpg?auto=webp&fit=crop&height=304&quality=70&width=200)

## Fast Facts

*   Was awarded the Bavarian and German Film Prize in 2002 for his role as a schizophrenic teenager in "The White Sound․"
*   Son of television director Hanno Bruhl
*   Became interested in acting because of his father's work on documentaries for television in Germany
*   Quote: "[On whether acting is his 'calling'] "I guess so․ That's what made it so easy in my life․ For me, as of the age of probably 15, I was sure that this is the thing I want to do․""
*   Quote: "[Why he got into acting] "It has something to do with liking to get attention, and also lying, or pretending something․ Like, my special thing as a kid was to play dead․․․I realized that I was good at it because each time my mom would scream․""
*   Consulted friend and "Rush" co-star Chris Helmsworth about taking the role of the villian in "Captain America: Civil War․"
*   Owns a tapas restaurant in Berlin frequently visited by celebrities
*   Speaks fluent Spanish, French, German, English and Portuguese
