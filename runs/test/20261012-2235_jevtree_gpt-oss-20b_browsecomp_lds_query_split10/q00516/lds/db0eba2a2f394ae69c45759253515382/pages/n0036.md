Wayne K @WayneKelso Sep 9, 2026 While I’m not a motor racing fan I still really enjoy films about the sport, such as F1, Ford V. Ferrari, Gran Turismo and, as of this morning, Rush. Based on one of the most intense rivalries in F1 history, a fact the film often exaggerates for dramatic purposes, it succeeds in the way it shows the 2 differing personalities and lifestyles, and how each has the same goal but a different motivation. Hunt is the hedonist who loves life and the opportunities motor racing gives him, Lauda is the tactician who uses ever brain cell at his disposal to achieve victory. Their relationships, especially Lauda’s, are badly neglected, making their wives and girlfriends feel like footnotes in their lives, and the racing scenes can be heavy on CGI & aggressive on editing. That said, they’re still exciting and dramatic, helped along by Hans Zimmer’s score. Even if you have no idea who either man is or what their contribution to motorsport was, Rush tells a story that’s very easy to get caught up in.
