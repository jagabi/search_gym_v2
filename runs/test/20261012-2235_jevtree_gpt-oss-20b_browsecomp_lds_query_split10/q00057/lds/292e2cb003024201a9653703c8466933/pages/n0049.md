# FDA Updates

[FDA Approval of Daraxonrasib: A New Era for Pancreatic Cancer Treatment](https://www.jons-online.com/web-exclusives/fda-approval-of-daraxonrasib)

[Breaking News](https://www.jons-online.com/jons-categories/167:breaking-news), [FDA Updates](https://www.jons-online.com/browse-by-topic/fda-approvals-news-updates)

[Web Exclusives](https://www.jons-online.com/web-exclusives)

Daraxonrasib (Rasonque) is now FDA approved for adults with metastatic pancreatic adenocarcinoma after prior systemic therapy or for those who are not candidates for multiagent regimens.

[Adagrasib Gains FDA Accelerated Approval for NSCLC with KRAS G12C Mutation](https://www.jons-online.com/lung-cancer-monthly-minutes/adagrasib-gains-fda-accelerated-approval-for-nsclc-with-kras-g12c-mutation)

[Lung Cancer](https://www.jons-online.com/lung-cancer-monthly-minutes), [FDA Updates](https://www.jons-online.com/browse-by-topic/fda-approvals-news-updates), [Web Exclusives](https://www.jons-online.com/jons-categories/146:web-exclusive)

[Web Exclusives](https://www.jons-online.com/web-exclusives)

Based on positive results of the KRYSTAL-1 clinical trial, adagrasib was granted accelerated FDA approval for patients with non–small-cell lung cancer harboring the _KRAS_ G12C mutation.

* * *

[Opdivo-Yervoy First Immunotherapy Combo Approved for First-Line Therapy of Patients with Metastatic NSCLC](https://www.jons-online.com/browse-by-topic/fda-approvals-news-updates/opdivo-yervoy-first-immunotherapy-combo-approved-for-first-line-therapy-of-patients-with-metastatic-nsclc)

[FDA Updates](https://www.jons-online.com/browse-by-topic/fda-approvals-news-updates), [Lung Cancer](https://www.jons-online.com/lung-cancer-monthly-minutes)

[July 2020 Vol 11, No 7](https://www.jons-online.com/issues/2020/july-2020-vol-11-no-7)

On May 15, 2020, the FDA approved a new indication for the first immunotherapy combination with the PD-1 inhibitor nivolumab (Opdivo; BMS) and the CTLA-4 inhibitor ipilimumab (Yervoy; BMS) for the first-line treatment of adults with metastatic non–small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC) and PD-L1 expression of ≥1%, as determined by an FDA-approved test, but without _EGFR_ or _ALK_ genomic translocations.

* * *

[FDA Approves New 400 mg Every-6-Weeks Dosing Regimen of Keytruda for All Indications](https://www.jons-online.com/browse-by-topic/fda-approvals-news-updates/fda-approves-new-400-mg-every-6-weeks-dosing-regimen-of-keytruda-for-all-indications)

[FDA Updates](https://www.jons-online.com/browse-by-topic/fda-approvals-news-updates), [In the News](https://www.jons-online.com/browse-by-topic/in-the-news)

[Web Exclusives](https://www.jons-online.com/web-exclusives)

On April 29, 2020, the FDA accelerated the approval of the oral poly ADP-ribose polymerase (PARP) inhibitor niraparib (Zejula; GlaxoSmithKline) as the first and only drug approved for first-line maintenance therapy of all patients with advanced ovarian cancer—including epithelial ovarian, fallopian tube, or primary peritoneal cancer—who have had a complete or partial response to first-line platinum-based chemotherapy.

[FDA Updates](https://www.jons-online.com/browse-by-topic/fda-approvals-news-updates), [In the News](https://www.jons-online.com/browse-by-topic/in-the-news)

On April 29, 2020, the FDA accelerated the approval of the oral poly ADP-ribose polymerase (PARP) inhibitor niraparib (Zejula; GlaxoSmithKline) as the first and only drug approved for first-line maintenance therapy of all patients with advanced ovarian cancer—including epithelial ovarian, fallopian tube, or primary peritoneal cancer—who have had a complete or partial response to first-line platinum-based chemotherapy.

[FDA Grants Priority Review to New Drug Application for the RET Kinase Inhibitor Selpercatinib](https://www.jons-online.com/web-exclusives/fda-grants-priority-review-to-new-drug-application-for-the-ret-kinase-inhibitor-selpercatinib)

[FDA Updates](https://www.jons-online.com/browse-by-topic/fda-approvals-news-updates), [In the News](https://www.jons-online.com/browse-by-topic/in-the-news), [Lung Cancer](https://www.jons-online.com/lung-cancer-monthly-minutes)

[Web Exclusives](https://www.jons-online.com/web-exclusives)

On January 29, 2020, Eli Lilly announced that the FDA granted priority review to their New Drug Application for selpercatinib (LOXO-292) for the treatment of patients with advanced _RET_ fusion-positive non–small-cell lung cancer, _RET_-mutant medullary thyroid cancer, and _RET_ fusion-positive thyroid cancer. The FDA is aiming to provide its decision on the approval of selpercatinib in the third quarter of 2020.

* * *

[FDA Grants Approval to Avapritinib for Patients with GIST](https://www.jons-online.com/web-exclusives/fda-grants-approval-to-avapritinib-for-patients-with-gist)

[FDA Updates](https://www.jons-online.com/browse-by-topic/fda-approvals-news-updates), [In the News](https://www.jons-online.com/browse-by-topic/in-the-news)

[Web Exclusives](https://www.jons-online.com/web-exclusives)

The FDA approval of avapritinib marks the first time a drug has been approved specifically for patients with GIST harboring _PDGFRA_ exon 18 mutations, which are involved in approximately 10% of GIST cases.

[Web Exclusives](https://www.jons-online.com/web-exclusives)

The FDA has confirmed another strong year regarding the approval of innovative new drugs and biosimilars, including several agents used in the treatment of patients with cancer.
