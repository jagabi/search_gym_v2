# Online Physician Referral Form

## What to Expect

Complete the form below to refer your patient to UT MD Anderson. For assistance, contact our Referring Provider Team at 1-855-502-3701, option 1.

To assist in the referral process, you may upload your [associated medical records](https://my.mdanderson.org/physicianreferral#) and insurance information on the next screen. Please gather any relevant documentation you wish to include before completing the form. Documents must meet the following requirements:

1.   Each document page should include the patient's name and date of birth
2.   All documents are in English prior to submission
