# Grants and Special Assessments

## Overview

There are a number of programs that provide grants and incentives for specific purposes such as public safety, services and infrastructure.

Open All  

[Rural Law Enforcement Grants (SB 22)](https://comptroller.texas.gov/economy/development/grants/rural/)
This program provides financial assistance to sheriff’s departments, constable’s offices and district and county attorney’s offices in eligible counties to ensure professional law enforcement and legal representation of the people’s interests throughout the state.

Other Resources for Rural Communities

The Broadband Development Office
To streamline broadband expansion across the state and allow every Texan to provide feedback about connectivity needs, the Broadband Development Office (BDO) established a robust external engagement process that fosters collaboration with local, regional and tribal entities.

If you would like to learn more about the BDO or provide input related to your community’s unique needs, please take advantage of these engagement opportunities. Your insight is essential to close the digital divide in Texas.

Refer to [The Broadband Development Office](https://comptroller.texas.gov/programs/broadband/) for program details and [BDO Outreach](https://comptroller.texas.gov/programs/broadband/outreach/) for how you can get involved.

Grants.gov
**[Grants.gov](http://www.grants.gov/)** is a central storehouse for information on over 1,000 federal grant programs and provides access to approximately $500 billion in annual awards

Health Resources & Services Administration
The **[Health Resources & Services Administration](http://www.hrsa.gov/grants/index.html)** makes grants to organizations to improve and expand health care services for underserved people in a variety of different areas including rural health programs.

Rural Health Information Hub
The **[Rural Health Information Hub](https://www.ruralhealthinfo.org/states/texas/funding)** is a product of the Federal Office of Rural Health Policy whose goal is to help rural communities and other rural stakeholders access the full range of available programs, funding, and research that can enable them to provide quality health and human services to rural residents.

Texas Department of Agriculture Grants and Services 
The **[Texas Department of Agriculture Grants and Services](http://www.texasagriculture.gov/GrantsServices/GrantsandServices.aspx)** compiles a variety of programs and funds to help enhance farms, rural business development, help establish permanent jobs, and the production of agriculture.
