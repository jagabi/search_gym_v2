# Refer a Patient

Thank you for referring your patient to UT MD Anderson for prevention, early detection, diagnosis, treatment or survivorship oncology services. When you refer to UT MD Anderson, your patient will receive a high level of care in a safe, compassionate environment. We provide oncology services at multiple locations across Houston, making it easy and convenient for your patients.

To refer a patient located in the United States, contact our Referring Provider Team and provide the patient/provider information listed below:

**[Referring Provider Team](https://www.mdanderson.org/for-physicians/refer-a-patient/referring-provider-team.html)  
 Phone:**1-877-632-6789, option 1  
**Web:**Utilize our fast patient online referral form at [MDAnderson.org/NewPatientReferralForm](https://my.mdanderson.org/physicianreferral)  
**Email:**[PhysicianReferrals@MDAnderson.org](mailto:PhysicianReferrals@MDAnderson.org)  
**Fax:** 713-563-2449,[Fax form](https://www.mdanderson.org/content/dam/mdanderson/documents/for-physicians/MDA%20Physician%20Fax%20Form.pdf)  
**EpicCare Link:**[MyLink.MDAnderson.org](https://mylink.mdanderson.org/EpicCareLink/common/epic_login.asp)  
**Hours:** Monday-Friday: 8 a.m.-11 p.m., Saturday-Sunday: 8 a.m.-7 p.m., CST

**Patient referral information:**

*   Legal name
*   Date of birth
*   Telephone number
*   Reason for referral
*   Medical or pathology reports pertaining to the referral
*   _Is the patient aware of the referral to UT MD Anderson?_
*   _Is the patient currently admitted in the hospital?_

**Referring health care provider information:**

*   Health care provider name and NPI
*   Point of contact
*   Practice name
*   Practice address
*   Practice telephone and fax number
*   Email (if available)

### Referral Process

Once our Referring Provider Team receives a referral, the following process occurs:

1.   **Referral is created and the provider is contacted.** A Health Information Specialist will initiate a referral, guide you through the process and answer your questions. _If you submitted a referral online, through EpicCare Link, e-mail or fax and have not received confirmation of receipt within two business days, please call the Referring Provider Team._
2.   **Patient is contacted.**The patient is contacted by a Patient Access Representative who will gather additional demographic and medical information.
3.   **Medical records are reviewed.**If additional medical reports are needed, we will request those from the patient or provider’s office. Patients are encouraged to take an active role in providing these records.
4.   **Insurance is verified.** We will verify insurance and seek financial authorization.
5.   **Patient receives an appointment.** The patient will receive an email with appointment details and instructions on how to download our MyChart application.
6.   **Referring provider is contacted.** A Health Information Specialist will follow-up with the referring provider regarding the patient’s appointment referral status.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I obtain my patient's medical records, radiology reports and laboratory results from UT MD Anderson?

*   Update your communication preferences through our [Referring Provider Team](https://www.mdanderson.org/for-physicians/refer-a-patient/referring-provider-team.html) to receive by fax or mail.
*   Retrieve reports by logging into Epic or by using the [myMDAnderson for Physicians](https://mylink.mdanderson.org/EpicCareLink/common/epic_login.asp) EpicCare Link portal.
*   Call our Health Information Management department at 713-792-6710.
*   Complete our [Release of Information authorization form](https://www.mdanderson.org/content/dam/mdanderson/documents/patients-and-family/becoming-our-patient/services-and-amenities/Patient%20Authorization%20Disclosure%20Form.pdf) and send by fax to 855-884-3253 or email to [ROI@MDAnderson.org](mailto:roi@mdanderson.org).

### How do I obtain my patient’s medical images from UT MD Anderson?

Contact our Film Library at 713-792-6210.

### Do I need a confirmed diagnosis to refer a patient to UT MD Anderson?

No, many of our cancer centers will see patients with a suspicion of cancer or benign tumors.

### Which insurance plans are in-network at UT MD Anderson?

View our [Insurance, Billing and Financial Support](https://www.mdanderson.org/patients-family/becoming-our-patient/planning-for-care/insurance-billing-financial-support.html) page for detailed information.

### What financial assistance options are available for my patients with no insurance?

*   Texas residents that meet eligibility requirements set by the Federal Poverty Index can request an application for [Patient Financial Assistance](https://www.mdanderson.org/content/dam/mdanderson/documents/patients-and-family/becoming-our-patient/planning-for-care/Patient-Financial-Assistance-Application.pdf) from UT MD Anderson.
*   For more information on UT MD Anderson’s financial assistance program, download: [Patient Financial Assistance: Information for Patients](https://www.mdanderson.org/content/dam/mdanderson/documents/patients-and-family/becoming-our-patient/planning-for-care/Patient-Financial-Assistance-Overview.pdf) or visit: [Uncompensated Care Program](https://www.mdanderson.org/patients-family/becoming-our-patient/planning-for-care/insurance-billing-financial-support/uncompensated-care-program.html).

### How do I know if my patient has an appointment with UT MD Anderson?

*   The Referring Provider Team follows up with the referring provider regarding the patient’s appointment status.
*   Contact the [Referring Provider Team](https://www.mdanderson.org/for-physicians/refer-a-patient/referring-provider-team.html) if you have submitted a referral online, through EpicCare Link, e-mail or fax methods and have not received confirmation of receipt within two business days.

### What if my patient speaks another language?

*   UT MD Anderson has [certified interpreters](https://www.mdanderson.org/patients-family/becoming-our-patient/international-center.html) for the following languages: Arabic, French, Mandarin, Spanish, Vietnamese and American Sign Language. We also provide interpreters for languages not listed, but a 24-hour notice is required.
*   Telephone interpreter services are available 24/7 for Spanish and Arabic.
*   Telecommunications devices (TDD) are available for deaf and hard-of-hearing patients.
*   Language assistance services are requested by the International Center staff for new patients and by the clinical team at the time of need for current and returning patients.

### I prefer my patients to contact UT MD Anderson and setup their own appointment. What patient-specific pathway should I recommend to them that allows me to receive follow-up notes?

*   Encourage your patients to list you as their follow-up physician and part of their care team.
*   Phone: 877-632-6789, option 1, Monday to Friday: 8 a.m. to 11 p.m., Saturday to Sunday: 8 a.m. to 7 p.m., CST
*   Online: [appointment request form](https://my.mdanderson.org/RequestAppointment)

### How do I enroll my patient in a clinical trial or review clinical trial options?

*   Providers and patients are encouraged to search through our [clinical trial database](https://www.mdanderson.org/patients-family/diagnosis-treatment/clinical-trials.html) to view the many types available.
*   Participation in a clinical trial requires a patient to schedule an appointment with UT MD Anderson to discuss treatment opportunities and eligibility criteria.
*   The Referring Provider Team can help providers to identify the appropriate research staff to discuss clinical trials and eligibility criteria specific to their patients.
