### Open your research with confidence

Our global open access publishing teams work each day across a diverse journal portfolio that ranges from endocrinology to ethnography. We are proud of the rigorous publishing practice throughout our portfolio, and robustness in our systems and platforms that reach hundreds of millions of researchers each year.

Sage’s independence means we’re free to offer you the personal support you need. We connect you with the right experts for peer review and we’ll work together to make your research the best it can be – and to maximize your impact after publication.

  

Research demands integrity. It’s been at the heart of our approach for sixty years and will endure as we anticipate the needs of the future.

### **Disciplines**

### **Special Collections**

Special Collections at Sage are curated for each journal to feature topics that are trending, that foster cross-disciplinary research, or explore particularly important topics in depth. Our specialist editorial teams work closely with each journal’s Editor-in-Chief to facilitate the assessment of all Special Collection proposals submitted by Guest Editor applicants, and they also oversee the launch of successful proposals into live collections. Every manuscript submitted to a Special Collection goes through the same robust and independent peer-review process that Sage applies to any other paper we publish.
