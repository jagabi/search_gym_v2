## **Information for authors**

### Overview

Whether you're a first-time author or a seasoned researcher, navigating the publishing process can be both exciting and complex. This collection of resources is designed to guide you every step of the way—from developing your manuscript and selecting the right journal, to understanding peer review,open access options, and post-publication promotion.Explore practical tools, expert advice, and step-by-step guides tailored to help you publish with confidence and impact.
