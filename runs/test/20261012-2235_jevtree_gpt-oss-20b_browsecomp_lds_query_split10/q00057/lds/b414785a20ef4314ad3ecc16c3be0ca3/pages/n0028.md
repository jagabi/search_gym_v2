# Rights & permissions

The Sage Rights team is committed to ensuring that your task of obtaining permission to reuse, republish, or distribute our content is a fast and convenient experience. We encourage the sharing of knowledge in support of a healthy and informed global community while protecting our intellectual property and that of the authors and societies with whom we partner.

The following information will guide you toward the most efficient path to copyright compliant reuse of Sage content:

*   Our[books permissions](https://us.sagepub.com/support/rights-permissions/books-permissions)pages provide information regarding Sage, Corwin, CQ Press, or Learning Matters content, including how to process a permission request through the[Copyright Clearance Center(CCC)](https://us.sagepub.com/support/rights-permissions/books-permissions/process-for-requesting-permission-books)
*   Our [journals permissions](https://us.sagepub.com/journals/permissions/overview)pages contain information regarding Sage Journal content, including how to process a permission request through[RightsLink](https://us.sagepub.com/journals/permissions/process-for-requesting-permission)
*   Our[Commercial Sales](https://advertising.sagepub.com/)pages provide you with information on content sponsorships such as reprints, ePrints, special supplements, custom print, and more
*   For information on full book translations and subsidiary rights, please see our [book translations and subsidiary rights guidelines](https://us.sagepub.com/support/rights-permissions/books-permissions/translations-and-subsidiary-rights-books)
*   You may contact us directly via the[Sage Permissions Portal](https://sage-cloud.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/10)if you are unable to process your request via CCC or RightsLink, or if you have a question that is not addressed in these pages.

*   [Books permissions](https://us.sagepub.com/rights-and-permissions#books-permissions)

## Contact us

The Copyright Clearance Center (CCC) grants permissions requests on our behalf. Please see the following pages for instructions on submitting a [book content request](https://us.sagepub.com/support/rights-permissions/books-permissions/process-for-requesting-permission-books) or a[journal content request](https://us.sagepub.com/journals/permissions/process-for-requesting-permission).

  

If you cannot process your request through the CCC, please contact the Permissions team through our [Sage Permissions Portal](https://sage-cloud.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/10).
