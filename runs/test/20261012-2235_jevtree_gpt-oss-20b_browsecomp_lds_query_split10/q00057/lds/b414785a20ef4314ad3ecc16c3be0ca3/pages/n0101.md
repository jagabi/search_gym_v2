## **Therapeutic Advances Series**

**World-leading open access journals in clinical medicine**

Sage’s Therapeutic Advances series of clinical journals was launched in 2008. Now comprising 20 journals, the Therapeutic Advances family has established itself as a high-quality gold open access series with internationally renowned Editorial Boards.

Many of the journals now have Impact Factors that place them in the first quartile in their respective fields; most are indexed in the Web of Science and other scholarly indexing services, and all are listed on PubMed soon after launch.

The journals are members of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) and have stringent standards for research ethics and reporting. All articles undergo a thorough editorial evaluation and rigorous peer review, with a minimum of two independent experts. All articles are freely available via PubMed Central and the journal websites, driving high visibility and global exposure.

  

We invite you to explore the series and to get in touch with our Editors.

*   We are delighted to welcome Lauren Kelly, Bushra Mahdi, Jemima Williamson and Josh Gazmin to the Therapeutic Advances Team!

*   _Therapeutic Advances in Medical Oncology_ has built a new sub-section of its Editorial Board – its Editorial Review Board – to help speed up peer-review while ensuring that quality of reviewer reports and journal ethical standards are upheld to the highest degree.
*   The Therapeutic Advances journals now publish video abstracts, plain language summaries and infographics alongside journal articles. Speak to the journal’s Editor to find out more.
*   The _Therapeutic Advances series_ no longer accepts preclinical research, including basic laboratory research and animal studies
*   The Therapeutic Advances journals now accept Plain Language Summaries of Publications. You can find out more here: [Plain Language Summaries](https://us.sagepub.com/journals/information-for-authors/preparing-your-manuscript/plain-language-summaries)
