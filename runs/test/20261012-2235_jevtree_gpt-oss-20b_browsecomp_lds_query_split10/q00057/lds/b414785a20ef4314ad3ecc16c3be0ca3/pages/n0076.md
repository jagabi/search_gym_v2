## **Information for authors**

### Publishing guidance & support

You believe your research will make a contribution to your field, and you’re ready to share it with your peers far and wide, but how do you go about getting it published, and what exactly does that involve?   
  
If this is you, this page is a great place to start. Here you'll find guidance to help you take your first steps toward publication with confidence — from choosing the right journal and submitting your article, to understanding what happens next. You'll also find [tips on spotting fraudulent emails (opens in a new tab)](https://us.sagepub.com/docs/default-source/rp-pages/info-for-authors/author-guide-to-avoiding-fraudulent-emails3c86c8bb-5aa8-473a-8472-71a93629bab2.pdf?sfvrsn=ea785284_1)and staying safe online throughout your publishing journey.
