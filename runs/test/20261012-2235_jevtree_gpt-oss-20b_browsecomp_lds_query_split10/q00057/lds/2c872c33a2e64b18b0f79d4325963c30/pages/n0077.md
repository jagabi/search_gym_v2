# Research Funding Opportunities

**The NIH posts all notices of funding opportunities (NOFOs) on grants.gov and grants.nih.gov,**the NIH’s single official source for grant and cooperative agreement funding opportunities. For more information, see [NOT-OD-25-143](https://grants.nih.gov/grants/guide/notice-files/NOT-OD-25-143.html).

## Find Funding Opportunities

[Use the NIH Grant search tool](https://grants.nih.gov/funding/explore-nih-opportunities?orgs=NCI) to find opportunities for NCI funding. To browse all NIH grant opportunities, clear the "NCI" preset filter.

## Explore NCI Research Priorities

Review [NCI Interest Areas](https://grants.nih.gov/funding/find-a-fit-for-your-research/nih-institutes-centers-offices/NCI#interest) and [Highlighted Topics](https://grants.nih.gov/funding/find-a-fit-for-your-research/nih-institutes-centers-offices/NCI#highlighted-topics) to learn about NCI priorities for cancer research and support.

## Policy Notices and Updates

Find specific notices of policies, guidelines, and other information in the [NIH Guide for Grants and Contracts](https://grants.nih.gov/funding/nih-guide-for-grants-and-contracts?type=notices&orgs=NCI). Users can [subscribe for updates](https://grants.nih.gov/funding/nih-guide-for-grants-and-contracts/subscribe) on this page.
