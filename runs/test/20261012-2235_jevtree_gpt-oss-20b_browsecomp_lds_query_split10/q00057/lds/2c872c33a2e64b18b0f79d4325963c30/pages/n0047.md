# **Funding**

Scope Note

As the largest public funder of biomedical research in the world, NIH supports a variety of programs from grants and contracts to loan repayment. Learn about assistance programs, how to identify a potential funding organization, and past NIH funding.

## I’m ready to search for funding opportunities...

![Image 5](https://grants.nih.gov/sites/default/files/uploaded/grants-contract-img.png)

All NIH grant and cooperative agreement notices of funding opportunities (NOFOs) are posted on Grants.gov, a central portal for federal agencies to announce discretionary funding opportunities.

Due to the number and breadth of opportunities available through NIH, we’ve provided additional search tools with NIH-specific filters to help quickly identify potential opportunities of interest.

You can also find generic [Parent Announcements for Investigator-Initiated Applications.](https://grants.nih.gov/funding/nih-guide-for-grants-and-contracts/parent-announcements)

[Explore NIH Opportunities](https://grants.nih.gov/funding/explore-nih-opportunities)[Grants.gov's Search Grants](https://simpler.grants.gov/search)
