# Resources

![Image 2: Twelve C's of different shapes in green, pink, and black are set in a grid with a picture of white flowers cropped into C shapes.](https://creativecommons.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Teams-2.png)

"[White-petalled wildflowers enjoying the sunshine](https://www.flickr.com/photos/40467171@N00/2907228676)" by [LASZLO ILYES](https://www.flickr.com/photos/laszlo-photo/), here cropped, is licensed under [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/).

Browse through and access a curated collection of resources created by the CC team and community contributors. These resources are designed to provide recommendations, best practices, and other information to support you in contributing to the commons and in reusing CC-licensed content.

[CC Platform Toolkit](https://creativecommons.org/platform/toolkit/): This toolkit is for platforms where users upload content.

[Open Licensing Resources for Foundations](https://creativecommons.org/about/legal-tools-licenses/legal-tools-licenses-resources/foundations/): This toolkit is for foundations interested in adopting an open license policy.
