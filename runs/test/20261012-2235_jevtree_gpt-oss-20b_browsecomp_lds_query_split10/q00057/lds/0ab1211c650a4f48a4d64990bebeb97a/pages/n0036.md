Five months after Creative Commons hosted an Exploratory Dialogue at UNESCO House that brought the international community together to consider the promise of equitable access to heritage, we returned to Paris with a smaller group of UNESCO Member State representatives and open heritage champions. This time, the conversation had a different purpose: moving from exploring why open heritage matters to underscoring the urgency of taking action to establish international standards.

## [A Principled Approach as the Sands Shift](https://creativecommons.org/2026/09/21/a-principled-approach-as-the-sands-shift/)

## [Guidance for Indigenous Collections and Data](https://creativecommons.org/2026/09/04/guidance-for-indigenous-collections-and-data/)

by [Brigitte Vézina](https://creativecommons.org/person/brigittevezina/), [Dee Harris](https://creativecommons.org/person/deecreativecommons-org/)[Open Culture](https://creativecommons.org/category/open-culture/)![Image 6](https://creativecommons.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/beads-1024x537.png)

Still from Awasəwehlαwə́lətinα wikəwαmok | [They Returned Home](https://localcontexts.org/films/they-returned-home/)(2023), Andreas Burgess & Jane Anderson in collaboration with James Francis, Jennifer Neptune & Darren Ranco. ©️ Penobscot Nation and Local Contexts. This material carries TK Attribution, TK Outreach, and TK Non-Commerical Labels. 

Creative Commons (CC) is happy to announce the launch of a new publication entitled Guidance for Indigenous Collections and Indigenous Data: Recommendations from Creative Commons and Local Contexts. This publication is the result of a collaboration with Local Contexts, as both organizations share commitments to equitable knowledge sharing, proper attribution, and respect for diverse cultural and community protocols. It aims to support cultural heritage institutions (CHIs) in navigating the complex legal, ethical, and cultural issues regarding open access to Indigenous heritage and associated data.

## [Guidance on Using CC Licenses in an AI Ecosystem](https://creativecommons.org/2026/09/03/guidance-on-using-cc-licenses-in-an-ai-ecosystem/)

by [Creative Commons](https://creativecommons.org/person/cc/)[Licenses & Tools](https://creativecommons.org/category/licenses-tools/)![Image 7](https://creativecommons.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Social-Header-7-1024x576.png)

“LG” by Creative Commons is licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Since 2002, Creative Commons (CC) licenses have served as legally enforceable tools for digital sharing. Beyond their functionality, they also signal a commitment to open sharing in the public interest. CC’s general guidance has been to acknowledge and support all valid uses for each of our current six licenses and two public domain tools.

## [25 Years of Partnership: CC at Wikimania 2026](https://creativecommons.org/2026/08/10/cc-at-wikimania-2026/)

by [Jocelyn Miyara](https://creativecommons.org/person/jocelyncreativecommons-org/), [Annemarie Eayrs](https://creativecommons.org/person/annemarie-eayrs/), [Dee Harris](https://creativecommons.org/person/deecreativecommons-org/)[Community](https://creativecommons.org/category/community/), [Events](https://creativecommons.org/category/about-cc/events/)![Image 11: The Wikimania Paris 2026 logo surrounded by pictures from CC workshops during the conference.](https://creativecommons.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Social-Header-2-1024x576.png)

Photos by Creative Commons, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/deed.en). Logo was created by Eclos Production, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/deed.en). 

With both Wikipedia and Creative Commons turning 25 this year, our participation in Wikimania 2026 felt especially meaningful. A quarter-century in, both organizations have evolved from scrappy experiments to global infrastructure. Our community has grown through a shared commitment to openness, access, and knowledge. The weeklong conference reaffirmed that commitment as AI is reshaping how we access knowledge and share online.
