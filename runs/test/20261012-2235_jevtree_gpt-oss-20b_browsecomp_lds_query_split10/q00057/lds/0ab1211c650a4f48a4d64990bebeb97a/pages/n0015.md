## Use our tools

The CC licenses and public domain tools give everyone, from individuals to large institutions, a standardized way to grant the public permission to use their work under copyright law.

Used by Wikipedia, Flickr, The Met, Khan Academy, and millions more globally!

[About CC Licenses](https://creativecommons.org/cc-licenses)

## Innovate with us

If we want ethical and equitable AI, we must protect and nourish the commons it relies on. Learn how we’re adapting to address sharing in the age of AI, from developing new frameworks like CC Signals, strengthening licensing guidance, and advocating for policies that keep access to knowledge open and equitable.

[Explore AI And the Commons](https://creativecommons.org/ai-and-the-commons/)
