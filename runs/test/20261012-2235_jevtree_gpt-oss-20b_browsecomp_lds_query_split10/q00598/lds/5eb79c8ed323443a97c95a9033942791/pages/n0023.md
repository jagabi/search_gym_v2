## About the PMC Journal List

The PMC Journal List includes journals that:

1) [have an agreement with NLM](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/about/guidelines/#agreements) to make the final published version of all their articles, or all their NIH-funded articles, available in PMC or

2) are historically-significant journals from the [Biomedical Journal Digitization project](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/about/scanning/).

The PMC archive also includes [select final published articles](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/about/selectivedeposit/) and [author manuscripts](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/about/authorms/) from other journals that are not searchable in the PMC Journal List. If you cannot find the journal you are looking for, refer to the [PMC User Guide](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/about/userguide/#browse) for assistance.
