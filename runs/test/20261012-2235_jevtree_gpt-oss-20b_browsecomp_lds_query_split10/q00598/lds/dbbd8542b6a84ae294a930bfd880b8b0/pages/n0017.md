PubMed Central® (PMC) is a free full-text archive of biomedical and life sciences journal literature at the U.S. National Institutes of Health's National Library of Medicine (NIH/NLM)

*   ## [About PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/about/intro/) ![Image 12](https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/pd-medc-pmc-cloudpmc-viewer/production/9b1d9d87/var/data/static/img/icon-about.png)  Discover a digital archive of scholarly articles, spanning centuries of scientific research.  
*   ## [User Guide](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/about/userguide/) ![Image 13](https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/pd-medc-pmc-cloudpmc-viewer/production/9b1d9d87/var/data/static/img/icon-guide.png)  Learn how to find and read articles of interest to you.  
*   ## [Collections](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/about/collections/) ![Image 14](https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/pd-medc-pmc-cloudpmc-viewer/production/9b1d9d87/var/data/static/img/icon-collections.png)  Browse the PMC Journal List or learn about some of PMC's unique collections.  
*   ## [For Authors](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/about/submission-methods/) ![Image 15](https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/pd-medc-pmc-cloudpmc-viewer/production/9b1d9d87/var/data/static/img/icon-authors.png)  Navigate the PMC submission methods to comply with a funder mandate, expand access, and ensure preservation.

## 12.5 million articles are archived in PMC.

Content provided in part by:

*   3037   
 Full Participation Journals Journals deposit the complete contents of each issue or volume.  
*   248   
 NIH Portfolio Journals Journals deposit all NIH-funded articles as defined by the NIH Public Access Policy.  
*   44   
 Selective Deposit Programs Publisher deposits a subset of articles from a collection of journals.
