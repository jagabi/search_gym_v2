Health Published on 21 Sep 2026 ### Meet a scientist who’s dedicated decades to solving one of prosthetics’ biggest problems Prof Joan Sanders of the University of Washington has spent her career working on better prosthetics for lower limb amputees, trying to solve problems that complicate everyday life for people who have lost a limb.
