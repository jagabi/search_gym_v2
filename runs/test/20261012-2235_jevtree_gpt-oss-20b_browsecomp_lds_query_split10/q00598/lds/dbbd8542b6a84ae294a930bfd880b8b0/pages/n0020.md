## Provides a holistic understanding of brain function from genes to behavior

One of the world's most cited neuroscience journals, covering brain structure, function and disorders from cells to systems and behavior.

[About the journal](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroscience/about)

## Your research is the real superpower

Every day, your research is taking steps – no matter how big or small – to build a better world. Our job is to help it get seen by the world.

[See our impact metrics](https://www.frontiersin.org/about/impact)

## 95% of researchers rate the quality of our articles as good or excellent

Our unique approach to research integrity combines the expertise of editorial boards, the rigour of our peer review teams, and the speed and precision of AI to uphold the highest quality standards.

[Read more about our research integrity](https://www.frontiersin.org/about/research-integrity)

Join our editorial board and collaborate with a team of independent researchers from around the world, carefully selected for their field expertise.
