# Frontiers Publishing Partnerships

True collaboration with the experts in open access publishing

[Contact us](https://publishingpartnerships.frontiersin.org/contact)

## Inspire   
your society community

Since 2007 Frontiers has pioneered open science, harnessing the power of cutting-edge technology to serve researchers’ needs. With us, your society can be part of the publishing revolution, transforming the way research is evaluated, published, and communicated to the world.

Societies face growing challenges to their commercial sustainability. By collaborating with Frontiers you can navigate these challenges with a partner who will empower you to deliver on your strategic vision - creating a journal that inspires and engages your community.

**Your society journal will benefit from:**

*   fully retained society ownership of your journal

*   support from a professional team including a dedicated journal manager

*   a roster of new strategies and ideas to support the development of your journal under an open access model

*   a digital editorial office that provides your editors with full independence to manage your journal online 24 hours a day, 365 days a year

*   access to an award-winning manuscript submission and peer review platform

*   design and communication support to create innovative promotional content that reaches the right audiences

*   effective engagement with your society members, the academic community, the media and the general public

*   a transparent and sustainable financial model

Frontiers is the 3rd most-cited and 6th largest research publisher and we publish the latest discoveries by the world's top experts.   
Our mission is to make science open – so that scientists can collaborate better and innovate faster to deliver the solutions that enable healthy lives on a healthy planet.   
Powered by custom-built technology, artificial intelligence, and rigorous quality standards, our research articles have been viewed more than 1.9 billion times, reflecting the power of research that is open for all.
