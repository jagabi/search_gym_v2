### All plans include

These features come with your free trial too.

#### Access to audiobooks and ebooks with unlocks

Each unlock equals one book from our library of 1.5M+ books — yours for the duration of your subscription.

#### Unlimited access to thousands of books

Enjoy 20k+ audiobooks, ebooks, and podcasts — no unlocks required.

#### Custom reading goals, advanced stats, and community via Fable

Fable is a reading app that helps you stay consistent and connect with other readers. You’ll get Fable Plus, the premium tier, for free.

#### Your reading life updates automatically

Every book you read on Everand keeps your Fable goals, streaks, and stats current once you link your accounts, no manual tracking needed.
