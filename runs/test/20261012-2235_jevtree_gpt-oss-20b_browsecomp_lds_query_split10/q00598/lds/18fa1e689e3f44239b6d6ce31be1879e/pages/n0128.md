# Visit to theatre

**Dear Audience!**

No admittance to the auditorium after the third bell.

When planning a visit to performances on the Roof of the New Stage, please, make sure that you are familiar with the Rules of Roof visiting.

Theatre box office work daily from 11:00 until 20:00. Lunch break is from 14:00 until 15:00.

**Theatre box office: +7 (812) 401-53-41**

Tickets for repertoire performances of the Alexandrinsky Theatre are sold at a reduced price – with 10% discount from 13:00 until 14:00.

**Group ticket reservations:**[marketing@alexandrinsky.ru](mailto:marketing@alexandrinsky.ru)
