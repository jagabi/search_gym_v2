### the act

We have seen this moment coming: design systems built to scale are now moving into countless contexts, the last missing piece falling into place. As they slip from controlled environments, they enter cities, organisations, services, everyday routines.  
  
What lived on screens now acts under real conditions, shaped by use, interpretation and circumstance. Now guidelines become values, values become behaviour — how services respond under pressure, how they prioritise, how principles drive decisions at scale. In the end, might it be these acts, rather than their images, that tell us what we have really designed?
