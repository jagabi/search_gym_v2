# Ujjain Lok Sabha constituency
| Elected year | [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_general_election,_2024 "Indian general election, 2024") |
**Ujjain** is one of the 29 [Lok Sabha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lok_Sabha "Lok Sabha") constituencies in the [Indian state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_and_union_territories_of_India "States and union territories of India") of [Madhya Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madhya_Pradesh "Madhya Pradesh"). It covers the entire [Ujjain district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ujjain_district "Ujjain district") and part of [Ratlam district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ratlam_district "Ratlam district"). This constituency came into existence in 1951 as one of the [9th Lok Sabha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9th_Lok_Sabha "9th Lok Sabha") constituencies in the erstwhile [Madhya Bharat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madhya_Bharat "Madhya Bharat") state. It has been reserved for candidates belonging to [scheduled castes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalit "Dalit") since 1966.
