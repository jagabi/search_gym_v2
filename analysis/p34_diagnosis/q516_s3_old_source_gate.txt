# Fernando Meirelles
## Early life
Fernando is the second youngest of four children. He saw his older brother, José Marcos, die in a car-bike accident when he was 4 years old. His two sisters, Márcia and Silvinha, graduated in theater and psychology, respectively. Fernando grew up in [Alto dos Pinheiros, West Zone of São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alto_de_Pinheiros_(district_of_S%C3%A3o_Paulo) "Alto de Pinheiros (district of São Paulo)"), and spent every vacation on the farms of relatives from both sides of his family. "Even I have a farm. I don't know why I bought it", he was quoted as saying.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Meirelles#cite_note-4)
His first experience with cinema was with his father, who often directed [8 mm films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8_mm_film "8 mm film") during his job at the university. He produced mostly [western](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_(genre) "Western (genre)") and [thriller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thriller_(genre) "Thriller (genre)")[parodies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parody "Parody"), using his relatives and friends as actors. At 11, in 1967, Fernando spent a year in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States "United States") in [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California "California"), where he had contact with the [hippie movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_hippie_movement "History of the hippie movement"), which impressed him. At 13, with a borrowed [Super 8 camera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_8_mm_film "Super 8 mm film"), Meirelles started producing small films, inspired by [Norman McLaren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_McLaren "Norman McLaren")'s animations.[_[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation\_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")_]

# Fernando Meirelles
## Career
[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Fernando_Meirelles&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Career")]
He studied at the [Architecture and Urbanism College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architecture_and_Urbanism_College,_University_of_S%C3%A3o_Paulo "Architecture and Urbanism College, University of São Paulo") in the [University of São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_S%C3%A3o_Paulo "University of São Paulo") during the 1980s. His graduation work was done in the form of a film, instead of the traditional designs of the other students: he went to [Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan "Japan") and bought professional video equipment to do the job. He presented it and graduated with the minimum acceptable grade.
While studying [architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architecture "Architecture") at the University of queens mary, Meirelles became involved in experimental film-making. After several years in independent television, he became an advertisement film director. He is still one of the partners of O2 Filmes, the biggest Brazilian advertisement firm, which has produced _[City of God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City\_of\_God\_(2002\_film) "City of God (2002 film)")_, _[Domésticas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maids\_(film) "Maids (film)")_ and _Viva Voz_.

# Fernando Meirelles
## Career
Along with four friends (Paul Morelli, Marcelo Machado, Dário Vizeu and Bob Salatini), Meirelles began his career with experimental films. Eventually, they formed an independent production company _[Olhar Eletrônico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olhar\_Eletr%C3%B4nico?action=edit&redlink=1 "Olhar Eletrônico (page does not exist)")_. Subsequently, new friends joined the group: Renato Barbiere, Agilson Araujo, Toniko and [Marcelo Tas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcelo_Tas "Marcelo Tas"). In 1982, the company aired TV programs on current affairs, as well as the children series _[Castelo Rá-Tim-Bum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castelo\_R%C3%A1-Tim-Bum "Castelo Rá-Tim-Bum")_, with 180 episodes. In addition to obtaining high ratings, they also introduced a refreshing humorous informality in news reporting.
By the end of the 1980s, he became increasingly interested on the advertising market. In 1990, Meirelles and friends closed down _Olhar Eletrônico_, opening an advertising business, O2 Films. One decade was enough for him to become one of the most important and sought-after advertising producers.
In 1997, Meirelles read the book [Paulo Lins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paulo_Lins "Paulo Lins")'s _[City of God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City\_of\_God\_(Lins\_novel) "City of God (Lins novel)")_. He decided to adapt it to film, which was done in 2002, and decided that the actors in it would be selected among the inhabitants of slums. In a final triage, from 400 children, they selected 200, with whom they worked for the shooting of the film. The filming was done with a professional crew. The film was a national and international success.

# Fernando Meirelles
## Career
In 2004, he was nominated for the [Academy Award for Best Director](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Director "Academy Award for Best Director") for _City of God_. Also, at the [2004 Cannes Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Cannes_Film_Festival "2004 Cannes Film Festival"), the movie received four nominations: Best Director, Best Adapted Screenplay, Best Cinematography, and Best Film Editing.
With the recognition, he was offered a job in [Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinema_of_the_United_States "Cinema of the United States"). With _[The Constant Gardener](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Constant\_Gardener\_(film) "The Constant Gardener (film)")_, he again received critical acclaim, receiving several nominations, including for four [Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Awards "Academy Awards") and the [Golden Globe Award for Best Director](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Director "Golden Globe Award for Best Director"). [Rachel Weisz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Weisz "Rachel Weisz") went on to win the [Academy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actress "Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress") and the [Golden Globe Award for Best Supporting Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actress_-_Motion_Picture "Golden Globe Award for Best Supporting Actress - Motion Picture"). Meirelles insisted that the soundtrack be based on the music of African countries, and most of the filming was done in Kenya.

# Fernando Meirelles
## Career
In 2007, he began shooting _[Blindness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blindness\_(2008\_film) "Blindness (2008 film)")_, a film adaptation of Nobel-prize winner [José Saramago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Saramago "José Saramago")'s book, _[Ensaio Sobre a Cegueira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blindness\_(novel) "Blindness (novel)")_. The film, which was released in 2008, was the opening film of the [Cannes Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannes_Film_Festival "Cannes Film Festival").
He was one of the creative directors of the opening ceremony of the [2016 Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Olympics "2016 Olympics") in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro "Rio de Janeiro"), working alongside [Daniela Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniela_Thomas "Daniela Thomas") and [Andrucha Waddington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrucha_Waddington "Andrucha Waddington")[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Meirelles#cite_note-5) on a shoestring budget equivalent to less than £5 million.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Meirelles#cite_note-6)

# Fernando Meirelles
## Career
In 2019, Meirelles directed and produced the [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO") original series _[Joint Venture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pico\_da\_Neblina\_(TV\_series) "Pico da Neblina (TV series)")_, in which marijuana has been legalized in Brazil. The series follows the story of a young drug trafficker from São Paulo who, after the legalization of marijuana in Brazil, decides to abandon his life on crime and sell his weed legally along with an inexperienced investor partner. In the same year, he directed the drama film _[The Two Popes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Two\_Popes "The Two Popes")_ for [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix"), starring [Anthony Hopkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Hopkins "Anthony Hopkins") and [Jonathan Pryce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Pryce "Jonathan Pryce").
