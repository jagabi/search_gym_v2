# Ujjain Lok Sabha Election results 2024
Ujjain, a Lok Sabha constituency in Madhya Pradesh, was formed in 1951 and is one of 29 constituencies in the state. It includes Ujjain and part of Ratlam district. Reserved for scheduled caste candidates since 1966, the 2019 election saw Anil Firojiya of BJP emerge victorious with a significant margin of 3,65,637 votes over Babulal Malviya of INC. Firojiya secured 7,91,663 votes, retaining his position as the current MP. The constituency comprises Mahidpur, Ujjain Dakshin, Nagada-khachrod, Tarana (sc), Alot (sc), Badnagar, Ujjain Uttar, and Ghatiya (sc) assembly constituencies. Notable for a 75.33% voter turnout in 2019, 10 candidates contested. Ujjain Lok Sabha constituency remains a significant political area in Madhya Pradesh.
