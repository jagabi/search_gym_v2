**Michael James Radford** (born 24 February 1946) is an English film director and screenwriter.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Radford#cite_note-NYT-1) He began his career as a documentary director and television comedy writer before transitioning into features in the early 1980s.

His best-known credits include the 1984 [film adaptation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nineteen_Eighty-Four_(1984_film) "Nineteen Eighty-Four (1984 film)") of [George Orwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Orwell "George Orwell")'s _[Nineteen Eighty-Four](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nineteen\_Eighty-Four "Nineteen Eighty-Four")_ starring [John Hurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hurt "John Hurt") and [Richard Burton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Burton "Richard Burton") (in his final role), the [Shakespeare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Shakespeare "William Shakespeare") adaptation _[The Merchant of Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Merchant\_of\_Venice\_(2004\_film) "The Merchant of Venice (2004 film)")_, the [true crime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_crime "True crime") drama _[White Mischief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White\_Mischief\_(film) "White Mischief (film)")_, and the 1994 Italian-language comedy drama _[Il Postino: The Postman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Il\_Postino:\_The\_Postman "Il Postino: The Postman")_, for which he won the [BAFTA Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Awards "BAFTA Awards") for [Best Direction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award_for_Best_Direction "BAFTA Award for Best Direction") and [Best Film Not in the English Language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award_for_Best_Film_Not_in_the_English_Language "BAFTA Award for Best Film Not in the English Language"),

and earned [Academy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Awards "Academy Awards") nominations for [Best Director](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Director "Academy Award for Best Director") and [Best Adapted Screenplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Adapted_Screenplay "Academy Award for Best Adapted Screenplay").

## Early life and career
[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Michael_Radford&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Early life and career")]
Radford was born on 24 February 1946, in [New Delhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Delhi "New Delhi"), [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidencies_and_provinces_of_British_India "Presidencies and provinces of British India"), to a British father and an Austrian [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish "Jewish") mother. He was educated at [Bedford School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bedford_School "Bedford School") before attending [Worcester College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worcester_College,_Oxford "Worcester College, Oxford"). After teaching for a few years, he went to the [National Film and Television School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Film_and_Television_School "National Film and Television School"), becoming a student there in its inaugural year.

## Early life and career
Between 1976 and 1982, Radford worked as a documentary film maker, mostly on projects for the [BBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC "BBC"), covering subjects such as Scottish islanders on the [Isle of Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isle_of_Lewis "Isle of Lewis") in the [Outer Hebrides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outer_Hebrides "Outer Hebrides") who believe in the literal truth of the Bible: _The Last Stronghold of the Pure Gospel_; the soprano [Isobel Buchanan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isobel_Buchanan "Isobel Buchanan"): _La Belle Isobel_; the singer songwriter [Van Morrison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Van_Morrison "Van Morrison"): _[Van Morrison in Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Van\_Morrison\_in\_Ireland "Van Morrison in Ireland")_; and the self-explanatory _The Making of [The Pirates of Penzance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Pirates\_of\_Penzance "The Pirates of Penzance")_. On the last two of these, Radford worked with the [cinematographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinematographer "Cinematographer")[Roger Deakins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Deakins "Roger Deakins"), who would later shoot two of Radford's feature films; _[Nineteen Eighty-Four](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nineteen\_Eighty-Four\_(1984\_film) "Nineteen Eighty-Four (1984 film)")_ and _[White Mischief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White\_Mischief\_(film) "White Mischief (film)")_.

## Early life and career
Another notable early work was _[Another Time, Another Place](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Another\_Time,\_Another\_Place\_(1983\_film) "Another Time, Another Place (1983 film)")_ (1983), a feature film set in [Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotland "Scotland") during [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II "World War II") and centred on a love story between a local woman and an [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy "Italy")[POW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prisoner_of_war "Prisoner of war").
Radford came to international attention with _[Nineteen Eighty-Four](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nineteen\_Eighty-Four\_(1984\_film) "Nineteen Eighty-Four (1984 film)")_, his adaptation of [George Orwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Orwell "George Orwell")'s novel _[1984](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nineteen\_Eighty-Four "Nineteen Eighty-Four")_, starring [John Hurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hurt "John Hurt") as Winston Smith, and in which [Richard Burton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Burton "Richard Burton") gave his final film performance.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Radford#cite_note-NYT2-2) The film was made in the time and place ([London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London "London"), April–June 1984) at which the book was set.

## Early life and career
Radford's next film, released in 1987, was _[White Mischief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White\_Mischief\_(film) "White Mischief (film)")_, a period drama set in [Kenya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenya "Kenya") during the 1940s.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Radford#cite_note-NYT2-2) Radford again wrote the screenplay, an adaptation of the novel by [James Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Fox_(journalist) "James Fox (journalist)") also called _White Mischief_.

## Early life and career
Michael Radford is most widely known as the writer and director of the 1994 film _[Il Postino: The Postman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Il\_Postino:\_The\_Postman "Il Postino: The Postman")_, which Radford adapted from the novel _[Ardiente Paciencia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ardiente\_paciencia "Ardiente paciencia")_ by [Antonio Skármeta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Sk%C3%A1rmeta "Antonio Skármeta").[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Radford#cite_note-NYT-1) The massive international success of the film (for many years it was the largest grossing non-English language film ever made) led to international acclaim for Radford and the star of the film [Massimo Troisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massimo_Troisi "Massimo Troisi"). Tragically, Troisi died, aged 41, the day after the filming of _Il Postino_ was completed. The film won many international film awards including the 'Best Film Not in the English Language' [BAFTA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Academy_of_Film_and_Television_Arts "British Academy of Film and Television Arts") for Radford, who was also nominated for the [Best Director](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Director "Academy Award for Best Director") and [Adapted Screenplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Writing_Adapted_Screenplay "Academy Award for Writing Adapted Screenplay")[Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Awards "Academy Awards").
