### Biography
Gretchen A. Macht, Ph.D., is an Associate Professor in the [Department of Mechanical, Industrial & Systems Engineering](https://web.uri.edu/mcise/) and the [Founder and Executive Director of the Engineering for Democracy Institute (EDI)](http://uri.edu/edi) at [The University of Rhode Island](http://uri.edu/).
EDI, formerly URI VOTES, has created tools and guidelines, helped implement legislative changes, and addressed both election-year and future potential challenges. A computational community ergonomist, Dr. Macht applies engineering principles to improve election administration function nationwide, directly assisting 35 election jurisdictions in 18 states representing over 26 million registered voters.
Notable awards and designations include: the [40 Under Forty Award by Providence Business News](https://pbn.com/gretchen-macht-34/) in 2019, an [Election Expert by MIT](https://electionlab.mit.edu/experts) in 2021, a finalist for the 2022 URI Teaching Excellence Award, a [Human Factors and Ergonomics Society Science Policy Fellow](https://www.hfes.org/About/Advocacy-and-Public-Policy/Science-Policy-Fellows) in 2023, and awarded the [2024 URI Advanced Career Faculty Research and Scholarship Excellence Award](https://web.uri.edu/research-admin/externalrelations/excellence-call-for-nominations/excellence-award-recipients/2024-research-scholarship-and-intellectual-property-excellence-awards-recipients/).

### Biography
Her research collaborations and support include the [Rhode Island Secretary of State](https://www.sos.ri.gov/), the [Rhode Island Board of Elections](https://elections.ri.gov/), [Democracy Fund](https://democracyfund.org/), [Stanford-MIT Healthy Elections Project](https://healthyelections.org/), [National Science Foundation](https://www.nsf.gov/), [Partnership for Large Election Jurisdictions](https://taketheplej.org/), [Elections Trust Initiative](https://www.electioninitiative.org/), and the [US Elections Assistance Commission](https://www.eac.gov/). Her public talks were sponsored by [The Boston Society of Architecture](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=An3adN-i9Rg), [RI Common Cause](https://www.commoncause.org/rhode-island/), [Center for Technology and Civic Life](https://www.techandciviclife.org/covid-19-webinars/#covid-webinar12), and the [Bipartisan Policy Center](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XV6aBI4GJR8&t=2s), as well as being featured in USA Today and NPR.
Professor Macht holds degrees from [The Pennsylvania State University](http://psu.edu/), with her [Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering](https://www.ime.psu.edu/index.aspx) with a specialization in Human Factors and Ergonomics, her [M.S. and B.S. in Industrial Engineering](https://www.ime.psu.edu/index.aspx) with tracks in Manufacturing, as well as a Postdoctoral Scholar in [Architectural Engineering.](https://www.ae.psu.edu/index.aspx)
Since joining the URI faculty in 2015, she has mentored seven Ph.D. and 22 master’s students, with over 50 undergraduates participating in her research group, spanning six URI colleges and hailing from five continents. She also enjoys being a co-leader of Girl Scout Troop 418.

# Gretchen Macht Named to PBN’s 40 Under Forty List
June 11, 2019
[![Image 5: Gretchen Macht in SIS lab](https://web.uri.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/8/Gretchen-in-lab-cropped-300x208.jpg)](https://web.uri.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/8/Gretchen-in-lab-cropped.jpg)
URI Assistant Professor Gretchen Macht in the Sustainable Innovation Solutions Lab
The Providence Business News (PBN) has named University of Rhode Island Assistant Professor Gretchen Macht to its [2019 40 Under Forty](https://pbn.com/event/2019-forty-under-40/) list.
The list recognizes individuals under the age of 40 who have excelled in their chosen profession and are involved in their communities.
“I am truly honored to receive this recognition,” said Macht, who has taught systems engineering at URI since 2015. “I attribute my success to the research I’ve conducted in the [Sustainable Innovative Solutions](http://egr.uri.edu/sislab/) (SIS) Laboratory and the support I’ve received from my family and the URI community.”
In the SIS Lab, Macht and her students use technology to research sustainable systems that are based on [human factors](https://www.uri.edu/features/the-human-factor/), such as how people think and behave.
[![Image 6: PBN 40 Under Forty logo](https://web.uri.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/8/40-Under-Forty-logo-296x300.png)](https://web.uri.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/8/40-Under-Forty-logo.png)One project Macht worked on in the last year was [RI VOTES](https://today.uri.edu/news/uri-professor-students-working-to-improve-rhode-islands-voting-system/) (Voter OperaTions & Election Systems). With the goal of streamlining the voting process in Rhode Island, Macht and her team used technology to examine the source of delays at voting precincts.
“Gretchen is applying engineering to some of the greatest challenges to sustainability of our time,” said URI College of Engineering Dean Raymond Wright. “Her work is interdisciplinary and novel. It’s wonderful to see this recognition.”
